## Why

A native regression shows that Chronomon: Holy Mode's attack-triggered deletion is classified as battle deletion, incorrectly excluding MarineBullmon's Decode. Sanmyojin Arrival then manually restarts the interrupted deletion with a different cause, and its self-trash shifts field indices into a slot-based replacement guard. The resulting legal prompts depend on field position. Training diagnostics must not turn that accidental behavior into a rules contract.

## What Changes

- Distinguish effect deletion from battle and security-battle deletion at the mutation entry points, including suspended effect continuations.
- Let hand/union Delay rewards finish inside the original immediate-effect window, preserving its subject, cause, destination, and unconsumed candidates.
- Key replacement recursion/commit guards by the permanent's battle-area incarnation instead of its current field slot.
- Add native regressions for real Chronomon/MarineBullmon/Arrival interactions, field compaction, clone continuation, order/decline choices, and independent removal routes.
- Update the previous nested-selection regression to assert the corrected flow and retain coverage of genuine nested continuation behavior.

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `permanent-deletion-semantics`: preserve removal provenance and the original immediate window through nested effects; distinguish stable subjects from field positions and replayed incarnations.
