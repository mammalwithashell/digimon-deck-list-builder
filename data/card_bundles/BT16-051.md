## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] By placing 1 [Kosuke Kisakata] from your hand as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon can't leave the battle area other than by deletion.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- It means that this Digimon can no longer leave the battle area by processing other than deletion. Such processing includes "return to the hand/deck," "place in the security stack," and "place under another Digimon or Tamer." It only leaves the battle area by deletion.
