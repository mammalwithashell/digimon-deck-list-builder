## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have no face-up security cards, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Security] [All Turns] All of your Digimon with the [NSp] trait gain ＜Alliance＞. [Main] Add your bottom security card to the hand. Then, place this card face up as the bottom security card.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card with the [NSp] trait from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3962** (2024-11-22) Q: Is the "while you have no face-up security cards" condition met when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q3963** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I also activate this card’s [Main] effect when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you can't add a card from your security stack to your hand, therefore you only place this card in your security stack.
- **Q3964** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens to cards placed face up in the security stack by effects? — A: They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
- **Q3965** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens upon a security check for a security card that is placed face-up? — A: The check is performed with the card left revealed. Other than rules for cards left revealed, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security checks.
- **Q3966** (2024-11-22) Q: Does a card's [Security] effect trigger upon a security check with that card placed face-up? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3967** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens if I shuffle a security stack that includes security cards placed face-up? — A: Any face-up cards are placed face down, then you shuffle the cards. After shuffling, all cards are left face-down.
- **Q3968** (2024-11-22) Q: I have just this card placed face-up in my security stack. I used my Digimon with the [NSp] trait to attack, and I activated the <Alliance> gained from this card's [All Turns] effect. If this card is then trashed by the 1st [All Turns] effect on my opponent's BT18-055 [AncientTroymon], what happens to the <Security A. +1> and added DP gained from <Alliance>? — A: Even <Alliance> is lost, the <Security A. +1> and added DP remain. _(related: BT18-055)_
