## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3985** (2024-11-22) Q: With this card’s first effect, the [When Digivolving] effect, the Digimon I choose to suspend can be either my own or my opponent’s? — A: Yes, you can suspend either player's Digimon.
- **Q3986** (2024-11-22) Q: Am I able to suspend only 1 Digimon and use this card’s first conditional effect? — A: No, you cannot. When effect conditions use the “by doing X, do may do Y” you must fulfill the conditions completely to use the effect. In this case, the designated number of Digimon must be suspended to use the first conditional effect.
- **Q3987** (2024-11-22) Q: Does this card’s [All Turns] effect trigger the turn this card is played? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3988** (2024-11-22) Q: Do Digimon played by both me and my opponent trigger this card’s [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q4442** (2025-02-28) Q: The memory is at 0, and my opponent has 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon] in the battle area. Can I declare to play this card, then suspend my opponent's 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon]? — A: No, you can't. You can't pay the cost because you can't use BT8-071 [Psychemon]'s effect to reduce it, therefore you can't declare to play this card in the first place. _(related: BT8-071)_
- **Q4443** (2025-02-28) Q: The memory is at 1 or more, and my opponent has 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon] in the battle area. Can I declare to play this card, then suspend my opponent's 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon]? — A: Yes, you can. You can't use BT8-071 [Psychemon]'s effect to reduce the play cost, but the original play cost can be paid, therefore you can declare to play this card. After declaring to play it, you can use the 1st effect to suspend 2 Digimon, but you can't reduce the play cost, therefore you must pay the original play cost of 11 in order to play this card. _(related: BT8-071)_
- **Q6721** (2026-06-19) Q: There is one Digimon without effects and one Digimon that isn't affected by effects in the battle area, and no others. Can I declare I'm playing this card, choose those two Digimon as targets to suspend, then only suspend the Digimon without effects? — A: No, you cannot. If you cannot suspend two Digimon for the conditions of this card's first effect, then pay its original play cost to play it. Furthermore, if the card's original play cost can't be paid and there are not two Digimon that can be suspended for its first effect, then you cannot declare you are playing it.
