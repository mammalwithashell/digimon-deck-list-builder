## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Activate 1 of the effects below. ・1 of your other Digimon may digivolve into a red level 4 Digimon card with [Free] in its traits from your trash for the cost. ・This Digimon and one of your other Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand for the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and one of your other Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand for the cost.
