## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Give 1 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ and -3000 DP until their turn ends. [Your Turn] When your opponent's security stack is removed from, this Digimon may digivolve into a [TB] trait Digimon card in the hand with the cost reduced by 2.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -2: Lv.4 or lower [TB] trait card

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 [TB] trait Digimon card with 5000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
