## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon with 2 or more same-level cards in its stack attacks, by suspending this Tamer, digivolve it into a Digimon card with the [Night Claw], [Light Fang], [Galaxy] or [CS] trait in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4931** (2025-07-04) Q: I have EX5-064 [Koh & Sayo] in the battle area. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play BT22-102 [Sayo]? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT22-072; this card is named in it)_
- **Q4977** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
