## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may use 1 Option card with [Plug-In] in its name or that's yellow and has a cost of 5 or less from your hand without meeting its color requirements or paying its cost. Place the Option card used with this effect on top of your security stack face down instead of placing it in your trash. [When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a [Sakuyamon] in your hand for a cost of 1, ignoring its digivolution requirements.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-041 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1960** (2025-11-21) Q: It's currently my turn after the turn when my opponent activated BT9-103 [Kongou]'s effect. If I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect and use an Option card, can I place that Option card on top of the security stack instead of placing it in the trash? — A: No, you can't place it in the trash. BT9-103 [Kongou]'s effect prevents cards from being added to your security stack, so the used Option card is trashed. _(related: BT9-103)_
- **Q1961** (2025-11-21) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!], and I use P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!]'s 1st [Main] effect to place that card in the battle area. What happens to "place the Option card used with this effect on top of your security stack face down instead of placing it in your trash" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect at such times? — A: P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!] can't be placed in the trash, therefore it can't be placed on top of the security stack. _(related: P-037)_
- **Q1962** (2025-11-21) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use BT4-105 [Tactical Retreat!], then I placed this card itself on top of the security stack. At such times, after using BT4-105 [Tactical Retreat!], will it be placed in the security stack by the "place the Option card used with this effect on top of your security stack face down instead of placing it in your trash" effect? — A: Yes, it will be placed in the security stack. _(related: BT4-105)_
- **Q5451** (2025-11-21) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use ST22-08 [Offensive Plug-In V], and ST22-08 [Offensive Plug-In V]'s [Main] effect linked that card to a Digimon. What happens to "place the Option card used with this effect on top of your security stack face down instead of placing it in your trash" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect at such times? — A: ST22-08 [Offensive Plug-In V] can't be placed in the trash, therefore it can't be placed on top of the security stack. _(related: ST22-08)_
