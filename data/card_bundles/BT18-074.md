## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Play 1 black card with a play cost of 7 or less among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, from this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may return to the hand or play without paying the cost 1 level 4 or lower black Digimon card.

## Official Q&A
- This card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect can play a black card with a play cost of 7 or less. This includes both Digimon cards and Tamer cards that are black and have play costs of 7 or less.
