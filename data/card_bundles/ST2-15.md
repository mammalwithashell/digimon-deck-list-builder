## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST2-15 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q626** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use the effect of this Option card to play a level 2 Digimon that is currently a digivolution card of one of my Digimon? — A: No. This card's effect can only be used on Digimon cards that are digivolution cards. It can't be used on Digi-Egg cards or Tamer cards in a Digimon's digivolution cards.
- **Q627** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this Option card's effect to play a digivolution card of one of my suspended Digimon, does it come into play suspended? — A: No. Since it is played as another Digimon, it is played unsuspended.
- **Q628** (2024-03-28) Q: If one of my Digimon has an effect applied to it that lasts a turn, then I use the effect of this Option card to play one of its digivolution cards as a Digimon, does that Digimon come into play with the same effect applied to it? — A: No. Since it is played as another Digimon, it is not affected by any effects of the original Digimon it was under.
- **Q629** (2024-03-28) Q: Can Digimon played by this card's effect attack the turn they were played? — A: No, since it would count as the turn they were played.
