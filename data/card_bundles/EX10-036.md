## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-036 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5112** (2025-09-05) Q: This card has multiple effects that trigger when it digivolves or when it attacks. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q5113** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I trash just 1 digivolution card for the conditions of this card's 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for the 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q5114** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash a total of 3 digivolution cards from my multiple different Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5115** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I place just 1 card in digivolution cards for the conditions of this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for the 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect require you to place the specified number of cards in digivolution cards.
