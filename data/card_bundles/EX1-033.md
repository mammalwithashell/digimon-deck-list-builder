## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] For the turn, the next time you would digivolve one of your Digimon into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits, reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3218** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s inherited effect reduce the digivolution costs of my other Digimon, or just the Digimon that has this card in its digivolution cards? — A: When any one of your Digimon digivolves, as long as it meets the requirements, the memory cost of the digivolution is reduced by 1.
- **Q3219** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card’s inherited effect, then digivolve into a Digimon without [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits. Does this waste the inherited effect? — A: No. As long as it’s the same turn, the effect will persist until you digivolve into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits.
- **Q3220** (2024-03-28) Q: If a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards somehow attacks 2 or more times in the same turn, does the -1 digivolution cost reduction stack? — A: Yes. Reduce the digivolution cost by 1 for each time that Digimon attacked during the same turn. However, once you digivolve into a Digimon card that has [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits, the accumulated digivolution cost reductions from this effect will be reset.
- **Q3221** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I reduce the memory cost of digivolutions in my breeding area with this card's inherited effect? — A: No, you can't reduce the memory cost of digivolutions performed in your breeding area with this card's inherited effect.
- **Q3222** (2024-03-28) Q: If I digivolve a Digimon with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in my battle area into another Digimon, does this card’s inherited effect reduce the memory cost of that digivolution by 1? — A: No, it only reduces the memory cost by 1 when digivolving one of your Digimon into a Digimon card in your hand with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect].
