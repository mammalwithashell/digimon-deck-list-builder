## Printed text (official)
### Effect
Your [Lucemon] can digivolve into this card in your hand for a memory cost of 7, ignoring this card's digivolution requirements. For every 10 cards in your trash, reduce the memory cost when playing this card from your hand by 3. [On Play][When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Tamers or level 6 or lower Digimon.
