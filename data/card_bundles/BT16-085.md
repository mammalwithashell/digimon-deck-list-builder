## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] You may play 1 [Veemon] or [Wormmon] from your hand without paying the cost. At the next end of your opponent’s turn, return it to the hand. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon digivolves into a blue or green Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory. If DNA digivolving, trash any 3 digivolution cards under your opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-085 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2677** (2024-03-28) Q: The Digimon played using this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect digivolved. Is that Digimon returned to the hand at the end of my opponent's turn? — A: Yes, it's returned. In such cases, only the top card is returned to the hand and all digivolution cards are trashed.
- **Q2678** (2024-03-28) Q: When one of my Digimon DNA digivolves into a blue or green Digimon, does the "trash any 3 digivolution cards under your opponent's Digimon" effect activate even if I don't suspend this Tamer? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. The rest of the effect won't activate if you don't perform the specified action in effects with "by X" conditions.
- **Q4254** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to play [BT16-040 Wormmon], it digivolved into this Digimon, then I used the [End of Your Turn] effect to move it to the breeding area. At such times, does [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s effect return this Digimon to the hand? — A: No, it doesn't. An effect that targets this Digimon will continue targeting it even if it's moved to the breeding area, but because it isn't affected by effects while in the breeding area, it isn't returned to the hand. _(related: BT16-040)_
