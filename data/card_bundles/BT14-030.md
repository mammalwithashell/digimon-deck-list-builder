## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2400** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect return any level Digimon when it returns my Digimon to the hand? — A: Yes, you can return your Digimon of any level to the hand.
- **Q2401** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect return my Digimon to the hand even when my opponent has no Digimon? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q2402** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to return my [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] from the battle area to the hand? — A: Yes, you can. If your [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] is chosen as the target to return to the hand for this card's effect, it will ultimately be placed at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck instead of the hand according to the rules, but it meets this card's "by returning 1 of your Digimon to the hand" condition. A card isn't actually added to the hand, therefore effects such as "when a card is added to the hand" don't trigger. _(related: EX2-007)_
- **Q2403** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to return my [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] from the battle area to the hand, what happens for "return 1 of your opponent's Digimon whose level is less than or equal to the returned Digimon's level to the hand"? — A: [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] is a Digimon without a level, therefore your opponent can't return a Digimon to the hand. _(related: EX2-007)_
- **Q2404** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to return my token that was played as a Digimon from the battle area to the hand? — A: Yes, you can. If your token is chosen as the target to return to the hand for this card's effect, it will ultimately be removed from the game instead of being returned to the hand according to the rules, but it meets this card's "by returning 1 of your Digimon to the hand" condition. A card isn't actually added to the hand, therefore effects such as "when a card is added to the hand" don't trigger.
