## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 purple level 3 Digimon card from your trash without paying its memory cost. [End of Attack][Once Per Turn] You may digivolve 1 of your other Digimon into a Digimon card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits from your trash without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect can’t ignore digivolution requirements. When activating this effect, you can only digivolve into a card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits if it also meets the digivolution requirements.
