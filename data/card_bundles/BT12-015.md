## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand][Main] By placing 1 [Agunimon] and 1 [BurningGreymon] from your trash under 1 of your [Takuya Kanbara]s in any order, digivolve it into this card as if that card is a level 4 red Digimon for the digivolution cost. [On Deletion] Return 1 [Takuya Kanbara] from your trash to your hand.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, treat the Tamer as if it is a Digimon when digivolving. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects are triggered. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you cannot digivolve a Tamer as if it is a Digimon with this effect.
