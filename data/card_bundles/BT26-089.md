## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-089 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7137** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q7138** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7139** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q7140** (2026-08-18) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
- **Q7141** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "after" in this card's [All Turns] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't suspend this Tamer, you can't process the part following after "after" in this card's [All Turns] effect.
- **Q7142** (2026-08-18) Q: When performing a security check, if a [Security] effect, a "when this Digimon checks an opponent's security" effect, and a "when a security stack is removed from" effect trigger simultaneously, what order do they activate in? — A: The [Security] effect is activated first. When a [Security] effect is checked, it is activated immediately without waiting. Activate the other triggered effects in order, starting with the turn player's effects.
