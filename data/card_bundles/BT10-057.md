## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 of your Digimon. Then, gain 1 memory for each of your suspended Digimon with [Vegetation], [Plant] or [Fairy] in any of their traits. If this effect gains 2 or more memory, this Digimon unsuspends and gains ＜Piercing＞ for the turn. [Your Turn] For every 2 of your suspended Digimon, this Digimon gains ＜Security A. +1＞ and +2000 DP.
