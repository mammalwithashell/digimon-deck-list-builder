## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, 1 of their Digimon can't activate [When Digivolving] effects and gets -3000 DP. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may use 1 Option card with the [Onmyōjutsu] or [Plug-In] trait from your hand or under your Tamers without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] By trashing your top security card, 1 of your Digimon with [Sakuyamon] in its name unsuspends.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST22-04 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5410** (2025-12-25) Q: What does "[When Digivolving] effects don't activate" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents [When Digivolving] effects on cards from activating. [When Digivolving] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
- **Q5411** (2025-12-25) Q: I used a card affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" to attack. Can I activate that card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect at such times? — A: Yes, you can. The effect can activate if it activates upon the [When Attacking] timing.
- **Q5412** (2025-12-25) Q: If a card is affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects," can other effects activate its [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, it can't be activated.
- **Q5413** (2025-11-21) Q: If a card is affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects," can I process just the "by" condition in its [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5414** (2025-12-25) Q: A card that was given "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" digivolved into a card that has a [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] effect. Does that count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn] even if that effect can't be activated upon the timing when that card digivolved? — A: No, it doesn't count. The effect can't activate upon the [When Digivolving] timing, therefore it doesn't count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn].
- **Q5415** (2025-12-25) Q: If I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to use an Option card from under a Tamer, what happens to that Option card? — A: As with the standard rules, a used Option card is temporarily treated as not being in any area. After using it and activating the Option card's [Main] effect, the card is trashed unless it's to be placed in an area.
- **Q5416** (2026-06-19) Q: During an attack by this card, I used the inherited effect on ST22-01 [Viximon] in this card's digivolution cards to digivolve it. Does this card's [End of Attack] inherited effect trigger at the end of the attack? — A: Yes, it triggers. _(related: ST22-01)_
- **Q5420** (2025-11-21) Q: ST22-04 [Taomon] with ST22-01 [Viximon] in digivolution cards attacks, and I use ST22-04 [Taomon]'s [When Attacking] effect to use an Option card. If I use ST22-01 [Viximon]'s inherited effect to digivolve this card at such times, can I activate this card's <Alliance>? — A: No, you can't activate it. <Alliance> is an effect that triggers upon an attack. In this case, ST22-04 [Taomon] attacks in its current state where this card's <Alliance> hasn't triggered, therefore it can't activate. _(related: ST22-01)_
