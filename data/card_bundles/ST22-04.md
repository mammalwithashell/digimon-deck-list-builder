## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, 1 of their Digimon can't activate [When Digivolving] effects and gets -3000 DP. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may use 1 Option card with the [Onmyōjutsu] or [Plug-In] trait from your hand or under your Tamers without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] By trashing your top security card, 1 of your Digimon with [Sakuyamon] in its name unsuspends.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents [When Digivolving] effects on cards from activating. [When Digivolving] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
