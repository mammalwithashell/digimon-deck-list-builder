## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If DNA digivolving, your opponent's Digimon's effects don't affect this Digimon for the turn. Then, delete all of their Digimon with the highest level. [End of Attack] You may play 1 card with [Greymon] in its name or the [Ver.1] trait and 1 card with [Garurumon] in its name or the [Ver.2] trait from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the costs. If this effect played, place this Digimon as your top security card.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-021 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4764** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this Digimon didn't DNA digivolve, this card's [When Digivolving] effect deletes all of your opponent's Digimon with the highest level.
- **Q4765** (2025-06-13) Q: What combinations of digivolution cards can be played by this card's [End of Attack] effect? — A: The following combinations are possible. ●1 card with [Greymon] in its name and 1 card with [Garurumon] in its name ●1 card with [Greymon] in its name and 1 card with the [Ver.2] trait ●1 card with the [Ver.1] trait and 1 card with [Garurumon] in its name ●1 card with the [Ver.1] trait and 1 card with the [Ver.2] trait
- **Q4766** (2026-03-13) Q: At the end of an attack by this card that has 1 card with [Greymon] in its name and 1 card with [Garurumon] in its name in its digivolution cards, can I activate this card's [End of Attack] effect and play just the card with [Greymon] in its name from this Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If this card's [End of Attack] effect activates, you must play the specified cards whenever possible.
- **Q4767** (2025-06-13) Q: At the end of an attack by this card that has just 1 card with [Greymon] in its name in its digivolution cards, can I activate this card's [End of Attack] effect and play just the card with [Greymon] in its name from this Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, just that card can be played.
- **Q4768** (2025-06-13) Q: What does "none of your [...] Digimon are affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP."
- **Q4769** (2025-06-13) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen. For example, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect.
- **Q4770** (2025-06-13) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q4771** (2025-06-13) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q4772** (2025-06-13) Q: If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q4773** (2025-06-13) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
