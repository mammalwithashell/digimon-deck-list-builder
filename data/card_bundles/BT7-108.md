## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] For each Digimon with [Hybrid] in its traits and each Tamer you have in play, delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
- No. The number of Digimon you can delete is equal to the total number of Digimon with [Hybrid] in their traits and Tamers you have in play. For example, if you have 2 Digimon with [Hybrid] in their traits and 1 Tamer in play, you can choose a total of 3 of your opponent’s Digimon.
