## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ [Your Turn] While [Phoenixmon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, attach [End of Attack] to all of this Digimon's [On Deletion] effects. [On Deletion] You may play 1 11000 DP or lower red Digimon card with [Avian], [Bird], [Beast], [Animal] or [Sovereign], other than [Sea Animal], in one of its traits from your hand without paying the cost. Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as the Digimon this effect played.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2614** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate an [On Deletion] effect at the end of an attack if the effect has conditions for activating, such as [EX4-053 Falcomon]'s inherited effect? — A: No. The effect will trigger, but it can't be activated because its conditions aren't met. _(related: EX4-053)_
- **Q2615** (2024-09-20) Q: I used this card's [Your Turn] effect to attach [End of Attack] to BT16-011 [Garudamon (X Antibody)] and BT13-014 [Garudamon]'s inherited effects. If I use BT13-014 [Garudamon]'s inherited effect to delete my opponent's BT13-065 [PlatinumSukamon] at the end of this card's attack, then <De-Digivolve> causes this card's [Your Turn] effect to be removed, can I then activate BT16-011 [Garudamon (X Antibody)]'s inherited effect? — A: No, it can't be activated. If the trigger conditions are no longer met before activation, it can't be activated. _(related: BT16-011, BT13-014, BT13-065)_
