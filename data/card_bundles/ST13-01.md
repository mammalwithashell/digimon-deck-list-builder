## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST13-01 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q764** (2024-03-28) Q: If I play 2 or more Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait at the same time, do I gain 1 memory for each Digimon played with this card's inherited effect? — A: No, even if you play 2 or more Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait at the same time with 1 effect, you can only gain 1 memory with this card's inherited effect.
- **Q765** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have 2 Digimon with this card in their digivolution cards, and play 1 Digimon with the [Legend-Arms] trait, can I activate both inherited effects and gain 2 memory? — A: Yes, in that situation you can gain 2 memory in total.
