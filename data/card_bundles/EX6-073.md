## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] You may place up to 7 [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait cards with different names from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards. If 4 or more were placed, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. [When Attacking] By returning 7 [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait cards with different names from this Digimon's digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck, delete 7 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. Then, trash the top 7 cards of their security stack. For each card this effect deleted, reduce the number of security cards trashed by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3823** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I reference [Beelzemon] and [Beelzemon (X Antibody)] as "cards with different names"? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3824** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I reference [Beelzemon] and [Beelzemon ACE] as "cards with different names"? — A: No, you can't. ACE isn't included in the card name.
- **Q3825** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect and placed 2 cards in this card's digivolution cards. If I activate this effect again during the same turn and place 2 more cards in this card's digivolution cards, is the "if 4 or more were placed" condition met? — A: No, it isn't met. This condition is only met if 4 or more cards were placed for a single activation.
- **Q3826** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I return just 1 digivolution card to the bottom of the deck for the conditions of this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. You must return the specified number of digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck for the conditions of this card's [When Attacking] effect.
- **Q3827** (2026-03-13) Q: I attempted to delete just 1 of my opponent's Digimon or Tamers using this card's [When Attacking] effect, but another effect prevented that Digimon or Tamer from being deleted. Is the number of cards to trash from the security stack still 7 for the part of the effect after "then"? — A: Yes, it remains 7.
- **Q6040** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [When Attacking] effect, and when my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area for the 1st process, an immediate-type effect such as a "when [...] would leave" effect caused this card to be removed from the battle area. Can I then process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect activates, it is to be fully resolved even if the card that activated the effect is removed from that area during the processing.
