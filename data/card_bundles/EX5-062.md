## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][Main][Once Per Turn] You may trash up to 3 cards in your hand. Then, play 1 purple Digimon card from your trash with the play cost reduced by 3. For each card trashed by this effect, further reduce it by 1. [Your Turn] When an effect plays one of your Digimon, delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, ＜Draw 1＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3661** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "for each card trashed by this effect, further reduce it by 1" in this card's [When Digivolving][Main] effect mean, exactly? — A: It means that when you use this effect to play 1 purple Digimon card from your trash with the play cost reduced by 3, you can further reduce it by 1 for each card trashed from your hand. For example, if you used this effect to trash 2 cards from your hand, you can play 1 purple Digimon card from your trash with its play cost reduced by 5.
- **Q3662** (2024-03-28) Q: If I don't trash any cards from my hand, can I use this card’s [When Digivolving][Main] effect to play a purple Digimon card from my trash with its play cost reduced by 3? — A: Yes. That's correct.
- **Q3663** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card’s [When Digivolving][Main] effect to trash a purple Digimon card from my hand, can I choose that card as the card to play from my trash? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3664** (2024-03-28) Q: When a single effect plays 2 or more of my Digimon at the same time, can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to delete 2 of my opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon, or use it to <Draw 1> twice? — A: No, you can't. Even if a single effect plays multiple Digimon at the same time, you can only use this effect to delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon, or use it to <Draw 1> once.
- **Q3665** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect activate when this card's effect itself plays a card? — A: Yes, it activates.
