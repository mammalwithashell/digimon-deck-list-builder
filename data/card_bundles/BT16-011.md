## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may return 1 red Digimon card from your trash to the hand. Then, if [Garudamon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent’s Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a red card returns from your trash to the hand, 1 of your Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] Trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.
