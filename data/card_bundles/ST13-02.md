## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] By placing this Digimon under 1 of your other Digimon that's black or has [Legend-Arms] in its traits as its bottom digivolution card, you may reveal the top card of your deck. If that card is a Digimon card with [Legend-Arms] in its traits and a play cost of 7 or less, you may play it without paying its memory cost. Add the rest to your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] If you have a Digimon that's black or has [Legend-Arms] in its traits in play, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST13-02 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q766** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I choose to not activate this card's [On Play] effect? — A: Yes, the effect reads "By placing this Digimon under 1 of your other Digimon [...] as its bottom digivolution card," so you may also choose to not place it as the bottom digivolution card. In such cases, it remains in the battle area as a Digimon like normal.
- **Q767** (2024-03-28) Q: When I reveal a card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect, can I choose to not play that card? — A: Yes, the effect reads "you may play it," so you may also choose to not play it. In such cases, you add it to your hand like any other card.
- **Q768** (2024-03-28) Q: I revealed a card with the [Legend-Arms] trait and a play cost of 7 or less from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect] when I had a [BT9-047 Pomumon]. What happens to that card? — A: It can't be played due to [BT9-047 Pomumon]'s effect, so you add it to your hand like any other card. _(related: BT9-047)_
