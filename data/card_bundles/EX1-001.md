## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Tamer card or 1 Digimon card with [Agumon] in its name among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A

- **Q3187** (2024-03-28) Q: If a Digimon card with [Agumon] in its name and a Tamer card are among the cards revealed with this card’s inherited effect, can I add both to my hand? — A: No, you can only add one or the other.
- **Q3188** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I add a non-red Digimon card with [Agumon] in its name or a non-red Tamer card to my hand off this card’s inherited effect? — A: Yes, you can.
