## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] All players trash cards in their hand until they have 5 cards left. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your effects trashes a card in your hand, you may unsuspend 1 of your Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-043 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3337** (2024-03-28) Q: Which player decides which cards are trashed from hand by this card’s [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Both players decide which cards to trash from their own hands.
- **Q3338** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect activate before or after drawing the digivolution bonus card? — A: It activates after you draw. [When Digivolving] effects trigger after a player finishes drawing the digivolution bonus card.
- **Q3339** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect work when I have 5 or fewer cards in hand? — A: If you have 5 or fewer cards in hand, you don't trash any cards.
