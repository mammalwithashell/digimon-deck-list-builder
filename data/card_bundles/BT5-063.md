## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If you don't have an [Arata Sanada] card in play, you may play one from your hand without paying its memory cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] All of your other Digimon with the same name as this Digimon gain ＜Rush＞. (This Digimon can attack the turn it comes into play.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-063 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1338** (2024-03-28) Q: A Digimon has <Rush> thanks to this card's inherited effect. If that Digimon digivolves into a Digimon with a different name, can that Digimon attack the same turn it entered play? — A: No, it can't. The Digimon loses <Rush> when its name changes, so it can't attack.
