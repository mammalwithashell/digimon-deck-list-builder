## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon would digivolve from level 5 to level 6, you may delete 1 of your Digimon to reduce the digivolution cost by 3.

### Security Effect

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-064 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3349** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card’s effect when a Digimon in my breeding area digivolves into a level 5 or level 6 card? — A: No, it doesn't activate for Digimon in breeding areas.
- **Q3350** (2024-03-28) Q: When one of my level 5 Digimon would digivolve into a level 6 and I delete that level 5 Digimon with this card's effect, what happens? — A: You can't continue the digivolution, and the level 6 Digimon card returns to your hand. The level 5 Digimon is deleted and no digivolution cost is paid.
