## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] While there are 10 or more cards in your trash, this Digimon gains ＜Retaliation＞. (When this Digimon is deleted after losing a battle, delete the Digimon it was battling.)

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While there are 10 or more cards in your trash, this Digimon gains ＜Retaliation＞. (When this Digimon is deleted after losing a battle, delete the Digimon it was battling.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-006 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1153** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 9 cards in my trash and a Digimon with this card as a digivolution card is deleted in battle. The deleted cards, including this one, are sent to my trash, and now I have over 10 cards in my trash. Does this card's <Retaliation> activate? — A: No. You lose this card's inherited effect when it is placed in the trash, so it can't activate.
- **Q1154** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 10 or more cards in my trash and a Digimon with this card as a digivolution card is deleted in battle. Before this card's effect has a chance to activate, another effect activates and reduces the number of cards in my trash to 9 or less. Does this card's <Retaliation> activate? — A: Yes, if you had 10 or more cards when it was deleted, you can activate <Retaliation>.
