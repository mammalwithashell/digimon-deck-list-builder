## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits in your hand to have this Digimon get +3000 DP for the turn. [Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon card from the trash, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

## Official Q&A
- No, this card can’t digivolve into a card from your trash on its own. When some other card’s effect digivolves this card into a card from your trash, this card’s effect will then reduce the digivolution cost.
