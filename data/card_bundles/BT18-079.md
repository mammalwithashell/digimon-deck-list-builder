## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] For each color your opponent's Digimon and Tamers have, trash 1 card from the tops of both players' decks. Then, for each card this effect trashed, this Digimon gets +1000 DP for the turn. [End of Attack] By deleting 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest level.

### Inherited Effect
＜Retaliation＞ (When only this Digimon is deleted in battle, delete the Digimon it battled.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-079 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3034** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a digivolution requirement that digivolves from a Tamer mean that the Tamer that will become a digivolution card is treated as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
- **Q3035** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q3036** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q3037** (2024-06-21) Q: My opponent has 1 red and blue Digimon and 1 red and yellow Tamer. How many cards do I trash from the tops of both players' decks when this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect activates? — A: 3 cards. In this case, there are 3 colors (red, blue, and yellow) in your opponent's Digimon and Tamers, therefore you trash 3 cards from the tops of both players' decks.
- **Q6659** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6660** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6661** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
