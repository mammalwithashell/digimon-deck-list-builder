## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] For each color your opponent's Digimon and Tamers have, trash 1 card from the tops of both players' decks. Then, for each card this effect trashed, this Digimon gets +1000 DP for the turn. [End of Attack] By deleting 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest level.

### Inherited Effect
＜Retaliation＞ (When only this Digimon is deleted in battle, delete the Digimon it battled.)

## Official Q&A
- No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
