## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may play 2 level 4 or lower Digimon cards with [Bagra Army] in their traits from your trash without paying their play costs. The Digimon played by this effect gain ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent's turn. [Opponent's Turn] When an effect would trash one of your other Digimon's digivolution cards, you may trash this Digimon's digivolution cards instead.

## Official Q&A
- This card's [Opponent’s Turn] effect will replace an effect that would trash digivolution cards. For example, if you replace a "trash the bottom digivolution card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, you trash the bottom digivolution card of this card. Replacement is possible even when this card doesn't have any digivolution cards.
