### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may delete 1 of your other Digimon to delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your other Digimon is deleted, you may play 1 level 3 purple Digimon card from your trash without paying its memory cost. Any [On Play] effects on Digimon played with this effect don't activate.
