## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may place up to 5 level 5 red and/or black cards with [Cyborg] in their traits and different card numbers from your hand and/or trash in this Digimon's digivolution cards to gain 1 memory for each card placed. [All Turns] This Digimon's DP can't be reduced. [All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted, you may prevent it by trashing 2 level 5 Digimon cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A
