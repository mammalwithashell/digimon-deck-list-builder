## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play a Digimon with [Sistermon] in its name, gain 1 memory.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While this Digimon has [Huckmon] in its name or [Royal Knight] in its traits, it gets +1000 DP.
