## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] By deleting this Digimon, activate 1 of the effects below: ・Place 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the [Virus] trait at the bottom of their security stack. ・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -5000 DP for the turn. [On Deletion] Place this card at the bottom of your security stack. Then, if you have a Tamer, you may hatch in your breeding area.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] Place 1 yellow card with the [Vaccine] trait from your hand at the bottom of your security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2485** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [When Attacking] effect delete this Digimon even when my opponent has no Digimon? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q2486** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to hatch in my breeding area when I have a Digimon in my breeding area and a Tamer? — A: No, you can't. You can't hatch a new Digi-Egg when you have another Digimon in your breeding area.
- **Q2487** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to place this card in my security stack when I have a Digimon in my breeding area and a Tamer, even if I can't hatch in my breeding area? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2488** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have a yellow card with the [Vaccine] trait in my hand when this card's inherited effect activates, do I have to place it at the bottom of my security stack? — A: No, you don't have to place it. If there are requirements for the target card and that card is in a private area, performing the action is optional.
