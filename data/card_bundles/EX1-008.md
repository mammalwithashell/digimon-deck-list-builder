## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] When this Digimon attacks a player, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Machine] or [Dragonkin] in its traits, it gains ＜Piercing＞. (When this Digimon attacks and deletes an opponent's Digimon and survives the battle, it performs any security checks it normally would.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3196** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon that gained <Security Attack +1> from another card’s effect, and which also gained <Piercing> from this card’s inherited effect. I attack an opponent's Digimon with it, and perform a security check with <Piercing>. The first card checked has a <De-Digivolve 1> [Security] effect, which trashes the top card of my attacking Digimon, turning it into a Digimon without [Machine] or [Dragonkin]. This causes the Digimon to also lose <Piercing>. Do I still perform the second check? — A: Yes. <Piercing> has already activated. Perform the additional check, assuming your Digimon still has <Security Attack +1>.
- **Q3197** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack my opponent with this Digimon. If they block it with one of their Digimon, does this card's effect activate? — A: Yes, it does. [When Attacking] effects activate before your opponent gets to activate <Blocker>.
