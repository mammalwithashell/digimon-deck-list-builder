## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 5 or higher Digimon card with the [Free] trait in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 4. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When one of your Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted other than by one of your effects, ＜Delay＞. ・By digivolving that Digimon into a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name in the hand without paying the cost, prevent that deletion.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 Tamer card with [Davis Motomiya] or [Ken Ichijoji] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-097 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2885** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve into a level 5 or higher Digimon card with the [Free] trait in the hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's [Main] effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q2886** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve into a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name in the hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's <Delay> effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q2887** (2024-06-21) Q: When BT16-025 [Paildramon] would be deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: No, you can't activate it. <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> and this card's <Delay> effect will trigger simultaneously, but if you use this card's effect to digivolve first, the triggered <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> on BT16-025 [Paildramon] will be negated and won't activate. _(related: BT16-025)_
- **Q2888** (2024-05-24) Q: When BT16-025 [Paildramon] is deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: No, you can't activate it. BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect can't be activated because it doesn't trigger when the Digimon is deleted by an opponent's effect. _(related: BT16-025)_
- **Q2889** (2024-05-24) Q: When a Digimon with BT16-025 [Paildramon] in its digivolution cards is deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: Yes, you can. <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> and this card's <Delay> effect will trigger simultaneously, and the triggered <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> on BT16-025 [Paildramon] can be activated, even if you use this card's effect to digivolve first. _(related: BT16-025)_
- **Q2890** (2024-06-21) Q: If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when multiples of my Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted at the same time other than by one of my effects, can I digivolve all of those Digimon and prevent them from being deleted? — A: No, you can't. 1 digivolution can only be performed on 1 Digimon. You can't perform separate digivolutions on multiple cards at the same time. Therefore, when multiples of your Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted other than by one of your effects, you can only use this card's effect to digivolve 1 of those Digimon. In addition, only the digivolved Digimon is prevented from being deleted.
- **Q2891** (2024-06-21) Q: [All Turns] When my Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted other than by one of my effects during my opponent's turn, I used this card's [All Turns] effect to digivolve that Digimon into EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]. Can I then use <Blitz> in EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]'s [When Digivolving] effect to attack? — A: No, you can't. Only the turn player can attack. In this case, your EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode] can't attack because it isn't your turn. _(related: EX3-063)_
