## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5038** (2025-09-05) Q: What does a card with "X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolution requirements, DNA digivolution, DigiXros requirements, burst digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, a card with [Knightmon] in its text would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q5039** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to return just 4 cards with [Lucemon] in their texts to the bottom of the deck? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. In order for the conditions of this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to be met, you must return 5 cards with [Lucemon] in their texts to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q5040** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to return 5 cards with [Lucemon] in their texts from my trash to the bottom of the deck, but then choose to not digivolve this Digimon into [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in my trash? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5041** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to digivolve into a BT7-111 [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in the trash? — A: No, you can't. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements. _(related: BT7-111)_
- **Q5734** (2025-12-25) Q: I chose 5 Digi-Egg cards with [Lucemon] in their texts from my trash for this card's [End of Your Turn] effect. At such times, the rules place the Digi-Egg cards at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck when they would be returned to the deck, but is this effect's "by" condition met? — A: Yes, it's met. The conditions will be met even if cards aren't actually placed in that area due to Digi-Egg card or token rules.
