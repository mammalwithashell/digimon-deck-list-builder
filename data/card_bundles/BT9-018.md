## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] For each Tamer your opponent has in play, suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon and gain 1 memory. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When an opponent’s Digimon with 6000 DP or less becomes suspended, you may delete that Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-018 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1816** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect gain me 1 memory per each of my opponent's Tamers? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q1817** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent has multiple Tamers but only 1 Digimon in play, do I gain 1 memory for each of my opponent's Tamers even though only 1 Digimon will be suspended? — A: Yes. You’ll gain 1 memory for each of your opponent’s Tamers.
- **Q1818** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent’s Digimon with 6000 DP or less becomes suspended, can I choose NOT to delete it with this card’s [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes. The effect reads, “You may delete that Digimon,” so you can choose not to. If you choose not to, the effect’s [Once Per Turn] requirement won’t be used up, either.
- **Q1819** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with a Digimon, and my opponent activates <Blocker> to suspend their Digimon with 6000 DP or less. I then activate this card’s [All Turns] effect to delete that Digimon. What happens to the attack? — A: When your opponent activates <Blocker>, the target of the attack is switched to the Digimon that activated <Blocker>, and that Digimon is then deleted by this Digimon's [All Turns] effect. Since the Digimon that activated <Blocker> was deleted, no battle occurs, and the attack ends.
- **Q1820** (2024-03-28) Q: I used an effect like [BT6-103 Blasted Disaster] that suspends multiple DIgimon to simultaneously suspend 2 of my opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. In such cases, can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to delete those 2 Digimon? — A: Yes you can, as long as they are simultaneously suspended. _(related: BT6-103)_
- **Q4287** (2025-01-24) Q: An opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less attacks during my opponent's turn, then this card's [All Turns] effect and an opponent's effect triggered simultaneously. At such times, if my opponent's effect activates first and the attacking Digimon's DP becomes 7000 or more, can I use the triggered [All Turns] effect on this card to delete that Digimon? — A: Yes, you can delete it.
