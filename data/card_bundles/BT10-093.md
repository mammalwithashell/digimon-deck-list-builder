## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When purple cards are placed under this Tamer, ＜Draw 1＞ (Draw 1 card from your deck), and gain 1 memory. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When 1 of your level 4 or higher Digimon cards with the [Bagra Army] trait would be played, by placing up to 3 purple Digimon cards from under your Tamers as that Digimon's bottom digivolution cards, reduce its play cost by 2 for each card placed.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-093 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2024** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to place up to 3 cards from under multiples of my Tamers in the digivolution cards of the Digimon card to be played? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2025** (2025-09-05) Q: When I would play a [Bagra Army] trait card with DigiXros requirements, do I process this card's [Your Turn] effect or the DigiXros of the card to be played first? — A: You process this card's [Your Turn] effect first. After processing the "when you would play" effect, you can then perform the DigiXros. Once you use this card's [Your Turn] effect to place cards under the card to be played, then you place the cards for the DigiXros under the cards you just placed for the effect.
- **Q2026** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect when I would play 2 or more level 4 or higher [Bagra Army] trait Digimon cards with DigiXros requirements at the same time? — A: No, you can't activate it. This card's [Your Turn] effect triggers when you would play 1 level 4 or higher [Bagra Army] trait Digimon card. It doesn't trigger when you would play 2 or more at the same time.
