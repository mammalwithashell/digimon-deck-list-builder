### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your [Glowing Dawn] trait cards would be played, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, reduce the cost by 1.

## Official Q&A
- [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
