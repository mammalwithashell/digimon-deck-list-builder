## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [All Turns] When your security stack is removed from, by suspending this Tamer, you may place the top 2 cards of your deck face down under this Tamer. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your [Glowing Dawn] trait cards would be played, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, reduce the cost by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-088 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6415** (2026-05-08) Q: In what order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q6416** (2026-05-08) Q: If I use this card's [All Turns] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q6417** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6418** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q6419** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
- **Q6420** (2026-05-08) Q: When I place multiple cards from the top of my deck face down under a Tamer without looking at them, in what order do I place them? — A: You place them 1 at a time under the Tamer starting with the top card of your deck.
- **Q6421** (2026-05-08) Q: 2 copies of this card are in the battle area. When my card with the [Glowing Dawn] trait would be played, can I activate the [Your Turn] effects on both copies of this card and reduce the play cost by a total of 2? — A: Yes, you can.
