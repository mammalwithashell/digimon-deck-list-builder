## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-160 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4273** (2024-11-22) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card attacks. In what order do they activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q4274** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card and EX8-065 [Ryutaro Williams] in the battle area. When this card attacks, I activate EX8-065 [Ryutaro Williams]'s [Your Turn] effect and digivolve this Digimon into a card in the hand. Can I then activate this card's <Raid> or [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. If an effect is lost while it's pending activation, it can no longer be activated. _(related: EX8-065)_
