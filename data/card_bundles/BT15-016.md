## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If your opponent has 4 or less memory, 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 8000 DP or more can't attack until the end of their turn. If they have 4 or more, delete 1 of their Digimon with 6000 DP or less.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] Return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 7000 DP or less to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- It means that the condition for "if your opponent has 4 or less memory" is fulfilled when the memory is at 1 through 10 on your side, 0, or 1 through 4 on your opponent's side at the timing when this effect activates. Similarly, the condition for "if they have 4 or more" is fulfilled when the memory is at 4 through 10 on your opponent's side at the timing when this effect activates.
