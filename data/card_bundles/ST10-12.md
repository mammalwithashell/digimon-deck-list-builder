## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash 1 card in your hand to reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow and 1 purple card with [Angel], [Archangel], or [Fallen Angel] in their traits among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] All of your yellow Digimon gain ＜Retaliation＞. (When this Digimon is deleted after losing a battle, delete the Digimon it was battling.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST10-12 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q744** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect force me to reveal 3 cards from the top of my deck when digivolving? — A: No. The effect reads, "You may trash 1 card in your hand to...," so if you choose not to trash a card in your hand, the rest of the effect won't activate.
- **Q745** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's [When Digivolving] effect reveals cards from the top of my deck. If I reveal yellow and purple cards that both have [Angel], [Archangel], or [Fallen Angel] in their traits, do I have to add them to my hand? — A: Yes. The effect reads, “Add 1 yellow and 1 purple card...to your hand,” so if you reveal cards that meet the requirements, you must add them to your hand.
