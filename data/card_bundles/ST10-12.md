## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash 1 card in your hand to reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow and 1 purple card with [Angel], [Archangel], or [Fallen Angel] in their traits among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] All of your yellow Digimon gain ＜Retaliation＞. (When this Digimon is deleted after losing a battle, delete the Digimon it was battling.)

- No. The effect reads, "You may trash 1 card in your hand to...," so if you choose not to trash a card in your hand, the rest of the effect won't activate.
