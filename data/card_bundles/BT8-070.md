## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If this Digimon has a red digivolution card, choose any number of your opponent's Digimon. If this Digimon has a black digivolution card, choose any number of your opponent's Tamers. The chosen cards' play costs must add up to 6 or less. Delete the chosen cards. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon is deleted, you may unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1751** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card has both red and black digivolution cards, can this card's [When Digivolving] effect delete both Digimon and Tamers? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q1752** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card has both red and black digivolution cards, can this card's [When Digivolving] effect delete both 6 play cost worth of my opponent’s Digimon, and 6 play cost worth of my opponent’s Tamers? — A: No. The total play costs of the chosen Digimon and Tamers must add up to 6 or less.
- **Q1753** (2024-03-28) Q: If a single red and black multicolor card is in this card’s digivolution cards, can I activate both parts of this card’s [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Yes, you can.
