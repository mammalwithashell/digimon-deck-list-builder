## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7090** (2026-08-18) Q: A Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards attacks, and another effect trashes a card from my hand. At such times, if I activate this card's inherited effect and perform a digivolution into [P-209 Titamon], can I then activate [P-209 Titamon]'s <Alliance>? — A: No, you can't. <Alliance> is an effect that triggers upon an attack. You can't activate it if the Digimon doesn't have <Alliance> upon the attack declaration. _(related: P-209)_
- **Q7091** (2026-08-18) Q: I trash 2 copies of this card from my hand, leaving me with 5 cards in my hand. At such times, if I activate the 1st effect on the 1st copy of this card to give me 6 cards in my hand, can I then use the 1st effect on the 2nd copy of this card to <Draw 1> again? — A: No, you can't. You must have 5 or fewer cards in your hand upon the actual activation timing for this card's 1st effect.
