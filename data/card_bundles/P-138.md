## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Veedramon] in its name and 1 blue Tamer card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon becomes unsuspended, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-138 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4244** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 3 cards I reveal from the deck with this card's effect include only a Digimon card with [Veedramon] in its name or a blue Tamer card, can I just add that 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q4245** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a Digimon card with [Veedramon] in its name and a blue Tamer card, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
