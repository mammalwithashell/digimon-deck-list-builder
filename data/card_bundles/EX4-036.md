## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-036 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3482** (2024-03-28) Q: What can be the target of <De-Digivolve 1> in this card's [End of Attack] effect? — A: It targets 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
- **Q3483** (2024-03-28) Q: An effect suspended another Digimon when I attacked my opponent's Digimon using my Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, and only my Digimon survived. Does this Digimon gain <Piercing> by this card's inherited effect and allow me to check my opponent's security stack? — A: No. You can't perform a security check even if you gain <Piercing> after deleting your opponent's Digimon in battle.
- **Q3484** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's "3 from Lv.4 w/[Gargomon] in name or 2-color w/green" special digivolution to digivolve from a "Lv.4 Digimon w/[Gargomon] in name" or "2-color Lv.4 Digimon w/green"? — A: Yes, that's correct. A Lv.4 Digimon is required for both ""w/[Gargomon] in name"" and ""2-color w/green.""
