## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name and 1 [Takato Matsuki] among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3289** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 4 cards. If the cards revealed only include either a card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name or [Takato Matsuki], can I still add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, as long as either a card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name or [Takato Matsuki] is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
- **Q3290** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 4 cards. If both a card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name and [Takato Matsuki] are among the cards revealed, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining card at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. This card's effect reads “Add 1 card [...] and 1 [Takato Matsuki] among them to your hand,” so you must add as many cards to your hand as possible. In this case, you must add both 1 card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name, and 1 [Takato Matsuki] to your hand.
- **Q3301** (2024-03-28) Q: If [EX2-008 Guilmon]’s [On Play] effect reveals this card from my deck, can I add it to my hand as a card that has [Gallantmon] in its name? — A: Yes, you can.
