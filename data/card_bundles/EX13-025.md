## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have 3 or more security cards, trash your top or bottom security card, ＜Draw 1＞ and gain 1 memory. Then, if you have 2 or fewer security cards, you may place 1 card with [Witchelny] in its text from your hand as the bottom security card. (Rule) Trait: Has [Witchelny].

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Dynasmon] or [Witchelny] in its text would leave the battle area by your opponent's effects, by trashing your top security card, it doesn't leave.
