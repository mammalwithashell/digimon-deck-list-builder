## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer with [Tai Kamiya] in its name, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Unsuspend 1 of your Digimon. Then, 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon until the end of your opponent's turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST15-15 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q816** (2024-03-28) Q: When my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name isn't affected by my opponent's Digimon's effects from this card's [Main] effect, can it be blocked by an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it can be blocked. <Blocker> is an effect that allows the Digimon with the effect to block, so it isn't affected by "isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon" effects.
- **Q817** (2024-03-28) Q: When my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name isn't affected by my opponent's Digimon's effects from this card's [Main] effect, is it affected by [Security] effects on my opponent's Digimon? — A: No, it isn't affected. [Security] effects on Security Digimon are treated as Digimon effects, so the "this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon" effect prevents such effects from affecting this Digimon.
