## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] 1 of your Digimon with [Veemon] or [Veedramon] in its name gains ＜Blocker＞ and ＜Evade＞ until your opponent's turn ends. [All Turns] When any of your Digimon with [Veedramon] in their names suspend, by suspending this Tamer, activate 1 of those Digimon's [When Digivolving] effects. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When any of your blue Digimon unsuspend, gain 1 memory.

[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2142** (2024-03-28) Q: When one of my Digimon with [Veedramon] in its name without a [When Digivolving] effect becomes suspended, can I suspend this card? — A: Yes you can. In such cases, even if there's no [When Digivolving] effect to target with this effect, this card still becomes suspended.
- **Q2143** (2024-03-28) Q: My Digimon with [Veedramon] in its name attacks and becomes suspended. After that, it digivolves into a different Digimon with the [When Attacking] effect of [BT9-109 X Antibody] in its digivolution cards. If the card it digivolved into has a [When Digivolving], if I suspend this card with this card's [All Turns] effect, can I activate the [When Digivolving] effect of the card it digivolved into? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT9-109)_
