## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-011 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1157** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a "may digivolve this card as if the Tamer is a Digimon" effect also treat the Tamer that will become a digivolution card as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: Yes, it also treats the Tamer as a Digimon when it digivolves. It treats it as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, digivolution won't be possible using this effect that also treats the Tamer as a Digimon.
- **Q1158** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q1159** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q1160** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q1161** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Security effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q1162** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Inherited effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q1163** (2024-03-28) Q: If I've digivolved this card on top of a tamer card and the Digimon is deleted, what happens to the tamer card? — A: It is trashed.
- **Q1743** (2024-09-20) Q: Is digivolution from a Tamer using BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s 1st effect possible while a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated? — A: Yes, such digivolution is possible. Effects that add to card information for digivolution aren't the same as effects that ignore digivolution requirements.
- **Q2624** (2025-01-24) Q: When does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger, exactly? — A: It will trigger when a Digimon is played by an effect such as the effect on BT5-092 [Nokia Shiramine] or when a Digimon digivolves by an effect such as the effect on P-103 [Offense Training]. It won't trigger for effects that aren't digivolution effects, such as BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s effect that treats a Tamer as a level 3 red Digimon or BT6-060 [Deputymon]'s effect that allows digivolution requirements to be ignored. _(related: BT5-092, P-103, BT6-060)_
- **Q3382** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent's red Tamer digivolves into a [BT4-011 Agunimon] when my Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 1? — A: Yes, the digivolution cost increases by 1. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may Digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 Digimon," so the Tamer is considered to be a Digimon without digivolution cards, and the digivolution cost increases by 1 due to this card's inherited effect.

Does the digivolution cost increase by 1 when my opponent digivolves a red Tamer with cards under it into a [BT4-011 Agunimon]? — A: No, it doesn't. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may Digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 Digimon," so a Tamer with cards under it is considered to be a Digimon with digivolution cards.

- **Q3422** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent activates this card's [On Play] effect. Can I digivolve an unsuspended Tamer into a [BT4-011 Agunimon] in the next turn? — A: No, you can't. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 red Digimon," so it is considered to be an unsuspended Digimon and can't digivolve.

- **Q4633** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose to not digivolve with this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
