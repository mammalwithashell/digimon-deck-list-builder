## Printed text (official)
### Effect
This card may digivolve from the hand onto one of your yellow Tamers as if that card is a level 3 yellow Digimon. [When Attacking] ＜Draw 1＞ [When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Hybrid] trait in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] If you have 7 or fewer cards in your hand, ＜Draw 1＞.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it also treats the Tamer as a Digimon when it digivolves. It treats it as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, digivolution won't be possible using this effect that also treats the Tamer as a Digimon.
