### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 2 cards with [Blue Flare] in their traits among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. If you have a [Kiriha Aonuma] in play, you may return 1 [MetalGreymon] from your trash to your hand instead. [On Deletion] ＜Save＞ (You may place this card under one of your Tamers.)

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has [Blue Flare] in its traits and your opponent has 2 or more Digimon in play, unsuspend this Digimon.
