## Official Q&A

- **Q4741** (2025-06-13) Q: When digivolution would occur by this card's [When Attacking] effect, can I activate EX9-070 [Meat]'s <Delay> effect and reduce the digivolution cost by 3? — A: No, you can't. EX9-070 [Meat]'s <Delay> effect is a [Main] effect timing, so you can only declare its activation when there is no unresolved processing. You can't declare activation of EX9-070 [Meat]'s <Delay> effect while this card's [When Attacking] effect is still processing. _(related: EX9-070)_
- **Q4751** (2025-06-13) Q: I use this Digimon with EX9-001 [Koromon] in its digivolution cards to attack, and I use EX9-001 [Koromon]'s inherited effect to digivolve this card. This card doesn't have <Raid> when it attacks, therefore <Raid> in its digivolution cards can't activate.
