## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-183 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4627** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to give "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks" to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the gained effect won't trigger.
- **Q4628** (2025-04-04) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to give "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks" to a Digimon, can I choose to not attack for the part of the effect after "then"? — A: Yes, you can.
