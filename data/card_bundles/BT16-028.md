## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers can't unsuspend until the end of their turn. Then, by suspending 1 of their Digimon or Tamers, 1 of your Digimon unsuspends. [All Turns] When effects play or digivolve your opponent's Digimon, if you have a Tamer, this Digimon may digivolve into [Imperialdramon: Fighter Mode] in the hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-028 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2623** (2024-03-28) Q: Which effect activates first, an [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect on an opponent's Digimon that was played/digivolved, or this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player activates their effect first.
- **Q2624** (2025-01-24) Q: When does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger, exactly? — A: It will trigger when a Digimon is played by an effect such as the effect on BT5-092 [Nokia Shiramine] or when a Digimon digivolves by an effect such as the effect on P-103 [Offense Training]. It won't trigger for effects that aren't digivolution effects, such as BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s effect that treats a Tamer as a level 3 red Digimon or BT6-060 [Deputymon]'s effect that allows digivolution requirements to be ignored. _(related: BT5-092, P-103, BT4-011, BT6-060)_
