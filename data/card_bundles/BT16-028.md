## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers can't unsuspend until the end of their turn. Then, by suspending 1 of their Digimon or Tamers, 1 of your Digimon unsuspends. [All Turns] When effects play or digivolve your opponent's Digimon, if you have a Tamer, this Digimon may digivolve into [Imperialdramon: Fighter Mode] in the hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player activates their effect first.
