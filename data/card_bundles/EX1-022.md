## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If a Digimon card with [Free] in its traits is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, unsuspend this Digimon and suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. [Your Turn] For each color in this Digimon's digivolution cards, it gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No. “For each color” refers to unique colors. Blue is a single color, so the effect only grants +1000 DP.
