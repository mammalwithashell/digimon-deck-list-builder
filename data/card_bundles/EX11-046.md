## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Choose 1 of your opponent's highest play cost Digimon and delete all of their other Digimon. Then, if this Digimon has 4 or more [Vemmon] in its digivolution cards, until your opponent's turn ends, it gains ＜Blocker＞ and isn't affected by their effects. [End of Opponent's Turn] This Digimon may digivolve into [Galacticmon] in the hand or trash, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -6: 8 cards w/[Vemmon] in text
