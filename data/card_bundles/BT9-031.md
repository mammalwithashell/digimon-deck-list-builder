## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Unsuspend this Digimon, and it gains ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent’s turn. (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.) [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon becomes unsuspended, if [MetalGarurumon] or [X Antibody] is in its digivolution cards, return all of your opponent’s Digimon with the lowest level to their owners’ hands.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [MetalGarurumon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
