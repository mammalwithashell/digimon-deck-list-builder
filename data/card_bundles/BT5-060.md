## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Look at the top card of your deck. [On Deletion] Reveal 3 cards from the top of your deck. You may play 1 [Monitamon] among them without paying its memory cost. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.
