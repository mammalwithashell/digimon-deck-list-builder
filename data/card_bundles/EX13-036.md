## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] [On Play] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP for the turn. If there are 6 or fewer total cards in both players' security stacks, instead all of their Digimon get -7000 DP for the turn. [When Digivolving] By trashing the top security card of 1 player with the most security cards, you may activate 1 of this Digimon's [Security] effects. [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] [Counter] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 of each player's Digimon as the top security cards.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -5: Lv.5 × Lv.4 × Lv.3, all yellow w/[Holy Beast] trait

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-036 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7318** (2026-09-18) Q: What does an "instead" effect mean, exactly? — A: The processing shown after "instead" in the effect replaces the standard processing. For example, if an effect reads "Delete your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, instead delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP," and you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, you delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP instead of deleting 1 of their Digimon with 6000 DP or less.
- **Q7319** (2026-09-18) Q: What happens for "1 player with the most security cards" if both players have the same number of security cards? — A: The player that activated the effect chooses 1 player.
- **Q7320** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I activate a [Counter] effect and then activate another [Counter] effect? — A: No, you can't. Only 1 [Counter] effect can be activated per an attack.
- **Q7321** (2026-09-18) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card digivolves. In what order do they activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q7322** (2026-09-18) Q: Do this card's Assembly requirements mean that each Lv.5/Lv.4/Lv.3 card must be yellow and have the [Holy Beast] trait? — A: Yes, they must each satisfy the requirements.
- **Q7323** (2026-09-18) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack, does it battle with the attacking Digimon after its [Security] effect activates? — A: Yes, it battles after its [Security] effect activates.
- **Q7324** (2026-09-18) Q: Can this card's [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] [Counter] effect activate even if this card is the only Digimon in the battle area? — A: Yes, it can. If activated, only this Digimon is placed on top of the security stack.
