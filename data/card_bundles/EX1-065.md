## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] At the end of the battle, you may play 1 [Diaboromon] Token without paying its memory cost. (Diaboromon Tokens are level 6 white Digimon with a memory cost of 14, 3000 DP, and Mega/Unknown/Unidentified traits.) [Opponent's Turn] All of your [Diaboromon] gain ＜Blocker＞. (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-065 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3251** (2024-03-28) Q: I have this Digimon and a [Diaboromon] Token in my battle area. My opponent attacks with a Digimon, and deletes this Digimon with a [When Attacking] effect. Can I block the attack with my [Diaboromon] Token? — A: No. If your [Diaboromon] was deleted by a [When Attacking] effect, your [Diaboromon] Token will have already lost <Blocker> by the time you reach blocker timing.
- **Q3252** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I play a Token with this card’s [Security] effect at the end of the battle even if this card loses the battle against the attacking Digimon? — A: Yes. The [Security] effect activates as soon as the card is flipped during the security check, which means you can play the Token regardless of the battle's outcome.
- **Q3253** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is flipped over during a security check, but the attacking Digimon still has security checks remaining. Is the Token played off this Digimon’s [Security] effect first, or do I have to wait until my opponent finishes the rest of their security checks? — A: The Token is played first. Once all [Security] effects and effects triggered by battle have been activated, proceed to the next security check.
