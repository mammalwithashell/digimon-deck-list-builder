## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-050 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6624** (2026-06-19) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q6625** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6626** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card cannot attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q6627** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6628** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6629** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
