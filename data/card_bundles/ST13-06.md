[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ (This Digimon can attack when your opponent has 1 or more memory.) When DNA digivolving, for every 4 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 20 or less and trash the top card of your opponent's security stack. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When a card is removed from a player's security stack, unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST13-06 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q775** (2024-03-28) Q: When I DNA digivolve into this card, is it possible to attack with the <Blitz> in the [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Yes, [When Digivolving] <Blitz> can activate so long as the opponent somehow has 1 or more memory at the time.
- **Q776** (2024-03-28) Q: When I DNA digivolve into this card and it has 8 digivolution cards, do I delete 2 of my opponent's Digimon with play costs of 20 or less and trash the top 2 cards of their security stack? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q777** (2024-03-28) Q: When I DNA digivolve into this card, my opponent has 1 or more memory. If there is an inherited [When Attacking] effect when I attack with the <Blitz> in the [When Digivolving] effect, do I activate the [When Attacking] effect before this card's "for every 4 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 20 or less and trash the top card of your opponent's security stack" effect? — A: No, this card's <Blitz> and "for every 4 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 20 or less and trash the top card of your opponent's security stack" are a single effect, therefore if DNA digivolving, the latter part of the effect will continue to activate after the attack has been declared and won't be interrupted by another effect. After the whole effect has finished activating, the [When Attacking] effect will activate.
