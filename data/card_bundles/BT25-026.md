## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Trash the bottom 3 digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, 1 of their Digimon with no digivolution cards can't suspend until their turn ends. [Your Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them are red, this Digimon may digivolve into [Dianamon] in the trash with the cost reduced by 2.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] This Digimon's attack target can't change.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it triggers. However, this effect can only be activated when that Digimon is red.
