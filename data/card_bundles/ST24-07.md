## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST24-07 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6214** (2026-05-08) Q: Is this card treated as an Option card with the traits shown on this card? — A: Yes, it is. This card is treated as an Option card with the DATA SQUAD trait.
- **Q6215** (2026-05-08) Q: I used [GeoGrey Sword], and its [Main] effect caused the DP of my opponent's Digimon to become 0. At such times, is the Digimon with a DP of zero deleted? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. After trashing the used Option card or digivolving by Arts Digivolve, all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted upon the rule check. Then, if the deleted card has an [On Deletion] effect, it will trigger simultaneously with this card's [When Digivolving] effect.
