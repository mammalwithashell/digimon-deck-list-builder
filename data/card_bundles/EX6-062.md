## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3806** (2024-03-28) Q: When DNA digivolving into this card, can I DNA digivolve using a level 6 yellow Digimon and a level 6 black Digimon, or using a level 6 green Digimon and a level 6 purple Digimon? — A: No, you can't. This card's DNA digivolution can only be performed using a combination of 1 level 6 yellow or black Digimon and 1 level 6 green or purple Digimon.
- **Q3807** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Partition (yellow/black Lv.6 & green/purple Lv.6)> effect to play a level 6 yellow Digimon and a level 6 black Digimon or a level 6 green Digimon and a level 6 purple Digimon from digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. This card's <Partition (yellow/black Lv.6 & green/purple Lv.6)> effect can only play a combination of 1 level 6 yellow or black Digimon and 1 level 6 green or purple Digimon.
- **Q4736** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this Digimon didn't DNA digivolve, this card's [When Digivolving] effect will return 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of the deck for each of this card's level 6 digivolution cards.
