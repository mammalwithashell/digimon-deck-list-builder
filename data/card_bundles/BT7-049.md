## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may digivolve this Digimon into a green level 6 card among them without paying its memory cost. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-049 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1587** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to reveal cards from my deck, but then choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can choose to not digivolve. In such cases, you return all of the revealed cards to the bottom of your deck in any order.
- **Q1588** (2026-03-13) Q: When a digivolution would occur by this card's [When Attacking] effect, when exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw, and where do I draw from? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when this card is placed on top of that Digimon for the digivolution, and you draw from the unrevealed cards in your deck. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q1589** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before returning the remaining cards to the bottom of the deck? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q1590** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I activate this card's [When Attacking] effect when I have 2 or fewer cards in my deck? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you reveal as many cards from your deck as possible.
