## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1808** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reads, “if this Digimon has [MetalGreymon] or [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards”. Does having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits count? — A: No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [MetalGreymon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
- **Q1809** (2024-03-28) Q: After having digivolved into this card, I place an [X Antibody] card in this card’s digivolution cards later that turn. Does this Digimon get +3000 DP? — A: No. If a [MetalGreymon] or [X Antibody] isn’t in this Digimon’s digivolution cards at the time its [When Digivolving] effect activates, it doesn’t gain +3000 DP from its effect.
