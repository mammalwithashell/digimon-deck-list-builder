## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-039 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2961** (2024-06-21) Q: What does the "change 1 Digimon's original DP" effect do, exactly? — A: Such effects change the original DP shown on the target card to the predetermined value shown in the effect. For example, if a Digimon has an original DP of 8000 and it's chosen as the target for a "[Main] Change the original DP of 1 of your opponent's Digimon to 3000 until the end of their turn" effect, that Digimon's original DP will become 3000 instead of 8000.
- **Q2962** (2024-06-21) Q: What happens if a Digimon that got +1000 DP has its original DP changed by the "change 1 Digimon's original DP" effect? — A: Its original DP is changed, then +1000 is added to the changed DP. Values added to DP don't affect the original DP.
- **Q2963** (2024-06-21) Q: What happens if a Digimon has its original DP changed and is then affected by a "change 1 Digimon's original DP" effect again? — A: The original DP is changed to the most recent value.
- **Q2964** (2024-06-21) Q: Can a Digimon without DP be affected by the "change 1 Digimon's original DP" effect? — A: No, it can't. Effects that change information can't change information that a card didn't originally have. For example, if a Digimon has its original DP changed by an effect and then its top card becomes a card without DP due to a <De-Digivolve> effect, it will no longer be affected by the effect that changes the original DP.
