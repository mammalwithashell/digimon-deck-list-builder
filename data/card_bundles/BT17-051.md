## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place up to 4 level 5 or lower [Argomon] from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards. Then, delete up to 4 levels' total worth of your opponent's Digimon. For every 2 [Argomon] in this Digimon's digivolution cards, add 1 to this level deletion effect's maximum. [All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP for every 2 [Argomon] in its digivolution cards. [Opponent's Turn] None of your opponent's Tamers can unsuspend.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-051 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2806** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card’s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place just 2 cards in digivolution cards when I have 4 level 5 or lower [Argomon] in my trash? — A: Yes, you can. The effect reads "up to 4," therefore you can place any number from 1 to 4.
- **Q2807** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to delete up to 4 levels' worth of Digimon, can I delete my opponent's "Lv.-" Digimon? — A: No, you can't. "Lv.-" Digimon don't have a level, therefore they can't be chosen as targets for an effect that deletes up to 4 levels' worth of Digimon.
- **Q2808** (2024-03-28) Q: BT17-048 [Argomon] was deleted while it had an [Argomon] with an [On Deletion] effect in its digivolution cards. I first used BT17-048 [Argomon]'s [On Deletion] effect to play this card. At such times, if I use this card's [On Play] effect to place [Argomon] in digivolution cards before activating the [On Deletion] effect, what happens to that effect? — A: If a card is removed from the trash before a triggered [On Deletion] effect activates, that [On Deletion] effect can't be activated. _(related: BT17-048)_
