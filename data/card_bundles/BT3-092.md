## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-092 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1105** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this card, and use its [When Attacking] effect to play [MaloMyotismon]. This Digimon is then deleted, but do I gain a memory from [MaloMyotismon]'s "[All Turns] When another Digimon is deleted, gain 1 memory" effect? — A: Yes, BT3-92 [MaloMyotismon] is in play when this card is deleted, so you gain 1 memory. _(a ruling on BT3-086; this card is named in it)_
- **Q1109** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this card, and use its [When Attacking] effect to play [MaloMyotismon]. This Digimon is then deleted, but do I gain a memory from [MaloMyotismon]'s "[All Turns] When another Digimon is deleted, gain 1 memory" effect? — A: Yes, [BT3-92 MaloMyotismon] is in play when this card is deleted, so you gain 1 memory. _(a ruling on BT3-087; this card is named in it)_
- **Q1118** (2024-03-28) Q: I have this Digimon in play when one of my other Digimon attacks, deletes an opponent's Digimon, and is deleted in return. Do I gain two memory? — A: Yes, both you and your opponent's Digimon were deleted, so you gain two memory.
- **Q1119** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I gain 1 memory from this Digimon's effect when a Security Digimon is deleted? — A: No, Security Digimon aren't considered to be Digimon, so you don't gain any memory.
- **Q1120** (2024-03-28) Q: When I had this Digimon, another of my Digimon attacked, and both my Digimon and my opponent's Digimon were deleted in the battle. Do I gain 2 memory at such times? — A: Yes, the +1 memory effect activates twice, earning you two memory.
- **Q1121** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent and I both have this Digimon in play. If another Digimon is deleted, what happens? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the turn player gains a memory first, followed by the non-turn player gaining a memory, returning the memory gauge to its original position.
- **Q1122** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon attacks an opponent's Digimon and both Digimon end up being deleted, can I gain a memory off this card's effect? — A: No. This Digimon was deleted, so the [All Turns] effect doesn't activate.
