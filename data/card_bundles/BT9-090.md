## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Tapirmon] and 1 2-color black card among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon would digivolve into a 2-color black Digimon card, you may suspend this Tamer to reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-090 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1892** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 3 cards. If the cards revealed only include either a [Tapirmon] or a 2-color black card, can I still add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, as long as either a card with [Tapirmon] or a 2-color black card is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
- **Q1893** (2024-03-28) Q: If a black Digimon with an effect such as "this Digimon is also treated as red" that treats it as a different color is among the cards revealed for this card's effect, can I add that card to my hand? — A: No. Effects don't activate for cards revealed from your deck.
