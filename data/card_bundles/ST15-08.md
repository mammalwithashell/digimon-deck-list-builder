## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Agumon] or 1 Tamer card with [Tai Kamiya] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. ＜Blocker＞ (At blocker timing, by suspending this Digimon, it becomes the attack target.)

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn]When an attack target is switched, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST15-08 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q811** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card’s effect to play a Digimon with [Agumon] in its name such as [Agumon Expert] or [BushiAgumon]? — A: "No, you can't. In order to play an [Agumon], the Digimon card's name can only be [Agumon]."
- **Q812** (2024-03-28) Q: If I attack my opponent using a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and my opponent blocks, does this card's inherited effect activate? — A: Yes. Blocking causes the attack target to be switched, so the effect activates.
- **Q813** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s inherited effect activate when an attack target is switched for a Digimon other than a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q6160** (2026-05-08) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack, does its [Security] effect activate, then does it battle with the attacking Digimon? — A: Yes, its [Security] effect activates, then it battles.
