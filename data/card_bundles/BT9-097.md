## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would use this card, if you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards in play, reduce the memory cost of this card by 2. [Main] Return 1 of your opponent’s level 6 or lower Digimon to its owner’s hand. Then, unsuspend 1 of your Digimon with [Garurumon] in its name.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
- No. [X Antibody] in this effect's text refers to the card name, therefore only a card with [X Antibody] as its card name will meet the condition. It doesn't refer to the [X Antibody] trait.
