## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] If it's your turn, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 4 Digimon card with the [Angel] or [Free] trait in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] Trash the top digivolution card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon.


## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-016 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2816** (2026-05-08) Q: This card has BT16-016 [Patamon] in its digivolution cards and attacked an opponent's Digimon that has 1 digivolution card. At such times, can I use BT16-016 [Patamon]'s inherited effect to trash the digivolution card of the opponent's Digimon and then activate this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's [All Turns] effect triggers when this card attacks an opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards. It won't trigger if it attacks a Digimon with digivolution cards, therefore you can't activate it.
