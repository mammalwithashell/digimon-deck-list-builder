## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would play this card, by deleting 1 of your level 2 Digimon in the breeding area, reduce the play cost by 2. [On Play] ＜Draw 1＞. Then, trash 1 card in your hand. [All Turns] This Digimon can't digivolve. [On Deletion] By returning 2 cards with [Gizmon] in their names from your trash to the bottom of the deck in any order, you may play 1 [Gizmon: AT] from your trash without paying the cost.
