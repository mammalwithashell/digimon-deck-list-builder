## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 of your opponent's Digimon as any of their other Digimon's bottom digivolution card or under any of their Tamers. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers digivolve or effects place cards under them, by trashing any 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards, trash your opponent's top security card.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: 2 Digimon cards w/[Bagra Army] trait When this would be played, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each one reduces the play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5142** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I trash just 1 digivolution card for the conditions of this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [All Turns] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q5143** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place a card under a Tamer that already has cards under it, in what order do I place the cards? — A: If there are already any cards stacked under the Tamer, you place the new card at the bottom.
- **Q5144** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 1 of my opponent's Digimon under another of their Digimon, one that isn't affected by effects? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5145** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's Digimon under my opponent's other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card, will the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards leave the battle area? — A: Yes, it will leave the battle area. The digivolution cards of the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards will be trashed at the same time.
- **Q5146** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's Digimon under one of their Tamers, does that Tamer gain the placed card's inherited effect? — A: No, it doesn't. A Digimon will gain an inherited effect, but a Tamer doesn't gain it.
- **Q5147** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's BT12-038 [GeoGreymon] from the battle area as the bottom digivolution card of their BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] that's treated as a Digimon, does that BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] gain BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect? — A: Yes, it gains the inherited effect. If BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] suspends, BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect will trigger. However, as soon as it is no longer treated as a Digimon, it will lose inherited effects. _(related: BT12-038, BT17-087)_
- **Q5148** (2025-09-05) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger when either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon gets linked? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
