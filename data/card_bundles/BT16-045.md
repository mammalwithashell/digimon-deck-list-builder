## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, 1 of your Digimon gets +3000 DP until the end of your opponent's turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may switch the target of attack to 1 of your suspended Digimon with the [Insectoid] trait.
