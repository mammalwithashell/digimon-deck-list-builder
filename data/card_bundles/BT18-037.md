## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2957** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a digivolution requirement that digivolves from a Tamer mean that the Tamer that will become a digivolution card is treated as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
- **Q2958** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q2959** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q2960** (2024-06-21) Q: If cards that are targets of this effect are found in my security stack, do I have to add as many cards to my hand as possible? — A: No, you can also choose to not add them to your hand. If you don't add them, you shuffle your security stack without gaining <Recovery +1 ≪Deck≫>.
- **Q6605** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6606** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6607** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
