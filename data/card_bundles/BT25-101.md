## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-101 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6475** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "after" in this card's [Main] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't trash 1 [TS] trait card from your hand, you can't process the part after "after" in this card's [Main] effect.
- **Q6476** (2026-05-08) Q: This card is linked to a Digimon. If this card activates a link effect, is that effect considered a Digimon effect or an Option card effect? — A: It's considered a Digimon effect.
- **Q6477** (2026-05-08) Q: Due to an effect, I can't use an Option card. Can I pay this card's link cost and link to one of my Digimon? — A: Yes, you can link it. Linking a card isn't the same as using an Option card, therefore a card can still get linked when you can't use Option cards.
- **Q6478** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I also use this card's [Main] effect to link to a Digimon in the breeding area? — A: Yes, you can also link to a Digimon in the breeding area.
- **Q6479** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6480** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use a "you may link this card to 1 of your Digimon on the field without paying the cost" effect to link to a Digimon even if it doesn't have DP and is in the breeding area? — A: Yes, you can. However, a Digimon without DP won't be considered to have DP from link DP.
- **Q6481** (2026-05-08) Q: A card that has <Piercing> in its link effects and this card got linked to my Digimon that has <Link +1>. That Digimon attacked my opponent's Digimon, but both Digimon had the same DP and both lost the battle. At such times, if I activate the [All Turns] effect in this card's link effects immediately before my Digimon would be deleted and trash the card that has <Piercing> in its link effects, only the opponent's Digimon would be deleted in battle, but can I activate <Piercing> in those link effects? — A: No, you can't. The [All Turns] effect in this card's link effects triggers and activates immediately before both Digimon lose the battle and would be deleted. <Piercing> is lost when your opponent's Digimon is deleted, therefore you can't activate it.
