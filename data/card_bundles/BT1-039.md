## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-039 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q893** (2025-11-21) Q: Can I trash just 1 card in my hand for this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [When Attacking] effect require you to trash the specified number of cards in the hand.
- **Q894** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's effect after I attack with it and resolve all battles and checks? — A: No, you can't. This effect can only be activated after the attack declaration and before the counter timing.
