## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-020 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4309** (2025-01-24) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q4665** (2025-04-25) Q: What does a "your opponent can't play XX by effects" effect do, exactly? — A: Such effects prevent your opponent from playing XX by effects. With this card's effect, your opponent can no longer play Digimon or Tamers by effects such as BT10-018 [Gaossmon]'s [On Deletion] effect or BT9-099 [Sunrise Buster]'s [Main] effect. This effect affects the opponent player. _(related: BT10-018, BT9-099)_
- **Q4666** (2025-04-25) Q: I activate a "your opponent can't play XX by effects" effect, and in the same turn, my opponent activates an effect that reveals cards and plays a specified card from their deck. Is the card revealed from the deck but not played? — A: Yes. Your opponent reveals cards, but then doesn't play the specified card.
- **Q4667** (2025-04-25) Q: Can an opponent's Digimon be played if I activate a "your opponent can't play Digimon by effects" effect, and in the same turn, I activate an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: Yes, it can be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon by effects" is an effect that prevents your opponent from playing Digimon by effects. Your effects can still play an opponent's Digimon. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q4668** (2025-04-25) Q: Can my Digimon be played if I activate a "your opponent can't play Digimon by effects" effect, and in the same turn, my opponent activates an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: No, it can't be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon by effects" is an effect that prevents your opponent from playing Digimon by effects. Your Digimon can't be played by an opponent's effect. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q6245** (2026-05-08) Q: If I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect, can my opponent use an effect to play a Digimon or Tamer into their breeding area? — A: No, you can't.
