## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. During your opponent's next unsuspend phase, none of your opponent's Digimon can unsuspend. [When Attacking] Gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
- No. The Digimon suspended by this effect -- along with all of your opponent's other suspended Digimon -- remain suspended during their unsuspend phase.
