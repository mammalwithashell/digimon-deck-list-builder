## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST1-09 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q604** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect activate when I attack my opponent's Digimon? — A: No. If your opponent doesn't block, the effect doesn't activate. Regardless of what the target of attack is, the effect activates when a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards attacks an opponent's Digimon and has its attack target changed to an opponent's Digimon that blocks.
- **Q942** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has [ST1-09 MetalGreymon], [BT1-021 MetalGreymon], and [BT1-114 MetalGreymon] in play in their battle area. I use this card's When Digivolving effect to choose [ST1-09 MetalGreymon]. Are all of my opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards deleted? — A: Yes. The card numbers may be different, but the names match. All of your opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards are deleted. _(a ruling on BT1-084; this card is named in it)_
