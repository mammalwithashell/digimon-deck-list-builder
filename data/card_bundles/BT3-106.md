## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-106 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1143** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card give <Security Attack +2> to a Digimon with both <Blocker> and <Reboot>? — A: No. Even if the Digimon has both <Blocker> and <Reboot>, the card's effect can only be used once to grant <Security Attack +1>.
- **Q1144** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to give <Security Attack +1> to one of my Digimon, but if digivolution and <De-Digivolution> causes that Digimon to lose either <Blocker> or <Reboot>, what happens to the <Security Attack +1>? — A: A Digimon that loses <Blocker> or <Reboot> also loses <Security Attack +1>, as it is no longer affected by this card's effect.
