## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Your opponent reveals the top 3 cards of their deck. Choose 1 Digimon card among them, and delete up to its play cost's total worth of your opponent's Digimon. Return the revealed cards to the top or bottom of the deck.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-067 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2439** (2024-03-28) Q: If the card chosen with this card's effect has a play cost of 5, can it delete up to 5 of my opponent's Digimon? — A: No. If the chosen card has a play cost of 5, choose a number of your opponent's Digimon whose total play cost add up to 5 or less and delete them.
- **Q2440** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card’s effect returns revealed cards to my opponent's deck, which player decides the order the cards are placed in and whether the cards are placed on the top or bottom of the deck? — A: The player that activated this effect chooses.
