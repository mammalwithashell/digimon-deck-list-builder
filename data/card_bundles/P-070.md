## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] At the end of the battle, reveal the top card of your deck. If it’s a black Digimon card with a play cost of 4 or less, you may play it without paying its memory cost. Add the remaining cards to your hand. Then, add this card to its owner’s hand.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. This effect reads "you may play it without paying the cost," therefore you may choose to not play it. In such cases, because the effect reads "add the remaining cards to your hand," you add the card to your hand.
