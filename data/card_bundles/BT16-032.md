## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-032 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2627** (2026-05-08) Q: What does "end the attack" mean, exactly? — A: After this effect activates, the current timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. For example, if this effect activates during the attack declaration timing, it will make a transition to the end of attack timing. A transition to the counter timing or block timing won't occur, and the attack won't succeed.
- **Q2628** (2026-05-08) Q: Can an "end the attack" effect end an attack by a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: Yes, such attacks can be ended. "End the attack" effects are effects that change the timing, they don't affect an attacking Digimon.
