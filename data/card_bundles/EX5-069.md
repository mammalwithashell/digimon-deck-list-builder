## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] By trashing 1 card in your hand, delete 1 of your opponent's level 6 or lower Digimon. If this effect trashed a card with the [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When an effect plays an opponent's Digimon, ＜Delay＞. ・ You may play 1 [Leviamon] from your trash without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3674** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's "if this effect trashed a card with the [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait" refer to the card trashed from my hand or my opponent's level 6 or lower Digimon that I deleted? — A: It refers to the card trashed from your hand.
- **Q3675** (2024-03-28) Q: If an effect plays an opponent's Digimon while this card is in the battle area, do I have to activate <Delay>? — A: No, <Delay> effects are "trash this card in your battle area to activate" effects, therefore you can choose to not trash the card in order to prevent them from activating.
- **Q3676** (2024-03-28) Q: When an effect plays an opponent's Digimon into the breeding area, does this card's <Delay> activate? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q3677** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent uses [BT13-020 ShineGreymon: Burst Mode]'s effect to play [Marcus Damon] as a Digimon, does this card's <Delay> activate? — A: No, it doesn't. [Marcus Damon] is played as a Tamer by [BT13-020 ShineGreymon: Burst Mode] effect and then it's treated as being a Digimon by an effect, therefore it isn't considered to be played as a Digimon. _(related: BT13-020)_
- **Q3678** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's <Delay> also activate when one of my effects plays an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q4735** (2025-06-13) Q: This card is placed in the battle area while EX5-063 [Leviamon] and BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)] are in the trash. When an opponent's Digimon is played by an effect, can I use this card's <Delay> effect to play EX5-063 [Leviamon], then digivolve that Digimon into BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)] using its own {Trash} [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, you can. When the opponent's Digimon is played by an effect, this card's <Delay> effect and BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]'s {Trash} [All Turns] effect will trigger simultaneously. You activate this card's <Delay> effect first and play EX5-063 [Leviamon] from the trash. EX5-063 [Leviamon]'s effect derived triggers, then BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]'s triggered {Trash} [All Turns] effect can digivolve EX5-063 [Leviamon] into BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]. _(related: EX5-063, BT15-081)_
