## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Add this card to the hand. [Main] Play 1 [Diaboromon] Token without paying the cost. Then, place this card as the bottom digivolution card of 1 your Digimon with [Diaboromon] in its name and without [Doomsday Clock] in its digivolution cards. (Digimon/Cost 14/Lv. 6/White/Mega/Unknown/Unidentified/3000 DP) [Start of Your Turn] If your battle area has 4 placed [Doomsday Clock]s, you win the game.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an opponent's effect, by deleting 1 of your other [Diaboromon], prevent it from leaving. [End of Opponent’s Turn] Place 1 [Doomsday Clock] from this Digimon's digivolution cards in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-100 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2896** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place this card in the digivolution cards of a [Diaboromon] Token that was played by this effect? — A: No, you can't. Cards can't be placed under tokens.
- **Q2897** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "you win the game" in this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect mean, exactly? — A: When this effect activates, the player that activated the effect wins the game.
- **Q2898** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when [...] would leave the battle area" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when that Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
- **Q2899** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use the [End of Opponent's Turn] effect in this card's inherited effects to place this card itself in the battle area when this card is in a Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
