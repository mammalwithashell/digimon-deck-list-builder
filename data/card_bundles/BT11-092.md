## Official Q&A

- **Q2117** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect if an effect at the start of my turn or during my unsuspend phase or breeding phase causes the memory counter to move to my opponent's side and it becomes my opponent's turn? — A: No, it doesn't activate if it becomes your opponent's turn before your main phase.
