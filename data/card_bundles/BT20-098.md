## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] By returning 9 levels' total worth of Digimon cards from your opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, you may play 1 [Ghost] trait Digimon card of each returned card's level from your trash without paying the costs. Then, the Digimon this effect played gain ＜Rush＞ and ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent's turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card with the [Ghost] trait from your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-098 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4439** (2025-01-24) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to return Digimon cards from my opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck until the total of their levels is 8 or less? — A: No, you can't. A "by doing X" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. In the case of this card's [Main] effect, you must return Digimon cards to the bottom of the deck until the total value is the specified level.
- **Q4440** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to return a level 3 and a level 6 Digimon card from my opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, what level of Digimon cards can I play from my trash? — A: You can play level 3 and level 6 Digimon cards. 1 of each card with the same level as the returned cards can be played.
- **Q4441** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to return 3 level 3 Digimon cards from my opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, can I play 3 level 3 Digimon cards from my trash? — A: Yes, you can. 1 of each card with the same level as the returned cards can be played.
