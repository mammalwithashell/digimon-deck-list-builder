## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3009** (2026-06-19) Q: What is a {Hand} effect? — A: A {Hand} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in your hand. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than your hand.
- **Q3010** (2024-06-21) Q: Can I use this card's [Hand] [Main] effect to place just 1 [Beetlemon] or [MetalKabuterimon] from my trash under my Tamer? — A: No, you can't. A "by doing X" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. In order for the conditions for this card's [Hand] [Main] effect to be met, you must place 1 [Beetlemon] and 1 [MetalKabuterimon] under your Tamer.
- **Q3011** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a digivolution requirement that digivolves from a Tamer mean that the Tamer that will become a digivolution card is treated as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
- **Q3012** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q3013** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q6646** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6647** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6648** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
