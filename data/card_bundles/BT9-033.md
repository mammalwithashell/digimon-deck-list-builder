## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1831** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's effect also prevent me from using Option card effects to play Digimon? — A: Yes. Option card effects are effects just like any other, so you can't use them to play Digimon.
- **Q1832** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is in play, can I activate effects that reduce play costs? — A: Yes. Effects that only reduce play costs can still be activated. Effects that reduce play costs AND play Digimon can’t be activated.
- **Q5205** (2025-10-03) Q: Can players play Digimon into the breeding area by effects while this card is in the battle area? — A: No, they can't.
