## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent's Turn] While you have a blue Digimon in play, this Digimon gains ＜Blocker＞. (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST9-07 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q711** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent attacks with a Digimon and deletes my only blue Digimon with its [When Attacking] effect. I have no other blue Digimon in play, but can I still block the attack with this Digimon? — A: No. The [When Attacking] effect activates before reaction timing, which is when <Blocker> triggers. By the time reaction timing occurs, this Digimon no longer has <Blocker>, so it cannot block.
