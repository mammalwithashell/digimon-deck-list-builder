## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Deletion] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. (Trash 1 card from the top of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Stop trashing when you would trash a level 3 card or the Digimon's last card.)

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted, by deleting 1 other Digimon with [Sukamon] in its name, prevent that deletion.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-065 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2307** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's inherited effect delete an opponent's [Sukamon] to prevent itself from being deleted? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q2308** (2024-06-21) Q: I have [Sukamon] A and [Sukamon] B with this card in their digivolution cards in the battle area. When [Sukamon] A would be deleted, I attempted to delete [Sukamon] B using this card's inherited effect. On the other hand, when [Sukamon] B would be deleted, I attempted to delete [Sukamon] A using this card's inherited effect. At such times, can [Sukamon] A activate this card's inherited effect again? — A: No, it can't be activated. Until the processing for the 1st activation has resolved, only 1 activation is possible for each immediate-type effect. In your case, until the deletion of the 1st only [Sukamon] A is resolved, only 1 activation is possible for each "when [...] would" effect, therefore the effect that has already activated for [Sukamon] A can't be activated again.
- **Q2615** (2024-09-20) Q: I used this card's [Your Turn] effect to attach [End of Attack] to BT16-011 [Garudamon (X Antibody)] and BT13-014 [Garudamon]'s inherited effects. If I use BT13-014 [Garudamon]'s inherited effect to delete my opponent's BT13-065 [PlatinumSukamon] at the end of this card's attack, then <De-Digivolve> causes this card's [Your Turn] effect to be removed, can I then activate BT16-011 [Garudamon (X Antibody)]'s inherited effect? — A: No, it can't be activated. If the trigger conditions are no longer met before activation, it can't be activated. _(a ruling on BT16-015; this card is named in it)_
