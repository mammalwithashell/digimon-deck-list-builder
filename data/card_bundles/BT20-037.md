## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Security A.+1＞ ＜Partition (Yellow Lv.6 & Green/Black Lv.6)＞ (When this Digimon with each of the specified digivolution cards would leave the battle area other than by your effects or a battle, you may play 1 each of the specified cards without paying the costs.) ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4347** (2025-01-24) Q: If this card has 2 level 6 digivolution cards, can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to suspend 2 of my opponent's Digimon or Tamers and gain 2 memory? — A: Yes, for each of this Digimon's level 6 digivolution cards, you suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers and gain 1 memory.
- **Q4348** (2025-01-24) Q: What does "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents [On Play] effects on cards from activating. Triggered [On Play] effects can no longer activate when a card is played or when effects such as "activate that card's [On Play]" effect" activate.
- **Q4349** (2025-01-24) Q: I used a card affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects" to attack. Can I activate that card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect at such times? — A: Yes, you can. The effect can activate if it's triggered upon the [When Attacking] timing.
- **Q4350** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card is affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects," can it activate its own [On Play] effect using an effect such as "activate 1 of that card's [On Play] effects"? — A: No, it can't be activated.
- **Q4351** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card is affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects," can another card's [On Play] effect be activated using an effect such as "activate 1 of that card's [On Play] effects as an effect of this card"? — A: No, it can't be activated.
- **Q4352** (2025-01-24) Q: Can I use "activate 1 of that card's [On Play] effects as an effect of this card" on a card that isn't affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects" to activate the [On Play] effect on another card that's affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects"? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4353** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card is affected by "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects," can I perform just the "by doing X" condition in its [On Play] effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q4354** (2025-04-04) Q: A card digivolved into a card that has an [On Play] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] effect while "none of your opponent's Digimon can activate [On Play] effects" was activated. Does that count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn] even if that effect can't be activated upon the timing when that card is played? — A: No, it doesn't count. The effect can't activate upon the [On Play] timing, therefore it doesn't count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn].
- **Q4605** (2025-04-04) Q: If BT20-037 [Chaosmon: Valdur Arm] has this card, a yellow level 6 card, and a green level 6 card in its digivolution cards, then this card's inherited effect causes its DP to become 0 and it's deleted, does BT20-037 [Chaosmon: Valdur Arm]'s <Partition (Yellow Lv.6 + Green/Black Lv.6)> trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers. When deletion occurs by DP becoming 0, it's considered deletion by the rules, therefore <Partition>'s trigger conditions are met. _(a ruling on BT20-099; this card is named in it)_
- **Q4718** (2025-06-13) Q: Does this card's [When Digivolving] effect prevent both my opponent's Digimon and Tamers from activating [On Play] effects or unsuspending until the end of their turn? — A: That's correct, both your opponent's Digimon and Tamers are prevented from activating [On Play] effects or unsuspending.
- **Q4841** (2025-06-13) Q: My opponent's BT20-037 [Chaosmon: Valdur Arm]'s [When Digivolving] effect is preventing my [On Play] effects from activating. When this card attacks or my Digimon digivolves into it, can I use its [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] to place a card from my hand or trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card, then activate the placed card's [On Play] effect? — A: You can place the card, but you can't activate any of its [On Play] effects. _(a ruling on EX9-073; this card is named in it)_
