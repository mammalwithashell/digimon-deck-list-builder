## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and 1 of their Tamers. Until the end of your opponent's turn, all of their Digimon and Tamers don't unsuspend. [When Attacking] Trash the top card of your opponent’s security stack for every 2 of your opponent's suspended Digimon and/or Tamers.
