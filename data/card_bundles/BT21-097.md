## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [Appmon] trait on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Appmon] or [App Driver] trait among them to the hand. Trash the rest. Then, place this card in the battle area. [End of Your Turn] ＜Delay＞ ・You may link 1 card from your hand with 1 of your Digimon without paying the cost.
