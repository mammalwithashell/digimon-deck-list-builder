## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] If [Gallantmon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon gets +4000 DP and 1 of their Digimon gets -4000 DP. [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] [Once per Turn] Delete 1 of your opponent's 10000 DP or lower Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, trash their top security card and this Digimon unsuspends. [All Turns] While you have 0 or less memory, this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3975** (2024-11-22) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card digivolves. In what order do they activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q3976** (2024-11-22) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 10000 DP or less. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] effect by not choosing a card to delete? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent has a Digimon with 10000 DP or less, you must choose that card and delete it.
- **Q3977** (2024-11-22) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 10000 DP or less. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] effect by choosing an opponent's Digimon with 10000 DP or less and an effect that prevents deletion? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3978** (2024-11-22) Q: What position on the memory gauge does "while you have 0 or less" memory refer to? — A: It refers to when the memory gauge is at 0 or further to the right.
- **Q3979** (2024-11-22) Q: What does "isn't affected by [...] effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digmon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q3980** (2024-11-22) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen.
- **Q3981** (2025-04-04) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q3982** (2024-11-22) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q3983** (2024-11-22) Q: If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q3984** (2025-06-13) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
