## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. You may digivolve 1 of your Digimon into 1 non-white Digimon card among them without paying its memory cost. If you don't, add 1 Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 Tamer card from your hand without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-072 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3362** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to reveal cards from my deck, but then choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can choose to not digivolve. If you don't digivolve, you add 1 Digimon card among the revealed cards to your hand, then return all of the remaining cards to the bottom of your deck in any order.
- **Q3363** (2026-03-13) Q: When a digivolution would occur by this card's [Main] effect, when exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when this card is placed on top of that Digimon for the digivolution, and you draw from the unrevealed cards in your deck. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q3364** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before returning the remaining cards to the bottom of the deck? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q3365** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I activate this card's [Main] effect when I have 4 or fewer cards in my deck? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you reveal as many cards from your deck as possible.
