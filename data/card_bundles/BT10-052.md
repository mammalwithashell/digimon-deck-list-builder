- **Q1975** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is suspended, can it itself become the target when its [Opponent's Turn] effect switches the target of an opponent's Digimon's attack? — A: Yes, you can choose to do so.
- **Q1976** (2025-06-13) Q: When one of my opponent's Digimon attacks, in what order do that Digimon's [When Attacking] effect and this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect activate? — A: They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player can activate their [When Attacking] effect first.
