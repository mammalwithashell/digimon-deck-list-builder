## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait and 1 Tamer card with inherited effects among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 Tamer card with inherited effects from your hand with the play cost reduced by 2.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
