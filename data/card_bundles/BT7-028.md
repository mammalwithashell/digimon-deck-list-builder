## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] You may play 1 level 3 Digimon card or 1 [Whamon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards as another Digimon without paying its memory cost. [Your Turn] When you play a Digimon from one of your Digimon's digivolution cards, return 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to its owner's hand. Trash all of the digivolution cards of that Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-028 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1548** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon with digivolution cards is targeted by my opponent’s <De-Digivolve>, resulting in it becoming one of the Digimon in its digivolution cards. When this happens, can I return 1 of my opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to its owner's hand with this card's [Yout Turn] effect? — A: No. Having a digivolution card become a Digimon as a result of <De-Digivolve> does not count as playing that card, so this card's [Your Turn] effect does not trigger.
