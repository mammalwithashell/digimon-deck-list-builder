## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-101 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2698** (2024-03-28) Q: My Digimon attacks and digivolves into this card by an effect during the attack. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to attack again? — A: No, you can't. An attack declaration can't be made during an attack.
- **Q5452** (2025-11-21) Q: This card is in the battle area, and it has X Antibody in its digivolution cards. My opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less and a [When Attacking] effect attacks, and now it's suspended. At such times, this card's [All Turns] effect causes that Digimon's DP to become 0. Which is processed first, the deletion by a rule check or that Digimon's [When Attacking] effect? — A: The deletion by a rule check is processed first. As soon as your opponent's Digimon attacks and suspends, that Digimon's [When Attacking] effect triggers, but the rule check occurs before it activates. The Digimon with 0 DP will be deleted upon the rule check before the [When Attacking] effect can activate.
