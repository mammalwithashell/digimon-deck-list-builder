## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards, you may play 1 Tamer card with the [Night Claw] or [Light Fang] trait from your hand without paying the cost. This effect can't play cards with the same name as any of your Tamers.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with the [Night Claw], [Light Fang] or [Galaxy] trait would be deleted, by trashing 2 same-level cards from its digivolution cards, it isn't deleted.

## Official Q&A

- **Q4930** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
- **Q4931** (2025-07-04) Q: I have EX5-064 [Koh & Sayo] in the battle area. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play BT22-102 [Sayo]? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: EX5-064, BT22-102)_
