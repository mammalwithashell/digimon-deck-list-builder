## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 memory or less, set your memory to 3. [Your Turn] When one of your green or blue Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives, you may suspend this Tamer to gain 1 memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.
