### Effect

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-109 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q978** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a level 6 Digimon with a digivolution cost of 3, and use this card's effect to reduce that cost by 4, to -1. Can I digivolve into that Digimon by gaining a memory? — A: No, digivolution costs can't be reduced to less than zero. You can digivolve the card for free, but you can't gain memory with effects that reduce digivolution costs.
- **Q979** (2024-03-28) Q: Does activating this card's effect also reduce the digivolution cost of cards in my breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't. Since the card doesn't specify breeding areas, the effect doesn't apply to Digimon there.
- **Q980** (2024-03-28) Q: After using this card, can I reduce the digivolution cost of my level 5 green Digimon digivolving to level 6 when an effect to digivolve one of my Digimon is activated? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1736** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this Digimon in play. During my turn, I use an Option card like [BT1-109 Smashed Potatoes] or [EX1-071 Win Rate: 60%!] which has an effect that activates “the next time one of your Digimon digivolves”. After that, all of my opponent’s Digimon become suspended. If I then digivolve one of my Digimon, are the effects from the Option cards I used earlier negated? — A: No. You already used the Option cards and activated their effects, so this card’s [Opponent’s Turn] effect can’t negate them. _(related: EX1-071)_
