## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q900** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to play a Digi-Egg in its digivolution cards to my battle area? — A: No, Digi-Egg cards aren't Digimon cards, so they can't be played with this effect.
- **Q901** (2024-03-28) Q: I suspend this card as I declare its attack. If I use its effect to play a Digimon card from its digivolution cards, does that Digimon enter play suspended? — A: No, it's treated as a separate Digimon, and enters play unsuspended.
- **Q902** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's effect to play a Digimon card from its digivolution cards, does that Digimon carry over any effects currently applied to this Digimon? — A: No, it's treated as a separate Digimon, and doesn't receive any effects that currently apply to the original Digimon.
- **Q903** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's effect to play a Digimon card with an [On Play] effect, does that effect activate as the Digimon enters play? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q904** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's effect to play a Digimon card with an [On Play] effect, when does that effect activate? — A: It activates immediately after the card enters play. Even if there are other [When Attacking] and "when one of your Digimon attacks" effects waiting to be activated, the [On Play] effect was just triggered, so it activates first.
- **Q905** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to play a Digimon card. Can it attack this turn? — A: No, it's considered to have just entered play, so it can't attack.
- **Q906** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon has a level 4 or lower Digimon card in its digivolution cards, do I have to activate this card's effect? — A: Yes, as long as you are able to.
