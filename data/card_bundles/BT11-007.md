## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 red Digimon card with [Vaccine] in its traits and 1 red Tamer card among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If you have a red Tamer in play, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-007 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2049** (2024-03-28) Q: If the red Tamer cards among the cards revealed from my deck do not have the [Vaccine] trait, does that mean I can't add any cards to my hand? — A: No, even if it does not have the [Vaccine] trait, you can add 1 red Tamer card to your hand. However, you can't add a red Digimon card to your hand if it doesn't have the [Vaccine] trait.
- **Q2050** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 3 cards. If the cards revealed only include either a red Digimon card with the [Vaccine] trait or a red Tamer card, can I still add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, as long as either a red Digimon card with the [Vaccine] trait or a red Tamer card is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
- **Q2051** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's effect reveals 3 cards. If both a red Digimon card with the [Vaccine] trait and a red Tamer card are among the cards revealed, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. This card's effect reads “Add 1 red Digimon card [...] and 1 red Tamer card among them to your hand,” so you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q2052** (2024-03-28) Q: If both a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and my red Tamer are deleted at the same time, does the "gain 1 memory" effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate.
