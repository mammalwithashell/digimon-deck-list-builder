## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] ＜Draw 1＞. (Draw 1 card from your deck.) Then, you may digivolve 1 of your Digimon into a Digimon card in your hand that can digivolve for a digivolution cost of 3 or less without paying its digivolution cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, add this card to your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3354** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card to digivolve into a Digimon card with a special digivolution costing 3 or less? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3355** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card to DNA Digivolve into a Digimon card with a DNA Digivolution costing 3 or less? — A: No. The effect reads “You may digivolve 1 of your Digimon,” so you can’t use it to DNA Digivolve.
- **Q3356** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card to digivolve one of my Tamers into a Digimon that can digivolve from Tamers? — A: No. The effect reads “You may digivolve 1 of your Digimon,” so you can’t use it to digivolve a Tamer.
- **Q3357** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I ignore digivolution requirements when digivolving by the effect of this card, as long as the cost is 3 or less? — A: No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can't ignore digivolution requirements unless some other effect that permits you to do so.
- **Q3358** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a card in my hand with an effect that reads “1 of your Digimon may digivolve into this card in your hand for a digivolution cost of 3.” Can I use this card’ to digivolve into it? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3359** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate effects that reduce digivolution costs like <Digisorption> or [EX1-071 Win Rate: 60%!] to reduce the digivolution cost of a card in my hand from 4 or more to 3 or less, and then use this card to digivolve into it? — A: No, those effects are only active when the digivolution is taking place, which means they reduce the digivolution cost after a player has announced a digivolution. As such, at the time this card's effect is activated, the digivolution cost hasn't been reduced, and you can't choose the card with this card’s effect. _(related: EX1-071)_
- **Q3360** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon card with multiple digivolution requirements: one with a digivolution cost of 3, and one with a digivolution cost of 4. Can I use this card to digivolve into that Digimon card and apply the requirement with a cost of 4? — A: No. Even if the card has a digivolution requirement with a cost of 3, if you want to digivolve off the requirement with a cost of 4, you can’t use this card to digivolve.
