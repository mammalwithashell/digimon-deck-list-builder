## Official Q&A
- activates twice. doesn't activate once. Even if a Tamer card or Option card becomes the top card after trashing the top card, can trash a card regardless of its card category, therefore you go on to trash the 2nd card. However, if a card with an "isn't affected by effects" becomes the top card after the first , it won't be affected by the second and won't be trashed.
