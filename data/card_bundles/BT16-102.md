## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2699** (2024-03-28) Q: When "isn't affected by your opponent's effects" is gained from this card's [When Digivolving] effect, can the target of attack be switched to this Digimon using a "switch the target of attack" effect such as <Raid> on an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it can. A "switch the target of attack" effect such as <Raid> affects the attacking Digimon itself, therefore the target of attack can be switched to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects.
- **Q2700** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [All Turns] effect activate when a security card is removed from my security stack by one of my own effects? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q2701** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if [Magnamon (X Antibody)] or an [Armor Form] trait card isn't in this card's digivolution cards, this card's [When Digivolving] effect will unsuspend this Digimon.
