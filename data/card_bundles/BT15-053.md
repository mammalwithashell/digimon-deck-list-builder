## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT15-053 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2536** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is unsuspended, then it's suspended by an opponent's "Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. That Digimon can't unsuspend during your opponent's next unsuspend phase" effect, are effects that prevent unsuspending during the unsuspend phase negated? — A: Yes. It's no longer affected by your opponent's effects as soon as it's suspended, so it can now unsuspend.
- **Q2537** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect prevent [Security] effects on my opponent's Security Digimon from affecting this Digimon when it's suspended? — A: Yes, it isn't affected. [Security] effects on Security Digimon are treated as Digimon effects, so the "this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon" effect prevents such effects from affecting this Digimon.
