## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [All Turns] When one of your level 5 or higher [Free] trait Digimon would be deleted in battle, by suspending this Tamer, you may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card from that Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. [End of Your Turn] 1 of your [Free] trait Digimon may attack an opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2863** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to suspend this Tamer when my level 5 or higher [Free] trait Digimon would be deleted in battle, even if I don't have any level 4 or lower Digimon cards in that Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can. Even if the part after the conditions specified with "by X" in an effect can't be resolved, the effect can still activate.
- **Q2864** (2024-03-28) Q: When my level 5 or higher [Free] trait Digimon would be deleted in battle, I used this card's [All Turns] effect to play a level 4 or lower Digimon card with <Retaliation> in its inherited effects from that Digimon's digivolution cards. Can I use <Retaliation> to delete the battled opponent's Digimon at such times? — A: No, you can't. The card is removed from digivolution cards before <Retaliation> can activate.
- **Q2865** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to attack even when the [Free] trait Digimon is suspended? — A: No, you can't. This card's [End of Your Turn] effect does not allow you to attack with Digimon that are in a state where they can't attack.
- **Q2866** (2024-03-28) Q: When I have 2 copies of this Tamer and 2 unsuspended [Free] trait Digimon, can I activate the [End of Your Turn] effect on both copies of this card and attack using both of the [Free] trait Digimon? — A: No, you can't. The effect will trigger on both copies, but after the attack declaration is made for the 1st Digimon and effects such as [When Attacking] effects have finished activating, the effect on the 2nd copy of this card that was pending activation will activate before the counter timing. However, a new attack declaration can't be made during an attack, and only the 1st Digimon's attack can be performed as a result.
