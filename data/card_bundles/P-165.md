## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-165 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4275** (2024-12-20) Q: An opponent's Digimon attacks using an effect at the end of their turn. Then, if this card was checked upon that Digimon's attack and this card was played using its [Security] effect, when do I delete the [Familiar] Token that was played by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: It isn't deleted at this timing, it's deleted at the end of your opponent's next turn.
- **Q5756** (2025-12-25) Q: Do I delete the token that was played by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect at the end of my opponent's turn? — A: Yes, it's deleted upon the deletion timing.
- **Q5757** (2025-12-25) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a token played by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously. Therefore, the turn player can choose the processing order.
