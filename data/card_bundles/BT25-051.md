## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Blocker＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your Digimon with [Beast], [Animal] or [Sovereign], other than [Sea Animal], in any of its traits or the [Shaman] or [TS] trait gets +3000 DP until your opponent's turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon wins a battle, ＜Draw 1＞.

## Official Q&A
- It triggers when the battle is won. When the Digimon with this effect wins a battle, the opponent's Digimon that lost the battle is deleted, then the "when this Digimon wins a battle" effect can be activated.
