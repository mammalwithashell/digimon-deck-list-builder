## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][When Attacking][Once Per Turn] By trashing the top card of your security stack, you may play 1 yellow level 4 or lower card from your hand without paying the cost. Digimon played by this effect gain ＜Rush＞ for the turn. [All Turns] All of your yellow Digimon gain ＜Barrier＞. [Opponent's Turn] All of your opponent's Digimon with levels greater than or equal to the total cards in both players' security stacks gain ＜Security A. -2＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3597** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect work, exactly? — A: This effect gives <Security A. -2> to all of your opponent's Digimon with levels greater than or equal to the total cards in both players' security stacks. For example, if the total cards in both players' security stacks is 6, then all of your opponent's level 6 and higher Digimon will get <Security A. -2>.
- **Q3598** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "total cards in both players' security stacks" in this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect mean? — A: It means the total number of cards in both you and your opponent's security stacks. For example, if you have 3 cards in your security stack and your opponent has 2 cards in their security stack, the total security cards will be 5 cards.
- **Q3599** (2024-03-28) Q: If the number of security cards decreases, does the level for the targets for <Security A. -2> in this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect also decrease at that time? — A: Yes, that's correct.
