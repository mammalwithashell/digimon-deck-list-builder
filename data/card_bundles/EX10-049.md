- **Q5132** (2025-09-05) Q: What does an "instead" effect mean, exactly? — A: The processing shown after "instead" in the effect replaces the standard processing. For example, if an effect reads "Delete your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, instead delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP," and you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, you delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP instead of deleting 1 of their Digimon with 6000 DP or less.
- **Q5395** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if your opponent doesn't have 10 or fewer cards in their hand, this card's [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] effect will delete 1 of their level 3 or lower Digimon.
