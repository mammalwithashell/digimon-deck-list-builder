## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Blocker＞ [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] If your opponent has 10 or fewer cards in their trash, trash the top 3 cards of both players' decks. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon. If your opponent has 10 or more cards in their trash, add 2 to this effect's level maximum. (Rule) Trait: Has [Fallen Angel] Type.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] This Digimon gains ＜Security A. +1＞ for the turn. If your opponent has 10 or fewer cards in their trash, instead trash the top 2 cards of both players' decks.

## Official Q&A
- The processing shown after "instead" in the effect replaces the standard processing. For example, if an effect reads "Delete your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, instead delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP," and you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, you delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP instead of deleting 1 of their Digimon with 6000 DP or less.
