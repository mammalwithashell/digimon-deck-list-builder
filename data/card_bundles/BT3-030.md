## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 level 4 or lower digivolution card of 1 of your Digimon cards as another Digimon without paying its memory cost. [Your Turn] All of your level 4 or lower Digimon gain ＜Jamming＞. (This Digimon can't be deleted in battles against Security Digimon.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1064** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I have to activate this card's [When Evolving] effect? — A: No, you can choose not to use it.
- **Q1065** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect on a Level 4 Digimon card that's part of this card's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1066** (2024-03-28) Q: Will this card's [Your Turn] effect result in my Level 4 Digimon with <Jamming> losing <Jamming> when Digivolving to Level 5 or above? — A: Yes, because the effect is only active during [Your Turn], the moment the Digimon becomes Level 5 or above, the effect no longer applies to the Digimon, and it will lose <Jamming>.
