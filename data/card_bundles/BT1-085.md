## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-085 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q945** (2024-03-28) Q: I have both this card and [ST2-12 Matt Ishida] in play. How do I activate their effects? — A: You can resolve their effects in any order. For example, if you're starting the turn at 1 memory, you can resolve this card's effect first to go up to 3 memory, then activate [ST2-12 Matt Ishida]'s effect to go to 4 memory and above.
- **Q946** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have two copies of this card in my battle area, does my Digimon with 4 or more digivolution cards gain <Security Attack +1> twice? — A: Yes. Each card's effect activates individually: <Security Attack +1> gets applied twice, allowing you to check two extra cards.
- **Q947** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to give one of my Digimon <Security Attack +1>, which I use to attack my opponent. The card revealed from the first check has a security effect that reduces my Digimon's digivolution cards to 3 or fewer. Does it still have <Security Attack +1>? — A: Your Digimon loses <Security Attack +1>, and the second check isn't performed.
