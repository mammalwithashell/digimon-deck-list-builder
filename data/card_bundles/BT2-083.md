## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Return 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of their deck. (Trash all of the digivolution cards of that Digimon.) [On Deletion] If this card had digivolution cards, you may play this card from your trash without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT2-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1035** (2024-03-28) Q: When playing this card from my trash using its [On Deletion] effect, do the cards it had as digivolution cards return to my battle area as well? — A: No, this card's [On Deletion] effect only returns this card to play. Any cards that were in its digivolution cards go to the trash.
