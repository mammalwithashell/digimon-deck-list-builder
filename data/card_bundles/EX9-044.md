## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, by suspending 1 of your [WG] trait Digimon, reduce the play cost by 4. [On Play] [When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. Then, 1 of their Digimon or Tamers can't unsuspend until their turn ends. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them have the [WG] trait, 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a [WG] trait Digimon card in the hand.
