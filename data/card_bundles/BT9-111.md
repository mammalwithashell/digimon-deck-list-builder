[When Digivolving] Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the highest play cost. [End of Your Turn][Once Per Turn] You may return up to 7 non-Digi-Egg cards with [X Antibody] in their traits from this Digimon's digivolution cards to the bottom of your deck in any order; if you do, gain 1 memory for each card returned.

## Official Q&A

- **Q1926** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card’s [End of Your Turn] effect gains me enough memory to bring the memory gauge to 0 or greater on my side, does it briefly become my opponent’s turn before switching back to my turn again? — A: No. In this case, your turn simply continues without switching over.
- **Q1927** (2024-03-28) Q: If [BT9-109 X Antibody] is in this card’s digivolution cards, can this card’s [End of Your Turn] effect return [BT9-109 X Antibody] from this card’s digivolution cards to the bottom of my deck? — A: Yes. You're not trashing it, so there’s nothing preventing you from returning it to your deck. _(related: BT9-109)_
