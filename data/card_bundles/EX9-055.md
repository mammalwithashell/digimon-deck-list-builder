## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If there are 4 or more total [Negamon] in your trash and your Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 [Abbadomon Core] from your hand or trash to your empty breeding area without paying the cost. [End of All Turns] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 level 6 or lower Digimon card with [Negamon] in its text from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same level as the placed card.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -6: 4 [Negamon] When this would be played, by placing the specified cards from the trash under it, reduce the play cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, the conditions are met. This effect's conditions are met when you have 4 or more total [Negamon] in your trash or your Digimon's digivolution cards.
