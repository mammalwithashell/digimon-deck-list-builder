## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-093 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2468** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to search my security stack but then choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2469** (2026-03-13) Q: When a digivolution would occur by this card's [Main] effect, when exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when a card is placed on top of the Digimon to digivolve. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q2470** (2026-05-08) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before processing the part of the effect after "then"? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q4176** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT14-093 Emissary of Hope] to digivolve this Digimon into a card with the [Shaman] or [Wizard] trait in my security stack. In such cases, can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to trash cards from my security stack? — A: No, you can't. The period starting from the use of [BT14-093 Emissary of Hope] until the digivolution is complete and you shuffle your security stack is considered checking your security stack, so it isn't affected by other cards' effects. Therefore, you can't use this card's effect to trash cards from your security stack.
