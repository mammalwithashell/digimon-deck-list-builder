## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may place 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card from your trash under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. Then, up to 4 of your opponent's Digimon get -1000 DP for each of this Digimon's colors until the end of your opponent's next turn. [Your Turn] This Digimon is treated as also having the colors of its digivolution cards. While this Digimon has 4 or more colors, it gets +4000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1763** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s [Your Turn] effect work, exactly? — A: The effect allows this card to be treated as both white (its original color), and as the colors of its digivolution cards. For example, if a red card and green card are in this card’s digivolution cards, this card is treated as a 3-color white/red/green card.
- **Q1940** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve a [BT8-013 BetelGammamon] into a [BT8-084 Kimeramon] and place this card as one of [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s digivolution cards with its [When Digivolving] effect. Can I now activate [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect through this card's inherited effect and attack? — A: No, you can't. If this card is added to digivolution cards by [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, you get [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s <Blitz> effect through this card, but the time to trigger <Blitz> has already passed, so <Blitz> doesn't activate. _(a ruling on BT10-011; this card is named in it)_
