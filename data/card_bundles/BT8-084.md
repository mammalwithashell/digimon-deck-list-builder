## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may place 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card from your trash under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. Then, up to 4 of your opponent's Digimon get -1000 DP for each of this Digimon's colors until the end of your opponent's next turn. [Your Turn] This Digimon is treated as also having the colors of its digivolution cards. While this Digimon has 4 or more colors, it gets +4000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- The effect allows this card to be treated as both white (its original color), and as the colors of its digivolution cards. For example, if a red card and green card are in this card’s digivolution cards, this card is treated as a 3-color white/red/green card.
