## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-078 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2369** (2024-05-24) Q: I used this card's [On Play] effect to trash a BT13-007 [King Drasil_7D6] with a BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE] in its digivolution cards. Is BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE]'s <Overflow> processed at such times? — A: Yes, it's processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, <Overflow> is processed because a card is placed in the trash from under another card. _(a ruling on BT13-112; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2851** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this Digimon didn't DNA digivolve, this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect will delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
- **Q6006** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, and when my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area for the 1st process, an immediate-type effect such as a "when [...] would leave" effect caused this card to be removed from the battle area. Can I then process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect activates, it is to be fully resolved even if the card that activated the effect is removed from that area during the processing.
