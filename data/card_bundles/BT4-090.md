## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-090 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1240** (2024-03-28) Q: I pay memory to digivolve into this Digimon. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to attack even though my memory gauge is now at 1 or greater on my opponent's side? — A: Yes, you can. It doesn't become your opponent's turn until the attack ends or you declare that you're not making an attack.
- **Q1241** (2024-03-28) Q: I play a Digimon, then digivolve into this card on the same turn. Can I attack with it? — A: No, this effect doesn't ignore the ironclad rule that Digimon can't attack the turn they came into play, so you can't attack.
- **Q1242** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have other effects that activate on digivolution, can I attack before activating those effects after digivolving into this card? — A: No. You can activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect first and announce your attack, but you must activate all other effects before making the attack itself.
- **Q1243** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I attack my opponent's unsuspended Digimon with this card when not attacking through its [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't.
