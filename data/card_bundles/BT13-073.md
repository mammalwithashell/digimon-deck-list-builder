## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2311** (2024-03-28) Q: If an effect such as <Decoy> prevents deletion when this suspended Digimon would be deleted, can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to unsuspend this Digimon? — A: No, you can't. This card's [All Turns] effect is triggered when one of your Digimon with [Chessmon] in its name is deleted, therefore it won't trigger if such a Digimon isn't deleted.
