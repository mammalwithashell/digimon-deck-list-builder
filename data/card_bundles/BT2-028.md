## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT2-028 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1003** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to unsuspend this card if it digivolves while suspended? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1004** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards. If that Digimon is already unsuspended, does an effect that reads "unsuspend 1 of your Digimon" activate this card's inherited effect? — A: No. If an effect unsuspends a card that's already unsuspended, it doesn't count as unsuspending it.
