## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have a Tamer with [Tai Kamiya] in its name, return 1 card with [Garurumon], [Greymon] or [Omnimon] in its name from your trash to the hand.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and another of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-007 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2706** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to return 2 cards with [Garurumon], [Greymon], or [Omnimon] in their names from my trash to the hand when I have 2 Tamers with [Tai Kamiya] in their names? — A: No, you can't. Even if you have multiple Tamers with [Tai Kamiya] in their names, you can only return 1 card to the hand.
- **Q2707** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly? — A: At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon in your hand that has [DNA Digivolution]. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
- **Q2708** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card's inherited effect, can I DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolution]? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolution].
- **Q2709** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
