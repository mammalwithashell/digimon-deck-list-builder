## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an effect places the top card of this Digimon in its digivolution cards, this Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-001 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3526** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when an effect places the top card of this Digimon in its digivolution cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when a digivolution card or the top card of the Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards is placed in this Digimon's digivolution cards by an effect such as [EX5-007 Coronamon]'s inherited effect. It doesn't include cases where a card becomes a digivolution card through digivolution. _(related: EX5-007)_
- **Q5393** (2025-12-25) Q: I have a Digimon with the [Light Fang] or [Night Claw] trait and only this card in its digivolution cards. If I activate EX5-064 [Koh & Sayo]'s [On Play] [Main] effect and place the top card of that Digimon as the bottom digivolution card, then digivolve this card into a level 3 Digimon card, can I activate this card's inherited effect? — A: No, you can't. _(related: EX5-064)_
