## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less. If this effect didn't delete, this Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While you have 0 or less memory, add 2000 to this Digimon's DP-based deletion effects' maximums.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-010 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2718** (2024-03-28) Q: When my opponent has a Digimon with 4000 DP or less, can I activate this card's effect and gain 3000 memory but choose not to delete my opponent’s Digimon? — A: No. If there's a valid target, you must choose it when you activate the effect. You can't choose to not activate the effect when there is a valid target.
- **Q2719** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 4000 DP or less and an effect that reads, “This Digimon can't be deleted by your opponent’s effects,” and another Digimon with 4000 DP or less but no effect. Can I intentionally have this card’s effect target the Digimon with the effect preventing deletion and have this Digimon get 3000 DP? — A: Yes. You can choose your opponent’s Digimon with 4000 DP or less as a target, even if the chosen Digimon has an effect that prevents it from being deleted, in order to get the “if this effect didn't delete” part of the effect.
- **Q2720** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "while you have 0 or less memory" in this card’s inherited effect mean, exactly? — A: This inherited effect's condition is fulfilled if the memory is at 0 through 10 on your opponent's side when a DP-based deletion effect activates on a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards.
- **Q2721** (2024-05-24) Q: What does an "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" effect do? — A: Such effects increase the maximum value shown for DP-based deletion effects. For example, while "[Your Turn] Add 1000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" is activated, "[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less" will allow you to delete an opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less.
- **Q2722** (2024-05-24) Q: If "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" is activating, can the maximum be increased for an effect such as "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon's DP" that references a Digimon's DP for deletion? — A: No, it can't be increased. You can't increase the maximum of a DP-based deletion effect that doesn't show a numerical value.
