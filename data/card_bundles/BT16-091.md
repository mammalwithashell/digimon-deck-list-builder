## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may play 1 [Aquilamon] or [Gatomon] from your hand without paying the cost. Then, 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand. The Digimon this effect DNA digivolved may gain ＜Security A. +1＞ for the turn and attack a player.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Hawkmon] or [Salamon] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-091 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2664** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT16-091 Beastly Storm Dance of Affection]'s effect to DNA digivolve into this card, then I used [BT16-091 Beastly Storm Dance of Affection]'s effect to attack. At such times, can a Digimon played using this card's [When Digivolving] effect gain <Rush> and attack? — A: Because [BT16-077 Dinobeemon] is already using [BT16-091 Beastly Storm Dance of Affection]'s effect to attack, a played Digimon can gain <Rush>, but it can't use this card's effect to attack. _(a ruling on BT16-077; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2689** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to play Digimon without DNA digivolving? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2690** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I choose to not play Digimon for this card's [Main] effect but DNA digivolve using 2 of my Digimon that were already in the battle area? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2691** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to give the DNA digivolved Digimon <Security A. +1> without attacking? — A: No, you can't. You must choose to gain <Security A. +1> and attack a player or choose to neither gain <Security A. +1> or attack.
