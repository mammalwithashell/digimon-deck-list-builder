## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost. [Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Start of Your Main Phase] You may place up to 1 [Hybrid] trait card with different names from your trash under this Tamer. For each of your other Tamers, add 2 to the maximum number this effect may place. (Rule) Name: Also treated as [Takuya Kanbara]/[Koji Minamoto].

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] This Digimon with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait may attack a player.

- **Q2927** (2024-06-21) Q: BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto] digivolved into this card, and the memory gauge moved to 1 on my opponent's side. Then, I used this card’s [When Digivolving] effect to attack and used this card's [Your Turn] effect to unsuspend it. Can I then use the [End of Your Turn] inherited effect on BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto] in this card's digivolution cards to attack a player? — A: Yes, it can attack. When digivolving into this card, the turn end conditions won't be met until all of the resulting processing has been resolved. In this case, this card's [When Digivolving] effect, [Your Turn] effect, and any effects triggered by them must first be resolved, and then the turn end conditions will be met. Then, the end of the turn timing will occur and BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto]'s [End of Your Turn] effect will trigger.
- **Q3045** (2024-06-21) Q: What does this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect do, exactly? — A: You may choose 1 card with a combination of having the [Hybrid] trait and a different name from your trash and place it under this Tamer. For each of your other Tamers, you add 2 to the maximum number of cards this effect may place under this Tamer. For example, if you have 1 Tamer other than this Tamer, you can choose up to 3 cards with the [Hybrid] trait and different names from your trash and place them under this Tamer.
- **Q3046** (2024-06-21) Q: My Digimon is attacking using an effect at the end of my turn. Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] inherited effect to attack a player using a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it can't attack. A new attack declaration can't be made during an attack. In such cases, even if you activate this card's [End of Your Turn] inherited effect when you already have an attacking digimon, a new attack can't be performed using a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards.
