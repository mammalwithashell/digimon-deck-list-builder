## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-045 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3931** (2024-11-22) Q: My opponent has no Digimon with as much or more DP as this card in the battle area. I used this card to attack an opponent's Digimon, and the opponent's Digimon is deleted in the battle. Then, if an [On Deletion] effect on the deleted opponent's Digimon activates and my opponent now has a Digimon with as much or more DP as this Digimon, can I use the gained <Piercing> to perform a security check? — A: Yes, you can use <Piercing> to perform a security check. As soon as this card deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle, the <Piercing> gained from this card's [Your Turn] effect and the deleted Digimon's [On Deletion] effect will trigger simultaneously, then the turn player's <Piercing> will activate first. When <Piercing> activates, "perform check before End of Attack timing" processing will be pending, and the check will be performed immediately before the End of Attack timing, even if <Piercing> is lost after activation.
- **Q3932** (2024-11-22) Q: My opponent has no Digimon with as much or more DP as this card in the battle area. This card performs a security check, the [Security] effect on the 1st card checked activates, and my opponent now has a Digimon with as much or more DP as this card. Can I then use the <Security A. +1> gained from this card's [Your Turn] effect to perform a 2nd security check? — A: No, a 2nd security check can't be performed using that <Security A. +1>. The <Security A. +1> gained from this card's [Your Turn] effect is lost as soon as your opponent has a Digimon with as much or more DP as this card, therefore you can't use the gained <Security A. +1> to perform a security check.
- **Q6043** (2026-03-13) Q: Is a "while your opponent has no Digimon with XX" condition also met when my opponent has no Digimon? — A: Yes, it's met.
