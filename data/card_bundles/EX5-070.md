## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Security] Add this card to the hand. [Main] 1 of your Digimon without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [X Antibody] trait in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1. If it did, place this card as its bottom digivolution card. (Rule) Name: Also treated as [X Antibody].

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects, from this Digimon's digivolution cards, return 1 Digimon card to the hand and place 1 [X Antibody] on top of your security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3679** (2024-03-28) Q: If a Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards, does that mean that it isn't treated as "1 of your Digimon without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards"? — A: That's correct. This card has a "Name: Also treated as [X Antibody]" rule, therefore a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards isn't treated as "1 of your Digimon without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards."
- **Q3680** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect only activate if both [X Antibody] and a Digimon card are in the digivolution cards? — A: No, it activates even if only this card is in the digivolution cards.
- **Q3681** (2024-03-28) Q: If a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards would be deleted other than by one of my effects, then an effect such as <Decoy> prevents it from being deleted, does this card's inherited effect activate? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q3682** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card and then place this card in digivolution cards without digivolving 1 of my Digimon without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If digivolution isn't performed using this card's effect, you can't place this card in digivolution cards.
- **Q4260** (2024-03-28) Q: If [EX5-070 X Antibody Proto Form] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, this Digimon would be deleted, and [EX5-070 X Antibody Proto Form]'s inherited effect places it on top of my security stack, does this Digimon's [On Deletion] effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. [EX5-070 X Antibody Proto Form]'s inherited effect will trigger/activate "when this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects," but since [EX5-070 X Antibody Proto Form] isn't in this Digimon's digivolution cards at the time of activation of the [On Deletion] effect because it's placed in your security stack before this Digimon is deleted, the "if [X Antibody] was in this Digimon's digivolution cards" condition isn't met, so the effect doesn't activate. _(a ruling on P-145; this card is named in it)_
