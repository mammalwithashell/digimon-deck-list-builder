## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (By trashing this card after the placing turn, activate the effect.) ・1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a yellow Digimon card in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-105 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4196** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve into a card in my hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's <Delay> effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q4197** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to burst digivolve or DNA digivolve into a card in my hand? — A: No, you can't burst digivolve or DNA digivolve.
- **Q4198** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve a Tamer into a Digimon card with a "your Tamer is treated as a Digimon and can digivolve" effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's <Delay> effect digivolves 1 Digimon. It doesn't digivolve Tamers.
- **Q4199** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's <Delay> effect but choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4883** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I activate this card's {Hand} [Main] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-105 [Physical Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: No, you can't. _(a ruling on BT22-036; this card is named in it)_
- **Q5211** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has a level 6 or higher Digimon, or I have 3 or more [Hero] trait Tamers with different names. Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-105 [Physical Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT21-040; this card is named in it)_
- **Q5255** (2025-10-03) Q: I have [Makiko Date]. Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-105 [Physical Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT23-026; this card is named in it)_
