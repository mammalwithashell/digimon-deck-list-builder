## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. If your [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] is chosen as the target to return to the hand for this card's effect, it will ultimately be placed at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck instead of the hand according to the rules, but it meets this card's "return 1 of your other Digimon to its owner's hand" condition. A card isn't actually added to the hand, therefore effects such as "when a card is added to the hand" don't trigger. Related Cards
