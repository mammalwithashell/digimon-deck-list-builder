## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-063 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4920** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q4921** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
- **Q4922** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [All Turns] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if [Kyoko Kuremi] isn't in this Digimon's digivolution cards and this Digimon's stack doesn't have 2 or more same-level cards, this card's [All Turns] effect will unsuspend this Digimon.
- **Q5774** (2025-12-25) Q: I have BT22-063 [Alphamon] with just BT22-101 [Kyoko Kuremi] in its digivolution cards. Can I activate this card's <Delay> effect to place that BT22-063 [Alphamon] under BT22-101 [Kyoko Kuremi] and gain 2 memory? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT22-101)_
- **Q6683** (2026-06-19) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q6684** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6685** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6686** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6687** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
