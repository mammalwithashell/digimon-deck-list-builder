## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5216** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place this card as the bottom digivolution card, and then play a specified card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5217** (2025-10-03) Q: If this card doesn't have digivolution cards, can I activate its [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't activate it.
- **Q5218** (2025-10-03) Q: This card only has Digi-Egg cards in its digivolution cards. Can I activate this card's [Main] effect? — A: Yes, it can be activated. After activation, you place this card under the Digi-Egg cards, then you can play a specified card. However, if the top card is now a card that doesn't have DP, it will then be trashed by rule checks.
- **Q5219** (2025-10-03) Q: This card only has a card with 1000 DP in its digivolution cards. If another effect reduces the DP by 1000, can I activate this card's [Main] effect? — A: Yes, it can be activated. After activation, you place this card under the digivolution cards, then you can play a specified card. However, if the top card's DP is now 0, it will then be deleted by rule checks.
