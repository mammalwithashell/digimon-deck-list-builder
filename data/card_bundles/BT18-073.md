## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, by deleting 1 of your Digimon with the [Composite] trait, reduce the play cost by 4. [On Play] [When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ all of your opponent's Digimon. [On Deletion] 1 of your [Kimeramon] and 1 [Machinedramon] in the trash may DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand. (Rule) Trait: Has [Composite] Type.

### Inherited Effect

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3014** (2024-06-21) Q: When this card would be played, I deleted my BT18-015 [Kimeramon], then I played this card. At such times, does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect trigger simultaneously with BT18-015 [Kimeramon]'s [On Deletion] effect? — A: Yes, they trigger simultaneously. However, if you activate BT18-015 [Kimeramon]'s [On Deletion] effect first and then DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] using [Kimeramon] and this card while its [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect has triggered, this card will be removed from the battle area before the [On Play] effect can activate. _(related: BT18-015)_
- **Q3015** (2024-06-21) Q: If I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand, do the DNA digivolution requirements have to be met for [Millenniummon]? — A: Yes, the DNA digivolution requirements have to be met. In addition, DNA digivolution can't be performed unless the [Millenniummon] has DNA digivolution.
- **Q3016** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's [On Deletion] effect allow my [Kimeramon] and the BT18-073 [Machinedramon] itself that activated the effect in the trash to DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand? — A: Yes, that's correct. The [On Deletion] effect activates after the card is placed in the trash, therefore this card itself in the trash can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand.
- **Q3017** (2024-11-22) Q: What are the areas where [Kimeramon] and [Machinedramon] can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand using this card's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Your [Kimeramon] in the battle area and [Machinedramon] in your trash can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand.
