## Printed text (official)
### Effect
If you have a Tamer in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] The next time one of your Digimon digivolves this turn, you may trash 1 Digimon card in your hand of the same color as the digivolving Digimon to reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 4.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3258** (2024-03-28) Q: After using this card, I digivolve a red Digimon into a white Digimon card. What color card can I trash from my hand to reduce the memory cost of the digivolution? — A: You can trash a red Digimon card to reduce the digivolution cost. The card you can trash with this effect must match the color of the Digimon performing the digivolution in your battle area.
- **Q3259** (2024-03-28) Q: I’m digivolving a Digimon in my breeding area. Can I reduce the digivolution cost by 4 with this card's effect? — A: No, this effect can't reduce digivolution costs of Digimon in breeding areas.
- **Q3260** (2024-03-28) Q: During the turn I use this card, if I have a blue Digimon in my battle area with an effect that reads, “This Digimon is also treated as red,” can I trash a red Digimon card in my hand to reduce the digivolution cost by 4? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3261** (2024-03-28) Q: After using this card, I activate [BT7-085 Takuya Kanbara]’s [Main] effect. Can I use this card's effect specifically to trash cards with [Hybrid] in their traits from my hand, so that I can place them under [Takuya Kanbara] using its effect? — A: No, you can't. You don't trash the card in your hand with this effect until you digivolve. [BT7-085 Takuya Kanbara]’s [Main] effect reads, “You may place 5 cards with [Hybrid] in their traits from your trash under this Tamer in any order,” and is used to digivolve into [EmperorGreymon]. As such, you place the cards under [Takuya Kanbara] before you begin digivolving, at which point you can trash a card in your hand with this card's effect. _(related: BT7-085)_
- **Q3262** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving from a multicolor Digimon, what color cards can I trash with this card’s effect to reduce the digivolution cost? — A: The card you trash can match any one of the Digimon’s colors. For example, when digivolving a blue/green Digimon, you can trash either a blue or a green card.
- **Q3263** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I reduce the memory cost of a DNA digivolution with this card's effect? If I can, what color cards can I trash with this card’s effect? — A: Yes, you can. The card you trash can match any of the colors of either Digimon. For example, when DNA digivolving a blue Digimon and green Digimon, you can trash either a blue or green card.
- **Q3264** (2024-03-28) Q: After using this card, can I reduce the digivolution cost of my digivolving Digimon by trashing 1 Digimon card in the hand with the same color when an effect to digivolve one of my Digimon is activated? — A: Yes, you can.
