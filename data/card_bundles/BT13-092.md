## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Search your opponent's hand, and trash 1 card among it. Then, if they have 7 or fewer cards in their hand, they add the top card of their security stack to the hand. [When Attacking] By returning 1 Digimon card from your opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the same name as that card.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. This card’s [When Digivolving] effect can only be used to search your opponent's hand for the card you choose to trash from their hand.
