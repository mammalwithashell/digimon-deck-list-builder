## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If this Digimon's name is [Koromon], this Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon. [All Turns] This Digimon gains all the names of level 3 or lower cards in its digivolution cards. [On Deletion] You may play 1 Tamer card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Kari Kamiya] in its name from your hand without paying the cost, or hatch in your breeding area.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 Tamer card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Kari Kamiya] in its name from your hand without paying the cost, or hatch in your breeding area.

For example, if a level 3 [Agumon] in the battle area digivolves into this card, that Digimon will be considered to digivolve into a Digimon card with the names [Greymon] and [Agumon].
- **Q2903** (2026-02-06) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also give it the names from (Rule) on level 3 or lower cards? — A: Yes, it also gains the names from (Rule).
- **Q4713** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this card's name isn't [Koromon], this card's [When Digivolving] effect deletes 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon.
- **Q5965** (2026-02-06) Q: What does this card's [On Deletion] effect do, exactly? — A: This effect allows you to choose between playing a Tamer card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Kari Kamiya] in its name from your hand without paying the cost or hatching a Digi-Egg in your breeding area.
