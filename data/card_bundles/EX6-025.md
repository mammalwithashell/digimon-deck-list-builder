## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Attacking] 1 Digimon may gain ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of your opponent's turn. Then, if DigiXrosing, reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Gokuumon], 1 [Sagomon], 1 [Cho-Hakkaimon] and 1 [Shakamon] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, return 1 yellow Digimon card from its digivolution cards to the hand.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: [Gokuumon]/[Sagomon]/[Cho-Hakkaimon] When you would play this card, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each placed card reduces the play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] 1 Digimon may gain ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of your opponent's turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-025 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3732** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I place 1 each of [Gokuumon], [Sagomon], and [Cho-Hakkaimon] for this card's DigiXros requirements? — A: No, you can't. You can place 1 [Gokuumon], 1 [Sagomon], or 1 [Cho-Hakkaimon] under this card.
- **Q3733** (2024-03-28) Q: If DigiXrosing when this card would be played, can I also activate the part after "if DigiXrosing" in this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect when this Digimon attacks? — A: No, you can't. The "if DigiXrosing" part of the effect can only activate when DigiXrosing.
- **Q3734** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect or inherited effect to give 1 of my Digimon <Security A. -1>? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3735** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when [...] would leave the battle area" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
- **Q3736** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger when this card is in the battle area and would be placed in another Digimon’s digivolution cards for a DigiXros? — A: Yes, it triggers. Because a Digimon also leaves the battle area when it's placed in digivolution cards for a DigiXros, the effect triggers and activates.
- **Q3737** (2024-03-28) Q: I chose this card as a target for a DigiXros. Then, I used this card's [All Turns] effect to return a yellow Digimon card to the hand. At such times, can the card returned to the hand be placed for the DigiXros? — A: No, it can't. You must choose all of the cards to place when you declare the DigiXros. After choosing them, the chosen cards are placed under the card to DigiXros. This card's [All Turns] effect triggers and activates when the chosen cards would be placed, so after activation has occurred, you can't re-do your choices for the cards to place for the DigiXros.
