## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, by placing 1 of each face-up [Dark Masters] trait card with different names from your security stack under this card, reduce the play cost by 4 for each card placed. [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 of each [Dark Masters] trait card with different names from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the costs. Then, all of your [Dark Masters] trait Digimon gain ＜Rush＞ for the turn. At turn end, delete the Digimon this effect played.

- **Q5742** (2025-12-25) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's [On Play] effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously.
