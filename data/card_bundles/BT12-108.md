## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a level 6 Digimon with the [Machine] trait in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Choose 1 of your Digimon with the [Machine] or [Cyborg] trait. Delete that Digimon and 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to that Digimon's DP.

### Security Effect
[Security] By trashing 1 card with the [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait from your hand, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost less than or equal to the trashed card's play cost.
