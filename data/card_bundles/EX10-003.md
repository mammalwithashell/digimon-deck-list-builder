## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-003 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5007** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I trash just 2 digivolution cards for the conditions of this card's inherited effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this inherited effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q5008** (2025-09-05) Q: What does "end the attack" mean, exactly? — A: After this effect activates, the current timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. For example, if this effect activates during the attack declaration timing, it will make a transition to the end of attack timing. A transition to the counter timing or block timing won't occur, and the attack won't succeed.
- **Q5009** (2025-09-05) Q: Can an "end the attack" effect end an attack by a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: Yes, such attacks can be ended. "End the attack" effects are effects that change the timing, they don't affect an attacking Digimon.
