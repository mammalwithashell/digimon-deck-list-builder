## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP and that Digimon's [When Digivolving] effects don't activate.

### Security Effect
[Security] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents [When Digivolving] effects on cards from activating. [When Digivolving] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
