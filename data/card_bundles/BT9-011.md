## Official Q&A
- Such effects increase the maximum value shown for DP-based deletion effects. For example, while "[Your Turn] Add 1000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" is activated, "[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less" will allow you to delete an opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less.
