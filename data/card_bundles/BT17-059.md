## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] By placing 1 [Doomsday Clock] from your hand or trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, you may play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens without paying the cost. (Digimon/Cost 14/Lv. 6/White/Mega/Unknown/Unidentified/3000 DP) [Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may switch the target of the attack to 1 of your Digimon with [Diaboromon] in its name.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2813** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card’s [When Digivolving] effect to place [Doomsday Clock] from my hand or trash as a digivolution card, can I choose to not play [Diaboromon] Tokens? — A: Yes, you can. This effect reads "you may play," so if you perform the conditions specified for "by X," you may choose whether or not to activate the effect shown in text.
- **Q2814** (2024-03-28) Q: Does the inherited effect on a card like BT2-059 [Kurisarimon] or BT2-053 [Keramon] trigger twice when I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens? — A: No, it doesn't. You can play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens, but they are played at the same time during a single timing, therefore the effect only triggers once. _(related: BT2-059, BT2-053)_
- **Q2815** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I also use this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect to switch the attack target to my unsuspended Digimon with [Diaboromon] in its name? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5646** (2025-12-25) Q: I attack my opponent's Digimon, and an attempt is made to delete this Digimon using an effect. After that, I activate this card's [All Turns] effect and play BT17-059 [Diaboromon]. Can I then activate BT17-059 [Diaboromon]'s [Opponent's Turn] effect? — A: No, you can't. _(a ruling on BT24-065; this card is named in it)_
