## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] This Digimon is also treated as blue. [Opponent's Turn] All of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards gain ＜Security Attack -1＞. (This Digimon checks 1 fewer security card.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-040 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1075** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this Digimon digivolve into a Digimon with a digivolution condition of "Blue, Level 5"? — A: Yes, since this Digimon is treated as both yellow and blue during your turn, it can digivolve into a Digimon with a requirement of "Blue, Level 5."
- **Q1076** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is in my breeding area. Can I digivolve it into a Digimon that requires a blue Digimon? — A: No, you can't. [Your Turn] effects don't activate from breeding areas, so the card isn't treated as a yellow card. You can't digivolve it into a Digimon that requires a blue Digimon.
- **Q1077** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this Digimon in play. If I digivolve one of my Digimon that currently has no digivolution cards, does its <Security Attack -1> immediately disappear? — A: Yes, the moment your Digimon gains a digivolution card, it no longer counts as a "Digimon with no digivolution cards," and loses <Security Attack -1>.
- **Q1772** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s “If you have a Digimon in play with...2 or more colors in one of its digivolution cards” count a Digimon with a card like [BT3-040 Shakkoumon]’s “This Digimon is also treated as blue” in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't. Effects that grant additional colors don't do anything as digivolution cards, so the card is only treated as a single color.
