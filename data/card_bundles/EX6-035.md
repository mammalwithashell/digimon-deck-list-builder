## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3756** (2025-12-25) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to reduce the DP of multiple Digimon? — A: No, you can't. You choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon, then that Digimon's DP is reduced by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q3757** (2024-03-28) Q: If I don't play a Digimon card from my hand, can the part after "then" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect activate? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q5726** (2025-12-25) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a card with an [On Play] effect. At such times, can I activate the [On Play] effect on the played card before processing the part of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect after "then"? — A: No, you can't. Once all of the processing is resolved for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, then you can activate the [On Play] effect on the played card.
- **Q5727** (2025-12-25) Q: All of my Digimon get -5000 DP. I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a Digimon with 5000 DP or less. At such times, is the played Digimon deleted before the part of the effect after "then"? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
