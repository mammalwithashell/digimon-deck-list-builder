## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1594** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with [EX1-035 Kabuterimon] and digivolve into this card with its [When Attacking] effect. Can I then digivolve again on the same attack with this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, it is too late to trigger this card's [When Attacking] effect, since the Digimon didn't have it when the attack was declared. _(a ruling on BT7-051; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3223** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve using this card’s [When Attacking] effect. If the Digimon I digivolve into has a [When Attacking] effect, does that effect activate? — A: No, the time to trigger [When Attacking] effects has already passed, so the effect doesn't activate.
- **Q3224** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving with this card's [When Attacking] effect, paying the digivolution cost moves the memory gauge to 1 or more on my opponent’s side. What happens to the attack? — A: The attack continues. The turn doesn’t switch over until the attack has ended.
- **Q3227** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with [EX1-035 Kabuterimon] and digivolve into this card with its [When Attacking] effect. Can I then digivolve again during the same attack with this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, the time to trigger [When Attacking] effects has already passed, so the effect doesn't activate. _(a ruling on EX1-040; this card is named in it)_
