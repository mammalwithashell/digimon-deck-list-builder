## Printed text (official)
### Effect
The name of this card/Tamer is also treated as [Kiriha Aonuma]/[Nene Amano]. [Start of Your Main Phase] If there are 2 or more Digimon in play, gain 1 memory. [All Turns] When you would play 1 Digimon card with the [Blue Flare] or [Twilight] trait with DigiXros requirements, by suspending this Tamer, you may place 1 card from under your Tamers and 1 card from your trash as digivolution cards for a DigiXros.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3490** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] effect to play a [EX4-062 Kiriha Aonuma & Nene Amano] from my hand without paying the cost? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3491** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [On Play] effect activate when I have [EX4-062 Kiriha Aonuma & Nene Amano] in play? — A: No. [EX4-062 Kiriha Aonuma & Nene Amano] is a card that's also treated as [Nene Amano], therefore the "If you don't have [Nene Amano] in play" condition isn't met and the effect doesn't activate.
- **Q3502** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect activate if there are 2 or more Digimon in the battle areas including my opponent's Digimon, rather than just my Digimon? — A: Yes. If you and your opponent have a total of 2 or more Digimon in play, the effect activates.
- **Q3503** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have multiple Tamers with cards under them, can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to perform a DigiXros for each of my Tamers using a card from under each one? — A: No, you can't. Even if you have multiple Tamers with cards under them, you can only use 1 card from under 1 of them when you choose cards for a DigiXros.
- **Q3504** (2024-08-01) Q: What does this card's (Rule) mean, exactly? (*This card's "The name of this card/Tamer is also treated as [Kiriha Aonuma]/[Nene Amano]" has been changed to "(Rule) Name: Also treated as [Kiriha Aonuma] and [Nene Amano].") — A: This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also has the [Kiriha Aonuma] and [Nene Amano] names.
