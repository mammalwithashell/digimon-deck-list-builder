## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less. If you have a Digimon in play with 2 or more colors, or with 2 or more colors in one of its digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 7000 DP or less instead.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-096 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1771** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s “If you have a Digimon in play with...2 or more colors in one of its digivolution cards” count a Digimon that has both a red card and a blue card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't. The effect is looking for a Digimon with a digivolution card that has 2 or more colors.
- **Q1772** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s “If you have a Digimon in play with...2 or more colors in one of its digivolution cards” count a Digimon with a card like [BT3-040 Shakkoumon]’s “This Digimon is also treated as blue” in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't. Effects that grant additional colors don't do anything as digivolution cards, so the card is only treated as a single color. _(related: BT3-040)_
