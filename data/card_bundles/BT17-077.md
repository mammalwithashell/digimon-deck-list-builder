## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2847** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens to Digi-Egg cards in the trash when this card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect activates? — A: If a Digi-Egg card is affected by an effect that places it at the bottom of the deck, it will be placed at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck.
- **Q2848** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "if this effect returned level 7 white cards" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect mean that it will activate even if I return a card such as BT12-112 [Shoutmon X7: Superior Mode] that has 2 or more colors including white? — A: Yes, that’s correct. _(related: BT12-112)_
- **Q2849** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I gain 6 memory if I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to return 2 level 7 white cards? — A: No, you don't.
- **Q4711** (2025-06-13) Q: Which player returns cards from their trash to the bottom of the deck for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: The player that activated the effect chooses themselves or their opponent, then all of the cards in the chosen player's trash are returned to the bottom of the deck.
