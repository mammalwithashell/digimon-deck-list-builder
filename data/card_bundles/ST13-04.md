## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a card in your hand that's black or has [Legend-Arms] in its traits, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] You may DNA digivolve this Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play into a Digimon card in your hand for its DNA digivolve cost.

## Official Q&A
- At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve the Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon that has a DNA digivolve requirement from your hand. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
