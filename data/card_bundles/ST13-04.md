## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a card in your hand that's black or has [Legend-Arms] in its traits, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] You may DNA digivolve this Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play into a Digimon card in your hand for its DNA digivolve cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST13-04 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q770** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly? — A: At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve the Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon that has a DNA digivolve requirement from your hand. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
- **Q771** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card's inherited effect, can I DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn’t have a DNA digivolve requirement? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn’t have a DNA digivolve requirement.
- **Q772** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
