## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may place 1 [Eater (Species Form)] from this Digimon's digivolution cards as the bottom digivolution card of your [Mother Eater] in the breeding area. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon checks your opponent's security stack, you may play 1 Tamer card with the [CS] trait from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
{Breeding} [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your Digimon cards with the [Eater] trait would be played, you may reduce the play costs by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-080 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4945** (2025-07-04) Q: A Digimon with 2 copies of this card in its digivolution cards is in the breeding area. When an [Eater] trait card would be played, can I activate the inherited effect on just 1 copy of this card and reduce the play cost by 1? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4946** (2025-07-04) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q4947** (2025-07-04) Q: What is a {Breeding} effect? — A: A {Breeding} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the breeding area. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the breeding area.
