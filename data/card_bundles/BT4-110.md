## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 3 or less. For each of your Digimon with [D-Brigade] in its type, add +1 to the maximum play cost of the Digimon you can choose with this effect.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
- If you have a Digimon with [D-Brigade] in its type, you can choose a Digimon with a play cost of 4 or less. (3 or less + 1 = 4 or less.) If you have 2 Digimon with [D-Brigade], you can choose a play cost of 5 or less, if you have 3 Digimon it becomes 6 or less, and so on.
