## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Collision＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may link 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card from your hand or this Digimon's digivolution cards to this Digimon without paying the cost. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When effects trash any of this Digimon's link cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -8000 DP for the turn.

### Link DP
+4000 DP

### Link Condition
＜Link＞ [Appmon] trait: Cost 3 (Plug this card from the hand or battle area sideways into the specified Digimon in the battle area.)

### Link Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing 1 of its link cards, it doesn't leave.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5086** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I also activate this card's link effect by trashing this card itself when it's a link card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5087** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5088** (2025-09-05) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger even when a card would get linked by an effect to an already linked card whose link card is trashed and replaced? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q5089** (2025-09-05) Q: I used <Link +X> and have a Digimon that has multiple link cards including this card. Can I use this card's Link effect to trash one of that Digimon's link cards other than this card? — A: Yes, you can.
