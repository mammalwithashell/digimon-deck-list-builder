## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, place this card in your battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (Trash this card in your battle area to activate the effect below. You can't activate this effect the turn this card enters play.) ・Gain 2 memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Place this card in its owner's battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1961** (2025-11-21) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!], and I use P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!]'s 1st [Main] effect to place that card in the battle area. What happens to "place the Option card used with this effect on top of your security stack face down instead of placing it in your trash" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect at such times? — A: P-037 [Yellow Memory Boost!] can't be placed in the trash, therefore it can't be placed on top of the security stack.
- **Q4153** (2024-03-28) Q: When I place this card in my battle area, can I use a yellow Option card even if I have no other yellow Digimon or Tamers in play? — A: No. Having this card in your battle area doesn't fulfill color requirements.
- **Q4154** (2024-03-28) Q: When I place this card in my battle area, can I activate this card's <Delay> effect even if I have no other yellow Digimon or Tamers in play? — A: Yes, if this card is in your battle area, you can activate <Delay>, regardless of color requirements. Color requirements only matter when using Option cards.
