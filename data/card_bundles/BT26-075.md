## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-075 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7100** (2026-08-18) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card is deleted. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if you first activate <Ascension> and this card is removed from the trash, the other effects on this card that were pending activation can no longer activate.
- **Q7101** (2026-08-18) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack, does it battle with the attacking Digimon after its [Security] effect activates? — A: Yes, it battles after its [Security] effect activates.
- **Q7102** (2026-08-18) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack and its [Security] effect is activated, is the effect treated as a Digimon effect or an Option card effect? — A: It's treated as a Digimon effect. When an effect from a DUAL Card's Digimon side is activated, it is treated as a Digimon effect.
- **Q7103** (2026-08-18) Q: While the effect "Option cards don't activate [Security] effects" is activated, can this card's [Security] effect activate when it is checked? — A: No, it can't. Since DUAL cards checked from the security stack are treated as both Digimon and Option cards, it can't be activated.
