## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Attacking] ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. [Your Turn] This Digimon can only digivolve into white Digimon. [End of Opponent’s Turn] Delete this Digimon. Then, you may play 1 Digimon card with the [Dark Masters] trait, other than [Machinedramon], from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
＜Reboot＞ (This Digimon also unsuspends during your opponent's unsuspend phase.)
