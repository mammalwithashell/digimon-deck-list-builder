## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] Trash any 1 digivolution card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. This Digimon can't be blocked for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] When this Digimon would be deleted, you may play 1 [Gomamon] from its digivolution cards without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-020 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2390** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "This Digimon can't be blocked" in this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect activate even when my opponent can't trash a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q2391** (2024-03-28) Q: I have both this card and another [Gomamon] with an [On Deletion] inherited effect in the digivolution cards of the same one of my Digimon. When that Digimon is deleted, can I activate this card's inherited effect, play the other [Gomamon] from the digivolution cards, and activate that [On Deletion] inherited effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. This card’s inherited effect reads "When this Digimon would be deleted," so it interrupts the deletion when it activates. Therefore, the [Gomamon] with the [On Deletion] effect is removed from the digivolution cards before the [On Deletion] effect can be triggered.
