## Printed text (official)
### Effect
For each of your opponent's Digimon, reduce this card's use cost in the hand by 1. [Main] Your opponent's effects can't play Digimon until their turn ends. Then, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effects.

- **Q1773** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent has 7 or more Digimon in play, does this card’s effect get reduced to negative 1, effectively gaining me a memory for using it? — A: No. It just reduces the cost of the card to 0. Note that in general, costs can’t be reduced to less than 0.
- **Q1774** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card, then attack my opponent. During the security check, I flip over a Digimon card with the effect, “[Security] At the end of the battle, play this card without paying its memory cost.” What happens to this Security Digimon? — A: After the battle between the attacking Digimon and Security Digimon, the effect prevents the Digimon from being played, so it's placed in the trash.
- **Q1775** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent used this card on my [BT7-063 DarkKnightmon]. [DarkKnightmon]'s [All Turns] effect is a "when this Digimon would be deleted" effect that interrupts deletion when it activates, but can I use this card's "your opponent can't play Digimon by effects until the end of their turn" effect to prevent [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] from being played from [DarkKnightmon]'s digivolution cards? — A: Yes, they can't be played. This card's [Main] effect was changed to "Your opponent can't play Digimon by effects until the end of their turn. Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less." Therefore, first the effect that prevents playing Digimon is applied, then Digimon with 6000 DP or less are deleted. Please see the notices regarding changes to the rules/rulings on the official website. _(related: BT7-063)_
- **Q1776** (2025-04-04) Q: If this card's effect is activated on the opponent's turn, how long does the effect last? — A: When the effect of this card is activated on the opponent's turn, it lasts until the end of that turn.
