## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have a Digimon with the [Free] trait or a Tamer with [Ken Ichijoji] in its name in play, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When one of your 2-color blue and green Digimon attacks, by suspending this Tamer, that Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name in your hand for the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-090 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2225** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect when I attack with a 2-color green/blue (in that specific order) Digimon or with a Digimon that is blue and green because of an effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2226** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I also activate this card's [Your Turn] effect when my 3-color Digimon with blue and green attacks? — A: No, you can't. Since this card's effect specifies "one of your 2-color blue and green Digimon", you can't activate this card's [Your Turn] effect if your Digimon has 3 or more colors, even if those colors include blue and green.
