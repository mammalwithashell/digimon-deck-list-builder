### Effect
＜Armor Purge＞ (When this Digimon would be deleted, you may trash the top card of this Digimon to prevent that deletion.) [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn. If you have a blue or yellow Tamer in play, or if you have a card with the [Armor Form] trait in your trash, 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less can't attack or block until the end of your opponent's turn.
