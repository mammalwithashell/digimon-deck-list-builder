## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. Add 1 blue card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (By trashing this card after the placing turn, activate the effect.) ・1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a blue Digimon card in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

### Security Effect
[Security] Place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-104 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q697** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I digivolve this Digimon to an [UlforceVeedramon] in my hand when an effect to digivolve one of my Digimon is activated if my opponent has a level 6 or higher Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2758** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q2767** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q4023** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use an effect such as P-103 [Offense Training]'s <Delay> that digivolves a Digimon, can my [Gabumon] digivolve into this card in the hand when I have 2 or fewer security cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4192** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve into a card in my hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's <Delay> effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q4193** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to burst digivolve or DNA digivolve into a card in my hand? — A: No, you can't burst digivolve or DNA digivolve.
- **Q4194** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve a Tamer into a Digimon card with a "your Tamer is treated as a Digimon and can digivolve" effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's <Delay> effect digivolves 1 Digimon. It doesn't digivolve Tamers.
- **Q4195** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's <Delay> effect but choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4877** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I activate this card's {Hand} [Main] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-104 [Mental Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: No, you can't.
