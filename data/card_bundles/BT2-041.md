## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend all of your yellow Tamers. For each Tamer you suspend this way, activate the following effect: ・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -4000 DP for the turn. [Your Turn] This Digimon gets +1000 DP for each Tamer you have in play.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT2-041 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1014** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to suspend 2 or more yellow Tamers. Can I choose 2 or more of my opponent's Digimon and give all of them -4000 DP? — A: Yes, the [When Evolving] effect activates separately for each yellow Tamer you suspend, and you get to choose the target for each effect.
- **Q1015** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect two or more times. Are they treated as activating at separate timings? — A: No, they're treated as activating at the same timing.
- **Q1230** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent uses the effect on a card like [BT2-041 ShineGreymon] to suspend 2 or more of their Tamers, does this card's inherited effect allow me to gain a memory for each Tamer suspended? — A: No. Even if your opponent suspends multiple Tamers simultaneously, this card's inherited effect treats all of it as a single timing instance, so you only gain 1 memory.
