## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. Add 1 purple card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (By trashing this card after the placing turn, activate the effect.) ・1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a purple Digimon card in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

### Security Effect
[Security] Place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-108 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4208** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve into a card in my hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's <Delay> effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q4209** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to burst digivolve or DNA digivolve into a card in my hand? — A: No, you can't burst digivolve or DNA digivolve.
- **Q4210** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve a Tamer into a Digimon card with a "your Tamer is treated as a Digimon and can digivolve" effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's <Delay> effect digivolves 1 Digimon. It doesn't digivolve Tamers.
- **Q4211** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's <Delay> effect but choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4212** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 10 or more cards in my trash. Can I use this card’s <Delay> effect to digivolve my [Impmon] into [BT2-111 Beelzemon] in my hand? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT2-111)_
- **Q5204** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 10000 DP or more, or my Tamers have 3 or more total colors. Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-108 [Wisdom Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on ST21-10; this card is named in it)_
- **Q5335** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I activate this card's {Hand} [Main] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-108 [Wisdom Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: No, you can't. _(a ruling on BT23-065; this card is named in it)_
- **Q6392** (2026-05-08) Q: I have a Tamer with [Three Musketeers] in its text. Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-108 [Wisdom Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT25-082; this card is named in it)_
