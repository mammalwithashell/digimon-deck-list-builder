## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent's Turn][Once Per Turn] When an effect adds cards to your opponent's hand, by trashing 1 of this Digimon's digivolution cards, your opponent trashes cards in their hand equal to the number of cards added to their hand by the effect. [On Deletion] ＜Save＞ (You may place this card under one of your Tamers.)

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: 1 Digimon card w/[Bagra Army] in traits When you would play this card, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each placed card reduces the play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] When an effect trashes this digivolution card, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1998** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my opponent's Digimon attacks, <Draw 1> activates twice from both a [When Attacking] effect and an inherited effect, and their hand increases by 2 cards. Will this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect trash 2 of my opponent's cards in their hand? — A: No, you can only trash cards equal to the number of cards added by one effect. Even if they were triggered at the same time, different effects are still separate from each other, and you can only activate this effect for one of them, so in this case you can count only one of the <Draw 1> and only 1 card in your opponent's hand will be trashed.
- **Q1999** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect activate after an effect that adds and trashes cards in your opponent's hand so that the result is net 0 total cards gained, like "<Draw 2>. Then, trash 2 cards in your hand"? — A: Yes, it activates. This card's effect trashes the number of cards that were drawn. In the example given in the question, after trashing 2 cards from their hand, your opponent would trash an additional 2 cards, for a total of 4 cards trashed.
- **Q2008** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I activate this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect when I would choose and trash digivolution cards by activating the [Opponent's Turn] effect on a BT10-077 [MadLeomon] without digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can. If you were able to choose and trash this card's digivolution cards after the replacement, the condition for BT10-077 [MadLeomon]'s [Opponent's Turn] effect will be met. _(a ruling on BT10-084; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3832** (2024-05-24) Q: If my opponent activates BT10-077 [MadLeomon]'s [Opponent's Turn] effect when I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to draw 5 cards from the top of my deck, how many cards do I trash from my hand? — A: 5 cards. BT10-077 [MadLeomon]'s [Opponent's Turn] effect trashes cards equal to the number of cards added. _(a ruling on EX7-013; this card is named in it)_
