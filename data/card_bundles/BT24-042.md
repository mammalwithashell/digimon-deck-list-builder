## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Demon] or [Titan] trait, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your hand is trashed from, this [Demon] or [Titan] trait Digimon may digivolve into [Titamon] or a [Titan] trait Digimon card in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT24-042 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5630** (2025-12-25) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger when this card is in the breeding area and would digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Demon] or [Titan] trait? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q5631** (2025-12-25) Q: A Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards attacks, and another effect trashes a card from my hand. At such times, if I activate this card's inherited effect and perform a digivolution into P-209 [Titamon], can I then activate P-209 [Titamon]'s <Alliance>? — A: No, you can't. <Alliance> is an effect that triggers upon an attack. You can't activate it if the Digimon doesn't have <Alliance> upon the attack declaration. _(related: P-209)_
