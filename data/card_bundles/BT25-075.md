## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if you have fewer Digimon than your opponent, reduce the cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may link up to 2 cards from your hand or trash to any of your Digimon without paying the cost. Then, for each of your link cards, ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ all of your opponent's Digimon. [All Turns] All of your [TS] trait Digimon gain ＜Rush＞ and ＜Link +1＞. [Your Turn] When your Digimon get linked, one of them may attack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-075 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6370** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if a Digimon gains <Link +1> from an effect and then it later loses that <Link +1>? — A: Upon losing <Link +1>, its maximum number of link cards will become 1, and any link cards that exceed the maximum will be trashed upon the rule check timing. The link cards to trash are chosen by the player.
- **Q6371** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6372** (2026-05-08) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger even when this card itself gets linked? — A: Yes, it triggers.
