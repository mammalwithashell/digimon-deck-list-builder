## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] If it's your turn, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 4 Digimon card with the [Holy Beast] or [Free] trait in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] All of your opponent's Security Digimon get -3000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
