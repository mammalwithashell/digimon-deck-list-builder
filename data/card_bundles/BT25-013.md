## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] By trashing 1 card in your hand, you may return 1 red or blue [Iliad] trait Digimon card from your trash to the hand. [Your Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them are blue, this Digimon may digivolve into [Flaremon] in the hand with the cost reduced by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] This Digimon gets +2000 DP.
