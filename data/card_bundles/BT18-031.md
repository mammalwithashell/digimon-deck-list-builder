### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait and 1 yellow Tamer card with inherited effects among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your Tamers with inherited effects are played, gain 1 memory.
