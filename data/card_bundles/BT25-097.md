## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have no face-up security cards, you can ignore this card's color requirements. {Security} [All Turns] All of your yellow or purple [TS] trait Digimon gain ＜Alliance＞. While you have a Digimon with [Junomon] in its name, they also gain ＜Scapegoat＞. [Main] Add your bottom security card to the hand and place this card face up as the bottom security card. Then, you may play 1 yellow or purple [TS] trait Digimon card from your hand with the cost reduced by 3.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 4 or lower yellow or purple [TS] trait Digimon card from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-097 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6457** (2026-05-08) Q: Is the "while you have no face-up security cards" condition met when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, it's met.
- **Q6458** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I also activate this card's [Main] effect when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you can't add a card from your security stack to your hand, therefore you only place this card in your security stack.
- **Q6459** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens to cards placed face up in the security stack by effects? — A: They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
- **Q6460** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens upon a security check for a security card that is placed face-up? — A: The check is performed with the card left revealed. Other than rules for cards left revealed, the rules apply in the same manner as standard checks.
- **Q6461** (2026-05-08) Q: Does a card's [Security] effect trigger upon a security check with that card placed face-up? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q6462** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if I shuffle a security stack that includes security cards placed face-up? — A: Any face-up cards are placed face down, then you shuffle the cards. After shuffling, those cards are left face-down.
- **Q6463** (2026-05-08) Q: I have 1 [Junomon] and 1 yellow or purple [TS] trait Digimon. When both of those 2 of my Digimon would be deleted at the same time, the <Scapegoat> gained from this card's effect was activated by 1 of my Digimon, and that [Junomon] was deleted. At such times, I no longer have a Digimon with [Junomon] in its name so <Scapegoat> is lost, but is my Digimon that activated <Scapegoat> prevented from being deleted? — A: Yes, it's prevented from being deleted. Even if an activated effect is lost, the processing is performed until it ends.
