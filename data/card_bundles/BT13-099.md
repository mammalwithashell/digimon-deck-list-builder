## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When one of your yellow Digimon becomes suspended, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -1000 DP until the end of your opponent's turn. [End of Your Turn][Once Per Turn] If there're 6 or fewer total cards in both players' security stacks, until the end of your opponent's turn, this Tamer is also treated as a 3000 DP Digimon, can't digivolve, and gains ＜Blocker＞.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- It causes that card to also be treated as a Digimon with X000 DP. For example, if a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon, it can attack like a standard Digimon, and it will gain inherited effects from cards stacked under it. However, even if a Tamer is played and then treated as a Digimon in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn.
