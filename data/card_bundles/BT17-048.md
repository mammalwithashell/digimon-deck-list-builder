## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be digivolved into, by suspending up to 5 Tamers, reduce the digivolution cost by 1 for each Tamer this effect suspended. [Opponent's Turn] None of your opponent's Tamers can unsuspend. [On Deletion] If you have 4 or more [Argomon] in your trash, you may play 1 level 6 [Argomon] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By suspending 1 of your [Rhythm], unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-048 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2799** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's "when this card would be digivolved into" effect allow me to choose the targets to suspend from among my Tamers and my opponent's Tamers when this card would be digivolved into? — A: Yes, you can choose the targets.
- **Q2800** (2024-03-28) Q: This Digimon was deleted when I had 3 [Argomon] in my trash. At such times, can I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to play 1 level 6 [Argomon] from my hand without paying the cost? — A: Yes, you can. If you have 4 or more [Argomon] in your trash after this card or [Argomon] in this card's digivolution cards is sent to the trash, you can use this card's [On Deletion] effect to play a level 6 [Argomon].
- **Q2808** (2024-03-28) Q: BT17-048 [Argomon] was deleted while it had an [Argomon] with an [On Deletion] effect in its digivolution cards. I first used BT17-048 [Argomon]'s [On Deletion] effect to play this card. At such times, if I use this card's [On Play] effect to place [Argomon] in digivolution cards before activating the [On Deletion] effect, what happens to that effect? — A: If a card is removed from the trash before a triggered [On Deletion] effect activates, that [On Deletion] effect can't be activated.
