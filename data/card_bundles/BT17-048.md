## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be digivolved into, by suspending up to 5 Tamers, reduce the digivolution cost by 1 for each Tamer this effect suspended. [Opponent's Turn] None of your opponent's Tamers can unsuspend. [On Deletion] If you have 4 or more [Argomon] in your trash, you may play 1 level 6 [Argomon] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By suspending 1 of your [Rhythm], unsuspend this Digimon.
