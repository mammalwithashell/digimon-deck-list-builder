### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your black Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 black Digimon. [On Play][When Digivolving] Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your Digimon gains ＜Blocker＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-066 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2200** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a "may digivolve this card as if the Tamer is a Digimon" effect also treat the Tamer that will become a digivolution card as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: Yes, it also treats the Tamer as a Digimon when it digivolves. It treats it as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, digivolution won't be possible using this effect that also treats the Tamer as a Digimon.
- **Q2201** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q2202** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q2203** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q2204** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Security effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q2205** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Inherited effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q2206** (2026-06-19) Q: If I've digivolved this card on top of a Tamer card and the Digimon is deleted, what happens to the Tamer card? — A: It’s trashed.
- **Q4656** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose to not digivolve with this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
