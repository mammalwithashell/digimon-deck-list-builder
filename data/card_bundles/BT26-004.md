## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-004 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6954** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's inherited effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q6955** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6956** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search or look at them. The opponent player can't search or look at them.
- **Q6957** (2026-08-18) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
