### Effect
＜Blocker＞ [On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with the [SoC] trait and 1 such Tamer card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. If you added cards, trash 1 card in your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT15-011 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2494** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include only either a Digimon card or a Tamer card with the [SoC] trait, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q2495** (2024-03-28) Q: If the Tamer card revealed for this card's [On Play] effect doesn't have the [SoC] trait, does that mean I can't add it to my hand? — A: Yes. If the Tamer card doesn't have the [SoC] trait, you can't add it to your hand.
- **Q2496** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards revealed for this card's [On Play] effect include both a Digimon card and Tamer card with the [SoC] trait, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
