## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While your opponent has a white Digimon or Tamer, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your Digimon is unaffected by your opponent's Option cards and its DP can't be reduced.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- Its DP becomes the value it would have when it's unaffected by the DP reduction. For example, if a Digimon has an original DP of 5000 and its DP is reduced by -2000 (making its current DP 3000), its DP will no longer be reduced if you choose it for this card's [Main] effect, therefore its DP will become 5000.
