## Printed text (official)
### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 6 Digimon by placing 10 Tamer cards and/or cards with [Hybrid] in their traits from your hand and/or trash at the bottom of your deck in any order. ＜Security Attack +2＞ (This Digimon checks 2 additional security cards.) [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1680** (2025-04-04) Q: When can this card's 1st effect activate? — A: After declaring a digivolution and revealing this card, you can activate the effect immediately before choosing the card to digivolve. When you choose to activate it, if the "by" condition is met, you may also choose one of your Tamers in addition to Digimon for the card to digivolve. The chosen Tamer is also treated as a level 6 Digimon during this digivolution.
- **Q1681** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose to not digivolve after activating this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. After declaring a digivolution and activating this card's 1st effect, you must choose one of your cards to digivolve and then digivolve it, just as in the procedure for normal digivolution. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
- **Q1682** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose 10 total cards to return to the deck from both my hand and trash for this card's 1st effect, such as 5 cards from my hand and 5 cards from my trash? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1683** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose cards such as 5 Tamer cards and 5 [Hybrid] trait cards for the 10 cards to return to the deck for this card's 1st effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1684** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I return just 9 cards from my hand or trash to the bottom of the deck for this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this inherited effect require you to return the specified number of cards to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q1685** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I digivolve from a Digimon after activating this card's 1st effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1686** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I use BT18-096 [Lord of Devastation and Rebirth]'s [Main] effect to choose one of my Tamers, then activate this card's 1st effect and Digivolve that Tamer? — A: Yes, you can activate this card's 1st effect and digivolve it. However, you can't digivolve one of your Tamers into this card unless the conditions for activating this card's 1st effect are met. _(related: BT18-096)_
- **Q1687** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I use BT18-096 [Lord of Devastation and Rebirth]'s [Main] effect to choose one of my Digimon, then activate this card's 1st effect and digivolve one of my Tamers? — A: No, you can only digivolve the chosen Digimon. You use BT18-096 [Lord of Devastation and Rebirth]'s [Main] effect to choose one of your Digimon or Tamers, then digivolve the chosen card. You can activate this card's 1st effect, but you can't change the card to digivolve. _(related: BT18-096)_
- **Q1688** (2025-04-04) Q: If I activate EX1-071 [Win Rate: 60%!]'s [Main] effect and then declare digivolution of this card, do I perform EX1-071 [Win Rate: 60%!]'s "would digivolve" effect processing or activate this card's 1st effect first? — A: You activate this card's 1st effect first. You declare digivolution of this card, then activate this card's 1st effect immediately before choosing the card to digivolve. After that, you choose your Digimon to digivolve and process EX1-071 [Win Rate: 60%!]'s "would digivolve" effect. If you would digivolve from a Tamer, EX1-071 [Win Rate: 60%!]'s effect can't be processed. _(related: EX1-071)_
- **Q1689** (2025-04-04) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q1690** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a "may digivolve this card as if the Tamer is a Digimon" effect also treat the Tamer that will become a digivolution card as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: Yes, it also treats the Tamer as a Digimon when it digivolves. It treats it as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, digivolution won't be possible using this effect that also treats the Tamer as a Digimon.
- **Q1691** (2025-04-04) Q: When returning cards from my hand or trash to the bottom of the deck for this card's 1st effect, do I have to reveal them to my opponent? — A: Yes, you reveal them to your opponent. The cards to be returned are revealed, then both players confirm the order in which they're returned to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q1692** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q2048** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT7-112 Susanoomon]'s effect to return 10 Tamer cards and/or cards with the [Hybrid] trait to the bottom of my deck, then I chose to trash [BT7-112 Susanoomon] instead of digivolving it. Does this card's inherited effect still activate in such cases? — A: No. It doesn't activate in such cases because [BT7-112 Susanoomon] is trashed by the rules, not an effect. _(a ruling on BT11-006; this card is named in it)_
- **Q6533** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6534** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6535** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
