## Printed text (official)
### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 6 Digimon by placing 10 Tamer cards and/or cards with [Hybrid] in their traits from your hand and/or trash at the bottom of your deck in any order. ＜Security Attack +2＞ (This Digimon checks 2 additional security cards.) [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- After declaring a digivolution and revealing this card, you can activate the effect immediately before choosing the card to digivolve. When you choose to activate it, if the "by" condition is met, you may also choose one of your Tamers in addition to Digimon for the card to digivolve. The chosen Tamer is also treated as a level 6 Digimon during this digivolution.
