## Official Q&A

- **Q6964** (2026-08-18) Q: I have a Digimon with <Detach> that this card is linked to. That Digimon attacked my opponent's Digimon, but both Digimon had the same DP and both lost the battle. At such times, if I activate <Detach> immediately before my Digimon would be deleted and trash this card with <Piercing> in its link effects, only the opponent's Digimon would be deleted in battle, but can I activate <Piercing> in those link effects? — A: No, you can't. <Detach> triggers and activates immediately before both Digimon lose the battle and would be deleted. <Piercing> is lost when your opponent's Digimon is deleted, therefore you can't activate it.
