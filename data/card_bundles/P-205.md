## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-205 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5200** (2025-09-05) Q: When I used this card’s <Delay> effect to delete 1 of my Digimon with an [On Deletion] effect and play [Kimeramon] or [Millenniummon] from the trash, I declared an Assembly for the card to be played. If I place the deleted card for the Assembly at such times, can I activate that card’s [On Deletion] effect? — A: No, you can't. When a card with an effect that's pending activation leaves its current area while activation is pending, the effect can no longer be activated.
- **Q5397** (2025-10-03) Q: When this card's <Delay> effect would play a Digimon, can I declare a DigiXros or Assembly for that card and further reduce its play cost? — A: Yes, you can.
