### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 5 or less. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing 1 of your Option cards in the battle area, it doesn't leave.
