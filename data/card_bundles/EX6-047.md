## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Demon Lord] trait and 1 purple Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. If you added cards, trash 1 card in your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While your opponent has 6 or fewer cards in their hand, this Digimon gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
