## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Demon Lord] trait and 1 purple Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. If you added cards, trash 1 card in your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While your opponent has 6 or fewer cards in their hand, this Digimon gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-047 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3778** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include only either a card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Demon Lord] trait or a purple Option card, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3779** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal for this card's [On Play] effect include both a card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Demon Lord] trait and a purple Option card, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q3780** (2024-03-28) Q: If I add both a card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Demon Lord] trait and a purple Option card to my hand for this card's [On Play] effect, do I trash 2 cards in my hand? — A: No. Even if you add 2 cards to your hand, you only trash 1 card.
