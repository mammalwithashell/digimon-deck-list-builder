## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3241** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this Digimon attack my opponent when I don't have a Digimon with [Myotismon] in its name in play? — A: Yes, it can attack your opponent.
- **Q3242** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack my opponent with this Digimon while I don't have a Digimon with [Myotismon] in its name in play, and it’s blocked by an opponent’s Digimon. What happens? — A: This card’s [Your Turn] effect only prevents you from declaring an opponent's Digimon as the target of the attack. If this Digimon is blocked, conduct the battle between the two Digimon as normal.
