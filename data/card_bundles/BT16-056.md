### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place the top card of 1 of your opponent's [Vaccine] trait Digimon on top of their security stack. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When a card is added to your opponent's security stack, if they have 3 or more security cards, trash the top or bottom card of their security stack.
