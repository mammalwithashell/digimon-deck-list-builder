## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place the top card of 1 of your opponent's [Vaccine] trait Digimon on top of their security stack. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When a card is added to your opponent's security stack, if they have 3 or more security cards, trash the top or bottom card of their security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2645** (2024-03-28) Q: When my opponent had 2 cards in their security attack, they activated [ST10-06 Mastemon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, and the effect that places a card from their trash in their security stack and the effect that plays a level 5 or lower Digimon card activated. My opponent temporarily has 3 cards in their security stack, then it becomes 2 cards. Can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. Even if an effect activates and increases your opponent's security stack, their security stack must have 3 or more cards when the effect finishes activating. _(related: ST10-06)_
