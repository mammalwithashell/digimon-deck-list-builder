## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 purple Digimon card with [Ravemon] in its name or [Bird] or [Avian] in one of its traits and 1 [Keenan Crier] among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If deleted outside of a battle, your opponent trashes 1 card in their hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-053 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2614** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate an [On Deletion] effect at the end of an attack if the effect has conditions for activating, such as [EX4-053 Falcomon]'s inherited effect? — A: No. The effect will trigger, but it can't be activated because its conditions aren't met. _(a ruling on BT16-015; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3495** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include only either a purple Digimon card with [Ravemon] in its name or [Bird] or [Avian] in one of its traits or a [Keenan Crier], can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3496** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a purple Digimon card with [Ravemon] in its name or [Bird] or [Avian] in one of its traits and 1 [Keenan Crier], can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
