## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If it's your turn, search your security stack. This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Angel] or [Three Great Angels] trait among them with the digivolution cost reduced by 2. Then, shuffle your security stack. If this effect digivolved, you may place 1 Digimon card with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Three Great Angels] trait from the hand at the bottom of your security stack.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] All of your Digimon with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Three Great Angels] trait gain ＜Blocker＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-024 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2619** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to search my security stack but choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can choose to not digivolve. In such cases, you shuffle all of the searched cards and return them to the security stack.
- **Q2620** (2026-03-13) Q: When exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw when a digivolution would occur by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when a card is placed on top of the Digimon to digivolve. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q2621** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before returning the remaining cards to the security stack? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q3747** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT16-024 MagnaAngemon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve into this card, then I placed a card from my hand at the bottom of my security stack. At such times, does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect or its [All Turns] effect activate first? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player chooses the activation order. _(a ruling on EX6-028; this card is named in it)_
