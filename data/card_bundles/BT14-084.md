## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] By returning the top card of your security stack to the hand, you may place 1 yellow card with the [Vaccine] trait from your hand at the bottom of your security stack. [Your Turn] When a card is added to your security stack, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2456** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [On Play] effect to return 1 card from my security stack to the hand, then I placed 1 yellow card with the [Vaccine] trait from my hand at the bottom of my security stack. At such times, is a card considered to be added to my security stack, allowing this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate? — A: Yes, it will activate.
- **Q2457** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [On Play] effect to return 1 card from my security stack to the hand, do I have to reveal the returned card to my opponent? — A: No, there are no requirements for the card you return to your hand with this effect, so you don't have to reveal it to your opponent.
