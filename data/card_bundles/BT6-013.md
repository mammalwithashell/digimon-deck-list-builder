## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1407** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this Digimon digivolve into a Digimon with “Black/Level 5” digivolution requirements? — A: Yes. During your turn, this Digimon is treated as both a red and black Digimon, so it can digivolve into a Digimon with black digivolution requirements.
- **Q1408** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is in my breeding area. Can I digivolve it into a Digimon that requires a black Digimon? — A: No, you can't. [Your Turn] effects don't activate from breeding areas, so the card isn't treated as a black card. You can't digivolve it into a Digimon that requires a black Digimon.
