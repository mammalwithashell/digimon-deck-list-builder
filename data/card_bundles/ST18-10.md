## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Suspend 1 Digimon. If this effect suspended your Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits and 3000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon attacks your opponent's Digimon, this Digimon may unsuspend.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. This effect doesn't specify a player, so you can specify either player's Digimon as the effect's target.
