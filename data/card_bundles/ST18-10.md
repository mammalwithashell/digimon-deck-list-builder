## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Suspend 1 Digimon. If this effect suspended your Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits and 3000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon attacks your opponent's Digimon, this Digimon may unsuspend.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST18-10 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q844** (2024-04-19) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend my Digimon? — A: Yes, you can. This effect doesn't specify a player, so you can specify either player's Digimon as the effect's target.
- **Q845** (2024-04-19) Q: If I specify my suspended Digimon for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, can I resolve "if this effect suspended your Digimon" effects? — A: No, you can't. Even if you specify an already suspended Digimon as the target for a suspending effect, it isn't considered to be suspended by the effect.
- **Q846** (2024-04-19) Q: If <Raid> switches the target of attack from a player to an opponent's Digimon, does the [Your Turn] effect in this card's inherited effects trigger? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
