## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] By trashing the top card of your security stack, unsuspend this Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If there're 6 or fewer total cards in both players' security stacks, you may place 1 yellow card from your hand on top of your security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-031 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3595** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card’s [When Digivolving] effect to trash the top card of my security stack when this Digimon is already unsuspended? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3596** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "the total cards in both players' security stacks" in this card's inherited effect mean? — A: It means the total number of cards in both you and your opponent's security stacks. For example, if you have 3 cards in your security stack and your opponent has 2 cards in their security stack, the total security cards will be 5 cards.
