## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards would digivolve into this card, reduce the digivolution cost by 2. [When Digivolving] Return all of your opponent's Digimon with the highest level to the bottom of their owners' decks in any order. [When Digivolving][When Attacking][Once Per Turn] By placing 1 [X Antibody] or level 6 card from this Digimon's digivolution cards at the bottom of its owner's deck, reveal all of your opponent's security cards, and trash 1 of them. Place the rest in your opponent's security stack face down. Then, your opponent shuffles their security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-086 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1910** (2024-03-28) Q: While my opponent has this card's effect activated, I used [BT10-086 Omnimon (X Antibody)]'s effect to reveal all of my opponent's security cards. After choosing and trashing 1 card among them, can the rest be placed in the security stack? — A: Yes, the rest are placed in the security stack. When returning cards that were revealed from a private area such as the deck or security stack by an effect, it isn't considered adding cards to such areas, therefore this card's effect doesn't apply. _(a ruling on BT9-103; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2009** (2024-03-28) Q: Which player chooses which of my opponent's security cards is trashed by this card's [When Digivolving][When Attacking] effect? — A: The player that activated this effect chooses.
- **Q2010** (2024-03-28) Q: My security cards were revealed due to the [When Digivolving] effect of this opponent's card, and 1 of them was trashed. Can I treat this as "when a card is removed from your security stack" and activate [BT4-097 Kari Kamiya]'s effect? — A: Yes, you can activate it. When a revealed card is trashed from the deck or security stack, it is not considered to be trashed from the deck or security stack, but it is considered that a card was removed from the security stack, so [BT4-097 Kari Kamiya]'s effect can be activated. _(related: BT4-097)_
- **Q2277** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect to play [BT5-111 Omnimon X Anti-body], [BT10-068 Gankoomon (X Antibody)], and [BT10-086 Omnimon (X Antibody)] from the digivolution cards of my Digimon in the breeding area? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT13-019; this card is named in it)_
