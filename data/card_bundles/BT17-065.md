## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-065 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2817** (2024-03-28) Q: When exactly is the timing when this card's [Trash] [All Turns] effect triggers? — A: It triggers at the timing when this card is in the trash and your [Dorugamon] would be deleted. [Trash] means that an effect can trigger and activate when the card is in the trash.
- **Q2818** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's [Trash] effect to digivolve when my [Dorugamon] would be deleted by its DP becoming 0, can I prevent that deletion? — A: Yes, you can. However, the DP-reducing effect will be carried over after the [Trash] effect activates and digivolution occurs, therefore deletion will occur again if the DP is still 0 after the effect activates and digivolution occurs.
- **Q2819** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [Trash] effect to digivolve when my [Dorugamon] would be deleted due to its DP being reduced by 10000. Its DP became 0 due to the DP-reducing effect, but can I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect after it digivolves at such times? — A: No, you can't activate it. The Digimon is deleted before the [When Digivolving] effect activates.
- **Q2820** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 4 or less instead" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate instead of "Trash 1 card in your hand. Then, <Draw 1>"? — A: No, it activates instead of "Then, <Draw 1>."
