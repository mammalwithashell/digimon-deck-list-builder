## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 [RagnaLoardmon] and 1 Digimon card with the [Legend-Arms] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1048** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include only either [RagnaLoardmon] or cards with [Legend-Arms] in their types, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, if you only reveal one or the other, you only get to add one card to your hand.
- **Q1049** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include 2 [RagnaLoardmon] cards with [Legend-Arms] in their types, can I add both of those cards to my hand? — A: Yes, you may add them both to your hand.
- **Q1050** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include both a [RagnaLoardmon] card and a card with [Legend-Arms] in its type, do I only get to add 1 to my hand and have to return the rest to the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible. However, if the revealed [RagnaLoardmon] has the [Legend-Arms] trait, that card will be treated as a card with the [Legend-Arms] trait. By adding it to your hand, you can return other cards with the [Legend-Arms] trait to the bottom of the deck without adding them to your hand, and if there aren't any other [RagnaLoardmon] revealed, you can add just 1 [RagnaLoardmon] to your hand.
