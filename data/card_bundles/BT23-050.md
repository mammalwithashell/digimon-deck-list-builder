## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-050 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5317** (2025-10-03) Q: A Digimon's DP became 0 from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect. At such times, is that Digimon deleted before the rest of this effect's processing is resolved? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
- **Q5318** (2025-10-03) Q: I used BT23-017 [Betamon]'s inherited effect to play this card. Can I then use this card's [On Play] effect to DNA digivolve this card and another Digimon into a [Shakkoumon] in the hand? — A: No, you can't. BT23-017 [Betamon]'s inherited effect can't be used to DNA digivolve a Digimon that can't digivolve. _(related: BT23-017)_
- **Q5319** (2025-10-03) Q: I used BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards to attack, and I used its 1st <Alliance> to suspend another of my Digimon. BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s [When Attacking] effect triggers at the same time, I used it to activate its [On Play] effect, play this card, and I used this card's [On Play] effect to DNA digivolve this card and the Digimon I suspended with <Alliance> into a [Shakkoumon] in the hand. Can I then use BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s 2nd <Alliance> to suspend [Shakkoumon]? — A: Yes, you can. If BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards attacks, the 2 instances of <Alliance> and the [When Attacking] effect will trigger simultaneously. If you use the 1st instance of <Alliance> to suspend your Digimon, use the [When Attacking] effect to activate the [On Play] effect, and play this card, its [On Play] effect will derived trigger and activate first. If this card's [On Play] effect is used to DNA digivolve the card suspended by the 1st instance of <Alliance> and this card into a [Shakkoumon], it will digivolve unsuspended, therefore the 2nd instance of <Alliance> can suspend that [Shakkoumon]. In addition, the DP that BT23-101 [Hudiemon] gains from the 1st instance of <Alliance> will remain, even if it DNA digivolves. _(related: BT23-084, BT23-101)_
