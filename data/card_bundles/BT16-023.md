## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If you have 3 or more security cards, unsuspend 1 of your Digimon. If you have 3 or fewer, return 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has [Pulsemon] in its text, by trashing the top card of your security stack, unsuspend this Digimon.
