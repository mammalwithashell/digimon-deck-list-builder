### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [On Play] By adding your top security card to the hand, you may place 1 Digimon card with the [Zaxon] trait from your hand or trash face up as the bottom security card. [End of Your Turn] By suspending this Tamer, 1 of your level 6 Digimon with the [Machine] or [Zaxon] trait may attack a player.

[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.
