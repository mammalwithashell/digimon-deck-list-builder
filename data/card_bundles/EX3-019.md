## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Trash any digivolution card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] When an opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards would digivolve, increase the digivolution cost by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3348** (2024-03-28) Q: When one of my Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon with [Gallantmon] or [Growlmon] in its name, if I can no longer pay the digivolution cost due to the inherited effect on my opponent's [EX3-016 SnowAgumon] or [EX3-019 Paledramon] and the digivolution fails, does the Digimon from the attempted digivolution still gain the [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect from this card's effect? — A: Yes. This card's effect reads "when [...] would digivolve into," so the effect interrupts the digivolution when it activates. Therefore, it activates first and gives that Digimon <Blitz> even if the digivolution later fails. _(a ruling on EX2-056; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3386** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 2 Digimon in this card's digivolution cards, and my opponent's Digimon digivolves with no digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 2? — A: Yes, the inherited effect activates for each of the 2 Digimon, so the digivolution cost increases by a total of 2.
- **Q3387** (2024-03-28) Q: 2 of my opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards DNA digivolves when my Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 2? — A: No, the digivolution cost only increases by 1. This card's inherited effect reads "When an opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards would digivolve," so even in the case of a DNA digivolution where there are 2 of the target opponent's Digimon, the digivolution cost only increases by 1 with this effect because the digivolution only occurs once.
- **Q3388** (2024-03-28) Q: 1 of my opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards and 1 of my opponent's Digimon with digivolution cards DNA digivolves when my Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 1? — A: Yes, the digivolution cost increases by 1.
- **Q3389** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent's red Tamer digivolves into a [BT4-011 Agunimon] when my Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 1? — A: Yes, the digivolution cost increases by 1. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may Digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 Digimon," so the Tamer is considered to be a Digimon without digivolution cards, and the digivolution cost increases by 1 due to this card's inherited effect. _(related: BT4-011)_
- **Q3390** (2024-03-28) Q: My Digimon has this card in its digivolution cards. Does the digivolution cost increase by 1 when my opponent digivolves a red Tamer with cards under it into a [BT4-011 Agunimon]? — A: No, it doesn't. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may Digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 Digimon," so a Tamer with cards under it is considered to be a Digimon with digivolution cards. _(related: BT4-011)_
- **Q3391** (2024-03-28) Q: When a Digimon with no digivolution cards digivolves and the digivolution cost increases due to this card's inherited effect, what happens if I can't pay the digivolution cost as a result? — A: The digivolution fails, and the revealed card to digivolve into is returned to its owner's hand.
