## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] 1 of your non-white Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Sukamon] in its name in your hand without paying the cost, ignoring its digivolution requirements. (Rule) Name: Also treated as having [Sukamon].

### Security Effect
[Security] Until the end of your turn, change 1 of your opponent's Digimon into being white and having 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon].

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-097 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2473** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's effect digivolve any level Digimon into a Digimon card with [Sukamon] in its name in my hand as long as it is a non-white Digimon? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q2474** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's effect digivolve a Digimon in the breeding area into a Digimon card with [Sukamon] in its name in my hand? — A: No, it can't. This effect doesn't target Digimon in breeding areas.
- **Q2475** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "(Rule) Name: Also treated as having [Sukamon]" mean, exactly? — A: This is a special rule for this card that treats it as a card that has [Sukamon] in its name. This is a rule and not an effect, therefore this card is treated as having [Sukamon] in its name when the card is in your hand, trash, or any other area.
- **Q2476** (2024-03-28) Q: Also treated as having [Sukamon]" Can this card be selected as the target of an effect that targets exactly [Sukamon] due to the text "(Rule) Name: Also treated as having [Sukamon]"? — A: No, it can't. This card's name is treated as having [Sukamon] in its name, but it is not treated as [Sukamon].
- **Q2477** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card become a target for effects that activate for Digimon with [Sukamon] in their names? — A: No, it can't. This card is an Option card, therefore it can't become a target for an effect that targets Digimon.
- **Q2478** (2024-09-20) Q: What does a "change 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]" effect do, exactly? — A: This effect changes 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a Digimon whose original name is [Sukamon], its original color is white, and its original DP is 3000.
- **Q2479** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as BT15-035 [Geremon] has a (Rule) that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the (Rule) will also be changed. For example, BT15-035 [Geremon]'s (Rule) gives it an original name of [Geremon/Numemon], but if its original card information is changed by this effect, its original name becomes just [Sukamon]. _(related: BT15-035)_
- **Q2480** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as EX1-005 [Tyrannomon] has an effect that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the effect remains as-is. For example, if EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s effect will also treat it as green, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon. _(related: EX1-005)_
- **Q2481** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card has changed to a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon], then it digivolves or its top card is trashed by a <De-Digivolve> effect, what happens to that card's information? — A: It remains as a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]. The gained effect is carried over even if the Digimon digivolves or one or more of its top cards are trashed.
- **Q2482** (2024-09-20) Q: What happens if a card is changed to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, then its top card is trashed by <De-Digivolve> or other effect and the top card becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card? — A: It remains changed to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, but any card information that it didn't originally have can't be changed. If it becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card, or if it becomes a card that didn't originally have DP, then it will remain without DP.
