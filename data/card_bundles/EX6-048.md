## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] By trashing 1 card in your hand, until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon gains "[End of Attack] Delete this Digimon".

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, by deleting 1 of your other Digimon, end the attack.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it activates. Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon gains that effect, so the [End of Attack] effect will activate even if the affected Digimon digivolves or de-digivolves.
