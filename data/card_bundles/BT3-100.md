## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Trash up to 2 digivolution cards from the bottom of all of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if you have a green Digimon in play, suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

Can I suspend 1 of my opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards? — A: No, if you don't have a green Digimon in play when you activate the card's effect, you can't activate the part of the effect that suspends an opponent's Digimon.
