## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Puppet] or [LIBERATOR] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] When any of your Digimon digivolve into a [Puppet] trait Digimon, by returning this Tamer to the bottom of the deck, you may play 1 [Arisa Kinosaki] or 1 [Puppet] trait Digimon card from your hand with the play cost reduced by 3.
