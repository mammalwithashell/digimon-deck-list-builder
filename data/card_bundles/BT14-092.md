## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Choose 1 of your Digimon. Until the end of your opponent's turn, 3 of your opponent's Digimon with as many or fewer digivolution cards as that Digimon can't attack or block.

### Security Effect
[Security] 1 of your opponent's Digimon can't attack for the turn. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- No, it can't. If the number of digivolution cards under the opponent's Digimon targeted by this effect is less than or equal to those of your chosen Digimon at the timing when this card's effect activates, the effect will continue, even if the target's number of digivolution cards later increases.
