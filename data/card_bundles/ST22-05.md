## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST22-05 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5417** (2025-11-21) Q: I used this card's {Hand} [Counter] <Blast Digivolve> to digivolve my Digimon into this card during my opponent's counter timing, and I used its [When Digivolving] effect to play a [Pipe Fox] Token. At such times, can I use the [Pipe Fox] Token to block during the block timing? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5418** (2025-11-21) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to use an Option card from under a Tamer, what happens to that Option card? — A: As with the standard rules, a used Option card is temporarily treated as not being in any area. After using it and activating the Option card's [Main] effect, the card is trashed unless it's to be placed in an area.
- **Q5419** (2025-11-21) Q: If I use this card to attack and use its [When Attacking] effect to play a [Pipe Fox] Token, can I then use <Alliance> to suspend the [Pipe Fox] Token? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5420** (2025-11-21) Q: ST22-04 [Taomon] with ST22-01 [Viximon] in digivolution cards attacks, and I use ST22-04 [Taomon]'s [When Attacking] effect to use an Option card. If I use ST22-01 [Viximon]'s inherited effect to digivolve this card at such times, can I activate this card's <Alliance>? — A: No, you can't activate it. <Alliance> is an effect that triggers upon an attack. In this case, ST22-04 [Taomon] attacks in its current state where this card's <Alliance> hasn't triggered, therefore it can't activate. _(related: ST22-01, ST22-04)_
