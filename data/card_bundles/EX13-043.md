## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, you may return 1 of your opponent's lowest DP Digimon to the bottom of the deck. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play or use 1 [Mammal], [Beast], [Beastkin] or [Royal Knight] trait card from your hand with the cost reduced by 4. For each suspended Digimon, further reduce it by 1. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your suspended Digimon would leave the battle area other than by your effects, by unsuspending 1 of your Digimon, they don't leave.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -5: Lv.5 × Lv.4 × Lv.3, all green w/[Mammal]/[Beast]/[Beastkin] trait

## Official Q&A

- **Q7341** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, either can be suspended.
- **Q7342** (2026-09-18) Q: What does this card's [All Turns] effect do, exactly? — A: This effect triggers and can be activated when a Digimon specified by it leaves the battle area under the specified conditions.
