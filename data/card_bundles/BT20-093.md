## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may play 1 Digimon card with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in its text from your hand with the play cost reduced by 3. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When any of your Digimon with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in their texts would leave the battle area other than in battle, ＜Delay＞. ・2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into [Examon] in the hand.

[Security] You may play 1 Digimon card with [Dracomon] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area.
