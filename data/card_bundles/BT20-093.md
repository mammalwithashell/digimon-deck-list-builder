## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may play 1 Digimon card with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in its text from your hand with the play cost reduced by 3. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When any of your Digimon with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in their texts would leave the battle area other than in battle, ＜Delay＞. ・2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into [Examon] in the hand.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 Digimon card with [Dracomon] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-093 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4433** (2025-01-24) Q: What cards can be played using this card's [Main] effect? — A: 1 Digimon card with [Dracomon] in its text or 1 Digimon card with [Examon] in its text.
- **Q4434** (2025-01-24) Q: What cards trigger this card's [All Turns] effect when they would leave the battle area? — A: Any of your Digimon with [Dracomon] in their texts or any of your Digimon with [Examon] in their texts.
- **Q4435** (2025-06-13) Q: What does a card with "X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolution requirements, DNA digivolution, DigiXros requirements, burst digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, a card with [Knightmon] in its text would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q4436** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's <Delay> to DNA digivolve to DNA digivolve using 2 Digimon including one that would leave the battle area, does the DNA digivolved Digimon leave the battle area? — A: No, it doesn't leave the battle area. DNA digivolution results in a digivolution into a new Digimon that is considered a different Digimon from the one that would leave the battle area.
