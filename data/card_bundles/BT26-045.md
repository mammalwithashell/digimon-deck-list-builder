## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-045 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7036** (2026-08-18) Q: My opponent and I have the same number of cards in our hands. When I play this card from my hand, can I activate its first effect? — A: No, you can't. The card is still in your hand when you announce you are playing it from your hand. Therefore, the number of cards in your hand and your opponent's hand remains the same.
- **Q7037** (2026-08-18) Q: My opponent and I have the same number of cards in our hands. When I play this card from any area other than my hand, can I activate this card's first effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7038** (2026-08-18) Q: After I attack with this card and play a specified Digimon with its [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect, can I then suspend that Digimon with <Alliance>? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q7077** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to play [BT26-045 GranKuwagamon], can I activate [BT26-046 GranKuwagamon]'s 1st effect and reduce the play cost by a total of 11? — A: Yes, the play cost will be reduced by a total of 11.
