## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have 10 or more cards in your trash, your [Impmon] may digivolve into this card in your hand for a digivolution cost of 4, ignoring this card's digivolution requirements. [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon.
