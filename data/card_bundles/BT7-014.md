## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When one of your Digimon with a Tamer card in its digivolution cards digivolves into this card in your hand, reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 2. [When Digivolving] If a card with [Hybrid] in its traits is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, this Digimon gets +4000 DP for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits, it doesn't activate [Security] effects on Option cards it checks.
