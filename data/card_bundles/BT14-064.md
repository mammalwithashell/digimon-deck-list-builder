## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 card with the [D-Brigade] or [DigiPolice] trait and a play cost of 4 or less among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When one of your other Digimon is deleted, reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 [Commandramon] among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest.
