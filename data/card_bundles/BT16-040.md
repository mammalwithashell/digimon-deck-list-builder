## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] If it's your turn, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 4 Digimon card with the [Insectoid] or [Free] trait in your trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-040 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2634** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] effect to digivolve it into a level 4 Digimon card with the [Insectoid]/[Free] trait in the trash, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q4254** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to play [BT16-040 Wormmon], it digivolved into this Digimon, then I used the [End of Your Turn] effect to move it to the breeding area. At such times, does [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s effect return this Digimon to the hand? — A: No, it doesn't. An effect that targets this Digimon will continue targeting it even if it's moved to the breeding area, but because it isn't affected by effects while in the breeding area, it isn't returned to the hand. _(a ruling on P-143; this card is named in it)_
