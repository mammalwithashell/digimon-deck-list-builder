## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] trait and 1 [Mirei Mikagura] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-020 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3717** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect include only either a card with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] trait or a [Mirei Mikagura], can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3718** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect include both a card with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] trait and a [Mirei Mikagura], can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
