## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] trait and 1 [Mirei Mikagura] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn.
