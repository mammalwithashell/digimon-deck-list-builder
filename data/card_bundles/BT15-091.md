## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer with [Matt Ishida] in its name, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] By placing 1 [Garurumon] and 1 [WereGarurumon] from your trash as 1 of your [Gabumon]'s bottom digivolution cards, that Digimon may digivolve into [MetalGarurumon] in your hand, ignoring its digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Gabumon] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. This effect reads "may digivolve into [MetalGarurumon] in your hand," therefore you can choose to not digivolve after placing [Garurumon] and [WereGarurumon] from your trash under [Gabumon].
