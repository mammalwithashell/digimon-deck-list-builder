## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a level 5 [WarGrowlmon], [Taomon] or [Rapidmon], you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Play 1 [WarGrowlmon] Token (Digimon/red/6000 DP), 1 [Taomon] Token (Digimon/yellow/6000 DP) and 1 [Rapidmon] Token (Digimon/green/6000 DP). This effect can't play Tokens with the same names as your Digimon. Then, 1 of your level 5 Digimon gains ＜Alliance＞ twice for the turn and attacks.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 5 [WarGrowlmon], [Taomon] or [Rapidmon] from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-091 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3160** (2024-09-20) Q: What are the Digmon I can have that will allow me to ignore this card's color requirements according to its 1st effect? — A: 1 level 5 [WarGrowlmon], 1 level 5 [Taomon], or 1 level 5 [Rapidmon].
- **Q3161** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to play a [WarGrowlmon] Token, [Taomon] Token, and [Rapidmon] Token when I have [WarGrowlmon ACE]? — A: No, you can't play a [WarGrowlmon] Token. When a card has "ACE" written in its name, "ACE" isn't included in the card name.
- **Q3162** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I choose 1 of the played tokens for the part of this card's [Main] effect after "then," give <Alliance> to that Digimon twice, and attack with it? — A: No, you can't. The tokens played by this card's [Main] effect don't have a level. You can only choose your level 5 Digimon for the part of this card's [Main] effect after "then," therefore you can't choose these tokens.
- **Q3163** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to give <Alliance> to a Digimon twice, but choose to not attack with it? — A: No, you can't. The Digimon that was given <Alliance> twice must attack if possible.
- **Q3164** (2024-09-20) Q: What cards can be played using this card's [Security] effect? — A: 1 level 5 [WarGrowlmon], 1 level 5 [Taomon], or 1 level 5 [Rapidmon].
