## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a level 5 [WarGrowlmon], [Taomon] or [Rapidmon], you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Play 1 [WarGrowlmon] Token (Digimon/red/6000 DP), 1 [Taomon] Token (Digimon/yellow/6000 DP) and 1 [Rapidmon] Token (Digimon/green/6000 DP). This effect can't play Tokens with the same names as your Digimon. Then, 1 of your level 5 Digimon gains ＜Alliance＞ twice for the turn and attacks.
