## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] If you have 3 or more security cards, this Digimon gets +4000 DP until the end of your opponent's next turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When a card is added to your security stack, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-029 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3213** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon in play with this card in its digivolution cards. [BT1-087 T.K. Takaishi]’s effect adds a yellow card from my security stack to my hand, and adds a card to my security stack with <Recovery +1 (Deck)>. The number of cards in my security stack ultimately remained the same, but can I still gain 1 memory from this card’s inherited effect? — A: Yes. <Recovery +1 (Deck)> added a card to your security, so you gain 1 memory. _(related: BT1-087)_
- **Q3214** (2024-03-28) Q: This Digimon gets +4000 DP from its effect. During the same turn, I end up with 2 or fewer cards in my security stack. Does the +4000 DP granted by this card’s effect go away? — A: No. So long as you had 3 or more security cards at the time you activated this card’s inherited effect, the effect will persist until the end of your opponent’s next turn, even if you end up with 2 or fewer security cards.
