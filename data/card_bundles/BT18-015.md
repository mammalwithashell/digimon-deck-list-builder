## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2922** (2024-06-21) Q: If I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand, do the DNA digivolution requirements have to be met for [Millenniummon]? — A: Yes, the DNA digivolution requirements have to be met. In addition, DNA digivolution can't be performed unless the [Millenniummon] has DNA digivolution.
- **Q2923** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's [On Deletion] effect allow my [Machinedramon] and this card itself in the trash that activated the effect to DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand? — A: Yes, that's correct. The [On Deletion] effect activates after the card is placed in the trash, therefore this card itself in the trash can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand.
- **Q2924** (2024-11-22) Q: What are the areas where [Machinedramon] and [Kimeramon] can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand using this card's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Your [Machinedramon] in the battle area and [Kimeramon] in your trash can DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] in the hand.
- **Q3014** (2024-06-21) Q: When this card would be played, I deleted my BT18-015 [Kimeramon], then I played this card. At such times, does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect trigger simultaneously with BT18-015 [Kimeramon]'s [On Deletion] effect? — A: Yes, they trigger simultaneously. However, if you activate BT18-015 [Kimeramon]'s [On Deletion] effect first and then DNA digivolve into [Millenniummon] using [Kimeramon] and this card while its [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect has triggered, this card will be removed from the battle area before the [On Play] effect can activate. _(a ruling on BT18-073; this card is named in it)_
