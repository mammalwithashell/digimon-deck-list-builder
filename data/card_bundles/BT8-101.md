## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [Armor Form] in its traits in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -4000 DP for the turn, and all of your opponent's Digimon get -1000 DP for the turn for each card with [Armor Form] in its traits in your trash.

[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.
