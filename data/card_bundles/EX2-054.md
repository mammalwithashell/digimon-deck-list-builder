## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-054 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3345** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card's effect plays a Digimon, is it treated as a Security Digimon even in my battle area? — A: No, it's treated as a normal Digimon once it comes into play.
- **Q3346** (2024-03-28) Q: An opponent’s Digimon with <Security Attack+1> attacks me directly, and this card is flipped over during the first security check and played in my battle area by its [Security] effect. If I have a [Mother D-Reaper] with 6 or more digivolution cards in play, can this card's [Opponent’s Turn] effect prevent my opponent's second security check? — A: Yes. This card’s [Opponent's Turn] effect gives your opponent’s attacking Digimon <Security Attack -1> before the second security check, so the second security check does not occur, and the attack ends.
