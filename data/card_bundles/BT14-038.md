## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] If you have 3 or more cards with [Sukamon] in their names in your trash, you may play 1 level 6 Digimon card with [Etemon] in its name from your hand without paying the cost. [On Deletion] Place this card at the bottom of your security stack.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] Place 1 [Etemon] from your trash at the bottom of your security stack.

## Official Q&A
- This is a [Security] effect, so it happens before the battle. The battle with your Security Digimon happens after the activation of [On Play] effects on played Digimon or "When you play a Digimon" effects.
