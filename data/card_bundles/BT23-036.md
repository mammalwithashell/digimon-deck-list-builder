## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if your opponent has a Digimon with 10000 DP or more, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your other Digimon may digivolve into a level 6 or lower Digimon card with [Leomon] in its name or the [CS] trait in the hand without paying the cost. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your Digimon gains ＜Raid＞ for the turn. Then, that Digimon may attack.
