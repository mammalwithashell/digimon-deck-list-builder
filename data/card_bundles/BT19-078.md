## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] For each digivolution card of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]s, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -1000 DP for the turn. [Main] Place this Digimon as the bottom digivolution card of any of your [Mother D-Reaper]s without [ADR-01 Jeri] in their digivolution cards.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] When one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, you may play 1 [ADR-01 Jeri] from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. If you did, you may change the attack target to the Digimon this effect played.
