Once all of the processing for the activated effect has resolved, a rule check will occur, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.

For example, if text reads "level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait or is yellow or black," it refers to a level 4 or lower Digimon card that has the [CS] trait or a level 4 or lower Digimon card that is yellow or black.
