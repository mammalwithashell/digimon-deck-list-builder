## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash up to 2 blue cards in your hand. For each one, you may trash any 1 card under your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. Then, until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon or Tamers without cards under it can't suspend.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack][Once Per Turn] By returning 3 cards with [Jellymon] in their texts from your trash to the bottom of the deck in any order, unsuspend this Digimon.
