## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3948** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use "・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to give the same Digimon -2000 DP multiple times? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3949** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use "・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to give different Digimon -2000 DP? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3950** (2025-04-04) Q: I activated "・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect once, and the DP of the opponent's Digimon became 0. Is that Digimon deleted before the effect activates 3 more times? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. This card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect activates "・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn" 4 times, then all Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same timing.
- **Q3951** (2024-11-22) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to reduce the DP of an opponent's Digimon to 0, then I played EX8-062 [Piedmon ACE]. Does that EX8-062 [Piedmon ACE]'s [All Turns] effect trigger at such times? — A: Yes, it triggers. Even if you use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to reduce the DP of an opponent's Digimon to 0, it isn't yet deleted at that time. After you play EX8-062 [Piedmon ACE] from your trash and this effect is resolved, then all Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same timing. Digimon with a DP of 0 are deleted after playing EX8-062 [Piedmon ACE], then EX8-062 [Piedmon ACE]'s [All Turns] effect will trigger.
