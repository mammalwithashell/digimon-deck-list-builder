## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT24-079 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5659** (2025-12-25) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5660** (2025-12-25) Q: All of my Digimon get -5000 DP. I used this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play a Digimon with 5000 DP or less. At such times, is the played Digimon deleted before the part of the effect after "then"? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for this card's [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
