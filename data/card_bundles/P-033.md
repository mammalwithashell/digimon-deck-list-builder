## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] All of your black Digimon with 13000 DP or more gain ＜Piercing＞ (When this Digimon attacks and deletes an opponent's Digimon, it performs any security checks it normally would).

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon is black and has 13000 DP or more, this Digimon gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card).

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4145** (2024-03-28) Q: I have this Digimon in play, and attack with a black Digimon with 12000 DP, whose [When Attacking] effect grants itself +1000 DP, bringing its total DP up to 13000. Does this Digimon's effect allow that black Digimon to have <Piercing> during the battle? — A: Yes. This Digimon's effect becomes active as soon as your black Digimon reaches 13000 DP or more. Your black Digimon will conduct its battle with <Piercing>.
- **Q4146** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with a black Digimon with 12000 DP, whose [When Attacking] effect grants itself +1000 DP, bringing its total DP up to 13000. If this Digimon is in its digivolution cards, does this Digimon's inherited effect allow the black Digimon to have <Security Attack +1> during its battle? — A: Yes. This Digimon's inherited effect becomes active as soon as your black Digimon reaches 13000 DP or more. Your black Digimon will conduct its battle with <Security Attack +1>.
- **Q4147** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is in the digivolution cards of a black Digimon with 13000 DP, which I use to attack my opponent. The first card flipped over during the security check causes the black Digimon to <De-Digivolve>, resulting in it having 12000 DP or less. Does the black Digimon still get extra security checks from <Security Attack +1>? — A: This card's inherited effect is a [Your Turn] effect, so your black Digimon will lose <Security Attack +1> as soon as it drops below 13000 DP. If the Digimon has no other effects to increase the number of security checks, the attack will end without performing any further checks.
