## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [On Deletion] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 play cost 7 or lower Digimon card with [Mamemon] in its name or the [Mutant] trait among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.

For example, "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait or that is yellow or black" means either "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait" or "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card that is yellow or black." In both cases, the card must be level 4 or lower.
- **Q7391** (2026-09-18) Q: I am trying to play a card revealed by this card's effect. At this time, can I perform Assembly for the card I'm trying to play and place cards being revealed under it? — A: No, you can't. Cards being revealed by this card's effect have not been placed in the trash yet, so you can't place them under the card you're trying to play while performing Assembly for it.
