## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [On Deletion] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 play cost 7 or lower Digimon card with [Mamemon] in its name or the [Mutant] trait among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.
