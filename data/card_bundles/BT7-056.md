### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Kota Domoto] and 1 card with the [X Antibody] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your effects add to this Digimon's digivolution cards, gain 1 memory.
