## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] You may add the top card of your security stack to your hand to unsuspend this Digimon and have 1 of your opponent's Digimon get -6000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-048 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1209** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I always have to activate this card's effect when I attack? — A: No, you can choose not to.
- **Q1210** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [When Attacking] effect if I have no cards left in my security stack? — A: No. If you can't add cards from your security stack to your hand, you can't activate the effect.
- **Q1211** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this Digimon, then activate its [When Attacking] effect to unsuspend it and give 1 of my opponent's Digimon -6000 DP. If I attack again on the same turn, can I give 1 of my opponent's Digimon an additional -6000 DP? — A: No. The effect has [Once Per Turn], so it doesn't activate on any attacks past the first one.
