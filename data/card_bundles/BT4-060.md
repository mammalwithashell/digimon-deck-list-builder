## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1216** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to suspend one my own Digimon? — A: Yes, as long as it's a level 4 or lower Digimon, you can suspend it -- including your own Digimon.
- **Q1217** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to suspend a level 4 Digimon when it Digivolves? — A: No, digivolving doesn't count as the card being played, so you can't suspend it.
- **Q1218** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to suspend a level 4 or lower Digimon when it moves from the breeding area to the battle area? — A: No. Moving from the breeding area doesn't count as the card being played, so you can't suspend it.
