## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-014 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2612** (2024-03-28) Q: This card digivolved from [BT14-018 Goldramon]. This Digimon gains all effects of [Goldramon] in digivolution cards, but does [BT14-018 Goldramon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect activate? — A: Yes, it activates. This card's "this Digimon gains all effects of [Goldramon] in this Digimon's digivolution cards" effect is an [All Turns] effect, so it will activate as soon as the digivolution is confirmed. Therefore, [When Evolving] effects are gained and will trigger. _(related: BT14-018)_
- **Q2613** (2024-03-28) Q: This card digivolved from [EX3-035 Goldramon]. This Digimon gains all effects of [Goldramon] in digivolution cards, but after [EX3-035 Goldramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect activates and I return [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from the trash to the hand, can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from my hand? — A: Yes, you can. If this card's [All Turns] effect is used to gain a [When Digivolving] effect, this card's [When Digivolving] effect and the [When Digivolving] effect gained from the [All Turns] effect will trigger simultaneously upon the digivolution timing, therefore you may choose the activation order. _(related: EX3-035, EX3-069)_
