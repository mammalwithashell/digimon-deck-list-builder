## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q887** (2024-03-28) Q: During my opponent's turn, a Digimon with this digivolution card is deleted, and this card's inherited effect moves the memory gauge to 1 on my side. Is it now my turn? — A: Yes, after all effects and attacks resolve, it becomes your turn.
- **Q888** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon with this digivolution card is deleted after blocking an opponent's Digimon with <Piercing>. As a result, I gain 1 memory, moving the memory gauge to 1 on my side. Does my opponent's <Piercing> effect activate? — A: Yes, it does. Turns only switch after all attacks and effects activate and finish, so it becomes your turn after the <Piercing> check is performed and the attack finishes.
