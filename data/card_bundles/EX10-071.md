## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [Lucemon] in its name on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements. {Trash} [End of Your Turn] If you have a Digimon with [Lucemon] in its name, by returning this card to the bottom of the deck, trash your top security card and 1 of your Digimon attacks without suspending. [Main] Until your opponent's turn ends, 1 of your Digimon with [Lucemon] in its name gains ＜Raid＞, ＜Piercing＞, ＜Blocker＞ and +3000 DP.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Lucemon] from your trash without paying the cost.
