## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] By suspending 1 of your green Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with [Vegetation], [Plant], or [Fairy] in one of its traits and 3000 DP or less from your hand without paying its play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an effect suspends one of your Digimon, ＜Draw 1＞. (Draw 1 card from your deck.)

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. The Digimon is suspended by before the digivolution is complete, so the inherited effect cannot be triggered yet.
