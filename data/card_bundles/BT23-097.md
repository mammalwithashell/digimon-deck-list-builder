## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-097 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5385** (2025-10-03) Q: What is a {Trash} effect? — A: A [Trash] effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the trash. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the trash.
- **Q5570** (2025-12-25) Q: When one of my Digimon digivolves into [Belphemon (X Antibody)], in what order can I activate this card's {Trash} [Your Turn] effect and the effects that trigger upon digivolution into that Digimon? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
