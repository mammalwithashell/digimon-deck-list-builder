## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-034 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5101** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to give "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks" to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the gained effect won't trigger.
- **Q5102** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I trash just 1 digivolution card for the conditions of this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [All Turns] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q5103** (2025-09-05) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger when either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon attacks? — A: Yes, it triggers.
