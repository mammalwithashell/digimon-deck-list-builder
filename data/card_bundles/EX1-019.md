## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If a Digimon card with [Free] in its traits is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, unsuspend this Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Imperialdramon] in its name, it can't be blocked.

## Official Q&A
- If you attack with a Digimon that has this inherited effect while the effect is active, your opponent's Digimon can't change the target of the attack by blocking.
