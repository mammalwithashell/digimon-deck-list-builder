## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3204** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s inherited effect reads, “This Digimon can't be blocked.” How does this effect work, exactly? — A: If you attack with a Digimon that has this inherited effect while the effect is active, your opponent's Digimon can't change the target of the attack by blocking.
- **Q3205** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon with [Imperialdramon] in its name and this card in its digivolution cards. Can it attack an opponent’s suspended Digimon? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q3206** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent attacks with a Digimon with [Imperialdramon] in its name and this card in its digivolution cards. Even though I can't block it, can I still try to block with my Digimon with <Blocker> just to suspend my Digimon? — A: No, you can't. If your Digimon has the "...can't be blocked" effect, your opponent's Digimon can't block or suspend to block.
