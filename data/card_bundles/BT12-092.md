## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have a Digimon with [Agumon] or [Greymon] in its name, by paying 1 memory, for the turn, this Tamer is also treated as a 3000 DP Digimon and can't digivolve. [Your Turn] When this Tamer suspends, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a yellow card with [Greymon] in its name in the hand without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- It causes that card to also be treated as a Digimon with X000 DP. For example, if a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon, it can attack like a standard Digimon, and it will gain inherited effects from cards stacked under it. However, even if a Tamer is played and then treated as a Digimon in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn.
