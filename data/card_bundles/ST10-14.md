## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack. If you do, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of its owner's security stack.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, that's correct. If you choose to place an opponent’s Digimon at the top of their security stack, you are in effect choosing to trash the chosen Digimon.
