## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack. If you do, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of its owner's security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST10-14 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q746** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card’s effect to place an opponent’s Digimon at the top of their security stack, is that Digimon then trashed? — A: Yes, that's correct. If you choose to place an opponent’s Digimon at the top of their security stack, you are in effect choosing to trash the chosen Digimon.
- **Q747** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if I use this card while my opponent has [BT9-103 Kongou]'s effect activated? — A: Due to [BT9-103 Kongou]'s effect, cards can't be placed in security stacks, therefore this card's "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack" can't be activated, and the target Digimon will be left as-is. _(related: BT9-103)_
- **Q748** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card’s effect to place an opponent’s Digimon at the top of their security stack, does that Digimon’s [On Deletion] effect activate? Additionally, do effects on other Digimon and Tamers that care about Digimon being deleted also activate? — A: No. The Digimon isn’t being deleted, so none of the above occurs.
- **Q1909** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if my opponent activates this card's effect and I use [ST10-14 Chaos Degradation]? — A: Due to this card's effect, cards can't be placed in security stacks, therefore [ST10-14 Chaos Degradation]'s "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack" can't be activated, and the target Digimon will be left as-is.
