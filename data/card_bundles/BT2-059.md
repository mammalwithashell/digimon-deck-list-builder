## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT2-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1024** (2024-03-28) Q: What counts as "another Digimon with the same name as this Digimon"? Does this only count if I have another Digimon in play named [Kurisarimon]? — A: No. This effect doesn't refer to [Kurisarimon], but to the name of the Digimon this card digivolves into.
- **Q2814** (2024-03-28) Q: Does the inherited effect on a card like BT2-059 [Kurisarimon] or BT2-053 [Keramon] trigger twice when I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens? — A: No, it doesn't. You can play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens, but they are played at the same time during a single timing, therefore the effect only triggers once. _(related: BT2-053)_
