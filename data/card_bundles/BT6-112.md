## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When playing this card from your hand, reduce its play cost by 1 for each [Three Musketeers] Digimon card and Option card with a memory cost of 7 in your trash. [On Play] Return 1 Option card with a memory cost of 7 from your trash to your hand. Then, use 1 Option card with a memory cost of 7 in your hand without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1500** (2024-03-28) Q: When using this card’s [On Play] effect to use an Option card, can I use any 7-cost Option card, regardless of its color requirements? — A: No, you can’t ignore its color requirements.
- **Q1501** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use option cards that can change their cost to 7 on certain conditions such as [BT7-100 Qualialise Blast] or [BT11-108 DG Dimension] with this card’s [On Play] effect? — A: No, you can’t. Since those options are cards that have effects that “reduce the cost by…” or “its memory cost is equal to…” when they would be used, their cost has not actually changed when activating this card’s [On Play] effect, so they can’t be used with this card’s effect. However, since [BT2-099 Glorious Burst] always has the effect of reducing its use cost when used from the hand, if you have 2 yellow Tamers, it can be used with this card’s effect as its use cost will be 7. _(related: BT7-100, BT11-108, BT2-099)_
