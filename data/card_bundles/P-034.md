## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If you have 7 or more Digimon cards with [Devimon] in their names in your trash, you may play 1 [DanDevimon] from your trash without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. This card moving to your trash will cause you to have 7 Digimon cards with [Devimon] in their names in your trash. As such, you will be able to activate this card's [On Deletion] effect to play a [DanDevimon] from your trash.
