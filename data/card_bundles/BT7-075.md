## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When one of your Digimon with a Tamer card in its digivolution cards digivolves into this card in your hand, reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 2. [On Deletion] If a card with [Hybrid] in its traits is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 purple Tamer card from your trash without paying its memory cost.
