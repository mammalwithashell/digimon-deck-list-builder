## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Tyrannomon] in its name or the [Dinosaur] trait in the hand.

## Official Q&A
- The [When Digivolving] effect activates first. As soon as your Digimon attacks, the "when your Digimon attacks, you may digivolve that Digimon" effects trigger simultaneously. You activate the 1st effect, digivolve a Digimon, then its [When Digivolving] effect triggers, and you therefore activate it first due to the derived trigger rule.
