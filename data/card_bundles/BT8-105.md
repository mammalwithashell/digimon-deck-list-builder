## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Choose any number of your opponent's Digimon whose play costs add up to 15 or less and delete them.

### Security Effect
[Security] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 15 or less.

## Official Q&A
- You can choose any number of your opponent's Digimon, as long as the play costs of the chosen Digimon add up to 15 or less. For example, you could choose 1 Digimon with a play cost of 3 and 1 Digimon with a play cost of 12, or 1 Digimon with a play cost of 2 and 2 Digimon with play costs of 6.
