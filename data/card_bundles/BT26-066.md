- **Q7089** (2026-08-18) Q: A Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards attacks, and another effect trashes a card from my hand. At such times, if I activate this card's inherited effect and perform a digivolution into [P-209 Titamon], can I then activate [P-209 Titamon]'s <Alliance>? — A: No, you can't. <Alliance> is an effect that triggers upon an attack. You can't activate it if the Digimon doesn't have <Alliance> upon the attack declaration. _(related: P-209)_
