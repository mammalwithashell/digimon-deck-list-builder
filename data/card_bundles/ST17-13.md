- **Q833** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Security] effect allow me to ignore the digivolution requirements when I digivolve 1 of my Digimon into this card at the end of the battle? — A: No, you can’t ignore its digivolution requirements.

Security effects have top priority and activate when a card is flipped over for a security check.
