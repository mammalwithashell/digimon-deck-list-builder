## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST12-13 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q759** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens to "*Also treat as if name is [Sistermon Noir] and traits include [Virus]" on this card when it is in my hand or breeding area? — A: "*Also treat as if name is [Sistermon Noir] and traits include [Virus]" is a special rule for this card, not an effect. Therefore, this text also applies when the card is in your hand, breeding area, or any other area.
- **Q760** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card have the 4 traits, [Champion], [Puppet], [Virus] and [Data]? — A: This card is treated as having both the [Virus] and [Data] traits, but only 1 at a time, so it is treated as having 3 traits in total, 1 of them together with the [Champion] and [Puppet] traits.
- **Q5224** (2025-10-03) Q: I have BT10-085 [Sistermon Ciel]. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to play a BT6-084 [Sistermon Ciel] or ST12-13 [Sistermon Ciel] that's also treated as [Sistermon Noir]? — A: No, it can't be played. "With the same names" also refers to a card with even just 1 of the same name as any of your Digimon. This effect can't play cards with the same name as [Sistermon Ciel]. _(a ruling on BT23-013; this card is named in it)_
