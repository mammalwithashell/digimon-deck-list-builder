## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait and 1 Tamer card with inherited effects among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 Tamer card with inherited effects from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-009 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2715** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 3 cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include only either a card with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait or a Tamer card with an inherited effect, can I just add that 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q2716** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 3 cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a card with the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait and a Tamer card with an inherited effect, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q2717** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "Tamer card with inherited effects" mean, exactly? — A: It means a Tamer card with "Inherited Effect" in its lower text. A Tamer card with a [Security] effect in its lower text isn't a "Tamer card with inherited effects."
