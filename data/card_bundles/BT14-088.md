### Effect
[On Play] You may reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 level 3 Digimon card and 1 non-white Tamer card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Opponent's Turn] When an opponent's level 5 or higher Digimon attacks, by suspending this Tamer, move 1 of your Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area.
