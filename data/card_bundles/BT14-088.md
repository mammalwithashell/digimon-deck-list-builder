## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 level 3 Digimon card and 1 non-white Tamer card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Opponent's Turn] When an opponent's level 5 or higher Digimon attacks, by suspending this Tamer, move 1 of your Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-088 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2461** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal with this card's [On Play] effect don't contain both a level 3 Digimon card and a non-white Tamer card, do I still get to add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can add just 1 card to your hand when only 1 or the other is revealed.
- **Q2462** (2024-03-28) Q: When the cards revealed from my deck with this card's [On Play] effect contain both a level 3 Digimon card and a non-white Tamer card, am I allowed to add just one card to my hand? — A: No, you can't. You must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q2463** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect move a level 2 Digimon or cards like [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] or [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] from the breeding area to the battle area? — A: It can't move level 2 Digimon or cards like [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] because cards without DP can't be moved to the battle area. It can move [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] because it has DP. _(related: EX2-007, BT13-007)_
- **Q2464** (2026-03-13) Q: I used this card’s [Opponent's Turn] effect to move a Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area. Can I use <Blast Digivolve> to digivolve that moved Digimon during the following counter timing? — A: Yes, you can.
