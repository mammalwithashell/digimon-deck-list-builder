## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon card with the [CS] trait, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower Digimon card with the [Hudie] trait from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon this effect played can't digivolve and is deleted at the end of your opponent's turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5299** (2025-10-03) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger when this card is in the breeding area and would digivolve into a card with the [CS] trait? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q5300** (2025-10-03) Q: I have this card in the digivolution cards of a Digimon with <Alliance>. If that Digimon attacks and I use this card's inherited effect to play a Digimon, can I then use <Alliance> to suspend the played Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5565** (2025-12-25) Q: Do I delete the Digimon that was played by this card's inherited effect at the end of my opponent's turn? And if it isn't deleted, is it still prevented from digivolving? — A: Yes, it's deleted upon the deletion timing. If it isn't deleted by another effect when it would be deleted by this effect, it's still prevented from digivolving.
- **Q5566** (2025-12-25) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's inherited effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously. Therefore, the turn player can choose the processing order.
