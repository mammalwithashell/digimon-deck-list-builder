## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, reduce the play cost by 3 for each of your opponent's Digimon and Tamers. [On Play] [When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ all of your opponent’s Digimon. Then, delete all of their level 4 or lower Digimon. [End of Opponent’s Turn] [Once Per Turn] Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q792** (2024-03-28) Q: If my Digimon is targeted by the [End of Opponent's Turn] effect of my opponent's [BT9-112 DeathXmon] while it has this card in its digivolution cards, does this card's inherited effect prevent it from being deleted due to my opponent's effect? — A: No, this card's inherited effect has the [Opponent's Turn] timing, but the turns haven't changed at the end of your turn, so this card's inherited effect can't be activated and the effect of your opponent's [BT9-112 DeathXmon] can't be prevented.
- **Q1928** (2024-03-28) Q: When playing this card, do I reduce its memory cost by 3 for each pair of Digimon and Tamer cards my opponent has in play? — A: No, you reduce its memory cost by 3 based on the total number of Digimon and Tamer cards your opponent has in play. For example, if your opponent has 2 Digimon and 1 Tamer, this adds up to 3 cards total, which means you get to reduce the play cost by 9.
