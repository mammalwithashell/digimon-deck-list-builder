## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] At the end of the battle, play this card without paying the cost. [On Play] [When Digivolving] Trash the top 2 digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, 1 of their Digimon with no digivolution cards can't attack or block until their turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If you have 7 or fewer cards in your hand, ＜Draw 1＞. (Draw 1 card from your deck.)

## Official Q&A
- No, it can't. This effect affects 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards, and the processing prevents that Digimon from attacking or blocking until their turn ends. Even if it gains a digivolution card after being affected by the effect, the effect will continue processing.
