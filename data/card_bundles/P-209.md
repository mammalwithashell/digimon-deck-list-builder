## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Alliance＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] By trashing 1 card in your hand, suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. Then, 1 of their Digimon or Tamers can't unsuspend until their turn ends. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When your hand is trashed from, you may play 1 level 4 or lower [Demon] or [Titan] trait card from your trash without paying the cost. (Rule) Trait: Has [Demon] Type.
