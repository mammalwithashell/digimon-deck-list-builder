## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, if it's your turn, this Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits in the hand. For every other suspended Digimon, reduce this effect's digivolution cost by 1.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] This Digimon gets +2000 DP.
