## Printed text (official)
### Effect
{Hand} [Counter] ＜Blast Digivolve＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] To 1 of your opponent’s Digimon, give -4000 DP for the turn for each of your Digimon with the [Olympos XII] trait. Then, delete up to 7000 DP total worth of their Digimon. [End of Your Turn] 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into [GraceNovamon] in the hand. Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-191 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4980** (2026-05-08) Q: This card's effect caused the DP of my opponent's Digimon to become 0. At such times, is the Digimon with a DP of zero deleted? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for the activated effect has resolved, a rule check will occur, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
- **Q4981** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to reduce the DP of multiple Digimon? — A: No, you can't. You choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon, then that Digimon's DP is reduced by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q4982** (2025-07-04) Q: After DNA digivolving, can the DNA digivolved Digimon attack using this card's [End of Your Turn] effect? — A: Yes, it can attack.
- **Q4983** (2025-07-04) Q: If I choose to not use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to DNA digivolve, can I still use it to have 1 of my Digimon attack? — A: Yes, it can attack.
- **Q4984** (2025-07-04) Q: At the end of my turn, I used this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to DNA digivolve this card and 1 of my Digimon into [GraceNovamon], then I used that [GraceNovamon] to attack. Can I then activate this card's inherited effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's inherited effect doesn't trigger at such timings.
- **Q4985** (2025-07-04) Q: I have an unsuspended BT22-077 [Dianamon] with this card in its digivolution cards in the battle area. At the end of my turn, can I activate this card's inherited effect to attack, then use BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect to unsuspend? — A: Yes, you can. If you use this card's inherited effect to attack, then use BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect to unsuspend, the counter timing will occur once all other processing has been resolved. _(related: BT22-077)_
- **Q4986** (2025-07-04) Q: I have a suspended BT22-077 [Dianamon] with this card in its digivolution cards in the battle area. At the end of my turn, can I activate BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect, unsuspend, and then use this card's inherited effect to attack? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT22-077)_
