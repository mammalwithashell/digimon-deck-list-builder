## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may return 1 of your Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name to its owner's hand to delete all Digimon and Tamers. Trash all of the digivolution cards of the Digimon you returned with this effect.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-110 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1384** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's effect delete all of my Digimon and Tamers, too? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q5304** (2025-10-03) Q: I have this card and BT19-053 [QueenBeemon] in the battle area. My opponent uses BT5-110 [All Delete] and I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when multiples of my Digimon would leave the battle area at the same time, and this Digimon is prevented from leaving. What happens if BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect activates afterward? — A: BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect places all of your Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait into your security stack. In such cases, this card's [All Turns] effect prevents this Digimon from being deleted, but if BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect activates afterward, you can place all of your [Royal Base] trait Digimon that were affected by the deletion effect into your security stack. _(related: BT19-053)_
