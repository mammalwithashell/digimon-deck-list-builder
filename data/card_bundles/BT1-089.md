## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set your memory to 3. [Main] If you have a level 5 or higher green Digimon in play, you can suspend this Tamer to hatch 1 Digi-Egg card to an empty space in your breeding area, or move 1 level 3 or higher Digimon from your breeding area to your battle area.

### Inherited Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-089 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q736** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect activate when [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect moves a Digimon from my breeding area to my battle area? — A: No, moving a Digimon from your breeding area doesn’t count as playing it, so the effect doesn’t activate.
- **Q955** (2024-03-28) Q: I have both this card and [ST2-12 Matt Ishida] in play. How do I activate their effects? — A: You can resolve their effects in any order. For example, if you're starting the turn at 1 memory, you can resolve this card's effect first to go up to 3 memory, then activate [ST2-12 Matt Ishida]'s effect to go to 4 memory and above.
- **Q956** (2024-03-28) Q: What timing does [Main] occur at? — A: Like Main effects on Option cards, you can activate these effects at any time during your main phase. However, you can't activate them to interrupt an attack or another effect.
- **Q957** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's Main effect if I have a level 5 or higher green Digimon in my Breeding Area? — A: No, Digimon in breeding areas aren't checked by this effect.
- **Q958** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's Start of Your Turn effect activate if it is suspended? — A: Yes, it does. Unless otherwise specified, cards don't lose their effects when suspended.
- **Q959** (2024-03-28) Q: During my breeding phase, I hatch a level 2 Digimon from my Digi-Egg deck. I then digivolve that Digimon into a level 3 Digimon during my main phase. If I use this card's effect to move that Digimon to my battle area, can I attack with it immediately? — A: Yes, you can. Hatching Digimon and moving them to the battle area doesn't count as playing them, so you can attack with them immediately.
- **Q1142** (2024-03-28) Q: After my opponent activates this card's security effect, they use [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa] to move a Digimon from their breeding area to their battle area. Can that Digimon attack me immediately? — A: No, this card's security effect affects all of your opponent's Digimon, including Digimon that enter the battle area after the effect is activated.
- **Q1769** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [Opponent’s Turn] effect activate when an effect from a card like [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa] moves a level 3 Digimon from your opponent's breeding area to their battle area outside of their breeding phase? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q1844** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is in play, can I move one of my level 3 or higher Digimon from my breeding area to my battle area with [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect? — A: Yes. Moving a Digimon out of your breeding area does not count as playing a Digimon.
- **Q4252** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is targeted by an effect such as "1 of your opponent's Digimon gains <Security A. -1>," then this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, what happens to the effect? — A: If this Digimon is targeted by an effect and then moved to the breeding area, it remains targeted by the effect. However, it isn't affected by that effect. If it's moved back to the battle area using an effect such as [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect, it will be affected by that effect again.
- **Q4253** (2024-03-28) Q: After I activated this Digimon's [End of Your Turn] effect, I used an effect such as [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect to move this Digimon back to the battle area. Can I activate that Digimon's [End of Your Turn] effect again? — A: No, you can't. Even if a card is moved, once a [Once Per Turn] effect has activated, it can't be activated again.
