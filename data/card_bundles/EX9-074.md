## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Rush＞ ＜Security A. +1＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 level 4 or lower [DM] trait Digimon card from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same color as any of this Digimon's digivolution cards. If this Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards, instead delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors. [All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP for each color in its digivolution cards.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -7: 7 level 4 [DM] trait Digimon cards w/different names When this would be played, by placing the specified cards from the trash under it, reduce the play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5003** (2025-09-05) Q: What does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect do, exactly? — A: It allows you to place 1 level 4 or lower [DM] trait Digimon card from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card. Then, you choose and delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same color as any of this Digimon's digivolution cards. If this Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards, you instead choose and delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors. If you will delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors, you must choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon for each of the colors (red, blue, yellow, green, black, purple, and white) and delete it whenever possible.
- **Q5004** (2025-09-05) Q: This Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards. Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to choose 1 of my opponent's 2-color Digimon twice and delete it twice? — A: No, you can't. If you will delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, you must choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon for each of the colors (red, blue, yellow, green, black, purple, and white) and delete it whenever possible. You can't choose 1 Digimon multiple times.
- **Q5005** (2025-09-05) Q: This Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards, and my opponent has a red single-color Digimon and a red/blue 2-color Digimon. Can I choose my opponent's red/blue Digimon for red, but not choose or delete their red single-color Digimon? — A: No, you can't. If you will delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, you must choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon for each of the colors (red, blue, yellow, green, black, purple, and white) and delete it whenever possible. If they have a red single-color Digimon and a red/blue 2-color Digimon, you must reference the different colors and delete 1 of each whenever possible.
