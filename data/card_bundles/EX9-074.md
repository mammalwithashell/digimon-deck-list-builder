## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Rush＞ ＜Security A. +1＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 level 4 or lower [DM] trait Digimon card from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same color as any of this Digimon's digivolution cards. If this Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards, instead delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors. [All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP for each color in its digivolution cards.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -7: 7 level 4 [DM] trait Digimon cards w/different names When this would be played, by placing the specified cards from the trash under it, reduce the play cost.

## Official Q&A
- It allows you to place 1 level 4 or lower [DM] trait Digimon card from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card. Then, you choose and delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same color as any of this Digimon's digivolution cards. If this Digimon has 6 or more colors in its digivolution cards, you instead choose and delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors. If you will delete 1 of each of your opponent's Digimon with different colors, you must choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon for each of the colors (red, blue, yellow, green, black, purple, and white) and delete it whenever possible.
