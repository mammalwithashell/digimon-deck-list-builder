## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] Your other [Gaossmon] all get +3000 DP. [Opponent's Turn] Your opponent can't reduce digivolution costs.

## Official Q&A
- It negates effects that read, "reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by X," like and [BT3-103 Hidden Potential Discovered!]. Even if your opponent activates effects like these, it won't reduce the memory cost of their digivolutions. They have to pay the digivolution cost on the card in order to digivolve. Related Cards
