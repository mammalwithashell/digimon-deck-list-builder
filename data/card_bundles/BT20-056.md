## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4389** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve a Digimon in the breeding area, does that Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect trigger? — A: No, it doesn’t trigger.
- **Q4390** (2025-01-24) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q4724** (2025-06-13) Q: If an opponent's Digimon is attacking and another effect digivolves one of my Digimon into this card, does that meet the "if during an attack" condition in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Yes, it does.
