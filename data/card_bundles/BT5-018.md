### Effect
[When Attacking] You may trash 1 red Digimon card in your hand to add the trashed card's DP to this Digimon for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-018 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1294** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this Digimon, and use its [When Attacking] effect to trash a red Digimon card with 3000 DP in my hand to give this Digimon +3000 DP. If I use an effect to attack again in the same turn and use this card's effect to trash a red Digimon with 1000 DP in my hand, does this Digimon gain a total of +4000 DP? Or does it go from getting +3000 DP to just +1000 DP? — A: The effect persists during the same turn, so the Digimon gains a total of +4000 DP.
