## Printed text (official)
### Effect
If you have a Digimon with [Hybrid] in its traits in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Your level 4 Digimon can digivolve into 1 Digimon card in your hand with matching colors and [Ten Warriors] in its traits for its digivolution cost, ignoring its level.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

### Inherited Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
- If you have a Digimon with [Hybrid] in its traits in your battle area, you can use this Option card even if you don’t have a white Digimon or Tamer in play.
