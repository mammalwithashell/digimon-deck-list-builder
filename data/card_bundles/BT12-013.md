## Printed text (official)
### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your [Takuya Kanbara]s as if that card is a level 3 red Digimon for a digivolution cost of 2. [When Digivolving] This Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait, it gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4652** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose to not digivolve with this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
- **Q6542** (2026-06-19) Q: If an effect digivolves onto a Tamer as if it is a Digimon, do I treat the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: Yes, treat the Tamer as if it is a Digimon when digivolving. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects are triggered. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you cannot digivolve a Tamer as if it is a Digimon with this effect.
- **Q6543** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6544** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card cannot attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q6545** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6546** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6547** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
