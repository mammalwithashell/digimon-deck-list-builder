## Printed text (official)
### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your [Takuya Kanbara]s as if that card is a level 3 red Digimon for a digivolution cost of 2. [When Digivolving] This Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait, it gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
