## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When one of your Digimon with a Tamer card in its digivolution cards digivolves into this card in your hand, reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 2. [When Attacking] If a card with [Hybrid] or [Insectoid] in its traits is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, this Digimon can digivolve into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits in your hand for a memory cost of 3.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-051 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1592** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a card with [Hybrid] or [Insectoid] in its traits in this Digimon's digivolution cards. Does this card’s [When Attacking] effect force me to digivolve this Digimon into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits in my hand? — A: No. The effect reads, “This Digimon can digivolve,” so you can choose not to.
- **Q1593** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving with this card's [When Attacking] effect, paying the digivolution cost moves the memory gauge to 1 or more on my opponent’s side. What happens to the attack? — A: The attack continues. The turn doesn’t switch over just yet. If the memory gauge is still at 1 or more on your opponent’s side after the attack ends and any effects triggered by it finish activating, only then does the turn switch over.
- **Q1594** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with [EX1-035 Kabuterimon] and digivolve into this card with its [When Attacking] effect. Can I then digivolve again on the same attack with this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, it is too late to trigger this card's [When Attacking] effect, since the Digimon didn't have it when the attack was declared. _(related: EX1-035)_
- **Q1595** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving into a Digimon card in my hand with [Insectoid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits with this card's [When Attacking] effect, can I ignore that card's digivolution requirements? — A: No, you can’t ignore its digivolution requirements.
