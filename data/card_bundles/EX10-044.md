## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5126** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] effect to place a card under a Tamer that already has cards under it, in what order do I place the cards? — A: If there are already any cards stacked under the Tamer, you place the new card at the bottom.
- **Q5160** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [All Turns] effect to trash 2 cards including EX10-044 [Damemon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards, then play the trashed EX10-044 [Damemon] from the trash, can I activate EX10-044 [Damemon]'s inherited effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. When a card with an effect that's pending activation leaves its current area while activation is pending, the effect can no longer be activated. In this case, being trashed from digivolution cards will trigger EX10-044 [Damemon]'s inherited effect, but it leaves the trash before activation, therefore it doesn't activate. _(a ruling on EX10-058; this card is named in it)_
