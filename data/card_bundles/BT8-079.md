## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Trash the top 2 cards of your deck. Then, return 1 card with [Demon Lord] in its traits from your trash to your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When a card is trashed from your deck, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-079 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1760** (2024-03-28) Q: Is this card’s inherited effect triggered by the activation of an effect that reads, “Reveal the top X cards of your deck... Trash the remaining cards.”? — A: No, cards revealed from the top of your deck aren't considered to be “trashed from your deck,” so this card’s effect doesn’t trigger.
