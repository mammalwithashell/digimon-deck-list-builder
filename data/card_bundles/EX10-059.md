## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Choose 1 card in your opponent's hand without looking and place it as any of their Digimon's bottom digivolution card or under any of their Tamers. Then, by placing 3 [Bagra Army] trait Digimon cards from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards, delete 1 of their Digimon or Tamers with cards under it. [All Turns] This Digimon gains all [All Turns] effects on all level 6 [Bagra Army] trait Digimon cards in its digivolution cards.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -3: [Bagramon]×[DarkKnightmon] When this would be played, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each one reduces the play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5161** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I place just 2 [Bagra Army] trait Digimon cards from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards for the conditions of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect require you to place the specified number of cards from your trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards.
- **Q5162** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place a card under a Tamer that already has cards under it, in what order do I place the cards? — A: If there are already any cards stacked under the Tamer, you place the new card at the bottom.
- **Q5163** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 1 card from my opponent's hand in the digivolution cards of one of their Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent only has 1 Digimon that is unaffected by effects and no Tamers in their battle area, you can choose a card in their hand, but it can't be placed under that Digimon.
- **Q5164** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place a card from my opponent's hand under one of their Tamers, does that Tamer gain the placed card's inherited effect? — A: No, it doesn't. A Digimon will gain an inherited effect, but a Tamer doesn't gain it.
- **Q5165** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place BT12-038 [GeoGreymon] from my opponent's hand as the bottom digivolution card of their BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] that's treated as a Digimon, does that BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] gain BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect? — A: Yes, it gains the inherited effect. If BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] suspends, BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect will trigger. However, as soon as it is no longer treated as a Digimon, it will lose inherited effects. _(related: BT12-038, BT17-087)_
- **Q5166** (2025-09-05) Q: When this card would be played, I placed EX10-056 [Bagramon] under it for its DigiXros, then I played this card. I then used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 1 card from my opponent's hand as their Digimon's bottom digivolution card. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-056 [Bagramon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect placing a card from my opponent's hand as their Digimon's bottom digivolution card? — A: Yes, it triggers. _(related: EX10-056)_
- **Q5167** (2025-09-05) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 1 card from my opponent's hand as their Digimon's bottom digivolution card, then I placed 3 cards including EX10-056 [Bagramon] from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-056 [Bagramon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect placing a card from my opponent's hand as their Digimon's bottom digivolution card? — A: No, it doesn't trigger. _(related: EX10-056)_
- **Q5168** (2025-09-05) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 3 cards including EX10-058 [Lilithmon] from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards and delete my opponent's Digimon. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-058 [Lilithmon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect deleting my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it triggers. _(related: EX10-058)_
- **Q5169** (2025-09-05) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 3 cards including EX10-055 [Tactimon] from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards and chose my opponent's EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] as the target to delete. This caused the 1st [All Turns] effect on my opponent's EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] to interrupt, and this card was chosen as the target to delete. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-055 [Tactimon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers. If the gained [All Turns] effect from EX10-055 [Tactimon] activates, this card will be prevented from leaving, EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)]'s "by deleting 1 other Digimon" condition will no longer be met, and EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] will be deleted. _(related: EX10-055, EX7-061)_
