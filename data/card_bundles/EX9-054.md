## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. [On Deletion] You may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with [Negamon] in its text from your hand without paying the cost. For every 2 [Negamon] in your trash or your Digimon's digivolution cards, add 1 to this effect's level maximum.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When attack targets change, if this Digimon has [Abbadomon] in its name, it unsuspends.
