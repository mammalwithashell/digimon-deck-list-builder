## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5014** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to give <Collision> and "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks" to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the gained effect won't trigger.
- **Q5015** (2025-09-05) Q: I chose my opponent's BT16-048 [TyrantKabuterimon] for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect. If BT16-048 [TyrantKabuterimon] then attacks and is suspended at the start of the main phase, does its gained <Collision> activate? — A: No, <Collision> doesn't activate. As soon as BT16-048 [TyrantKabuterimon] suspends, its [All Turns] effect prevents it from being affected by your effects, and <Collision> doesn't activate. _(related: BT16-048)_
