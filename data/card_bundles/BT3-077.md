## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1097** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this card in play when I activate the effect on a card like [BT1-090 Gravity Crush], [BT1-021 MetalGreymon], [BT1-040 WereGarurumon], [BT1-058 Chirinmon], or [BT1-075 Digitamamon]. What happens? — A: You can't use Option/Digimon card effects to gain memory, but you can still lose memory if a card effect specifies as such. _(related: BT1-090, BT1-021, BT1-040, BT1-058, BT1-075)_
- **Q1098** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this card in play. Do I gain memory from [ST2-13 Hammer Spark]'s [Security] effect? — A: No, you don't gain any memory from it. _(related: ST2-13)_
