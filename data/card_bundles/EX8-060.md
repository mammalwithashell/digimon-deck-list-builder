## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] You may play 1 [NSo] trait Digimon card with a play cost of 3 or less from your trash without paying the cost. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them have the [NSo] trait, 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card with the [NSo] trait in the hand. Then, that DNA digivolved Digimon may attack.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your other Digimon, this Digimon unsuspends.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3941** (2024-11-22) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger when this card itself is played? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3942** (2024-11-22) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger when a card digivolves into this card itself? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3943** (2024-11-22) Q: This card attacks, and I use its [When Attacking] effect to play an [NSo] trait Digimon. If I then use this card's [Your Turn] effect to DNA digivolve using 2 of my Digimon, can I use that Digimon to attack? — A: No, it can't attack. A new attack declaration can't be made during an attack.
- **Q3944** (2024-11-22) Q: I used this card's [Your Turn] effect to DNA digivolve, then I attacked using that Digimon. At such times, in what order can I activate that Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect and [When Attacking] effect? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
