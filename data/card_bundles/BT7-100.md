## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When using this card from your hand, its memory cost is equal to the number of cards in your security stack. [Main] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn. Then, 1 of your [Rasenmon] gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn. (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card.)

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-100 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1501** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use option cards that can change their cost to 7 on certain conditions such as [BT7-100 Qualialise Blast] or [BT11-108 DG Dimension] with this card’s [On Play] effect? — A: No, you can’t. Since those options are cards that have effects that “reduce the cost by…” or “its memory cost is equal to…” when they would be used, their cost has not actually changed when activating this card’s [On Play] effect, so they can’t be used with this card’s effect. However, since [BT2-099 Glorious Burst] always has the effect of reducing its use cost when used from the hand, if you have 2 yellow Tamers, it can be used with this card’s effect as its use cost will be 7. _(a ruling on BT6-112; this card is named in it)_
- **Q1667** (2024-03-28) Q: My security stack is empty. Can I use this card without paying any memory? — A: Yes. When your security stack is empty, this card’s memory cost is 0.
- **Q1668** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card to have an opponent’s Digimon get -3000 DP, then play a [Rasenmon]. Can I have that [Rasenmon] gain <Security Attack +1>? — A: No. You didn’t have a [Rasenmon] in play when you used this card, so it's too late to give it <Security Attack +1>.
