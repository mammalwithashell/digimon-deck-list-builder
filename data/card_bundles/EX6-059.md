## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3169** (2024-09-20) Q: I tried to delete my opponent's EX6-059 [Barbamon] using this card's [Main] effect. In response, my opponent uses <Scapegoat> on EX6-059 [Barbamon] to delete their other Digimon and prevent EX6-059 [Barbamon] from being deleted. At such times, is this card's "if this effect deleted" condition met? — A: No, it isn't met. The "if this effect deleted" condition is only met when a Digimon is deleted by this card's effect. In this case, EX6-059 [Barbamon] wasn't deleted by this card's effect, therefore the "if this effect deleted" condition isn't met. _(a ruling on BT19-094; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3799** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to play a purple Tamer card from my trash? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3800** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "for each card in your opponent's hand, reduce this effect's play cost maximum by 1" in this card's [All Turns] effect mean, exactly? — A: It means that you reduce the maximum for the play cost of the card that can be played by this effect by 1 for each card in your opponent's hand. For example, if your opponent has 4 cards in their hand, you would reduce 10 by 4, resulting in a maximum play cost of 6, meaning that you can play 1 purple card with a play cost of 6 or less without paying the cost.
