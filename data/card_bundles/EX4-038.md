### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Greymon] in its name and 1 Digimon card with [Gabumon], [Garurumon], or [Omnimon] in its name among them to your hand. Place the rest on top of your deck in any order.
