### Effect
{Hand} [Main] If you have [Shoto Kazama], by placing 1 [Galemon] from your trash as your [Pteromon]'s bottom digivolution card, it digivolves into this card for a digivolution cost of 3, ignoring digivolution requirements. [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, you may play 1 3000 DP or lower green Digimon card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits from your hand without paying the cost. For each suspended Digimon, add 1000 to this effect's DP maximum.
