## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1940** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve a [BT8-013 BetelGammamon] into a [BT8-084 Kimeramon] and place this card as one of [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s digivolution cards with its [When Digivolving] effect. Can I now activate [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect through this card's inherited effect and attack? — A: No, you can't. If this card is added to digivolution cards by [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, you get [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s <Blitz> effect through this card, but the time to trigger <Blitz> has already passed, so <Blitz> doesn't activate. _(related: BT8-084)_
