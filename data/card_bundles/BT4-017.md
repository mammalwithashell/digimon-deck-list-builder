## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-017 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1173** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card is in my hand, deck, or trash and another copy is revealed in a security check, is this Digimon treated as a yellow card? — A: No. When a card is in any area other than your battle area, [Your Turn] effects do not apply. So this card is not treated as a yellow card and is treated only as a red card.
- **Q1174** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card is in the breeding area, can I digivolve a Digimon on top of it that requires a yellow Digimon? — A: No. [Your Turn] effects do not apply in the breeding area, so this card is not treated as a yellow card and is treated only as a red card.
- **Q1175** (2024-09-20) Q: What cards can be played using this card's [Main] effect? — A: 1 red Tamer card with a play cost of 4 or less or 1 yellow Tamer card with a play cost of 4 or less.
