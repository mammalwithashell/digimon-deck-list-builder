## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon or one of your [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait Digimon is played, by trashing 1 card in your hand, return the bottom 3 digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of the deck. Then, if they have as many or fewer total Digimon and Tamers as you, delete 1 of their Digimon with no digivolution cards. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by battle, place 1 [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait card from your trash as the bottom digivolution card of your [Gate of Deadly Sins] in the breeding area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-061 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3803** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's 1st [All Turns] effect trigger when this card itself is played? — A: Yes, it triggers and activates.
- **Q3804** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when [...] would leave the battle area other than by [...] a battle" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area other than by battle" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash other than as a result of a battle, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
- **Q3805** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's 2nd [All Turns] effect to place this Digimon itself as the bottom digivolution card of my [Gate of Deadly Sins] in the breeding area? — A: No, you can't. This Digimon hasn't yet been sent to the trash because this effect activates when this Digimon would leave the battle area, so you can't choose it from the trash as the card to place as the bottom digivolution card.
