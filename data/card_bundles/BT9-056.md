### Effect
[When Attacking] If a card with [Leomon] in its name or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon or Tamers. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an opponent’s Digimon or Tamer becomes suspended, unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
