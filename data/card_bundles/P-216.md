## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Blocker＞ [On Play] You may play 1 [Dark Masters] trait Digimon card from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon this effect played can't digivolve and is deleted at turn end. [On Deletion] You may play 1 face-up [Dark Masters] trait Digimon card from your security stack without paying the cost. At the end of your turn, delete the Digimon this effect played.

### Inherited Effect

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it's deleted upon the deletion timing. If another effect prevents the Digimon played by the [On Play] effect from being deleted by this effect, it's still prevented from digivolving.
