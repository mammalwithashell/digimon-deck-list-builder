## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent's Turn][Once Per Turn] When a card is removed from your security stack, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack. [On Deletion] Your opponent trashes 2 cards in their hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-088 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1235** (2024-03-28) Q: You use this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect to trash a card in your opponent's security stack. Does that card's [Security] effect activate? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q1236** (2024-03-28) Q: Which player chooses the cards to trash from your opponent's hand with this card's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Your opponent chooses the 2 cards to trash from their hand.
- **Q1237** (2024-03-28) Q: While this Digimon is in my battle area, my opponent's Digimon checks my security stack and flips over [BT1-107 Holy Wave]. Its [Security] effect activates, adding a card to my security stack. I ended up with the same number of cards in my security stack, but does this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect still activate? — A: Yes, a card was removed from your security stack, so it activates. _(related: BT1-107)_
- **Q1238** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 2 copies of this Digimon in my battle area when an opponent's Digimon does a security check, removing a card from my security stack. Do the [Opponent's Turn] effects on both copies activate, trashing 2 of my opponent's security cards? — A: Yes, they both activate, trashing 2 of your opponent's security cards.
- **Q1239** (2024-03-28) Q: If my opponent uses a card like [BT2-020 Gallantmon] to trash a card in my security directly, can I activate this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT2-020)_
