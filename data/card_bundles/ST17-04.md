## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 level 3 or lower Digimon. If this effect deleted one of your Digimon, you may play 1 level 3 Digimon card with [Terriermon] or [Lopmon] in its name from your trash without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While this Digimon is suspended, it gets +1000 DP.
