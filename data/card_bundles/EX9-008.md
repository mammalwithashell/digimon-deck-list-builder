## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-008 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4751** (2025-06-13) Q: I use this Digimon with EX9-001 [Koromon] in its digivolution cards to attack, and I use EX9-001 [Koromon]'s inherited effect to digivolve this card. Can I activate <Raid> in this card's inherited effects at such times? — A: No, you can't. <Raid> is an effect that triggers when a Digimon with this effect attacks. This card doesn't have <Raid> when it attacks, therefore <Raid> in its digivolution cards can't activate. _(related: EX9-001)_
