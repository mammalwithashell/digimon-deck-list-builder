## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main][Once Per Turn] By suspending 1 of your green Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with [Vegetation], [Plant], or [Fairy] in one of its traits and 3000 DP or less from your hand without paying its play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an effect suspends one of your Digimon, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-053 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1977** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I suspend this card itself to activate this card's [Main] effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1978** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect activate if 1 of my Digimon attacks with <Blitz> and I suspend it? — A: No, suspending to declare an attack is not the same as being suspended by an effect even when attacking via <Blitz>, so the inherited effect does not activate.
