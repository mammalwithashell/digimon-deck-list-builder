## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with the [Xros Heart] trait to the hand and place 1 such Digimon card under this Tamer. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] When 1 of your Digimon with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer, cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for its DigiXros.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- When you play a Digimon card with DigiXros requirements, normally you can only DigiXros with Digimon from your hand or battle area. However, this effect also allows you to DigiXros with Digimon cards placed under a Tamer.
