## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with the [Xros Heart] trait to the hand and place 1 such Digimon card under this Tamer. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] When 1 of your Digimon with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer, cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for its DigiXros.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-087 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2011** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "by suspending this Tamer, you may place cards from under your Tamers as digivolution cards for a DigiXros" effect do, exactly? — A: When you play a Digimon card with DigiXros requirements, normally you can only DigiXros with Digimon from your hand or battle area. However, this effect also allows you to DigiXros with Digimon cards placed under a Tamer.
- **Q2012** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect also allow me to DigiXros with cards placed under a Tamer other than this Tamer? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q2013** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect also allow me to DigiXros using the digivolution cards of a Digimon that digivolved from a Tamer? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q2014** (2024-03-28) Q: If I play 2 or more Digimon cards with DigiXros requirements at the same time, can I activate this card's effect? — A: No, this card's effect reads "when you would play 1 Digimon with DigiXros requirements", so if you play 2 or more Digimon cards at the same time, the effect cannot be activated.
- **Q2015** (2024-03-28) Q: If I play 1 Digimon card with DigiXros requirements and 1 card without DigiXros requirements at the same time, can I activate this card's effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's effect can be activated when you would play 1 Digimon card with DigiXros requirements only.
