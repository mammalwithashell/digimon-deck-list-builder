## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1754** (2025-09-05) Q: What does "players can't reduce play costs" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents costs from being reduced when play costs are to be paid. This effect affects both players.
- **Q1755** (2025-09-05) Q: If a "players can't reduce play costs" effect has activated, can a card be played using a "play without paying the cost" effect? — A: Yes, it can be played. "Play this card without paying the cost" effects aren't effects that reduce the play cost.
- **Q1756** (2025-09-05) Q: If a "players can't reduce play costs" effect has activated, can a play cost be reduced using an effect on a Digimon in the breeding area? — A: No, play costs can't be reduced. A "players can't reduce play costs" effect isn't an effect that affects cards that activate play-cost-reducing effects, it's an effect that affects players. Therefore, the target players can't reduce play costs.
- **Q1757** (2025-09-05) Q: If a "players can't reduce play costs" effect has activated, is DigiXrosing possible? — A: Yes, DigiXrosing is possible. However, it isn't possible to reduce play costs through DigiXrosing. Therefore, a player can't declare to play a card in a state where its play cost can't be paid.
- **Q3283** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has [BT8-071 Psychemon] in play. Can this Digimon’s [Your Turn] effect still reduce the play cost of cards with [D-Reaper] in their traits when I would play them? — A: No. [BT8-071 Psychemon]’s effect doesn’t target Digimon themselves, so you can’t activate this card's effect to reduce the play cost of cards.
- **Q4442** (2025-02-28) Q: The memory is at 0, and my opponent has 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon] in the battle area. Can I declare to play this card, then suspend my opponent's 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon]? — A: No, you can't. You can't pay the cost because you can't use BT8-071 [Psychemon]'s effect to reduce it, therefore you can't declare to play this card in the first place.
- **Q4443** (2025-02-28) Q: The memory is at 1 or more, and my opponent has 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon] in the battle area. Can I declare to play this card, then suspend my opponent's 2 Digimon including BT8-071 [Psychemon]? — A: Yes, you can. You can't use BT8-071 [Psychemon]'s effect to reduce the play cost, but the original play cost can be paid, therefore you can declare to play this card. After declaring to play it, you can use the 1st effect to suspend 2 Digimon, but you can't reduce the play cost, therefore you must pay the original play cost of 11 in order to play this card.
