## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 green or blue card with [Dramon] in its name and 1 card with [Examon] in its name among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon with [Dramon] or [Examon] in its name becomes suspended, this Digimon gets +1000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3413** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 4 cards. If the cards revealed only include a green or blue card with [Dramon] in its name or a card with [Examon] in its name, can I still add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, as long as either a green or blue card with [Dramon] in its name or a card with [Examon] in its name is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
- **Q3414** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's effect reveals 4 cards. If both a green or blue card with [Dramon] in its name and a card with [Examon] in its name are among the cards revealed, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining card at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. This card's effect reads “Add [...] to your hand,” so you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
