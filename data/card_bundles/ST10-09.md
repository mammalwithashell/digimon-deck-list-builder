## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST10-09 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q741** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I play this Digimon with its [Security] effect even if it loses the battle against the attacking Digimon? — A: Yes, regardless of the battle's outcome, you get to play this Digimon at the end of the battle.
- **Q742** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [On Play] effect activate when played with this card’s [Security] effect? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q743** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is flipped over during a security check, but the attacking Digimon still has security checks remaining. Does this Digimon’s effect activate first, or do I have to wait until my opponent finishes the rest of their security checks? — A: This Digimon is played after the battle with the attacking Digimon, and before the next check is performed. Likewise, this Digimon’s [On Play] effect activates before your opponent’s next check.
