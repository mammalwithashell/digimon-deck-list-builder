## Printed text (official)
### Effect
If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Delete all Digimon with play costs of 7 or less.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-105 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1489** (2024-03-28) Q: "If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements.” How does this effect work, exactly? — A: If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play in your battle area, you can use this Option card even if you don’t have a black Digimon or Tamer in play.
- **Q1490** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s effect also delete all of my Digimon with play costs of 7 or less? — A: Yes. Both your and your opponent delete all Digimon with play costs of 7 or less.
