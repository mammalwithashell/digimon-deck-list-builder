## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST20-11 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4457** (2025-04-04) Q: What does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect do, exactly? — A: You choose 1 of your Digimon for every 2 colors your Tamers have. The target Digimon aren't affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects until their turn ends.
- **Q4458** (2025-04-04) Q: What does "none of your [...] Digimon are affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digmon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP."
- **Q4459** (2025-04-04) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen. For example, a Digmon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect.
- **Q4460** (2025-04-04) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q4461** (2025-04-04) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q4462** (2025-04-04) Q: If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q4696** (2025-06-13) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
