## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits and 1 card with the [Vortex Warriors] or [LIBERATOR] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] This Digimon gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST18-04 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q839** (2024-04-19) Q: If the 3 cards revealed for this card's [On Play] effect include only either 1 card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits or 1 card with the [Vortex Warriors] or [LIBERATOR] trait, can I just add that 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can. Even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q840** (2024-04-19) Q: If the 3 cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include both a card with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits and a card with the [Vortex Warriors] or [LIBERATOR] trait, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No, you can't. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
