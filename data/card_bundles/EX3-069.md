## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] ＜Draw 1＞. Then, place this card in your battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (By trashing this card in your battle area, activate the effect below. You can't activate this effect the turn this card enters play.) ・Play 1 Digimon card with [Four Great Dragons] in its traits from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon played by this effect can't digivolve to level 7, and at the end of your opponent's turn, delete that Digimon.

[Security] Place this card in its owner's battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2613** (2024-03-28) Q: This card digivolved from [EX3-035 Goldramon]. This Digimon gains all effects of [Goldramon] in digivolution cards, but after [EX3-035 Goldramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect activates and I return [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from the trash to the hand, can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from my hand? — A: Yes, you can. If this card's [All Turns] effect is used to gain a [When Digivolving] effect, this card's [When Digivolving] effect and the [When Digivolving] effect gained from the [All Turns] effect will trigger simultaneously upon the digivolution timing, therefore you may choose the activation order. _(a ruling on BT16-014; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3402** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [On Deletion] effect to place a [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from my hand in the battle area. Does [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons]'s "[Main] <Draw 1>. Then, place this card in your battle area." effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. This card's [On Deletion] effect only places a [Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from your hand in the battle area when you don't already have a [Trial of the Four Great Dragons] placed. _(a ruling on EX3-025; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3433** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's effects read "The Digimon played by this effect can't digivolve to level 7, and at the end of your opponent's turn, delete that Digimon." Do I delete that Digimon even if digivolution cards are added by an effect and then it becomes another Digimon due to an effect such as <De-Digivolve>? — A: Yes, it is still a Digimon that was played by this effect, so you delete it at the end of your opponent's turn.
- **Q3434** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's effect to play a Digimon, can I DNA digivolve that Digimon into a level 7 Digimon? — A: No, if the Digimon to digivolve into is level 7, DNA digivolving is not possible either.
- **Q5722** (2025-12-25) Q: Do I delete the Digimon that was played by this card's <Delay> effect at the end of my opponent's turn? And if it isn't deleted, is it still prevented from digivolving to level 7? — A: No, you only delete it at the end of your opponent's first turn after it's played by this effect. If another effect prevents it from being deleted at the end of your opponent's first turn when it would be deleted, that Digimon isn't deleted at the end of your opponent's following turns. If it isn't deleted by another effect when it would be deleted by this effect, it's still prevented from digivolving to level 7.
- **Q5723** (2025-12-25) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's <Delay> effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously. Therefore, the turn player can choose the processing order.
