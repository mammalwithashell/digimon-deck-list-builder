## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait Digimon, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] 1 of your Digimon or Tamers may digivolve into [Susanoomon] in the hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, by placing up to 4 of your Tamers with different colors as 1 of your [Susanoomon]'s bottom digivolution cards, gain 1 memory for each placed card.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 Tamer card with inherited effects from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.
