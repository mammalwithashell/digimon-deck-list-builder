## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q875** (2024-03-28) Q: How long does the 2000 DP boost from this card's inherited effect last? — A: It lasts until the end of the turn you activated it.
- **Q876** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card's inherited effect to boost its DP. Later, this digivolution card is trashed. Does the DP boost go away? — A: Yes, the +2000 DP boost ends.
- **Q877** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect activate when attacking an opponent's Digimon? — A: No. If your opponent doesn't block, the effect doesn't activate. Regardless of what the target of attack is, the effect activates when a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards attacks an opponent's Digimon and has its attack target changed to a blocking opponent's Digimon.
