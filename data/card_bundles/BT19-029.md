## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] By trashing your top security card, gain 1 memory.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this yellow Digimon with the [Data] or [Witchelny] trait would leave the battle area by your opponent's effects, by trashing your top security card, it doesn't leave.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-029 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3087** (2024-09-20) Q: What cards will trigger this card's inherited effect when they would leave the battle area? — A: A yellow Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and the [Data] trait or a yellow Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and the [Witchelny] trait.
- **Q3095** (2024-09-20) Q: I have this card with BT19-029 [Tapirmon] in digivolution cards in the battle area. When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an opponent's effect, can I first activate this card's [All Turns] effect and gain <Recovery +1 (Deck)>, then use BT19-029 [Tapirmon]'s inherited effect to trash a security card? — A: Yes, you can. This card's [All Turns] effect and BT19-029 [Tapirmon]'s inherited effect will trigger simultaneously. The player can choose the activation order of these effects.
