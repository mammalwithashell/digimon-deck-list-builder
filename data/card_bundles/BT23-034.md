## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if you have a Tamer with the [Zaxon] trait, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Until your opponent's turn ends, 1 of their Digimon can't activate [When Digivolving] effects and gets -6000 DP. [On Deletion] Place this card face up as the bottom security card.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents [When Digivolving] effects on cards from activating. [When Digivolving] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
