## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Give 1 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -2＞ until their turn ends. Then, if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards, this Digimon may digivolve into [PlatinumNumemon] in the hand for a digivolution cost of 4, ignoring digivolution requirements.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon card with the [CS] trait, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.

## Official Q&A
- It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
