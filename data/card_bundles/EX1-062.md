## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3247** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack my opponent with this Digimon and flip over [BT2-105 Spider Shooter] during the security check. The <De-Digivolve> trashes this card. Does this card’s [End of Attack] effect activate? — A: No. This card is no longer in play at the end of the attack, so the [End of Attack] effect doesn't activate. _(related: BT2-105)_
