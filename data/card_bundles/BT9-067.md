### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Place 1 [Raijinmon], 1 [Fujinmon], and 1 [Suijinmon] from your trash under this Digimon in any order as its bottom digivolution cards. Gain 1 memory for each card placed. [When Attacking] If the level 6 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards have 3 or more colors among them, this Digimon gets +3000 DP until the end of your opponent's turn. ## Official Q&A

- **Q1854** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card’s [On Play][When Digivolving] effect if I don't have exactly 1 [Raijinmon], 1 [Fuijinmon], and 1 [Suijinmon] in my trash? — A: Yes. If there are 4 or more colors present, this card’s [When Attacking] effect grants both +3000 DP to this Digimon and <De-Digivolves 1> 1 of your opponent’s Digimon.
