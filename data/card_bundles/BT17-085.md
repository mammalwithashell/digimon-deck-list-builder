## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Main] By placing this Tamer and 1 [Kyubimon] and 1 [Taomon] in your trash as 1 of your [Renamon]'s bottom digivolution cards, that Digimon may digivolve into [Sakuyamon] in the hand for a digivolution cost of 4, ignoring digivolution requirements. If this effect digivolved, you may return 1 Option card from your trash to the hand.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-085 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2867** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place this Tamer and 1 [Kyubimon] and 1 [Taomon] from my trash as my [Renamon]'s bottom digivolution cards, but then choose to not digivolve into [Sakuyamon]? — A: Yes, you can. The effect reads "may digivolve," so you can choose whether or not to digivolve.
- **Q2868** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve 1 of my [Renamon] into EX4-030 [Kuzuhamon]? — A: No, you can't. EX4-030 [Kuzuhamon] is treated as if it has [Sakuyamon] in its name. This effect doesn't treat it as if it has the name [Sakuyamon], therefore digivolution isn't possible. _(related: EX4-030)_
