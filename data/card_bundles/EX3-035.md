## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may return 1 card with [Four Great Dragons] in its traits from your trash to your hand. [When Attacking] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn. Then, by returning 1 [Magnadramon], 1 [Azulongmon], and 1 [Megidramon] from your trash to the bottom of your deck in any order, trash the top 2 cards of your opponent's security stack.

- **Q2613** (2024-03-28) Q: This card digivolved from [EX3-035 Goldramon]. This Digimon gains all effects of [Goldramon] in digivolution cards, but after [EX3-035 Goldramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect activates and I return [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from the trash to the hand, can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to use [EX3-069 Trial of the Four Great Dragons] from my hand? — A: Yes, you can. If this card's [All Turns] effect is used to gain a [When Digivolving] effect, this card's [When Digivolving] effect and the [When Digivolving] effect gained from the [All Turns] effect will trigger simultaneously upon the digivolution timing, therefore you may choose the activation order. _(a ruling on BT16-014; this card is named in it)_
