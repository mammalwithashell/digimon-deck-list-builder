## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if you have a Digimon with [Knightmon] or [Lucemon] in its name, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, their Digimon's effects don't affect 1 of your Digimon. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your Digimon with [Knightmon] or [Lucemon] in their texts are played, ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

### Security Effect
[Security] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 3 or less.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
