## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-055 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3937** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash a total of 3 digivolution cards from my multiple different Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3938** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I trash just 2 digivolution cards for the conditions of this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by doing X" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q3939** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to place Digi-Egg cards with the [Mineral] or [Rock] trait from my trash as digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3940** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I activate this card's [End of Your Turn] effect but choose to not to place cards with the [Mineral] or [Rock] trait from my trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If this card's [End of Your Turn] effect activates, you must place at least 1 card in digivolution cards. However, if you choose to not activate this effect, it won't activate and you don't place any cards in digivolution cards.
