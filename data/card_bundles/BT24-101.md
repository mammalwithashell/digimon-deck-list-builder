## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT24-101 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5714** (2025-12-25) Q: I have 0 security cards. What is the digivolution cost if I use this card's 3rd digivolution requirement to digivolve from a level 5 card with [Aegiochusmon] in its name? — A: It can digivolve for digivolution cost of 0.
- **Q5715** (2025-12-25) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] to reduce the DP of 1 of my opponent's Digimon even when I have 0 security cards and therefore can't trash one? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5716** (2026-05-08) Q: This card's effect caused the DP of my opponent's Digimon to become 0. At such times, is the Digimon with a DP of zero deleted? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for the activated effect has resolved, a rule check will occur, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
- **Q5717** (2025-12-25) Q: In what order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q5718** (2025-12-25) Q: If I activate this card's 2nd [All Turns] effect when multiples of my Digimon or Tamers with the [TS] trait would leave the battle area at the same time, are all of those Digimon and Tamers prevented from leaving? — A: Yes, all of those Digimon are prevented from leaving. This card's [All Turns] effect affects all Digimon without having to choose them.
