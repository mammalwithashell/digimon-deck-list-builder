## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-094 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3167** (2024-09-20) Q: If my opponent doesn't trash a security card for this card's [Your Turn] effect, which player performs <Recovery +1 (Deck)>? — A: You perform <Recovery +1 (Deck)>.
- **Q3168** (2024-09-20) Q: I have 0 security cards. If I activate this card's [Main] effect, do I delete my opponent's Digimon until they have 0 Digimon? — A: Yes, you delete their Digimon until they have 0 left.
- **Q3169** (2024-09-20) Q: I tried to delete my opponent's EX6-059 [Barbamon] using this card's [Main] effect. In response, my opponent uses <Scapegoat> on EX6-059 [Barbamon] to delete their other Digimon and prevent EX6-059 [Barbamon] from being deleted. At such times, is this card's "if this effect deleted" condition met? — A: No, it isn't met. The "if this effect deleted" condition is only met when a Digimon is deleted by this card's effect. In this case, EX6-059 [Barbamon] wasn't deleted by this card's effect, therefore the "if this effect deleted" condition isn't met. _(related: EX6-059)_
- **Q5551** (2025-12-25) Q: What is a {Trash} effect? — A: A {Trash} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the trash. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the trash.
- **Q5552** (2025-12-25) Q: When one of my Digimon digivolves into [Lucemon (X Antibody)], in what order can I activate this card's {Trash} [Your Turn] effect and the effects that trigger upon digivolution into that Digimon? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
