## Printed text (official)
### Effect
This card/Digimon is also treated as having [Leomon] in its name. [When Digivolving] You may play 1 green or blue Tamer card with a play cost of 4 or less from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play another Digimon by an effect, 1 of your Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn. (This Digimon may attack the turn it was played.)

## Official Q&A
- This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also includes the [Leomon] name. This card can be chosen for text that specifies cards with [Leomon] in their names. This card can't be chosen for text that specifies cards with the name [Leomon].
