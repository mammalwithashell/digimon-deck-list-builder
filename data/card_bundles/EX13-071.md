## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] You may place your deck's top card face down under this Tamer. Then, if your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Main] [Once Per Turn] By trashing 3 bottom face-down cards from under any of your Tamers and placing 1 each of level 4 and level 5 [Holy Beast] trait yellow Digimon cards from your trash as 1 of your [Kudamon]'s bottom digivolution cards, it may digivolve into [Kentaurosmon] in the hand or trash, ignoring level and with the cost reduced by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7442** (2026-09-18) Q: If I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q7443** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7444** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q7445** (2026-09-18) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
- **Q7446** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I trash only 2 specified cards or place only 1 of the specified cards as a digivolution card for the conditions of this card's [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't. All of the conditions within "by doing X" must be fulfilled. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to trash 3 specified cards and place 1 each of the specified cards as digivolution cards.
- **Q7447** (2026-09-18) Q: My Tamer currently has three or more face-down cards under it, and my trash is empty. Can I activate this card's [Main] effect? — A: No. You can't activate this card's [Main] effect unless you have three or more face-down cards under one of your Tamers and one of each specified card in your trash. However, if you have all of those cards, you may activate this card's [Main] effect and place the cards trashed from under your Tamer under your [Kudamon].
- **Q7448** (2026-09-18) Q: If a Digimon such as [BT5-021 Syakomon] with a "players can't reduce play costs" effect is in the battle area, can I activate this card's [Main] effect and digivolve 1 of my Digimon into a specified card? — A: You can activate the effect and digivolve, but its cost won't be reduced. _(related: BT5-021)_
- **Q7449** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve the specified card into a [Kentaurosmon] that doesn't meet the digivolution requirements, ignoring level? — A: No, you can't. You can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements, with its Lv. being the only exception.
- **Q7450** (2026-09-18) Q: If a text says "XX card with AA or BB," does that mean AA and BB both have to be XX? — A: Yes, both have to be XX. For example, "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait or that is yellow or black" means either "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait" or "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card that is yellow or black." In both cases, the card must be level 4 or lower.
