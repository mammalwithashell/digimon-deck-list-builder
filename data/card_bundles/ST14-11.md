## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with the [Evil], [Wizard], or [Demon Lord] trait among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon digivolves into a purple Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, return 1 card from your hand on top of your deck and gain 1 memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can activate this effect. "return 1 card from your hand on top of your deck" is not a "by , " effect ("by returning..."), so you gain 1 memory even when you can't perform that action. However, if you do have cards in your hand, you must return 1 card on top of the deck.
