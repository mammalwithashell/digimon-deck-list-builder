## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP. If no Digimon is deleted by this effect, unsuspend this Digimon. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an opponent’s Digimon is deleted, if [Gallantmon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.
