## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5051** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I also activate this card's link effect by trashing this card itself when it's a link card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5052** (2025-09-05) Q: I used <Link +X> and have a Digimon that has multiple link cards including this card. Can I use this card's Link effect to trash one of that Digimon's link cards other than this card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5053** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5054** (2025-09-05) Q: I chose a target to suspend using this card's [All Turns] effect. At such times, if the target can't suspend, does that mean it can't gain "can't unsuspend"? — A: No, even if this effect didn't suspend the target, it can still give it "can't unsuspend."
- **Q5055** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to give "can't unsuspend" to an opponent's Digimon or Tamer without choosing a target to suspend? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5056** (2025-09-05) Q: If this card's [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] effect didn't suspend an opponent's Digimon or Tamer, does that mean it doesn't count toward the [Once Per Turn] limit? — A: No, it counts toward a use of [Once Per Turn]. Even if it didn't suspend an opponent's Digimon or Tamer, the "can't unsuspend" part of the effect is mandatory, therefore it counts toward the [Once Per Turn] limit.
