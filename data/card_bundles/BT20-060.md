## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4398** (2025-01-24) Q: A Digimon's DP became 0 from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect. At such times, is that Digimon deleted before the rest of this effect's processing is resolved? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
- **Q4399** (2025-01-24) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q4726** (2025-06-13) Q: I have this card and BT20-060 [Alphamon: Ouryuken ACE] in the battle area. I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to give <Rush> to this card itself, then I use this card to attack a player. If the attack causes a security check on my opponent, BT20-060 [Alphamon: Ouryuken ACE]'s effect gives me +3 memory, and the turn doesn't end, does this card still have <Rush>? — A: Yes, it still has it. The text reads "for the turn," so it will last until the turn ends and switches to your opponent's turn. _(a ruling on BT20-102; this card is named in it)_
