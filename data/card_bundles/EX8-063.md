## Official Q&A
- No, it counts toward a use of [Once Per Turn]. The "your opponent may trash 1 card in their hand" effect allows your opponent to choose whether or not they will discard a card in their hand. The effect will be considered to have activated when they choose whether or not to discard it, therefore it counts as a use of [Once Per Turn].
