## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. If [Leon Alexander] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, all of your opponent's Digimon gain ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of their turn. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] If you have 3 or more security cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn. If you have 3 or fewer, ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞. Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack an opponent's Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When a card is removed from your security stack, if this Digimon has [Fenriloogamon] in its name, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -8000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-040 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2792** (2024-03-28) Q: This Digimon has [Leon Alexander] in its digivolution cards, and I activated its [When Digivolving] effect. Can it give <Security A. -1> to my opponent's Digimon that are played into the battle area afterward? — A: Yes, it can. This card's [When Digivolving] effect affects "all of your opponent's Digimon," therefore it will also apply to opponent's Digimon that are newly played until the end of their turn.
- **Q2793** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have exactly 3 cards in my security stack, do I activate both the "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP" effect and <Recovery +1 (Deck)> when activating this card's [End of Your Turn] effect? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q2794** (2024-03-28) Q: When I have 3 security cards, can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to gain <Recovery +1 (Deck)> and then use 1 of my Digimon to attack an opponent's Digimon if my security stack increases to 4 cards? — A: Yes, you can. Once the [End of Your Turn] effect activates, you can perform the part of the effect after "then" regardless of the number of security cards.
