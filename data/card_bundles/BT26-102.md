## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Use Req. ([Seven Code] trait)＞ (Specified cards let you ignore color requirements.) [Main] By placing 6 [Seven Code] trait Digimon cards from your battle area, link cards or trash as 1 of your [Seven Code] trait Digimon's bottom digivolution cards, that Digimon may digivolve into [Dantemon] in the hand, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower [Appmon] trait card from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7127** (2026-08-18) Q: What happens when I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect and use a [BT26-102 Seven Code PAD] that was revealed from the deck? — A: Place the specified cards from your battle area, link cards or trash under a specified Digimon with the used [BT26-102 Seven Code PAD]'s [Main] effect and digivolve that Digimon into [Dantemon]. Then, perform a digivolution bonus draw from the unrevealed deck. Afterwards, trash [BT26-102 Seven Code PAD] and perform the "return the remaining cards to the deck" action.
- **Q7128** (2026-08-18) Q: A [Seven Code] trait card gets linked to this card, and this card's [Your Turn] effect and the linked card's [When Linking] effect trigger simultaneously. If I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect first and use [BT26-102 Seven Code PAD]'s [Main] effect, can I activate the [When Linking] effect on the card linked to this card after placing it as a digivolution card. — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7183** (2026-08-18) Q: From what areas can I place the specified cards to meet the conditions of this card's [Main] effect? — A: You can place them from your Digimon in the battle area, your Digimon's link cards, or cards in your trash. Additionally, you can choose a total of 6 cards from any or all of these areas. For example, 1 from your Digimon in the battle area, 2 from link cards, and 3 from your trash.
- **Q7184** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I only place 5 specified cards as digivolution cards to meet the conditions of this card's [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to place 6 of the specified cards as digivolution cards.
- **Q7185** (2026-08-18) Q: When placing multiple cards into the place specified by this card's effect, can I choose what order to place them in? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q7186** (2026-08-18) Q: After processing "by doing x" in this card's [Main] effect, can I choose not to digivolve? — A: Yes.
