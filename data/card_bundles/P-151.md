## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [LIBERATOR] trait, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [LIBERATOR] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, you may play 1 card with the [LIBERATOR] trait and a play cost of 3 or less from your hand without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effects.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. You can activate the part of the effect after "then" regardless of whether or not you could add a card with the [LIBERATOR] trait to your hand.
