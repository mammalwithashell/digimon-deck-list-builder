## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-026 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3892** (2024-11-22) Q: What position on the memory gauge does "if you have 1 or more memory" refer to? — A: It refers to when the memory gauge is at 1 or further to the left on your side.
- **Q3893** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card in the battle area. An opponent's Digimon digivolved into a Digimon card that has <Blitz>, and I now have 1 or more memory. Can that Digimon use <Blitz> to attack at such times? — A: No, it can't attack. This card's [All Turns] effect will activate as soon as you have 1 or more memory, therefore none of your opponent's Digimon can suspend, and they can't attack unless they can suspend.
