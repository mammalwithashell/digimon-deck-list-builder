## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-007 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1402** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 2 copies of this Digimon in my battle area. If I play a Tamer with [Tai Kamiya] in its name, can I activate the effects on both Digimon to gain a total of 2 memory? — A: Yes. The effects on both Digimon activate, so you gain 2 memory.
- **Q1403** (2024-03-28) Q: I play a Tamer with [Tai Kamiya] in its name from my security using its security effect. If this Digimon’s effect causes the memory gauge to move to 1 or greater on my side, does it immediately become my turn? — A: No. The turn doesn’t switch over until the attack completely finishes resolving.
