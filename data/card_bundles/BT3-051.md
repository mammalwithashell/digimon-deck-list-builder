### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 level 5 and 1 level 6 Digimon card among them to your hand. Trash the remaining cards.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-051 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1085** (2024-03-28) Q: The cards I reveal with this effect include only a level 5 or level 6 digimon, not both. Can I add that card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q2827** (2024-03-28) Q: If I reveal 2 copies of this card using BT3-051 [Dokugumon]'s [On Play] effect, can I add both copies to my hand? — A: Yes, you can. The effect reads "also treated as level 6," therefore this card can be treated as both level 5 and level 6, so you can add 2 copies to your hand. _(a ruling on BT17-068; this card is named in it)_
