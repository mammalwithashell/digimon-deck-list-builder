## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if there are 12 or more levels' total worth of Digimon, reduce the cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 [TS] trait Digimon card with 6000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any Digimon are played or digivolve, you may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, if played or digivolved by an effect, delete 1 of your opponent's lowest DP Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6375** (2026-05-08) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when this card itself is played or when one of my Digimon in the battle area would digivolve into this card? — A: Yes, it triggers. In addition, the "if played or digivolved by an effect" condition will be met if it was played or digivolved by an effect.
- **Q6376** (2026-05-08) Q: Only suspended Digimon are in the battle area. Can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when a Digimon is played or digivolves? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6377** (2026-05-08) Q: I activated this card's [All Turns] effect when a method other than an effect played or digivolved a Digimon. Can I then activate this card's [All Turns] effect again when an effect plays or digivolves a Digimon? — A: No, you can't. This effect's [Once Per Turn] has already been used upon the 1st activation, therefore it can't activate again.
- **Q6378** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I choose to not activate this card's [All Turns] effect when an effect plays or digivolves a Digimon? — A: No, you can't. If a Digimon is played or digivolved by an effect, you must delete an opponent's Digimon.
- **Q6946** (2026-08-18) Q: If I don't activate this card's [All Turns] effect when a Digimon is played or digivolved by some method other than an effect, can I activate it later when another Digimon is played or digivolved? — A: Yes. If the Digimon isn't played or digivolved by an effect and you don't process the "you may suspend" effect to use the [Once Per Turn] limit, it triggers the next time a Digimon is played or digivolved. If the Digimon is played or digivolved by an effect, the condition "if played or digivolved by an effect" is met even if you don't process the "you may suspend" effect, requiring the delete effect to be processed which uses the [Once Per Turn] limit.
- **Q7002** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play [BT25-077 Bacchusmon], can I activate [BT25-077 Bacchusmon]'s 1st effect and reduce the play cost by a total of 10? — A: Yes, the play cost will be reduced by a total of 10.
