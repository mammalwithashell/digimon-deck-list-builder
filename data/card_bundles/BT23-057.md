### Effect
When this card would be played, by returning 3 cards with [Huckmon], [Sistermon] or [Jesmon] in their names from your trash to the top or bottom of the deck, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 [Hinukamuy] Token. (Digimon/White/6000 DP/＜Alliance＞＜Reboot＞＜Blocker＞) Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 6 or less.
