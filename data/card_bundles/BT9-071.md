## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in one of its traits to the hand and trash 1 such card. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits from your trash for its digivolution cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1862** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s [On Play] effect reveals cards from the top of my deck. Can I only trash a card that has [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits? — A: Yes. Both the card you add to hand and the card you trash must have [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in their traits.
- **Q1863** (2026-02-06) Q: This card’s [On Play] effect reveals cards from the top of my deck. If only 1 card has [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits, can I choose whether to add it to my hand or trash it? — A: No. For this card's effect you reveal cards, add 1 card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits among them to your hand, then trash 1 card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits among them. Therefore, if there is only 1 card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits among the revealed cards, it's first added to the hand, so you can't trash it.
- **Q1864** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have cards in my trash with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in their traits, does this card's inherited effect let me digivolve any card into any of them, regardless of digivolution requirements? — A: No. This effect can’t ignore digivolution requirements. When activating this effect, you can only digivolve into a card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits if it also meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q1865** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card’s inherited effect to digivolve into a card with a [When Attacking] effect. Does that effect activate immediately? — A: No. The [When Attacking] effect wasn't present when you declared the attack, so it doesn't activate.
