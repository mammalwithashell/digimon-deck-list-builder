## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3399** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [Start of Opponent's Main Phase] to suspend my [EX3-044 Breakdramon] or [EX3-074 Examon] and had my opponent attack. At such times, how do I process effects such as "when this Digimon is suspended" effects on these Digimon and [When Attacking] effects on the attacking Digimon? — A: When this Digimon is suspended effects and the [When Attacking] effect are triggered by a single effect, therefore they are considered to trigger simultaneously. According to the simultaneous trigger rules, first the turn player's [When Attacking] effects will activate, then the non-turn player's "when this Digimon is suspended" effects will activate. _(a ruling on EX3-024; this card is named in it)_
