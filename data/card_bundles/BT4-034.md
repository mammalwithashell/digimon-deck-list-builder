## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-034 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1201** (2024-03-28) Q: When this Digimon attacked, my opponent didn't have any Digimon with digivolution cards, and I wasn't able to use the [When Attacking] effect to trash digivolution cards. Do I still get to trigger <Draw 1> and gain 1 memory? — A: No. If you didn't trash any digivolution cards with the [When Attacking] effect, you don't get to trigger <Draw 1> or gain 1 memory.
