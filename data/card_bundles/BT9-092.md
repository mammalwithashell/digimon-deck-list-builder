[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with the [X Antibody] trait and 1 such Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] When any of your Digimon digivolve into a same-level Digimon with the [X Antibody] trait, by suspending this Tamer, ＜Draw 1＞ and gain 1 memory.
