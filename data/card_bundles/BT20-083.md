## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4409** (2025-01-24) Q: What is a [Breeding] effect? — A: A [Breeding] effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the breeding area. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the breeding area.
- **Q4410** (2025-01-24) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q4411** (2025-01-24) Q: I have a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards in my breeding area. Can I use this card's inherited effect to suspend that Digimon and play this card itself from digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
