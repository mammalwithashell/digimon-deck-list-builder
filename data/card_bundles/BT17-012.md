### Effect
This card may digivolve from the hand onto one of your red Tamers as if that card is a level 3 red Digimon. ＜Raid＞ [When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Hybrid] trait in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2731** (2024-09-20) Q: I have BT17-012 [BurningGreymon] with BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto] in its digivolution cards. At the end of the turn, I use BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto]'s inherited effect to have BT17-012 [BurningGreymon] attack, and I use BT17-012 [BurningGreymon]'s [When Attacking] effect to digivolve into this card. If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve into [AncientGreymon] at such times, the end of the turn timing has already passed, but does this card's effect delete that [AncientGreymon]? — A: No, it won't be deleted. Effects that are processed at specific timings will be processed when that timing arrives, similar to triggered effects. If this card's effect is used to digivolve at the end of the turn, the end of the turn timing has already passed, therefore this card's "delete this Digimon at the end of the turn" isn't processed. _(a ruling on BT17-011; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2732** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a "may digivolve this card as if the Tamer is a Digimon" effect also treat the Tamer that will become a digivolution card as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: Yes, it also treats the Tamer as a Digimon when it digivolves. It treats it as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, digivolution won't be possible using this effect that also treats the Tamer as a Digimon.
- **Q2733** (2026-06-19) Q: When using the [Takuya Kanbara]: Cost 2 digivolution requirement to digivolve, can I use a "may digivolve this card as if the Tamer is a Digimon" effect to treat that [Takuya Kanbara] as a Digimon for digivolving? — A: Yes, you can. If you do, it's treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects will trigger. In addition, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates, it won't be possible to use this effect to treat that Tamer as a Digimon.
- **Q2734** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q2735** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played. _(related: EX3-053)_
- **Q2736** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card. _(related: P-103)_
- **Q2737** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Hybrid] trait in the hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's [When Attacking] effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q2738** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card attacks, does its <Raid> or [When Attacking] effect activate first? — A: They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player chooses the effect to activate first. However, if you activate the [When Attacking] effect first and digivolve, <Raid> can no longer be activated.
- **Q4658** (2025-04-04) Q: Can I choose to not digivolve with this card's 1st effect? — A: No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
- **Q6556** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6557** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
