## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [D-Brigade] or [DigiPolice] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the top or bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects, by deleting 1 of your other Digimon with the [D-Brigade] trait, prevent it from leaving.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2425** (2024-03-28) Q: If one of the cards revealed from my deck by this card's [On Play] effect is a Tamer card or Option card with the [D-Brigade] or [DigiPolice] trait, can I add that card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2426** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's inherited effect activate when an opponent's effect would place a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards in another of my Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it can. This effect can interrupt the removal of a card from the battle area, therefore by deleting 1 of your other Digimon with the [D-Brigade] trait, this card will be prevented from being placed in your other Digimon's digivolution cards.
- **Q2427** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "When [...] would [...] leave the battle area" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
