## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Search your security stack. You may play 1 level 5 or lower [Angel] or [Archangel] trait Digimon card among it without paying the cost. Then, shuffle it, and 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP for the turn. [All Turns] When one of your Digimon with the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Three Great Angels] trait would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects or a battle, by trashing the top card of your security stack, prevent them from leaving. (Rule) Trait: Has [Angel] Type.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3748** (2024-03-28) Q: If I don't play a Digimon card, can the part after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q3749** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when [...] would leave the battle area other than by [...] a battle" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area other than by battle" refers to when that Digimon is placed in the trash other than as a result of a battle, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
- **Q3750** (2024-06-21) Q: If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when multiples of my Digimon with the [Angel], [Archangel], or [Three Great Angels] trait would leave the battle area at the same time other than by one of my effects or a battle, are all of those Digimon prevented from leaving? — A: Yes, all of those Digimon are prevented from leaving. This card's [All Turns] effect affects all Digimon without having to choose them. In such cases, the effect triggers once when multiples of your Digimon with the [Angel], [Archangel], or [Three Great Angels] trait would leave the battle area at the same time other than by one of your effects or a battle, and by trashing the top card of your security stack, all of those Digimon are prevented from leaving.
