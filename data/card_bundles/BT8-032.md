## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 10000 DP or less to its owner's hand. [When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If a blue card is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, unsuspend 1 of your Digimon. If a green card is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
