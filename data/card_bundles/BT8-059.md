## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1738** (2024-09-20) Q: What does a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect do, exactly? — A: This effect prevents players from ignoring digivolution requirements. This effect affects both players.
- **Q1739** (2024-09-20) Q: Is DNA digivolution or Burst Digivolve possible while a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated? — A: Yes, DNA digivolution and Burst Digivolve are possible.
- **Q1740** (2024-09-20) Q: Is digivolution without paying the cost possible while a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated? — A: Yes, such digivolving is still possible. Digivolving without paying the cost isn't the same as ignoring the digivolution requirements.
- **Q1741** (2024-09-20) Q: If a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated, is digivolution possible using effects such as those that ignore parts of the digivolution requirements such as levels? — A: No, players can’t ignore part of the digivolution requirements.
- **Q1742** (2024-09-20) Q: Is digivolution using BT10-067 [Justimon: Critical Arm]'s [When Attacking] effect possible while a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated? — A: No, such digivolution isn't possible. _(related: BT10-067)_
- **Q1743** (2024-09-20) Q: Is digivolution from a Tamer using BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s 1st effect possible while a "players can't ignore digivolution requirements" effect is activated? — A: Yes, such digivolution is possible. Effects that add to card information for digivolution aren't the same as effects that ignore digivolution requirements. _(related: BT4-011)_
