## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-007 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5012** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to give DP to either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5013** (2025-09-05) Q: Both I and my opponent have 1 EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] in our battle areas. If this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect gives +3000 DP to my opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE], what happens to the [All Turns] effects and DP of both our EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s? — A: The [All Turns] effects on both EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s activate and their DPs become 15000. Because the DP of your opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] becomes 15000, the [All Turns] effect on your EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] will activate. In turn, this causes the [All Turns] effect on your opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] to activate, it can no longer be affected by the +3000 DP from this card's effect, and this [All Turns] effect will give it +3000 DP. In addition, the conditions for both [All Turns] effects will still be met even after your opponent's turn ends and this card's effect is lost, and the DP becomes 15000 for both. _(related: EX10-010)_
