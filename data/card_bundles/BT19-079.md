## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [All Turns] When any of your [Xros Heart] trait Digimon cards with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer, cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for their DigiXros.

## Official Q&A

- **Q3138** (2024-09-20) Q: What does this card's [All Turns] effect do, exactly? — A: Normally, you can place cards from your hand or battle area under a card to be played for a DigiXros, but when this card's [All Turns] effect is activated, you can also place cards from under your Tamers for that card's DigiXros.
- **Q3139** (2024-09-20) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also allow me to place cards from under a Tamer other than this Tamer for a DigiXros? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q3140** (2024-09-20) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when multiples of the specified cards would be played? — A: Yes, it triggers. Cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for each of those cards' DigiXros.
