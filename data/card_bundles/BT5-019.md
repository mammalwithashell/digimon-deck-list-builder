### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ (This Digimon can attack when your opponent has 1 or more memory.) [When Digivolving] You may place 1 red Digimon card from your hand at the top of this Digimon's digivolution cards. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1289** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 5 cards revealed with this card's effect include 2 copies of [BT5-019 ShoutmonDX], can I add both copies to my hand? — A: Yes, [BT5-019 ShoutmonDX] has [Shoutmon] in its name, and it also has <Blitz>. The effect is looking for "1 Digimon card with [Shoutmon] in its name and 1 Digimon card with <Blitz>," which means you can add both to your hand. _(a ruling on BT5-009; this card is named in it)_
- **Q1295** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve a level 5 Digimon into this card. Can I place a red level 4 or lower Digimon card or level 7 Digimon card from my hand at the top of this card's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1296** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect to place a level 3 Digimon card at the top of its digivolution cards. This Digimon is then targeted with a <De-Digivolve 1> effect, trashing this card. What happens to the Digimon? — A: It becomes a level 3 Digimon. The digivolution cards placed below it remain, and their inherited effects can still be activated.
- **Q1297** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect to place a level 3 Digimon card at the top of its digivolution cards. This Digimon is then targeted with a <De-Digivolve 2> effect. What happens? — A: The <De-Digivolve> effect trashes this card, and the Digimon becomes a level 3 Digimon. <De-Digivolve> can't be used to trash any more cards once the Digimon it's targeting becomes level 3, and the effect ends. Any digivolution cards placed below it remain, and their inherited effects can still be activated.
- **Q1298** (2025-11-21) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card digivolves. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q1299** (2024-03-28) Q: This card has 2 [When Digivolving] effects. If <Blitz> resolves first, does the other [When Digivolving] effect resolve after the attack ends? — A: No. If you activate <Blitz> first, after activating any [When Attacking] or [When one of your Digimon attacks] effects, you activate the other [When Digivolving] effect before the counter timing occurs. _(related: BT5-017)_
