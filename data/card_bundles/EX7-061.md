## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than in battle, if [Lilithmon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, by deleting 1 other Digimon, it doesn't leave. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When other Digimon are deleted, if it's your turn, you may play 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost. If it's your opponent's, trash their top security card.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX7-061 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3866** (2024-06-21) Q: I attempted to delete 1 of my other Digimon using this card's 1st [All Turns] effect when this card would leave the battle area other than in battle, but I couldn't delete that Digimon due to another effect. Does this card leave the battle area at such times? — A: Yes, it leaves the battle area. If the "by doing X" condition isn't met, the rest of the effect isn't processed. In such cases, this card leaves the battle area if the deletion isn't possible.
- **Q3867** (2024-06-21) Q: I tried to delete my opponent's EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] using this card's 1st [All Turns] effect. At such times, if my opponent tries to delete this card using the 1st [All Turns] effect on the chosen EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)], does this card’s 1st [All Turns] effect trigger again? — A: No, it doesn’t trigger. As soon as the player chooses to activate an [X Per Turn] effect, it will count toward 1 use of [X Per Turn]. In this case, as soon as you attempt to delete another Digimon using this card's 1st [All Turns] effect, it will count toward [1 Per Turn], and the effect won't trigger again during that turn.
- **Q5169** (2025-09-05) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 3 cards including EX10-055 [Tactimon] from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards and chose my opponent's EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] as the target to delete. This caused the 1st [All Turns] effect on my opponent's EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] to interrupt, and this card was chosen as the target to delete. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-055 [Tactimon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers. If the gained [All Turns] effect from EX10-055 [Tactimon] activates, this card will be prevented from leaving, EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)]'s "by deleting 1 other Digimon" condition will no longer be met, and EX7-061 [Lilithmon (X Antibody)] will be deleted. _(a ruling on EX10-059; this card is named in it)_
