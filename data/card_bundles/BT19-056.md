### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Dragonkin] or [Cyborg] trait and 1 [Ryo Akiyama] or [Device] trait Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.
