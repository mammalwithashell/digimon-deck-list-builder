## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may place 1 [SkullKnightmon] and/or 1 [DeadlyAxemon] from your hand and/or trash in this Digimon's digivolution cards in any order. [All Turns] When this Digimon is deleted, you may play 1 [SkullKnightmon] and/or 1 [DeadlyAxemon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards suspended without paying their memory costs.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-063 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1621** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [On Play] effect place cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards if I don’t have both a [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] in either my hand or trash? — A: Yes. You don't have to place both a [SkullKnightmon] and a [DeadlyAxemon]. You can also place either of them on their own from either your hand or trash in this Digimon's digivolution cards.
- **Q1622** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [On Play] effect place a [SkullKnightmon] in my hand and a [DeadlyAxemon] in my trash in this Digimon’s digivolution cards? — A: Yes. You can mix and match [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] from both your hand and trash to place in this Digimon’s digivolution cards.
- **Q1623** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have both [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, does this card’s [All Turns] effect require me to play both? — A: No. The effect reads, “you may,” so you can choose whether to play them. However, if both [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] are in digivolution cards, you must choose to either play both of them or play none of them.
- **Q1775** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent used this card on my [BT7-063 DarkKnightmon]. [DarkKnightmon]'s [All Turns] effect is a "when this Digimon would be deleted" effect that interrupts deletion when it activates, but can I use this card's "your opponent can't play Digimon by effects until the end of their turn" effect to prevent [SkullKnightmon] and [DeadlyAxemon] from being played from [DarkKnightmon]'s digivolution cards? — A: Yes, they can't be played. This card's [Main] effect was changed to "Your opponent can't play Digimon by effects until the end of their turn. Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less." Therefore, first the effect that prevents playing Digimon is applied, then Digimon with 6000 DP or less are deleted. Please see the notices regarding changes to the rules/rulings on the official website.
