## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-143 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3835** (2024-05-24) Q: Can my opponent use P-143 [Drimogemon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect to move it from the battle area to the breeding area during their next turn after this card's [When Digivolving] effect has activated? — A: No, it can't be moved. This card's [When Digivolving] effect also prevents effects from moving Digimon. _(a ruling on EX7-014; this card is named in it)_
- **Q4250** (2024-05-24) Q: If a card with <Overflow> is in this card's digivolution cards, is <Overflow> on that card processed when this card's [End of Your Turn] effect moves it to the breeding area? — A: No, it isn't processed. In this case, <Overflow> isn't processed because the card isn't removed from the area.
- **Q4251** (2024-03-28) Q: Are digivolution cards trashed when this Digimon has digivolution cards and it's moved to the breeding area using this card's effect? — A: No, they aren't trashed.
- **Q4252** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is targeted by an effect such as "1 of your opponent's Digimon gains <Security A. -1>," then this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, what happens to the effect? — A: If this Digimon is targeted by an effect and then moved to the breeding area, it remains targeted by the effect. However, it isn't affected by that effect. If it's moved back to the battle area using an effect such as [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect, it will be affected by that effect again. _(related: BT1-089)_
- **Q4253** (2024-03-28) Q: After I activated this Digimon's [End of Your Turn] effect, I used an effect such as [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect to move this Digimon back to the battle area. Can I activate that Digimon's [End of Your Turn] effect again? — A: No, you can't. Even if a card is moved, once a [Once Per Turn] effect has activated, it can't be activated again. _(related: BT1-089)_
- **Q4254** (2024-03-28) Q: I used [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to play [BT16-040 Wormmon], it digivolved into this Digimon, then I used the [End of Your Turn] effect to move it to the breeding area. At such times, does [BT16-085 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji]'s effect return this Digimon to the hand? — A: No, it doesn't. An effect that targets this Digimon will continue targeting it even if it's moved to the breeding area, but because it isn't affected by effects while in the breeding area, it isn't returned to the hand. _(related: BT16-085, BT16-040)_
- **Q4255** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is targeted by [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect and then this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, does [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. At the end of your turn, this card's [End of Your Turn] effect and the effect given by [EX6-070 Phantom Pain] will trigger simultaneously, then if this card's effect activates first and this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect will be pending activation, but it can't activate in the breeding area. _(related: EX6-070)_
- **Q4256** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is suspended when its effect moves it to the breeding area, is it still suspended? — A: Yes, it remains suspended.
- **Q4257** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is suspended when it's moved to the breeding area, is it unsuspended during my next unsuspend phase? — A: Yes, it is. During the unsuspend phase, you unsuspend all of your Digimon and Tamers that are placed in the breeding area and battle area.
- **Q4258** (2024-03-28) Q: Can my opponent declare an attack with this Digimon as the target when this Digimon is suspended in the breeding area? — A: No, they can't. Digimon in breeding areas can't be chosen as attack targets.
