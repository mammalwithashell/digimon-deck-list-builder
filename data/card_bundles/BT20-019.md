## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Alliance＞ [When Digivolving] If [Jesmon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, for the turn, 1 of your Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's effects. Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack. [Your Turn] All of your Digimon with [Sistermon] in their names or the [Royal Knight] trait gain ＜Piercing＞ and can also attack your opponent's unsuspended Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon is [Jesmon GX], all of your Digimon gain ＜Piercing＞ and can also attack your opponent's unsuspended Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4302** (2025-01-24) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to give "isn't affected by your opponent's effects" to 1 of my Digimon and use another Digimon to attack? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4303** (2025-01-24) Q: What does "isn't affected by [...] effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digmon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q4304** (2025-01-24) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen.
- **Q4305** (2025-04-04) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q4306** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q4307** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q4308** (2025-06-13) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
- **Q4717** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if [Jesmon] or [X Antibody] isn't in this card's digivolution cards, this card's [When Digivolving] effect will allow 1 of your Digimon to attack.
