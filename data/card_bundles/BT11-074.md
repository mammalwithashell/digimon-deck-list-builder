## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2102** (2024-03-28) Q: An opponent's Digimon without the highest DP attacks, and by using a [When Attacking] effect to increase its DP, it becomes the Digimon with the highest DP. In such cases, can the target of the attack be switched to this Digimon with this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect? — A: No, you can't. If Digimon did not have the highest DP when the attack was declared, this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect can't be activated.
- **Q2103** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's "When a Digimon becomes unsuspended" effect activate even when my opponent's Digimon becomes unsuspended? — A: Yes, it activates.
