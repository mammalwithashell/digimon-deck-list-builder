## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 [RagnaLoardmon] and 1 Digimon card with the [Legend-Arms] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1089** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include only either [RagnaLoardmon] or cards with [Legend-Arms] in their types, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, if you only reveal one or the other, you only get to add one card to your hand.
- **Q1090** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include 2 [RagnaLoardmon] cards with [Legend-Arms] in their types, can I add both of those cards to my hand? — A: Yes, you may add them both to your hand.
- **Q1091** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck include both a [RagnaLoardmon] card and a card with [Legend-Arms] in its type, do I only get to add 1 to my hand and have to return the rest to the bottom of my deck? — A: No, this effect allows you to add both cards.
