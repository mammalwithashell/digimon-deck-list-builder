## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's play cost 3 or lower Digimon or Tamers. For each card with [Etemon] or [Sukamon] in its name in your trash, add 1 to the maximum play cost this effect can choose. [Opponent's Turn][Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, by placing 1 card with [Etemon] or [Sukamon] in its name from your hand on top of your security stack, you may switch the target of attack to this Digimon or the player.
