## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [End of Your Turn] By suspending this Tamer and returning 1 of your Digimon with the [Hudie] trait to the hand, you may play 1 Tamer card with the [CS] trait from your hand without paying the cost. [All Turns] All of your [Hudie] trait Digimon get +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. In order for the conditions for this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to be met, you must suspend this Tamer and return 1 of your Digimon with the [Hudie] trait to the hand.
