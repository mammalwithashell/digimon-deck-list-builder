## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, their effects can't trash this Digimon's stacked cards. Then, to 1 of your opponent's Digimon, ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ for every 2 [Vemmon] in this Digimon's digivolution cards. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, you may play 1 [Vemmon] from its digivolution cards without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, by returning 2 [Vemmon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck, end that attack.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents cards stacked on top from being trashed by or other effects, and cards stacked on the bottom can't be trashed by effects such as those that trash digivolution cards.
