## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 2 cards with [Garurumon] or [X Antibody] in their names among them to the hand. Place the rest at the bottom of the deck. If you added cards, trash 1 card in your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Garurumon] or [Omnimon] in its name would be deleted in battle, by returning 2 non-Digi-Egg cards from your trash to the bottom of the deck, prevent that deletion.
