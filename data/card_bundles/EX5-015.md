## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 2 cards with [Garurumon] or [X Antibody] in their names among them to the hand. Place the rest at the bottom of the deck. If you added cards, trash 1 card in your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Garurumon] or [Omnimon] in its name would be deleted in battle, by returning 2 non-Digi-Egg cards from your trash to the bottom of the deck, prevent that deletion.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3552** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to add a card to my hand when only 1 card with [Garurumon]/[X Antibody] in its name is among the 4 revealed cards? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q3553** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 4 cards revealed for this card's effect include 1 each of a card with [Garurumon]/[X Antibody] in their names, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q3554** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect to add 2 cards with [Garurumon] in their names to my hand when 2 cards with [Garurumon] in their names and 1 card with [X Antibody] are among the 4 revealed cards? — A: Yes, you can.
