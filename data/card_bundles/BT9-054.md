## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand][Main] If you have a Digimon with [Justimon] or [Raidenmon] in its name in play, you may pay 1 memory to place this card under that Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. [When Digivolving] You may trash 1 Digimon card with [Machine] or [Cyborg] in its traits in your hand to suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 5000 DP or less. That Digimon doesn't unsuspend during your opponent's next unsuspend phase.
