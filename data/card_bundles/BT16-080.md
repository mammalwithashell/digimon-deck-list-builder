## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] If you have 3 or more security cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP for the turn. If you have 3 or fewer, delete 1 of their unsuspended Digimon. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an opponent's effect, if you have 3 or more security cards, by trashing the top card of your security stack, prevent it from leaving. [On Deletion] ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞ until there are 3 cards in your security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-080 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2666** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have exactly 3 cards in my security stack, do I activate both the "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP for the turn" effect and the "delete 1 of their unsuspended Digimon" effect when activating this card's [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] effect? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q2667** (2024-03-28) Q: How exactly do I perform "<Recovery +1 (Deck)> until there are 3 cards in your security stack" in this card’s [On Deletion] effect? — A: If you have 2 or fewer cards in your security stack when the effect activates, repeat <Recovery +1 (Deck)> until there are 3 cards in your security stack. You don't have to do anything when the effect activates if you have 3 or more cards in your security stack.
