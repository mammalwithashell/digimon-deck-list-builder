## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hudie] trait and 1 Tamer card or Option card with the [CS] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower Digimon card with the [Hudie] trait from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon this effect played can't digivolve and is deleted at the end of your opponent's turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-048 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5316** (2025-10-03) Q: I have this card in the digivolution cards of a Digimon with <Alliance>. If that Digimon attacks and I use this card's inherited effect to play a Digimon, can I then use <Alliance> to suspend the played Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5567** (2025-12-25) Q: Do I delete the Digimon that was played by this card's inherited effect at the end of my opponent's turn? And if it isn't deleted, is it still prevented from digivolving? — A: Yes, it's deleted upon the deletion timing. If it isn't deleted by another effect when it would be deleted by this effect, it's still prevented from digivolving.
- **Q5568** (2025-12-25) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's inherited effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously. Therefore, the turn player can choose the processing order.
