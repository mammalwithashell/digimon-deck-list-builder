## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Your Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them have the [ALYSION] trait, by suspending this Tamer, for the turn, 1 of your Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Option effects and gets +3000 DP.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
