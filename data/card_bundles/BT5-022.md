### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you trash a digivolution card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-022 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1305** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's inherited effect to return an opponent's Digimon to its owner's hand, can I gain 1 memory? — A: No. When you return a Digimon to its owner's hand, the game doesn't treat the digivolution cards as being trashed, so you don't gain 1 memory.
- **Q1306** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's inherited effect to trash the digivolution cards of 2 of my opponent's Digimon at the same time, can I gain 2 memory? — A: No. Even if you trash the digivolution cards of 2 of your opponent's Digimon at the same time, you can only use this effect to gain 1 memory per turn.
