## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5390** (2025-10-03) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q5391** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to place either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon as the bottom security card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5392** (2025-10-03) Q: Does this card's <Partition> also trigger when this card's [All Turns] effect is used to place this card itself as the bottom security card? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
