## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] By placing 1 Option card with the [Onmyōjutsu] or [Plug-In] trait from your hand under this Tamer, ＜Draw 1＞ and gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon] or [Sakuyamon] in its name attacks, by suspending this Tamer, you may use 1 [Onmyōjutsu] or [Plug-In] trait Option card with as high or lower a use cost as that Digimon's level from under this Tamer without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST22-07 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5427** (2025-11-21) Q: If an effect places a card under this card while it already has one or more cards under it, in what order do I place the cards? — A: You place it at the bottom of the cards.
- **Q5428** (2025-11-21) Q: A level 5 [Taomon] attacks, and this card's [Your Turn] effect triggers. At such times, if another effect digivolves the attacking Digimon into a level 6 [Sakuyamon], what is the cost of the Option card that can be activated using this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: 6 cost or less. The level of the attacking Digimon is referenced during the timing when this card's [Your Turn] effect activates.
- **Q5429** (2025-11-21) Q: My Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name attacks, and this card's [Your Turn] effect triggers. If another effect then digivolves the attacking Digimon and it no longer has [Renamon], [Kyubimon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name, can I still activate this effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5430** (2025-11-21) Q: If I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to use an Option card from under this card, what happens to that Option card? — A: As with the standard rules, a used Option card is temporarily treated as not being in any area. After using it and activating the Option card's [Main] effect, the card is trashed unless it's to be placed in an area.
