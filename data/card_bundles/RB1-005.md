## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Gammamon] in its text and 1 [Hiro Amanokawa] among them to the hand. Place the rest at the bottom of the deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Gammamon] in its text, it gets +2000 DP.
