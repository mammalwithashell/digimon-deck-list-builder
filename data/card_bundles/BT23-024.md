### Effect
＜Evade＞ ＜Link +1＞ (Add 1 to this Digimon's maximum links.) [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] You may link 1 Digimon card with the [Appmon] trait from your hand or this Digimon's digivolution cards to this Digimon without paying the cost. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-024 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5246** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to link a card that doesn't have <Link>? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5247** (2025-10-03) Q: What does "other than their highest play cost Digimon, none of your opponent's Digimon can suspend until their turn ends" in this card's [All Turns] effect mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents all of your opponent's Digimon from suspending, except their highest play cost Digimon. For example, if your opponent has 1 play cost 5 Digimon and 1 play cost 6 Digimon, the play cost 5 Digimon can't suspend.
- **Q5248** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent only has 1 play cost 5 Digimon. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, can my opponent's Digimon suspend? — A: Yes, it can suspend. Your opponent's Digimon with the highest play cost isn't affected by this effect.
- **Q5249** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has 2 play cost 5 Digimon. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, can either of those 2 Digimon suspend? — A: Yes, either can be suspended. Your opponent's Digimon with the highest play cost isn't affected by this effect.
- **Q5250** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent only has 1 play cost 5 Digimon. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, then an opponent's play cost 6 Digimon is played, can their other Digimon with a play cost of 5 suspend? — A: No, it can't suspend. This effect prevents all of your opponent's Digimon from suspending, except their highest play cost Digimon. If a Digimon is later played and becomes the one with the highest play cost, the Digimon that previously had the highest play cost will now be affected by this effect and can no longer suspend.
- **Q5251** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has 2 play cost 5 Digimon. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, then 1 of my opponent's Digimon digivolves into a play cost 6 Digimon, can their other Digimon with a play cost of 5 suspend? — A: No, it can't suspend. This effect prevents all of your opponent's Digimon from suspending, except their highest play cost Digimon. If a Digimon is later played and becomes the one with the highest play cost, the Digimon that previously had the highest play cost will now be affected by this effect and can no longer suspend.
- **Q5252** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has 1 play cost 5 Digimon and 1 play cost 6 Digimon. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, then my opponent's play cost 6 Digimon is removed from the battle area, can their other Digimon with a play cost of 5 suspend? — A: Yes, it can suspend. Your opponent's Digimon with the highest play cost isn't affected by this effect. If a Digimon with a lower play cost later becomes the one with the highest play cost, it will no longer be affected by this effect, and it can now suspend.
- **Q6025** (2026-05-08) Q: There are only opponent's Digimon with no play costs. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, can my opponent's Digimon suspend? — A: No, it can't suspend. If your opponent doesn't have a highest play cost Digimon, this effect prevents all of their Digimon from suspending.
- **Q6026** (2026-05-08) Q: There are only opponent's Digimon with no play costs. If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, then an opponent's Digimon with a play cost is played, can their Digimon without a play cost suspend? — A: No, it can't suspend. This effect prevents all of your opponent's Digimon from suspending, except their highest play cost Digimon.
