## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If your opponent has 3 or more Tamers, gain 1 memory. [All Turns] When an opponent plays a Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory if that Digimon is level 4 or higher, and ＜Draw 1＞ if it is level 3.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=RB1-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4109** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I suspend this Tamer when my opponent plays a Lv.- Digimon? — A: Yes, you can. However, Lv.- Digimon don't have a level, so the "gain 1 memory if that Digimon is level 4 or higher, and <Draw 1> if it is level 3" effect doesn't activate.
- **Q4110** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if I suspend this Tamer when my opponent plays both a level 3 and a level 4 or higher Digimon at the same time? — A: Both of the conditions are fulfilled, so you gain both 1 memory and <Draw 1>.
- **Q4111** (2024-03-28) Q: If I suspend this Tamer when my opponent plays 2 level 3 or level 4 or higher Digimon at the same time, do I gain <Draw 1> or 1 memory twice? — A: No, you don't. You only <Draw 1> once even if multiple level 3 Digimon are played, and you similarly only gain 1 memory even if multiple level 4 or higher Digimon are played.
