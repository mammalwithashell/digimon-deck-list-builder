## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may place up to 3 Digimon cards with [Bagra Army] in their traits from your trash under 1 of your Digimon as its bottom digivolution cards or under 1 of your Tamers. Then, if you have a Digimon or Tamer with [Bagra Army] in its traits in play, place 1 of your opponent's Digimon under 1 of your opponent's other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-109 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5208** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place 1 of my opponent's Digimon under another of their Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5977** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to place an opponent's Digimon under my opponent's other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card, will the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards leave the battle area? — A: Yes, it will leave the battle area. The digivolution cards of the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards will be trashed at the same time.
