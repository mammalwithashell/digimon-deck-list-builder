## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Your Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them have the [Flame] or [CS] trait, by suspending this Tamer, activate 1 of those Digimon's [Main] effects. If this activated any effect, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-092 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4961** (2025-07-04) Q: When a card with a [Main] inherited effect digivolves into a card with the [Flame] or [CS] trait, can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect and activate that [Main] effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4962** (2025-07-04) Q: If I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate BT22-010 [Meramon]'s [Main] effect, when do I process the part of the effect after "if this activated any effect"? — A: After all of BT22-010 [Meramon]'s [Main] effect has resolved, then you process the part of the effect after "if this activated any effect." If you use BT22-010 [Meramon]'s effect to attack, you gain 1 memory after the attack declaration. _(related: BT22-010)_
- **Q4963** (2025-07-04) Q: I used this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate a [Main] [Once Per Turn] effect. Can I activate that [Main] [Once Per Turn] effect again during the same turn? — A: No, you can't. If a [Once Per Turn] effect activates, it can't activate again during the same turn.
