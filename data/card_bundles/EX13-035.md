## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play up to 2 Digimon cards with [Chuumon], [Sukamon] or [Etemon] in their names and up to 6 total play cost from your hand or trash without paying the costs. By returning 10 such cards from your trash to the bottom of the deck, add 6 to the play cost maximum. [All Turns] While there are 3 or more Digimon with [Sukamon] or [Etemon] in their names, give all of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ and -3000 DP.
