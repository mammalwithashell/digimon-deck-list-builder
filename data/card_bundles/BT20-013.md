## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4293** (2026-03-13) Q: 2 copies of this card are in the battle area. Can I activate the [Main] effect on both copies of this card and play 1 card with [Sistermon] or [Gankoomon] in its name with the play cost reduced by 4? — A: No, you can't. You can't activate multiple card-playing effects at the same time.
- **Q4294** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to play BT20-05 [Gankoomon], can I activate BT20-05 [Gankoomon]'s 1st effect and reduce the play cost by a total of 6? — A: Yes, the play cost will be reduced by a total of 6. _(related: BT20-057)_
- **Q4295** (2025-01-24) Q: If I activate this card's [Main] effect while a Digimon such as ST12-03 [Solarmon] with a "players can't reduce play costs" effect is in the battle area, can I play 1 card with [Sistermon] or [Gankoomon] in its name? — A: You can activate the effect and play such a card, but its play cost won't be reduced. _(related: ST12-03)_
- **Q4296** (2025-01-24) Q: If I activate this card's [Main] effect while a Digimon such as BT9-047 [Pomumon] with a "players can't play Digimon by effects" effect is in the battle area, can I play 1 card with [Sistermon] or [Gankoomon] in its name? — A: You can activate the effect, but you can't use it to play such a card. _(related: BT9-047)_
