## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 Tamer card among them without paying the cost. Place the rest at the top or bottom of your deck in any order. [When Attacking][Once Per Turn] For each green or black Tamer you have in play, reveal 1 card from the top of your deck. You may play any number of green or black Digimon cards whose total play costs add up to 10 or less among them without paying the costs. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2089** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I intentionally choose cards with lower costs from among the cards revealed by this card’s effect so that the total play costs add up to less than 10? — A: Yes, you can choose any of the cards as long as the total play costs add up to 10 or less.
- **Q2090** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to reveal 2 cards from the top of my deck when I have a green and black 2-color Tamer? — A: No, you can't. A 2-color Tamer still counts as 1 Tamer.
