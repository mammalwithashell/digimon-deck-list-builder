## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 2 cards that are either black with [Knightmon] or [DeadlyAxemon] in their names or with [Twilight] in their traits among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-058 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1983** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens to the cards that weren't added to my hand from among the cards revealed by this card’s effects? — A: You place them at the bottom of your deck in any order. <a herf="https： //digimoncard.com/rule/rule_change/index.php#BT10-058">Please see the notices regarding changes to the rules/rulings on the official website. </a>
- **Q1984** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reveals 4 cards. If the cards revealed only include 1 card that is either black with [Knightmon] or [DeadlyAxemon] in its name or 1 card with the [Twilight] trait, can I still add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q1985** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's effect reveals 4 cards. If 2 cards that are either black with [Knightmon] or [DeadlyAxemon] in their names and/or the [Twilight] trait are among the cards revealed, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining card at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. This card's effect reads “Add 2 cards [...] among them to your hand,” so you must add as many cards to your hand as possible. However, if you have a black card with [Knightmon] or [DeadlyAxemon] in its name and the [Twilight] trait among the revealed cards and there are no other cards with [Knightmon] or [DeadlyAxemon] in their names, you can treat that card as your card with the [Twilight] trait and add it to your hand. Any other cards that have the [Twilight] trait will then be returned to the bottom of your deck. In such a case, it can be possible to add just 1 of the cards.
