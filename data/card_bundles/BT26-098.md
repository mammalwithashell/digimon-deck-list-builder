## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be used, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, reduce the cost by 2. [Main] By placing 1 [Sunflowmon] and 1 [Lilamon] from your trash as 1 of your [Lalamon]'s bottom digivolution cards, that Digimon may digivolve into [Rosemon] in the hand, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Lalamon] or [Yoshino Fujieda] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-098 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7173** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place just 1 of the specified cards in digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to place 1 of each of the specified cards as digivolution cards.
