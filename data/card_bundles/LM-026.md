## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand] [Counter] ＜Blast Digivolve＞ (Your Digimon may digivolve into this card without paying the cost.) [On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's 11000 DP or lower Digimon. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, play 1 [Guilmon] from its digivolution cards or your trash without paying the cost. If this effect played, place this Digimon as the played Digimon's bottom digivolution card. (Rule) Name: Also treated as [ChaosGallantmon].

### Special Rule
＜Overflow (-4)＞ (As this card moves from the field or under a card to an area other than those, lose 4 memory.)

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] Add 5000 to this Digimon's DP deletion effects' maximums.

## Official Q&A
- "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
