## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your Tamers becomes suspended, this Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn. Then, if this Digimon has 12000 DP or more, it gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn. (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card.) [All Turns] This Digimon gains all effects of cards with [Gammamon] in their names in this Digimon's digivolution cards.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon gains all effects of cards with [Gammamon] in their names in this Digimon's digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-011 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1938** (2024-03-28) Q: If more than 1 of my Tamers suspend at the same time, does this card's "+2000 DP" effect activate multiple times? — A: No, even if multiple Tamers suspend at the same time, this effect will only give +2000 DP.
- **Q1939** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve a Digimon with [Gammamon] in its name and a [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect into this card. Can I activate the [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect through this card's [All Turns] effect and attack? — A: Yes, if the memory gauge is now at 1 or greater on your opponent's side, you can activate <Blitz>.
- **Q1940** (2024-03-28) Q: I digivolve a [BT8-013 BetelGammamon] into a [BT8-084 Kimeramon] and place this card as one of [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s digivolution cards with its [When Digivolving] effect. Can I now activate [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s [When Digivolving] <Blitz> effect through this card's inherited effect and attack? — A: No, you can't. If this card is added to digivolution cards by [BT8-084 Kimeramon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, you get [BT8-013 BetelGammamon]'s <Blitz> effect through this card, but the time to trigger <Blitz> has already passed, so <Blitz> doesn't activate. _(related: BT8-013, BT8-084)_
- **Q1941** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have multiple digivolution cards with [Gammamon] in their names, does this card's [All Turns] effect and inherited effect give me all of their effects? — A: Yes, you gain all the effects from every applicable card, even if there are multiple of them.
- **Q1942** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect and inherited effect allow me to activate the inherited effect of a digivolution card with [Gammamon] in its name as an effect of this card? — A: No, this card's [All Turns] effect and inherited effect don't affect the inherited effects of digivolution cards with [Gammamon] in their names.
- **Q1943** (2024-03-28) Q: Due to an effect, this Digimon has another [BT10-011 Canoweissmon] and a [BT8-008 Gammamon] under it as its digivolution cards. If I play a red Tamer, how exactly does [BT8-008 Gammamon]'s "When you play a red Tamer, <Draw 1> (Draw 1 card from your deck)" effect work in this situation? — A: This card's effect and inherited effect mean that this Digimon gains [BT8-008 Gammamon]'s effects twice, so <Draw 1> is activated twice and you draw a total of 2 cards. _(related: BT8-008)_
- **Q3656** (2024-03-28) Q: When a [Dobermon] with [BT10-011 Canoweissmon] and [Gammamon] with an [On Play] effect in its digivolution cards digivolves into this card, does this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate the [On Play] effect on both this card and the [Gammamon] digivolution card? — A: Yes, they activate.
- **Q3660** (2024-03-28) Q: When a [Cerberusmon] with [BT10-011 Canoweissmon] and [Gammamon] with an [On Play] effect in its digivolution cards digivolves into this card, does this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate the [On Play] effect on both this card and the [Gammamon] digivolution card? — A: Yes, they activate.
- **Q4874** (2025-07-04) Q: When this Digimon digivolves into BT10-011 [Canoweissmon], I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect after gaining it using BT10-011 [Canoweissmon]'s [All Turns] effect, and I place a card with a [When Digivolving] effect and [Gammamon] in its name from my hand in that Digimon's digivolution cards. Can I activate the placed card's [When Digivolving] effect at such times? — A: No, you can't activate it.
- **Q4897** (2025-07-04) Q: When this Digimon digivolves into BT10-011 [Canoweissmon], I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect after gaining it using BT10-011 [Canoweissmon]'s [All Turns] effect, and I place a card with a [When Digivolving] effect and [Gammamon] in its name from my hand in that Digimon's digivolution cards. Can I activate the placed card's [When Digivolving] effect at such times? — A: No, you can't activate it.
