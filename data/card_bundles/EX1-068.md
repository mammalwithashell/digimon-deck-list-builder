## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-068 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2120** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent uses [EX1-068 Ice Wall!] while one of my Digimon is affected by this card's "isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards" effect. If it becomes my next turn and this card's effect expires, will that Digimon have the "[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory" effect? — A: Yes, this card's "isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards" effect expires while the effect of [EX1-068 Ice Wall!] is still active, so that Digimon will have [EX1-068 Ice Wall!]'s "[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory" effect. _(a ruling on BT11-093; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2121** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent uses [EX1-068 Ice Wall!] and I activate this card's "isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards" effect on one of my Digimon that has "[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory". What happens with the "[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory" effect in such cases? — A: Since that Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards anymore, [EX1-068 Ice Wall!]'s "[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory" effect ends. _(a ruling on BT11-093; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3255** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card, do I lose 2 memory every time my opponent attacks with a Digimon? — A: Your opponent loses the 2 memory. The memory marker will be moved 2 spaces towards you every time your opponent attacks with a Digimon.
- **Q3256** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card. During my opponent’s next turn, if they play a new Digimon or move a Digimon from their breeding area to their battle area, will those Digimon have “[When Attacking] Loses 2 memory” from this card’s effect? — A: Yes. The effect reads, “All of your opponent’s Digimon,” so any Digimon that enter the battle area after you use this card are still affected.
- **Q3257** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card. During my opponent’s next turn, they attack me with their Digimon's <Blitz> effect while I have 1 or more memory. Does my opponent still lose 2 memory? — A: Yes. Even if you have 1 or more memory, the attack with <Blitz> still takes place during your opponent's turn, which means that this card's effect is still active, and your opponent still loses 2 memory.
