## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Jamming＞ [When Digivolving] ＜Draw 1＞ and trash 1 card in your hand. Then, if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers can't suspend until their turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with the [Night Claw], [Light Fang] or [Galaxy] trait would be deleted, by trashing 2 same-level cards from its digivolution cards, it isn't deleted.

## Official Q&A
- It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
