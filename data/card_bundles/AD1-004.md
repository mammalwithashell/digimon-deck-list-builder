## Official Q&A
- Yes. You must activate this card’s inherited effect and use up its [Once Per Turn], even if the attacking Digimon doesn't have [Greymon] or [Omnimon] in its name. However, if the attacking Digimon doesn't have [Greymon] or [Omnimon] in its name, you can't reference DP or delete a Digimon.
