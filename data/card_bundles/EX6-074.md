## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn] When one of your [Holy Beast], [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] trait Digimon is played, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory. Then, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into [Angewomon] or [LadyDevimon] in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in the hand.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

At such times, I want to digivolve [ST10-04 Gatomon] into [Angewomon] or [LadyDevimon] with the cost reduced by 1, but can I use [ST10-04 Gatomon]'s [Your Turn] effect to reduce the digivolution cost again by 2? — A: Yes, that's correct. _(related: ST10-04)_

- **Q3814** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to DNA digivolve using Digimon cards in the hand that aren't specified in [DNA Digivolution]? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
