## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may trash 1 card in your hand. If you do, return 1 Digimon card with [Seven Great Demon Lords] or [Three Musketeers] in its type from your trash to your hand.

## Official Q&A
- No, the effect reads “you may,” so you can choose whether or not to trash the card. If you don't trash a card, you don’t get to return a Digimon card with [Seven Great Demon Lords] or [Three Musketeers] in its type from your trash to your hand.
