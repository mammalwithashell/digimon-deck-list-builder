## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Imperialdramon] in its name or the [Free] trait and 1 card with [Davis Motomiya] in its name among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand for the cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, as long as either a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name or the [Free] trait, or a Tamer card with [Davis Motomiya] in its name is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
