## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] [Opponent's Turn] When one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, if all of your Digimon and Tamers have the [D-Reaper] trait, for each digivolution card of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]s, the attacking Digimon gets -1000 DP for the turn. [Main] If you have no face-up security cards, by trashing your top security card, place this card face up as your top security card.

### Security Effect
[Security] From your hand and without paying the cost, you may play 1 [D-Reaper] trait card with as high or lower a play cost as the number of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]s' digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-100 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3176** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I activate this card's [Security] effect even if I have a Digimon with the [D-Reaper] trait and a Tamer without the [D-Reaper] trait in my battle area? — A: No, you can't. You can't activate it unless all of your Digimon and Tamers in the battle area have the [D-Reaper] trait.
- **Q3177** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I activate this card's [Security] effect even if all of my Digimon and Tamers in the battle area have the [D-Reaper] trait but my Digimon in the breeding area doesn't have the [D-Reaper] trait? — A: Yes, you can. When text doesn't specify an area, it means that it specifies, references, or affects the battle area. In the case of this card's [Security] effect, it can be activated if all of the Digimon and Tamers in your battle area have the [D-Reaper] trait.
- **Q3178** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I activate this card's [Security] effect even if I don't have any Digimon or Tamers with the [D-Reaper] trait in my battle area, but I do have a Digimon with the [D-Reaper] trait in my breeding area? — A: No, you can't activate it. When text doesn't specify an area, it means that it specifies, references, or affects the battle area. In the case of this card's [Security] effect, it can't be activated unless you have at least 1 Digimon or Tamer in your battle area with the [D-Reaper] trait.
- **Q3179** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I use this card from my hand when I have a face-up Security card? — A: Yes, you can. However, the condition "if you have no face-up security cards" in this card's [Main] effect won't be met, and the effect can't activate.
- **Q3180** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens to cards placed face up in the security stack by effects? — A: They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
- **Q3181** (2025-04-04) Q: If this card's {Security} effect triggers, can I choose to not activate it? — A: No, you can't. If the effect triggers, it must activate.
- **Q3182** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens upon a security check for a security card that is placed face-up? — A: The check is performed with the card left revealed. Other than rules for cards left revealed, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security checks.
- **Q3183** (2024-11-22) Q: Does a card's [Security] effect trigger upon a security check with that card placed face-up? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3184** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens if I shuffle a security stack that includes security cards placed face-up? — A: Any face-up cards are placed face down, then you shuffle the cards. After shuffling, all cards are left face-down.
