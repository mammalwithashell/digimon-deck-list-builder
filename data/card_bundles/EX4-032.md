## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 2-color green card and 1 Tamer card with [Henry Wong] in its name among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] When ＜Alliance＞ suspends one of your Digimon, this Digimon may digivolve into a 2-color green Digimon card in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-032 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3476** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include only either a 2-color green card or a Tamer card with [Henry Wong] in its name, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3477** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a 2-color green card and a Tamer card with [Henry Wong] in its name, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
