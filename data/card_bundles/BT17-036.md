## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an opponent's effect, by trashing the top card of your security stack, prevent it from leaving. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When an effect trashes from your security stack, this Digimon with [Leon Alexander] in its digivolution cards may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Pulsemon] in its text in the hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has [Pulsemon] in its text, by trashing the top card of your security stack, unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
