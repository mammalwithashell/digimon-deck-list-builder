## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5293** (2025-10-03) Q: All of my opponent's Digimon were given -6000 DP from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect. If an opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less and an [On Play] effect is played during the same turn, can that Digimon's [On Play] effect activate ? — A: No, it can't. A rule check will occur before the played Digimon's [On Play] effect activates. A Digimon with 0 DP will be deleted upon a rule check, therefore the effect can't activate because the played Digimon is deleted and the card leaves its current area before its effect that's pending activation activates.
- **Q5294** (2025-10-03) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q5295** (2025-10-03) Q: After gaining <Security A. +1> from this card's [All Turns] effect during my opponent's turn, can I gain <Security A. +1> again from this card's [All Turns] effect during my next turn? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5296** (2025-10-03) Q: When this card is used to check my opponent's security stack and it would be deleted in battle with a Security Digimon that has 12000 DP or more, I used this card's <Barrier> to trash a security card and prevent the deletion. At such times, if I gain <Security A. +1> from this card's [All Turns] effect, can I perform another security check on my opponent? — A: Yes, you can perform an additional security check.
