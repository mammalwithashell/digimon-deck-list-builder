## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Place 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon as the bottom digivolution card of another of your opponent's Digimon or under an opponent's Tamer. For each Tamer with a different color you have in play, add 1 to the maximum level you can choose with this effect. [End of Your Turn][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has 4 or more digivolution cards, this Digimon may attack without suspending.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has ＜Save＞ in its text, ＜Draw 1＞. (Draw 1 card from your deck.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2216** (2025-09-05) Q: What does this card's [When Digivolving] effect do, exactly? — A: You choose 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon and place it under another of your opponent's Digimon as its bottom digivolution card or under an opponent's Tamer. For each Tamer you have in play with a different color, you add 1 to the maximum level you can choose with this effect. If you have a multicolor Tamer, you reference its colors that are different from your other Tamers and count it as 1 Tamer with a different color. For example, if you have a red Tamer, a red/blue Tamer, and a blue/yellow Tamer, you would refer to the red, blue, and yellow colors, then add +3 to the maximum level you can choose.
- **Q2217** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to place a card under a Tamer that already has cards under it, in what order do I place the cards? — A: If there are already any cards stacked under the Tamer, you place the new card at the bottom.
- **Q2218** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to place 1 of my opponent's Digimon under another of their Digimon, one that isn't affected by effects? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q4995** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's Digimon under my opponent's other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card, will the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards leave the battle area? — A: Yes, it will leave the battle area. The digivolution cards of the Digimon to be placed in digivolution cards will be trashed at the same time.
- **Q4996** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's Digimon under one of their Tamers, does that Tamer gain the placed card's inherited effect? — A: No, it doesn't. A Digimon will gain an inherited effect, but a Tamer doesn't gain it.
- **Q4997** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to place an opponent's BT12-038 [GeoGreymon] from the battle area as the bottom digivolution card of their BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] that's treated as a Digimon, does that BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] gain BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect? — A: Yes, it gains the inherited effect. If BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] suspends, BT12-038 [GeoGreymon]'s inherited effect will trigger. However, as soon as it is no longer treated as a Digimon, it will lose inherited effects. _(related: BT12-038, BT17-087)_
- **Q4998** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to attack, can this card attack even if it's suspended? — A: Yes, it can attack.
