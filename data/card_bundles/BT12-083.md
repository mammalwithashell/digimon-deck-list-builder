## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Place 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon as the bottom digivolution card of another of your opponent's Digimon or under an opponent's Tamer. For each Tamer with a different color you have in play, add 1 to the maximum level you can choose with this effect. [End of Your Turn][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has 4 or more digivolution cards, this Digimon may attack without suspending.

## Official Q&A
- You choose 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon and place it under another of your opponent's Digimon as its bottom digivolution card or under an opponent's Tamer. For each Tamer you have in play with a different color, you add 1 to the maximum level you can choose with this effect. If you have a multicolor Tamer, you reference its colors that are different from your other Tamers and count it as 1 Tamer with a different color. For example, if you have a red Tamer, a red/blue Tamer, and a blue/yellow Tamer, you would refer to the red, blue, and yellow colors, then add +3 to the maximum level you can choose.
