## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST3-08 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q634** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's inherited effect to target one of my opponent's Digimon with 1000 DP or less and delete it? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q635** (2024-03-28) Q: A Digimon with this card as a digivolution card attacks an opponent's Digimon, then that Digimon's DP is reduced to 0 and deleted by this card's inherited effect. What happens to the attack? — A: No battle occurs, and the attack ends. If there are other effects to activate (When Attacking effects, etc.), the attack ends after activating them.
- **Q636** (2024-03-28) Q: A Digimon with this card as a digivolution card attacks an opponent's Digimon, then the opponent blocks with a Digimon that has <Blocker>. Can I use this card's inherited effect to reduce the DP of the blocking Digimon to 0 and delete it? — A: No, blocking can only be performed in the blocker timing that occurs after all [When Attacking] effects finish resolving. As such, once a block has been declared, the window for activating this card's inherited effect has already passed, so it can't be activated.
