## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have no Digimon other than [Vemmon], cards in your trash can also be placed for this card's DigiXros. [When Digivolving] You may place up to 2 [Vemmon] from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards. [End of Your Turn] If this Digimon has 4 or more digivolution cards, it may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Vemmon] in its text in the hand.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -1: 4 [Vemmon] When this would be played, you may place specified cards from the hand/battle area under it. Each one reduces the play cost.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any [Vemmon] return to the bottom of the deck from this Digimon's digivolution cards, this Digimon unsuspends and gains ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent's turn.

- **Q6014** (2026-03-13) Q: Is a "if you don't have XX Digimon" condition also met when I have no Digimon? — A: Yes, it's met.
