## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7247** (2026-09-18) Q: When this card would leave the battle area other than by my effects, I try using its [All Turns] effect to delete another Digimon but another effect prevents it. At this time, does this card not leave the battle area? — A: No, it leaves the battle area. If the "by doing X" condition isn't met, the rest of the effect isn't processed. In this case, this card leaves the battle area if the deletion isn't possible.
- **Q7248** (2026-09-18) Q: My opponent has a 12000 DP or higher Digimon. Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Counter] effect and fulfill the "if this effect didn't delete" portion by not selecting a card to delete? — A: No. If your opponent has a 12000 DP or higher Digimon, then you must select that card and delete it.
- **Q7249** (2026-09-18) Q: My opponent has a 12000 DP or higher Digimon with an effect that prevents deletion. Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Counter] effect and fulfill the "if this effect didn't delete" portion by choosing that Digimon? — A: Yes.
- **Q7250** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I activate a [Counter] effect and then activate another [Counter] effect? — A: No, you can't. Only 1 [Counter] effect can be activated per an attack.
- **Q7251** (2026-09-18) Q: Do this card's Assembly requirements mean that each Lv.5/Lv.4/Lv.3 card must have [Guilmon] or [Growlmon] in its name? — A: Yes, they must each satisfy the requirements.
- **Q7252** (2026-09-18) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect get its maximum increased by an "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" effect? — A: Yes. If the DP-based deletion effect's maximum is increased by 2000, deleting one of your opponent's 11000 DP or lower Digimon will be prevent it from leaving.
