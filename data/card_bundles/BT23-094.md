## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [CS] trait on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Until your opponent's turn ends, give 1 of their Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ and it can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Your Turn] When one of your [CS] trait Digimon attacks, ＜Delay＞. ・Until your opponent's turn ends, give 1 of their Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ and it can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-094 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5368** (2025-10-03) Q: What does "while you have (the specified card) on the field" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when you have the specified card in the battle area or breeding area.
- **Q5369** (2025-12-25) Q: What does "can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents [When Digivolving] and [When Attacking] effects on cards from activating. [When Digivolving] effects and [When Attacking] effects can no longer activate by triggering or when effects such as "activate that card's [When Digivolving]" effect" activate.
- **Q5370** (2025-12-25) Q: I used a card affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects" to attack. Can I activate that card's [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] effect at such times? — A: Yes, you can. The effect can activate if it activates upon the [End of Attack] timing.
- **Q5371** (2025-12-25) Q: If a card is affected by "can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects," can other effects activate its [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects? — A: No, it can't be activated.
- **Q5372** (2025-12-25) Q: If a card is given "can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects," can I process just the "by" condition for its [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q5373** (2025-12-25) Q: A card that was given "can't activate [When Digivolving] or [When Attacking] effects" digivolved into a card that has a [When Digivolving] [End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] effect. Does that count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn] even if that effect can't be activated upon the timing when that card digivolved? — A: No, it doesn't count. The effect can't activate upon the [When Digivolving] timing, therefore it doesn't count as an instance of that effect's [X Per Turn].
