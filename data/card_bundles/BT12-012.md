## Printed text (official)
### Effect
You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your [Takuya Kanbara]s as if that card is a level 3 red Digimon. [On Deletion] You may play 1 [Flamemon] from your hand suspended without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 [Takuya Kanbara] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: [Shoutmon] × [Ballistamon] × [Dorulumon] When you would play this card, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each placed card reduces the play cost.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. For this card's 1st effect, you declare a digivolution, then upon revealing this card, you can also choose a Tamer as the card to digivolve, and that Tamer is also treated as a level 3 Digimon. As with the normal digivolution procedure, after revealing this card and declaring a digivolution, you can't choose to not digivolve. In addition, you can't declare digivolution into this card in the first place unless there is a card on the field that you can digivolve.
