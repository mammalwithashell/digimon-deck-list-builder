### Effect
While you have a [Legend-Arms] trait Digimon, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] You may place 1 [Legend-Arms] trait Digimon card from your trash as 1 of your Digimon's bottom digivolution card. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When one of your Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects,＜Delay＞. ・You may play 1 [Legend-Arms] trait card from that Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost.
