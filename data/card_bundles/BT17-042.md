## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Argomon] and 1 [Rhythm] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] Gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-042 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2795** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 3 cards I reveal with this card's effect include only an [Argomon] or [Rhythm], can I just add that 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q2796** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both an [Argomon] and [Rhythm], can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
