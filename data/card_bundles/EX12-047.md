＜Piercing＞ ＜Security A. +1＞ ＜Ascension＞ (When this Digimon is deleted, you may place this card as the top security card.) [On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's lowest DP Digimon. ## Official Q&A
- The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if you first activate and this card is removed from the trash, the other effects on this card that were pending activation can no longer activate.
