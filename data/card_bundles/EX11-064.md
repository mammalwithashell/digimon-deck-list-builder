## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [On Play] Flip your opponent's top face-down security card face up. [Your Turn] When one of your [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait Digimon attacks, by suspending this Tamer, that Digimon may digivolve into a [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait Digimon card in the hand. For each of your opponent's face-up security cards, reduce this effect's digivolution cost by 1.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
