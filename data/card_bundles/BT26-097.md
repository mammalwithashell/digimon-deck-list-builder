## Printed text (official)
### Effect
Add 1 to this card's use cost for each of your security cards. [Main] By placing 1 of your Tamers with [Dan Yuki] or [Kanan Yuki] in their names as any of your [Aegiomon]'s bottom digivolution card, it may digivolve into [Jupitermon] in the hand or trash, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost. After, you may place 1 card with [Aegiochusmon] in its name in your trash as any of your [Jupitermon]'s top digivolution card.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower [TS] trait card from your hand without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.
