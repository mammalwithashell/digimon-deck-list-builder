## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][Main][Once Per Turn] You may play 1 green or [Royal Knight] trait Digimon card from your hand for the cost. When it would be played by this effect, reduce the play cost by 4. [All Turns] When you play another Digimon, all of your green and/or [Royal Knight] trait Digimon gain ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent's turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2299** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card’s [When Digivolving][Main] effect both for a digivolution into this card and for the main phase in the same turn? — A: No, you can't. This effect is a [Once Per Turn] effect, therefore it can't activate again during the same turn that it activated for [When Digivolving], even if the timings are different.
- **Q2300** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card’s [When Digivolving][Main] effect to play a card with the [Royal Knight] trait, can I activate both this card's effect and my [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6]'s effect to reduce the play cost according to each respective effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2301** (2024-03-28) Q: When I play a card with the [Royal Knight] trait or a green Digimon card, does this card's [All Turns] effect also give <Blocker> to that played Digimon? — A: Yes, it does. Note that this effect applies to ""all of your Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait and all your green Digimon,"" therefore any applicable Digimon that are placed in the battle area after this effect activates will also receive <Blocker>.
