## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][Main][Once Per Turn] You may play 1 green or [Royal Knight] trait Digimon card from your hand for the cost. When it would be played by this effect, reduce the play cost by 4. [All Turns] When you play another Digimon, all of your green and/or [Royal Knight] trait Digimon gain ＜Blocker＞ until the end of your opponent's turn.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. This effect is a [Once Per Turn] effect, therefore it can't activate again during the same turn that it activated for [When Digivolving], even if the timings are different.
