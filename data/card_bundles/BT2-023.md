## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When playing this card from your hand, reduce its memory cost by 1 for each of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A

- **Q1002** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has 5 or more Digimon with no digivolution cards in play, meaning this card's effect reduces its cost by 5, to -1. Can I play it by gaining a memory? — A: No, play costs can't be reduced to less than zero. You can play the card for free, but you can't gain memory with effects that reduce play costs.
