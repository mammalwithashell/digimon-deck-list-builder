## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP. If no Digimon is deleted by this effect, your opponent can't play Digimon with 5000 DP or less until the end of their turn. [When Attacking] If you have a Tamer in play, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3430** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon digivolved into a Digimon with [Rock Dragon], [Earth Dragon], [Machine Dragon], or [Sky Dragon] in its traits when I had 2 copies of this card in play. Can I suspend both copies of this card and activate the digivolved Digimon's [On Play] effects twice? — A: Yes, you can. However, you can't activate 2 effects at the same time, so you must activate them 1 at a time. After you suspend the first card and activate 1 of the digivolved Digimon's [On Play] effects, then you may suspend the second card and activate the second [On Play] effect. Note that if the first effect or another effect triggers an effect that deletes the digivolved Digimon or otherwise removes it from the battle area, the second effect doesn't activate.
- **Q3431** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon digivolved into a [EX3-012 Volcanicdramon] when I had 2 copies of this card in play. I used the effects on the first copy of this card to activate [EX3-012 Volcanicdramon]'s [On Play] effect and deleted all of my opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP. At this time, does the [On Deletion] effect of my opponent's deleted Digimon activate first or does the effect on the second copy of this card activate first? (2022/07/22) — A: The [On Deletion] effect is triggered afterward, so the [On Deletion] effect takes priority and activates first.
- **Q4669** (2025-04-25) Q: What does a "your opponent can't play XX" effect do, exactly? — A: Such effects prevent your opponent from playing XX. With this card's effect, your opponent's actions or effects can't play Digimon cards with 5000 DP or less. This effect affects the opponent player.
- **Q4670** (2025-04-25) Q: I activate a "your opponent can't play XX" effect, and in the same turn, my opponent activates an effect that reveals cards and plays a specified card from their deck. Is the card revealed from the deck but not played? — A: Yes. Your opponent reveals cards, but then doesn't play the specified card.
- **Q4671** (2025-04-25) Q: Can an opponent's Digimon with 5000 DP or less be played if I activate a "your opponent can't play Digimon with 5000 DP or less" effect, and in the same turn, I activate an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: Yes, it can be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon" is an effect that prevents your opponent's actions or effects from playing Digimon. Your effects can still play an opponent's Digimon with 5000 DP or less. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q4672** (2025-04-25) Q: Can my Digimon with 5000 DP or less be played if I activate a "your opponent can't play Digimon with 5000 DP or less" effect, and in the same turn, my opponent activates an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: No, it can't be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon" is an effect that prevents your opponent's actions or effects from playing Digimon. Your Digimon with 5000 DP or less can't be played by an opponent's effect. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q6508** (2026-05-08) Q: If I activate this card's [On Play] effect, can my opponent play Digimon with 5000 DP or less into their breeding area? — A: No, they can't.
