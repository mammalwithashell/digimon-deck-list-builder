## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a green Digimon or Tamer in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Activate the effect below. For each color your Digimon have, activate it again. ・1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -12000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-068 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3507** (2024-03-28) Q: If I activate this card's [Main] effect two or more times, can I give -6000 DP to different opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes. This [Main] effect activates again for each color your Digimon have, and you get to choose the target for each activation.
- **Q3508** (2024-03-28) Q: If I activate this card's [Main] effect two or more times, are they treated as activating at separate timings? — A: No, they're treated as activating at the same timing.
- **Q3509** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [Main] effect activate three times when I have 1 2-color Digimon? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3510** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have a red/blue Digimon and a red/black Digimon, do they count as 4 colors and make this card's [Main] effect activate five times? — A: No, multiple instances of the same color count as 1 color. Therefore, for the situation stated in your question, red, blue, and black would count as 3 colors, so the effect would activate four times.
- **Q3511** (2024-03-28) Q: If I activate this card's [Main] effect two or more times, can I activate the effect for different opponent's Digimon each time? — A: Yes, this effect activates multiple times, so you can choose different targets each time it activates.
