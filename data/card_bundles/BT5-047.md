## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-047 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1333** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is being deleted. Can I use this card's effect to place it at the bottom of 1 of my green Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1334** (2024-03-28) Q: This card that was targeted by [BT11-043 KingSukamon]'s effect and had its name changed to [Sukamon] is deleted. If I have no other [Palmon] in the trash, can I place this same card at the bottom of the digivolution cards of one of my green Digimon from the trash with it's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Yes, you can. The [On Deletion] effect is activated while treated as [Sukamon], but the name changing effect is lost when the card is deleted and placed in the trash, so you can treat this card as a [Palmon] card. _(related: BT11-043)_
