- **Q7453** (2026-09-18) Q: What kind of card does "a card with X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolve requirements, DNA Digivolve, DigiXros requirements, Burst Digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, "a card with [Knightmon] in its text" would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q7454** (2026-09-18) Q: If I use this card's [All Turns] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q7455** (2026-09-18) Q: A Digimon with [Knightmon] in its text is deleted, and that Digimon's [On Deletion] effect and this card's [All Turns] effect trigger simultaneously. At such a time, in what order does this card's [All Turns] effect and the deleted card's [On Deletion] effect activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if this card's [All Turns] effect is activated first and the deleted card is then placed under this card, the card pending activation has left the area, so its [On Deletion] effect can't activate.
