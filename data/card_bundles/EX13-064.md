## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-064 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7418** (2026-09-18) Q: If a text says "XX card with AA or BB," does that mean AA and BB both have to be XX? — A: Yes, both have to be XX. For example, "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait or that is yellow or black" means either "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [CS] trait" or "1 level 4 or lower Digimon card that is yellow or black." In both cases, the card must be level 4 or lower.
- **Q7419** (2026-09-18) Q: After giving <Rush> and <Collision> to a Digimon with this card's second [Your Turn] effect, can I choose not to attack with that Digimon? — A: No. The Digimon given <Rush> and <Collision> by this effect must attack if possible.
- **Q7420** (2026-09-18) Q: What kind of card does "a card with X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolve requirements, DNA Digivolve, DigiXros requirements, Burst Digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, "a card with [Knightmon] in its text" would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q7421** (2026-09-18) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q7422** (2026-09-18) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q7423** (2026-09-18) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card can't attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q7424** (2026-09-18) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q7425** (2026-09-18) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q7426** (2026-09-18) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
