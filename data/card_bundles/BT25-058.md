## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-058 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6345** (2026-05-08) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect suspend a different card from the one that's given "can't unsuspend until their turn ends? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q6346** (2026-05-08) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when this card itself is played by an effect or when one of my Digimon in the battle area would digivolve into this card by an effect? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q6347** (2026-05-08) Q: If this card's [All Turns] effect triggers, do I have to process "<De-Digivolve 1> 1 of your opponent's Digimon"? — A: Yes, you have to process it. However, "then, this Digimon and 1 of your opponent's Digimon may battle" is optional and you may choose whether or not to process it.
- **Q6348** (2026-05-08) Q: What does "may battle" mean, exactly? — A: This effect directly performs a battle. If this effect is activated, the chosen Digimon battle, as with the standard rules.
- **Q6349** (2026-05-08) Q: If 2 Digimon including a Digimon that has an "effects don't affect" effect are chosen for a "may battle" effect, can those Digimon battle? — A: Yes, they can battle. A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect can still be chosen for an effect. In addition, a battle itself is a rule that compares DP, not an effect. Therefore, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen, and then it can battle. If it loses the battle, it will be deleted as normal according to the rules.
