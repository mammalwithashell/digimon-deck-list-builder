## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When one of your Digimon with [Tyrannomon] in its name attacks, by suspending this Tamer, that Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Tyrannomon] in its name or the [Dinosaur] trait in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- The [When Digivolving] effect activates first. As soon as your Digimon attacks, the "when your Digimon attacks, you may digivolve that Digimon" effects trigger simultaneously. You activate the 1st effect, digivolve a Digimon, then its [When Digivolving] effect triggers, and you therefore activate it first due to the derived trigger rule. Related Cards
