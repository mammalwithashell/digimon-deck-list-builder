＜Alliance＞ [Main] [Once Per Turn] By paying 1 cost, you may play 1 play cost 3 or lower card with [Chuumon] or [Sukamon] in its name or the [CS] trait from your hand without paying the cost. Then, 1 of your level 3 or higher Digimon gains ＜Reboot＞ and ＜Blocker＞ until your opponent's turn ends.
