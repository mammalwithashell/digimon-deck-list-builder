## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST19-08 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q855** (2024-04-19) Q: Can I use this card's [Security] effect to play a Tamer card with the [LIBERATOR] trait and a play cost of 4 or less? — A: Yes, you can. You can play it as long as the card has the [LIBERATOR] trait and a play cost of 4 or less.
- **Q856** (2024-04-19) Q: Can I use this card's [Security] effect to play a Digi-Egg card with the [LIBERATOR] trait? — A: No, you can't. You can't play it because Digi-Egg cards don't have play costs.
- **Q6163** (2026-05-08) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack, does its [Security] effect activate, then does it battle with the attacking Digimon? — A: Yes, its [Security] effect activates, then it battles.
