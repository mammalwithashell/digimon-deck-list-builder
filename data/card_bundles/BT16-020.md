## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-020 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2775** (2024-03-28) Q: If both my hand and my opponent's hand increase when we both draw 1 card from our decks using an effect such as BT16-020 [GaoGamon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, does my opponent add 2 cards from the top of their security stack to the hand using this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: No, they don’t. Although cards are added to both players' hands, they are added at the same timing, therefore your opponent only adds 1 card from their security stack to the hand.
