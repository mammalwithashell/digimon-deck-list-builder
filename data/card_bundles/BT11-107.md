## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would use this card, if you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards in play, reduce the cost by 2. [Main] Choose any number of your opponent's Digimon and/or Tamers whose combined play costs are less than or equal to the play cost of 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, and delete all of the chosen cards. Then, 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name may attack a player.

### Security Effect
[Security] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the highest play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-107 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2134** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I choose cards so that the combined play cost is lower than the play cost of 1 of my Digimon with [Greymon] in their name on purpose with this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2135** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I attack with one of my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name that is suspended or was played this turn with this card's effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's effect does not allow you to attack with Digimon that can't attack.
- **Q2136** (2024-03-28) Q: Does the [When Attacking] effect of my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name activates when it attacks with this card's effect? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q2137** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I attack one of my opponent's Digimon instead of a player with one of my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name that has the effect "This Digimon can also attack your opponent's unsuspended Digimon" with this card's effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's effect only allows you to attack a player.
