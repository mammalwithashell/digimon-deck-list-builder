## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5189** (2025-09-05) Q: This card has multiple effects that trigger when it's played or when it digivolves. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q5190** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I return just 1 non-Digi-Egg card from my trash to the top of the deck for the condition of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect require you to return the specified number of cards to the top of the deck.
- **Q5191** (2025-09-05) Q: I have 20 or more cards in my trash. Can I use this card's <Blast Digivolve> to digivolve my level 3 Digimon with [Impmon] in its name into this card during the counter timing of my opponent's turn? — A: Yes, you can digivolve that card.
