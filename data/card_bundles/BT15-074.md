## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Blocker＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] Your opponent may trash 1 Digimon card in their hand. If they don't, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] While your opponent has no Digimon, this Digimon can't attack.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When an effect plays an opponent's Digimon, gain 1 memory.
