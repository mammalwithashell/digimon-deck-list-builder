## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4417** (2025-01-24) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to give <Rush> to 1 of my Digimon, but then choose to not use that Digimon to attack without suspending? — A: No, you can't. You must use the Digimon that was given <Rush> to attack without suspending if possible.
- **Q4418** (2025-01-24) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to attack even with a suspended Digimon? — A: Yes, it can be used to attack even with a suspended Digimon.
- **Q4419** (2025-04-04) Q: I have 2 copies of this card in the battle area. At the end of my turn, I used the [End of Your Turn] effect on the 1st copy of this card to attack. Can I then use the [End of Your Turn] effect on the 2nd copy of this card to attack? — A: No, an attack using the 2nd effect isn't possible. A new attack declaration can't be made during an attack. In this case, the [End of Your Turn] effects on both copies of this card trigger simultaneously at the end of your turn, and you use the [End of Your Turn] effect on the 1st copy of this card to attack. The 2nd [End of Your Turn] effect can be activated before the counter timing, but because you can't declare another attack during an attack, you won't be able to attack using the 2nd effect, even if you activate it.
- **Q4725** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if [Omnimon] or [X Antibody] isn't in this card's digivolution cards, this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect will return 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q4726** (2025-06-13) Q: I have this card and BT20-060 [Alphamon: Ouryuken ACE] in the battle area. I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to give <Rush> to this card itself, then I use this card to attack a player. If the attack causes a security check on my opponent, BT20-060 [Alphamon: Ouryuken ACE]'s effect gives me +3 memory, and the turn doesn't end, does this card still have <Rush>? — A: Yes, it still has it. The text reads "for the turn," so it will last until the turn ends and switches to your opponent's turn. _(related: BT20-060)_
- **Q5907** (2026-02-06) Q: I use this card's [On Deletion] effect to play BT20-102 [Omnimon (X Antibody)] and place this card into that Digimon's digivolution cards. If I then activate the played BT20-102 [Omnimon (X Antibody)]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, is the "if [Omnimon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards" condition met? — A: Yes, it's met.
- **Q6018** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, and when my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area for the 1st process, an immediate-type effect such as a "when [...] would leave" effect caused this card to be removed from the battle area. Can I then process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect activates, it is to be fully resolved even if the card that activated the effect is removed from that area during the processing.
