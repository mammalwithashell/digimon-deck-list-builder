## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, you may place up to 3 Digimon cards with the [Xros Heart] trait from this Digimon's digivolution cards under 1 of your Tamers. [On Deletion] You may play 1 play cost 4 or lower Digimon card with the [Xros Heart] trait from under your Tamers without paying the cost.
