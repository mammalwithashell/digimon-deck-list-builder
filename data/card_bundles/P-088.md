## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] By placing a Digimon with [Gammamon] in its name under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card, this card gets +2000 DP for the turn. [When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If this Digimon has 12000 DP or more, delete 2 of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less instead.
