## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer, you can ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] Choose 1 of your [Gallantmon], [Sakuyamon] or [MegaGargomon]. From your hand, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost, it may digivolve into a level 6 Digimon card with a different name that includes the chosen Digimon's name. (Rule) Name: Treated as including [Plug-In].

### Security Effect
[Security] Return 1 Digimon card from your trash to your hand, and add this card to your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-072 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3474** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use [EX4-072 Digital Translator]'s effect to digivolve [Sakuyamon] into this card? — A: Yes. This card is treated as having [Sakuyamon] in its name and it's a level 6 card with a name other than [Sakuyamon], therefore it can digivolve using [EX4-072 Digital Translator]'s effect. _(a ruling on EX4-030; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3515** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "a level 6 Digimon card in your hand with a different name that includes the chosen Digimon's name" on this card mean? — A: It means a level 6 Digimon card whose name partially contains the name of the chosen Digimon. For example, if you choose [WarGreymon], you can ignore the digivolution requirements and digivolve it into a level 6 Digimon card that includes [WarGreymon] as part of its name, such as [WarGreymon (X Antibody)], [BlackWarGreymon], or [BlackWarGreymon (X Antibody)].
- **Q3516** (2024-11-22) Q: If I choose [Sakuyamon] for this card's [Main] effect, can I digivolve that Digimon into EX4-030 [Kuzuhamon]? — A: Yes, it can be digivolved. _(related: EX4-030)_
- **Q3517** (2024-11-22) Q: If I choose EX4-030 [Kuzuhamon] for this card's [Main] effect, can I digivolve that Digimon into [Sakuyamon]? — A: No, you can't. _(related: EX4-030)_
- **Q3518** (2024-08-01) Q: What does this card's (Rule) mean, exactly? (*This card's "this card is also treated as having [Plug-In] in its name" has been changed to "(Rule) Name: Also treated as having [Plug-In].") — A: This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also includes the [Plug-In] name. This card can be chosen for text that specifies cards with [Plug-In] in their names.
