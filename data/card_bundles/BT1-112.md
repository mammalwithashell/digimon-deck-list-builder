## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] 1 of your Digimon gains the following effect for the turn: "When this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives, unsuspend it."

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. The effect has no limit to the number of times it can be used. As long as you keep deleting your opponent's Digimon, you can keep using the effect to unsuspend this card.
