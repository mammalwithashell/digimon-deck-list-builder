## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -8000 DP until their turn ends. Then, if they have 5 or more memory, gain 2 memory. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your [Chronicle] trait Digimon or Tamers are played, 1 of your Digimon may attack. Then, you may activate 1 of this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effects. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 [Chronicle] trait card without [Alphamon] in its name from your hand with the cost reduced by 6. It gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7392** (2026-09-18) Q: Due to this card's effect, the DP on one of my opponent's Digimon becomes 0. At this point in time, is that Digimon with 0 DP immediately deleted? — A: No, it is not immediately deleted at this point in time. All activated effects are resolved, after which a rule check occurs. At that point in time, all Digimon with 0 DP are deleted simultaneously.
- **Q7393** (2026-09-18) Q: What positions on the memory gauge does "if your opponent has 5 or more memory" refer to? — A: It refers to the positions at 5 and to the right of it on your opponent's side.
- **Q7394** (2026-09-18) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect also trigger when this card itself is played? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q7395** (2026-09-18) Q: Do this card's Assembly requirements mean that each Lv.5/Lv.4/Lv.3 card must have the [Chronicle] trait? — A: Yes, they must each satisfy the requirements.
- **Q7396** (2026-09-18) Q: I activate this card's [End of Your Turn] effect and play [EX13-057 Grademon], and this card's [Your Turn] effect and [EX13-057 Grademon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect both trigger simultaneously. At this time, if I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect then activate [EX13-057 Grademon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect after attacking with it, is the "if during an attack" condition in [EX13-057 Grademon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect fulfilled? — A: Yes, it is. _(related: EX13-057)_
