## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be used, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, reduce the cost by 2. [Main] By placing 1 [Peckmon] and 1 [Crowmon] from your trash as 1 of your [Falcomon]'s bottom digivolution cards, that Digimon may digivolve into [Ravemon] in the trash, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Falcomon] or [Keenan Crier] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to place 1 of each of the specified cards as digivolution cards.
