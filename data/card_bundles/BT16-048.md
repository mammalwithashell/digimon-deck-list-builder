## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 Digimon card with the [Insectoid] or [Larva] trait from your hand with the play cost reduced by 8. [All Turns] While this Digimon is suspended, it isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By suspending 1 of your other Digimon, return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as the Digimon this effect suspended to the bottom of the deck.

## Official Q&A

- **Q2640** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect prevent [Security] effects on my opponent's Security Digimon from affecting this Digimon while it's suspended? — A: Yes, it isn't affected. [Security] effects on Security Digimon are treated as Digimon effects, so the "this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of your opponent's Digimon" effect prevents such effects from affecting this Digimon.
- **Q4661** (2025-04-25) Q: What does a "your opponent can't play XX by effects" effect do, exactly? — A: Such effects prevent your opponent from playing XX by effects. With this card's effect, your opponent can no longer play Digimon by effects such as BT10-018 [Gaossmon]'s [On Deletion] effect or BT16-048 [TyrantKabuterimon]'s [When Digivolving] effect. This effect affects the opponent player. _(related: BT10-018)_

As soon as BT16-048 [TyrantKabuterimon] suspends, its [All Turns] effect prevents it from being affected by your effects, and <Collision> doesn't activate.
