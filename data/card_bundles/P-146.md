## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Security] Give 1 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ for the turn. [Main] Place this card as 1 of your non-white Digimon's bottom digivolution card.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted in battle, by placing 1 [Recharge Plug-In Q] from its digivolution cards as the bottom security card, it isn't deleted.
