## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Activate 1 of the effects below. ・You may play 1 [Xros Heart] trait Digimon card with 4000 DP or less from under your Tamers without paying the cost. ・By unsuspending 1 of your [Shoutmon EX6] and 1 of your [ShootingStarmon], 1 of your Digimon may attack a player.

[Security] You may play 1 [Xros Heart] trait Digimon card with 4000 DP or less from under your Tamers without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by doing X" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this card's [Main] effect require you to suspend both 1 of your [Shoutmon EX6] and 1 of your [ShootingStarmon].
