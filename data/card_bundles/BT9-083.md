## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] For each card with [Mega] in their traits in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent’s Digimon. Then, place 10 cards from your opponent’s trash at the bottom of their deck in any order. [Start of Your Turn] Trash the top card of this Digimon. If you do, trash the top card of your opponent’s security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1879** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [When Digivolving] effect, and when my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area for the 1st process, an immediate-type effect such as a "when [...] would leave" effect caused this card to be removed from the battle area. Can I then process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's effect? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect activates, it is to be fully resolved even if the card that activated the effect is removed from that area during the processing.
- **Q1880** (2026-03-13) Q: Which player chooses the order in which the cards are returned to the deck for this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: The player that activated this effect chooses.
- **Q1881** (2026-03-13) Q: If this card's [When Digivolving] effect would return a Digi-Egg card to the deck, is it returned to the Digi-Egg deck? — A: Yes, it's returned to the Digi-Egg deck.
- **Q1882** (2026-03-13) Q: Does this card's [When Digivolving] effect delete 1 of my opponent's Digimon for each card with the [Mega] trait in this card's digivolution cards and return 10 cards in my opponent's trash to the bottom of their deck for each card with the [Mega] trait in this card's digivolution cards? — A: No. For each card with the [Mega] trait in digivolution cards, you choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon and delete it, but you only return 10 cards from the trash to the deck.
- **Q1883** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to return cards from my opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck even if they only have 9 or fewer cards in their trash? — A: Yes, you can return them.
- **Q1884** (2026-03-13) Q: Do I have to activate this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect? — A: Yes, you must activate it.
- **Q1885** (2026-03-13) Q: This card is in the battle area and it has no digivolution cards. Does this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect trash this card? — A: No, it isn't trashed. If this card doesn't have any digivolution cards, it isn't considered to have any stacked cards, therefore it isn't trashed.
- **Q1886** (2026-03-13) Q: This card has digivolution cards, then I place 1 more card on top and digivolve it. At the start of my turn, I activate this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect and trash the top card. Does this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect activate from under it? — A: No, it doesn't activate.
