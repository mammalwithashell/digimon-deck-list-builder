## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] When a card is removed from your opponent’s security stack, gain 1 memory. [End of Attack][Once Per Turn] If [WarGreymon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, delete 1 of your opponent’s Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon’s DP.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [WarGreymon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
