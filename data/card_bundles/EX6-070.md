## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3820** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if this card's [Main] effect is given to a Digimon that activated a "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent’s effects until the end of your opponent's turn" effect? — A: A Digimon with a "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent’s effects" effect can also be chosen as a target for this effect, therefore your opponent's Digimon will be affected by this card's effect as soon as it loses the "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent’s effects" effect. As a result, it's deleted at the [End of Your Turn] timing.
- **Q4255** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is targeted by [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect and then this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, does [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. At the end of your turn, this card's [End of Your Turn] effect and the effect given by [EX6-070 Phantom Pain] will trigger simultaneously, then if this card's effect activates first and this Digimon is moved to the breeding area, [EX6-070 Phantom Pain]'s [End of Your Turn] effect will be pending activation, but it can't activate in the breeding area.
