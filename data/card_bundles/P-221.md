## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Security A.+1＞ ＜Partition (yellow Lv.6 & purple/black Lv.6)＞ (When this Digimon with each of the specified digivolution cards would leave the battle area other than by your effects or a battle, you may play 1 each of the specified cards without paying the costs.) [When Digivolving] If DNA digivolving, until your opponent's turn ends, their effects don't affect this Digimon. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -10000 DP until their turn ends.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
