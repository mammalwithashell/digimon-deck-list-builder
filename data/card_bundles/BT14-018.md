- **Q2385** (2024-03-28) Q: What's a token? — A: Tokens are special cards that some effects put into play to represent Digimon or Tamers. This card's effect, for example, plays tokens that are treated as Digimon named [Amon of Crimson Flame] and [Umon of Blue Thunder]. For a more detailed explanation of how tokens work, check the official rule manual.
- **Q2386** (2024-03-28) Q: Can [Amon of Crimson Flame] and [Umon of Blue Thunder] tokens played by this card's effect become targets for "Digimon with play costs of X or less" effects or "level X or higher/lower Digimon" effects? — A: No, they can't. The [Amon of Crimson Flame] and [Umon of Blue Thunder] tokens played by this card's effect can't become targets for effects with requirements related to play cost or level because these tokens don't have a play cost or level.
- **Q2387** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [All Turns] effect activate <Recovery +1 (Deck)> and delete my [Amon of Crimson Flame] or [Umon of Blue Thunder] when I only have one or the other? — A: Yes, it can. Even if you have only 1 [Amon of Crimson Flame] or [Umon of Blue Thunder] token, it will be deleted and <Recovery +1 (Deck)> will activate.
- **Q2388** (2024-03-28) Q: If this card's [All Turns] effect deletes 2 of my [Amon of Crimson Flame] and/or [Umon of Blue Thunder], does <Recovery +1 (Deck)> activate twice? — A: No, it only activates once. No matter how many [Amon of Crimson Flame] and/or [Umon of Blue Thunder] you delete at one time with this effect, <Recovery +1 (Deck)> will only activate once.
- **Q2389** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "When [...] would [...] leave the battle area" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
