## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST20-07 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4451** (2025-04-04) Q: What does a "your opponent can't reduce digivolution costs" effect do, exactly? — A: This effect prevents digivolution costs from being reduced when the costs are to be paid. This effect affects the opponent player.
- **Q4452** (2025-04-04) Q: Can my opponent digivolve a card using a "digivolve without paying the cost" effect while a "your opponent can't reduce digivolution costs" effect is activated? — A: Yes, it can be digivolved. "Digivolve without paying the cost" effects aren't effects that reduce the digivolution cost.
