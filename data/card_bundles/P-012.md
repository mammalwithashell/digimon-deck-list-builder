## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2189** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's effect reveals 4 cards. If [BT5-093 Tai Kamiya & Matt Ishida], [BT9-084 Tai Kamiya & Kari Kamiya], or [P-012 Tai Kamiya (V-Tamer)] is one of the cards revealed, can I add that card to my hand? — A: Yes, those are Tamer cards with [Tai Kamiya] in their names, so you can add one to your hand. _(related: BT5-093, BT9-084)_
- **Q4123** (2024-03-28) Q: At what timing does this card's [Main] effect activate? — A: Like Main effects on Option cards, you can activate these effects at any time during your main phase. However, you can't activate them to interrupt an attack or another effect.
- **Q4124** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's effect if I have a Digimon with [Veedramon] in its name in my breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't check Digimon in the breeding area.
- **Q4125** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have two copies of this Tamer as well as one [Veedramon] in play in my battle area, can I active the effects of both Tamers? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4126** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have one [Veedramon] in play in my battle area, can I activate this card's effect to make a Digimon that doesn't have [Veedramon] in its name get +1000 DP? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4133** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card and activate its effect if I have a [P-012 Tai Kamiya (V-Tamer)] in play? — A: No. This card's effect requires you to have a card named [Tai Kamiya] in play. [Tai Kamiya (V-Tamer)] isn't an exact match, so it can't be used to meet this card's requirements.
