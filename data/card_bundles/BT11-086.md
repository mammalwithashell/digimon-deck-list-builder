## Printed text (official)
### Effect
Cards in your trash can also be placed for this Digimon's DigiXros. [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card that is purple or has the [Xros Heart] trait from your trash without paying the cost. If DigiXrosing, add 1 to the number this effect plays. [All Turns] All of your Digimon with the [Xros Heart] trait or ＜Retaliation＞ gain ＜Rush＞ and ＜Blocker＞.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -3: 1 Digimon card w/[Xros Heart] trait When this would be played, you may place specified cards from the hand/battle area under it. Each one reduces the play cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, that's correct. In addition to Digimon from the hand or battle area, you may also place cards from the trash as digivolution cards for a DigiXros.
