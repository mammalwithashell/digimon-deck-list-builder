## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal 5 cards from the top of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Shoutmon] in its name and 1 Digimon card with ＜Blitz＞ among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has ＜Blitz＞, it gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-009 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1288** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 5 cards revealed with this card's effect don't include both a Digimon card with [Shoutmon] in its name and a Digimon card with <Blitz>, am I allowed to add one of the cards to my hand, or do both cards have to be present? — A: You can add a card to your hand. As long as one of the Digimon cards revealed has either [Shoutmon] in its name or <Blitz>, you can add it to your hand.
- **Q1289** (2024-03-28) Q: If the 5 cards revealed with this card's effect include 2 copies of [BT5-019 ShoutmonDX], can I add both copies to my hand? — A: Yes, [BT5-019 ShoutmonDX] has [Shoutmon] in its name, and it also has <Blitz>. The effect is looking for "1 Digimon card with [Shoutmon] in its name and 1 Digimon card with <Blitz>," which means you can add both to your hand. _(related: BT5-019)_
- **Q1290** (2024-09-20) Q: What cards are added to my hand by this card's [On Play] effect? — A: 1 card with [Shoutmon] in its name and 1 card with <Blitz>.
