## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Place 1 yellow or purple Digimon card from your trash on top of your security stack face down. When DNA digivolving, you may search your security stack for 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card and play it without paying its memory cost. Then, shuffle your security stack. [All Turns] When you play another Digimon using an effect, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon whose level is less than or equal to the played Digimon's level.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST10-06 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q734** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect activate when performing a non-DNA digivolve? — A: When performing a non-DNA digivolution, only the “Place 1 yellow or purple Digimon card from your trash on top of your security stack face down” part of the effect activates. The part of the effect that reads, “you may search your security stack for 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card and play it without paying its memory cost” only activates when DNA digivolving. “Then, shuffle your security stack” activates no matter what kind of digivolution you perform.
- **Q735** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also activate when the [Security] effect of one of my Security Digimon plays it into my battle area? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q736** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect activate when [BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa]'s effect moves a Digimon from my breeding area to my battle area? — A: No, moving a Digimon from your breeding area doesn’t count as playing it, so the effect doesn’t activate. _(related: BT1-089)_
- **Q737** (2025-07-04) Q: My level 4 Digimon was played by an effect, then this card's [All Turns] effect and another effect triggered simultaneously. I activated the other effect, then the level of my Digimon that was played by an effect increased from 4 to 5. If I then activate this card's [All Turns] effect, what levels of Digimon can I delete? — A: Level 4 or lower. If the same reference to a "played" trigger condition being performed is made for an effect when it has a "when X is played" trigger condition, the state when the effect has triggered will be referenced. In this case, your Digimon was level 4 when this card's [All Turns] effect triggered, therefore you can delete a level 4 or lower Digimon.
- **Q738** (2025-07-04) Q: My level 4 Digimon was played by an effect, then this card's [All Turns] effect and another effect triggered simultaneously. I activated the other effect, then my Digimon that was played by an effect was removed from the battle area. If I then activate this card's [All Turns] effect, can I delete an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, you can delete it. If the same reference to a "played" trigger condition being performed is made for an effect when it has a "when X is played" trigger condition, the state when the effect has triggered will be referenced. In this case, your Digimon was level 4 when this card's [All Turns] effect triggered, therefore you can delete a level 4 or lower Digimon.
- **Q2645** (2024-03-28) Q: When my opponent had 2 cards in their security attack, they activated [ST10-06 Mastemon]'s [When Digivolving] effect, and the effect that places a card from their trash in their security stack and the effect that plays a level 5 or lower Digimon card activated. My opponent temporarily has 3 cards in their security stack, then it becomes 2 cards. Can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. Even if an effect activates and increases your opponent's security stack, their security stack must have 3 or more cards when the effect finishes activating. _(a ruling on BT16-056; this card is named in it)_
