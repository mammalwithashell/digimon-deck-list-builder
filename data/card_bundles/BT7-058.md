- **Q1606** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [When Attacking] effect place one of my [DeadlyAxemon] with BT9-109 [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards at the bottom of this Digimon’s digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it can.
