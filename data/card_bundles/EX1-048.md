## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 level 6 Digimon card with [Machine] in its traits among them to your hand. Trash the remaining cards.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] While this Digimon has [Machine] in its traits, it gains ＜Blocker＞. (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)

## Official Q&A
- Yes. The effect reads, “You may,” so you can choose not to activate it. However, after revealing cards from the top of your deck, you must perform the rest of the effect to the best of your ability.
