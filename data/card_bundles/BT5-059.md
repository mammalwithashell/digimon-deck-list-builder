## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal 5 cards from the top of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Unidentified] in its type and 1 [Arata Sanada] card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
- You can add a card to your hand. As long as one of the cards revealed is either a Digimon card with [Unidentified] in its type or an [Arata Sanada] card, you can add it to your hand.
