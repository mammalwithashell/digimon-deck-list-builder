## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your effects places a digivolution card under this Digimon, this Digimon gets +3000 DP and ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn. (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card.) [When Attacking] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 Digimon card with [Legend-Arms] in its traits and a play cost of 7 or less among them without paying its memory cost. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon is [RagnaLoardmon], it doesn't activate [Security] effects on Option cards it checks.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, "You may play" means you can choose to play it or not. If you don't play it, return it to the bottom of the deck with the rest of the cards.
