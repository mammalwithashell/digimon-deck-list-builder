## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Delete all of your opponent's Digimon that share a name with it. [When Attacking] You can unsuspend this Digimon by returning 1 of this Digimon's level 6 digivolution cards to your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q939** (2024-03-28) Q: How can I digivolve into this card? — A: You can digivolve into this card from a red or blue level 6 Digimon by paying its digivolve cost.
- **Q940** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's When Digivolving effect to choose one of my opponent's Digimon. Is that Digimon deleted? — A: Yes. The chosen Digimon and all Digimon with the same name as that Digimon are deleted.
- **Q941** (2024-03-28) Q: I choose [Greymon] with this card's When Digivolving effect while my opponent has a [Greymon], [MetalGreymon], and [WarGreymon] in play. Are all of them deleted, or just [Greymon]? — A: Just [Greymon]. Only Digimon with names that completely match the specified name are deleted.
- **Q942** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has [ST1-09 MetalGreymon], [BT1-021 MetalGreymon], and [BT1-114 MetalGreymon] in play in their battle area. I use this card's When Digivolving effect to choose [ST1-09 MetalGreymon]. Are all of my opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards deleted? — A: Yes. The card numbers may be different, but the names match. All of your opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards are deleted. _(related: ST1-09, BT1-021, BT1-114)_
- **Q943** (2024-03-28) Q: If I attack with this Digimon when it has a level 6 Digimon card in its digivolution cards, do I always have to activate this card's When Attacking effect? — A: No, you can choose not to.
- **Q944** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to return a level 6 digivolution card to my hand but choose to not unsuspend this card? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q1033** (2024-03-28) Q: I have this Digimon and a [Diaboromon] token in play in my battle area when my opponent uses [BT1-084 Omnimon]'s [On Evolution] effect to choose [Diaboromon]. Are this Digimon and my [Diaboromon] token both deleted due to having the same name? — A: Yes. The token is considered a Digimon with the specified name, and is deleted. _(a ruling on BT2-082; this card is named in it)_
