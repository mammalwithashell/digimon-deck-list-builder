## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, by trashing 1 [Cyborg] or [Ver.2] trait card from your hand, reduce the play cost by 2. [On Play] [When Digivolving] By placing 1 Digimon card from your trash face down as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, from 1 of your opponent's Digimon, trash any 1 digivolution card for each of this Digimon's face-down digivolution cards. Then, return 1 of their Digimon with no digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your Digimon unsuspends.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-018 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4759** (2025-06-13) Q: When this card is played without paying the cost, can I use its 1st effect to trash a specified card from my hand? — A: Yes, you can. Even if you trash it, you play this card without paying the cost.
- **Q4760** (2025-06-13) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect trash digivolution cards from multiple Digimon? — A: No, it can't. You choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon, then choose its digivolution cards to be trashed by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q4761** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't place 1 Digimon card from your trash face down as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, the part of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect after "then" can't return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck.
