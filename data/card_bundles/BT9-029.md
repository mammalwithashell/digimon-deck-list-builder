## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand][Main] If you have a Digimon with [Justimon] or [Raidenmon] in its name in play, you may pay 1 memory to place this card under that Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. [When Digivolving] You may trash 1 Digimon card with [Machine] or [Cyborg] in its traits in your hand to place 1 of your opponent’s level 4 or lower Digimon at the bottom of its owner's deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] Return 1 of your opponent’s level 4 or lower Digimon to its owner’s hand.

## Official Q&A
- When this card is in your hand, you can activate it by revealing it from your hand during your main phase.
