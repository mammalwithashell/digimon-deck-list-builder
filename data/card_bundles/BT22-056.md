## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-056 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4908** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
- **Q4909** (2026-05-08) Q: This card's effect caused the DP of my opponent's Digimon to become 0. At such times, is the Digimon with a DP of zero deleted? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for the activated effect has resolved, a rule check will occur, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
