## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When effects trash this card from the security stack, activate its [Security] effects. {Security} [All Turns] When any of your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon] or [Sakuyamon] in their names would leave the battle area other than in battle, by trashing this card, 1 of those Digimon doesn't leave. [Main] ＜Draw 1＞. Then, place this card face up as the bottom security card.

### Security Effect
[Security] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -9000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST22-10 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5435** (2025-11-21) Q: If this card's 1st effect and a "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect trigger simultaneously when this card is trashed from the security stack, in what order do they activate? — A: The turn player activates their effect first. If both effects are the turn player's, they can activate them in any order.
- **Q5436** (2025-11-21) Q: What is a {Security} effect? — A: A {Security} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is face up in the security stack. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the face-up security cards.
- **Q5437** (2025-11-21) Q: I have multiple copies of this card face-up in my security stack. When my Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name would leave the battle area other than by battle, I activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 1st copy of this card, and that Digimon is prevented from leaving. Can I then activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 2nd copy of this card? — A: Yes, you can. The {Security} [All Turns] effects on the 2 copies of this card trigger simultaneously when your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name would leave the battle area other than by battle. After activating the effect on the 1st copy, the Digimon is prevented from leaving, then you can activate the {Security} [All Turns] effect on the 2nd copy of this card. After that, the 1st effect on the 2 copies of this card will trigger simultaneously, and you can activate them one at a time for a total of 2 activations.
- **Q5438** (2025-11-21) Q: I have this card placed face-up in my security stack. If this card's {Security} [All Turns] effect activates when multiples of my Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in their names would leave the battle area other than by battle, then this card is trashed from the security stack, what happens? — A: You choose 1 of your Digimon with [Renamon], [Kyubimon], [Taomon], or [Sakuyamon] in its name, and that Digimon is prevented from leaving the battle area. If you have another face-up copy of this card in your security stack, the {Security} [All Turns] effects on those copies will also activate, and you can choose 1 more Digimon that will be prevented from leaving.
