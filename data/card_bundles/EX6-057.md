## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon gains "[End of Your Turn] Delete this Digimon." [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by battle, by deleting 1 level 5 or lower Digimon, prevent it from leaving. [Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When another Digimon is deleted, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

## Official Q&A
- A Digimon with a "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent’s effects" effect can also be chosen as a target for this effect, therefore your opponent's Digimon will be affected by this card's effect as soon as it loses the "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent’s effects" effect. As a result, it's deleted at the [End of Your Turn] timing.
