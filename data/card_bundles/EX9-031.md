## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-031 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4785** (2025-06-13) Q: If there are 2 face-down cards then 1 face-up card in order from top to bottom in this card's digivolution cards, what card do I trash for this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect? — A: You trash the 1st face-down digivolution card from the bottom.
- **Q4786** (2025-07-04) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
