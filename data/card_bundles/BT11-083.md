## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash 1 card in your hand. If you do, you may return 1 [Mirei Mikagura] or 1 card with [Angel], [Archangel], or [Fallen Angel] in its traits from your trash to your hand. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play [Angewomon] or [Mirei Mikagura], gain 1 memory.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] While you have a yellow Digimon in play, all of your Digimon with [Angel], [Archangel], or [Fallen Angel] in their traits gain ＜Retaliation＞.
