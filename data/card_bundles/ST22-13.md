## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST22-13 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5445** (2025-11-21) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, either can be suspended.
- **Q5446** (2025-11-21) Q: At the end of the turn, I used <Vortex> on this card to attack an opponent's Digimon, this Digimon was deleted in the battle, and I used <Fortitude> to play this card from the trash. At such times, can I again use <Vortex> to attack? — A: No, you can't. <Vortex> on this card triggers at the timing upon the end of the turn. If this card attacks using the triggered <Vortex> effect, is deleted in battle, and is played using <Fortitude>, the <Vortex> on this card hasn't triggered after being played, therefore it can't activate.
