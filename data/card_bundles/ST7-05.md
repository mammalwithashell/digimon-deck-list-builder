## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST7-05 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q683** (2024-03-28) Q: A Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and an opponent’s Digimon are deleted simultaneously. Does this card’s inherited effect activate? — A: No. This card is placed in your trash before the effect can activate. It’s no longer in your battle area, so it doesn't activate.
- **Q2746** (2024-03-28) Q: The memory is at 0, I use this Digimon with ST7-05 [Growlmon] in its digivolution cards to attack, and it deleted an opponent's Digimon with <Retaliation> in battle. Is this Digimon deleted by the <Retaliation> effect at such times? — A: Yes, it's deleted. When your opponent's Digimon is deleted in battle, it causes ST7-05 [Growlmon]'s inherited effect and <Retaliation> on the opponent's Digimon to trigger simultaneously. The turn player's effect activates first, the memory becomes 1, and this Digimon's [Your Turn] effect can no longer activate, therefore it's deleted by <Retaliation>. _(a ruling on BT17-016; this card is named in it)_
