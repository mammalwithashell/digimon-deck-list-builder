## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ all of your opponent's Digimon. Then, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 5 or less. If no Digimon is deleted by this effect, none of your opponent's unsuspended Digimon can digivolve until the end of your opponent's turn. [Opponent's Turn] While you have a Tamer in play, this Digimon gains ＜Blocker＞ and ＜Reboot＞.

- **Q3421** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent activates this card's [On Play] effect. Can I use a <Digisorption> effect to suspend an unsuspended Digimon and then digivolve that Digimon in the next turn? — A: No, you can't. <Digisorption> effects read "when one of your Digimon digivolves," so unless the Digimon for the digivolution is in a state where it can digivolve, the effect can't activate.
- **Q3422** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent activates this card's [On Play] effect. Can I digivolve an unsuspended Tamer into a [BT4-011 Agunimon] in the next turn? — A: No, you can't. [BT4-011 Agunimon]'s effect reads "You may digivolve this card from your hand onto one of your red Tamers as if the Tamer is a level 3 red Digimon," so it is considered to be an unsuspended Digimon and can't digivolve. _(related: BT4-011)_
