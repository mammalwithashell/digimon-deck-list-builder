[Start of Your Main Phase] You may play 1 [Armadillomon] or [Patamon] from your hand without paying the cost. At the next end of your opponent’s turn, return it to the hand. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-088 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2681** (2024-03-28) Q: The Digimon played using this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect digivolved. Is that Digimon returned to the hand at the end of my opponent's turn? — A: Yes, it's returned. In such cases, only the top card is returned to the hand and all digivolution cards are trashed.
- **Q2682** (2024-03-28) Q: When one of my Digimon DNA digivolves into a black or yellow Digimon, does the "<De-Digivolve 1> 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect activate even if I don't suspend this Tamer? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. The rest of the effect won't activate if you don't perform the specified action in effects with "by X" conditions.
