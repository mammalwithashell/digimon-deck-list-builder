## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand] [Main] By paying 4 cost and placing this card as 1 of your level 5 or higher Digimon's bottom digivolution card, you may suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and attack an opponent's Digimon with the Digimon this was placed under. ＜Alliance＞ [End of Attack] You may place this Digimon as 1 of your other Digimon's bottom digivolution card.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When an opponent's effect would delete this Digimon, you may play 1 [Parasitemon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. [Your Turn] This Digimon gets +3000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-050 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2803** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to pay 4 cost even when I don't have any level 5 or higher Digimon? — A: No, you can't. The activation conditions include paying 4 cost and placing this card as 1 of your level 5 or higher Digimon's bottom digivolution card, therefore the effect can't be activated just by paying 4 cost.
- **Q2804** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to place this card as 1 of my level 5 or higher Digimon's bottom digivolution card and suspend 1 of my opponent's Digimon without attacking? — A: No, you can't. You must choose to suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and attack, or you must choose to not suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon and not attack.
- **Q2805** (2024-03-28) Q: If my Digimon with multiples of this card in its digivolution cards would be deleted, can I use this card's [All Turns] inherited effect to play multiples of [Parasitemon]? — A: Yes, you can. However, if this card is played before the effect activates, this card's [All Turns] inherited effect can no longer be activated.
