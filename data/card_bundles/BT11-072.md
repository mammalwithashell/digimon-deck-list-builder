## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][On Play] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Analogman] among them to your hand, and add 1 card with [Cyborg] or [Machine] in its traits to your hand or place it under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. Trash the rest. [On Deletion] By placing 1 of your [Analogman]s in play at the bottom of its owner's deck, you may play 1 [Machinedramon] from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-072 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2100** (2024-03-28) Q: If there is no [Analogman] among the cards revealed by this card's effect, can I add 1 card with the [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait to my hand or place it under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card? — A: Yes, you can add 1 card with the [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait to your hand or place it under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card even when no [Analogman] is among the revealed cards.
- **Q2101** (2024-03-28) Q: If there's no [Machinedramon] in my hand, can I place 1 of my [Analogman] at the bottom of its owner's deck with this card's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2214** (2025-04-04) Q: If this Digimon with [BT11-072 Machinedramon] in its digivolution cards is deleted, can I activate the [On Deletion] effect this Digimon gained from [BT11-072 Machinedramon]? — A: Yes, you can activate the [On Deletion] effect. When a Digimon is in the deletion process, it is checked for any [On Deletion] effects. Due to this card's [All Turns] effect, Chaosdramon (X Antibody) is considered to have the [On Deletion] effect of [BT11-072 Machinedramon].
