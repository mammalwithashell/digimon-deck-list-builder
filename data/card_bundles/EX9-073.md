## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 level 5 [Cyborg] or [Ver.5] trait card from your hand or trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card, activate 1 [On Play] effect on the placed card as an effect of this Digimon. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing its bottom 2 face-down or [Cyborg] trait digivolution cards, it doesn't leave.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -6: 4 level 5 [Cyborg] trait Digimon cards w/different names When this would be played, by placing the specified cards from the trash under it, reduce the play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4824** (2025-06-13) Q: At the end of an attack by EX9-073 [Machinedramon] that has this card in its digivolution cards, I use this card's inherited effect to unsuspend EX9-073 [Machinedramon] and then try to delete it. At such times, can I activate EX9-073 [Machinedramon]'s [All Turns] effect and prevent it from being removed? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on EX9-064; this card is named in it)_
- **Q4841** (2025-06-13) Q: My opponent's BT20-037 [Chaosmon: Valdur Arm]'s [When Digivolving] effect is preventing my [On Play] effects from activating. When this card attacks or my Digimon digivolves into it, can I use its [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] to place a card from my hand or trash as this Digimon's top digivolution card, then activate the placed card's [On Play] effect? — A: You can place the card, but you can't activate any of its [On Play] effects. _(related: BT20-037)_
- **Q4842** (2025-06-13) Q: Just 1 face-down card and 1 [Cyborg] trait card are in this card's digivolution cards. Can I trash those 2 digivolution cards for this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, you can. By trashing 2 total [Cyborg] trait cards and/or bottom face-down cards, this card's [All Turns] effect prevents this Digimon from leaving. Possible combinations of cards to trash include 2 [Cyborg] trait cards, 1 [Cyborg] trait card and 1 face-down card, or 2 face-down cards.
