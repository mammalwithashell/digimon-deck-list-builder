## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place up to 3 of your other blue Digimon as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards. Then, return all other level 4 or lower Digimon to the hand. For each digivolution card this effect placed, add 1 to this effect's level maximum. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When an effect adds to this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card with [Aqua] or [Sea Animal] in one of its traits from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. (Rule) Trait: Has [Aquatic] type.
