## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While this card is revealed from your deck, this card is also treated as level 6. When this card would be played from the hand, by returning 1 [Apocalymon] from your trash to the bottom of the deck, reduce the play cost by 3. [On Deletion] If deleted by an effect, you may play 1 [Gulfmon] or 1 level 6 [Dark Masters] trait Digimon card from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 level 5 or lower card with [Dark Masters] in its text from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, this Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn.

- **Q2826** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "while this card is revealed from your deck, this card is also treated as level 6" mean, exactly? — A: This effect means that this card is also treated as a level 6 card while it's flipped over from the deck by a "reveal cards from the deck" effect. If the effect is an effect such as "add 1 level 6 Digimon card from the revealed cards," you can use that effect to add this card to the hand.
- **Q2827** (2024-03-28) Q: If I reveal 2 copies of this card using BT3-051 [Dokugumon]'s [On Play] effect, can I add both copies to my hand? — A: Yes, you can. The effect reads "also treated as level 6," therefore this card can be treated as both level 5 and level 6, so you can add 2 copies to your hand. _(related: BT3-051)_

This Digimon is deleted by the rules, not the DP-reducing effect. Therefore, it doesn't meet the "if deleted by an effect" condition.
