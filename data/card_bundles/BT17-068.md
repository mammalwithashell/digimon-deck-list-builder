## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While this card is revealed from your deck, this card is also treated as level 6. When this card would be played from the hand, by returning 1 [Apocalymon] from your trash to the bottom of the deck, reduce the play cost by 3. [On Deletion] If deleted by an effect, you may play 1 [Gulfmon] or 1 level 6 [Dark Masters] trait Digimon card from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 level 5 or lower card with [Dark Masters] in its text from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, this Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
- This effect means that this card is also treated as a level 6 card while it's flipped over from the deck by a "reveal cards from the deck" effect. If the effect is an effect such as "add 1 level 6 Digimon card from the revealed cards," you can use that effect to add this card to the hand.
