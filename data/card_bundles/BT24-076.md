## Printed text (official)
### Effect
{Trash} [Main] If you have 4 or fewer cards in your hand, play this card with the play cost reduced by 2. [On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon. (Rule) Trait: Has [Dark Dragon] Type.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 level 4 or lower [Dark Dragon] or [Evil Dragon] trait Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- A {Trash} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the trash. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the trash.
