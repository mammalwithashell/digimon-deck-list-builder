## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may place 1 Digimon card with the [Royal Base] trait from your trash face up as the bottom security card. Then, delete up to 8 play cost's total worth of your opponent's Digimon. For each of your face-up security cards, add 2 to this effect's play cost maximum.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effects.

## Official Q&A
- They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
