[Main] Return 1 of your Digimon to its owner's hand to return up to 2 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to their owners' hands. Trash all of the digivolution cards of those Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. If your [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] is chosen as the target to return to the hand for this card's effect, it will ultimately be placed at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck instead of the hand according to the rules, but it meets this card's "by returning 1 of your Digimon to the hand" condition.
