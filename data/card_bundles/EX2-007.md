## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon can't attack and isn't affected by your opponent's effects. [Main][Once Per Turn] If you don't have another [Mother D-Reaper] in play, place 1 of your [ADR-02 Searcher]s from in play or from your hand under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you would play a card with [D-Reaper] in its traits from your hand, you may reduce its play cost by 1 for each of this Digimon's digivolution cards.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.
