## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-021 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q882** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this card, and gain 3 memory from the card's attack effect. If this Digimon is deleted as a result of the attack, do I still lose 3 memory at end of turn? — A: The effect has already activated, so you lose 3 memory at end of turn.
- **Q883** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with this card, and gain 3 memory from the card's attack effect. If I then announce that I'm passing, how much memory do I lose? — A: Announcing the pass moves the memory counter to 3 on your opponent's side. Since it's the end of the turn, you then lose an additional 3 memory, moving the memory counter to 6 on your opponent's side.
- **Q942** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has [ST1-09 MetalGreymon], [BT1-021 MetalGreymon], and [BT1-114 MetalGreymon] in play in their battle area. I use this card's When Digivolving effect to choose [ST1-09 MetalGreymon]. Are all of my opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards deleted? — A: Yes. The card numbers may be different, but the names match. All of your opponent's [MetalGreymon] cards are deleted. _(related: ST1-09, BT1-114)_
- **Q1080** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this card in play when I activate the effect on a card like [BT1-090 Gravity Crush], [BT1-021 MetalGreymon], [BT1-040 WereGarurumon], [BT1-058 Chirinmon], or [BT1-075 Digitamamon].
