## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may play 1 level 3 Digimon card from one of your Digimon's digivolution cards as another Digimon without paying its memory cost. If you do, you may place 1 blue Digimon card from your hand at the bottom of one of your Digimon's digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-027 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1546** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's [On Play] effect places a Digimon card from my hand under 1 of my Digimon. Can I place it under this Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1547** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's [On Play] effect plays a level 3 Digimon card from the digivolution cards of a suspended Digimon. Does the level 3 Digimon enter play suspended? — A: No. It enters play as a new Digimon, unsuspended. Additionally, it does not carry over any effects on the source Digimon.
