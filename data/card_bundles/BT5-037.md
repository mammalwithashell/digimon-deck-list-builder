## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may search your security stack for 1 Digimon card with [Warrior] or [Holy Warrior] in its type, reveal it, and add it to your hand. If you do, trigger ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞. (Place the top card of your deck on top of your security stack.) Then, shuffle your security stack.

## Official Q&A
- It means you can look at all of the cards in your security stack, without revealing them to your opponent. When you choose a card in your security stack to add to your hand, reveal it to your opponent. The other cards should remain hidden.
