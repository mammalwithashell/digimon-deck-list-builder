## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] All Digimon gain ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of your opponent's turn. [All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted or returned to the hand or deck, you may play 1 [Sanzomon] and 1 [Gokuumon], [Sagomon] or [Cho-Hakkaimon] from its digivolution cards without paying the cost. [Your Turn] Change all of your Digimon's ＜Security A. -＞ to ＜Security A. +＞. [End of Opponent’s Turn] [Once Per Turn] You may place 1 Digimon with ＜Security A.＞ on top of its owner's security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-031 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3751** (2024-03-28) Q: What kind of effect is this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: This effect changes your Digimon's <Security A. -> effects to <Security A. +>. For example, a Digimon with <Security A. -1> will be changed to have <Security A. +1>, and a Digimon with <Security A. -2> will be changed to have <Security A. +2>.
- **Q3752** (2024-03-28) Q: If my Digimon has 2 instances of <Security A. -1>, does this card's [Your Turn] effect change them to <Security A. +2>? — A: No, this effect changes them to 2 instances of <Security A. +1>, not <Security A. +2>.
- **Q3753** (2024-03-28) Q: Can a Digimon with either <Security A. +> or <Security A. -> be a target for this card's [End of Opponent’s Turn] effect? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q3754** (2024-03-28) Q: Can a Digimon with either <Security A. +> or <Security A. -> be a target for this card's [End of Opponent’s Turn] effect? — A: Yes, it can.
