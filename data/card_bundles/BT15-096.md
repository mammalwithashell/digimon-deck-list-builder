## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with the [Machine] or [Cyborg] trait to the hand and trash 1 such card. Return the rest to the top of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ ・You may play 1 level 5 or higher Digimon card with the [Machine] or [Cyborg] trait from your hand with the play cost reduced by 3.

### Security Effect
[Security] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with the [Machine] or [Cyborg] trait to the hand and trash 1 such card. Return the rest to the top of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT15-096 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2592** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I only trash cards with the [Machine]/[Cyborg] trait from among the cards revealed from my deck for this card's [Main] and [Security] effects? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q2593** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens when I use this card's effect and only 1 card with the [Machine]/[Cyborg]/ trait is among the cards revealed from my deck? — A: When only 1 of such cards is revealed, it's added to the hand according to the effect's processing order. You can't trash a card.
