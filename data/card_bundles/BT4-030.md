## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-030 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1195** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "this card can't be attacked" [Opponent's Turn] skill mean? — A: It means that as long as you meet the conditions for the skill, the card can't be targeted by your opponent's Digimon for attacks.
- **Q1196** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if this card activates <Blocker> and blocks the attack of one of my opponent's Digimon? — A: This card's effect only prevents your opponent's Digimon from attacking you, so blocking will work as normal.
- **Q1197** (2024-09-20) Q: What digivolution card must be present for this card's [Opponent’s Turn] effect to activate? — A: A [Hybrid] trait Digimon card or a blue Tamer card.
