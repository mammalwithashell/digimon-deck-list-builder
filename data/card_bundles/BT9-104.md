## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its traits in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may digivolve 1 of your Digimon into a Digimon card with [X Antibody] in its traits among them without paying its memory cost. Trash the rest. Then, place 1 card with [X Antibody] in its traits from your trash under 1 of your Digimon with [X Antibody] in its traits as its bottom digivolution card.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [X Antibody] in its traits among them to your hand. Trash the rest.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-104 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1911** (2026-03-13) Q: When a digivolution would occur by this card's [Main] effect, when exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw and where do I draw from? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when this card is placed on top of that Digimon for the digivolution, and you draw from the unrevealed cards in your deck. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q5976** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before trashing the remaining cards? — A: No, you can't.
