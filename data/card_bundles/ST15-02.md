## Official Q&A

- **Q807** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's effect activate even when my opponent only has a Digimon in their breeding area? — A: No, Digimon in breeding areas aren't checked by this effect.
- **Q808** (2024-03-28) Q: If I attack my opponent using a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards and my opponent blocks, does this card's inherited effect activate? — A: Yes. Blocking causes the attack target to be switched, so the effect activates.
- **Q809** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s inherited effect activate when an attack target is switched for a Digimon other than a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it activates.
