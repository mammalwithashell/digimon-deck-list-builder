## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If DNA digivolving, suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon, and this Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn. Then, this Digimon may attack an opponent's Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon with [Imperialdramon] in its name or the [Free] trait deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-055 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2181** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens to this card's [When Digivolving] effect if my opponent doesn't have any Digimon that can be attacked? — A: There is no target for the attack, so the effect ends without an attack being performed.
- **Q4706** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this Digimon didn't DNA digivolve, this card's [When Digivolving] effect allows this Digimon to attack an opponent's Digimon.
