## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] By placing 1 Digimon card with the [Blue Flare] or [Xros Heart] trait from your hand under any of your Tamers, gain 1 memory. [All Turns] When any of your [Blue Flare] trait Digimon cards with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer, cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for their DigiXros.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-081 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3142** (2024-09-20) Q: What does this card's [All Turns] effect do, exactly? — A: Normally, you can place cards from your hand or battle area under a card to be played for a DigiXros, but when this card's [All Turns] effect is activated, you can also place cards from under your Tamers for that card's DigiXros.
- **Q3143** (2024-09-20) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also allow me to place cards from under a Tamer other than this Tamer for a DigiXros? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3144** (2024-09-20) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when multiples of the specified cards would be played? — A: Yes, it triggers. Cards from under your Tamers can also be placed for each of those cards' DigiXros.
