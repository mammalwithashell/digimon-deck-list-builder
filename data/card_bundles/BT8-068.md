## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. For each of your opponent's Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with [Mamemon] in its name and a memory cost of 10 or less among the revealed cards without paying its memory cost. Trash the remaining cards. [All Turns] While you have another Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name in play, this Digimon gains ＜Security Attack +1＞. (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card.)

## Official Q&A
- You can reveal 3 cards, then trash all of them. Note that the effect reads, “you may,” so you can choose whether or not to activate the effect when digivolving, regardless of whether or not your opponent has any Digimon in play. If you choose not to activate it, you don't reveal or trash any cards.
