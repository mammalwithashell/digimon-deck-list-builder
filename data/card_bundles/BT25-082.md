## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If you have 1 or fewer Tamers, you may play 1 Tamer card with [Three Musketeers] in its text from your hand without paying the cost. [All Turns] While you have a Tamer with [Three Musketeers] in its text, this Digimon may digivolve into a [Three Musketeers] trait Digimon card for a digivolution cost of 4, ignoring digivolution requirements.
