## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Trash the bottom 2 digivolution cards of all of your opponent's Digimon. Until the end of their turn, none of their Digimon with no digivolution cards can suspend. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, you may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [Mammal], [Ice-Snow] or [Hybrid] trait from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. (Rule) Trait: Has [Ice-Snow] Type.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: [Kumamon]×[Korikakumon]

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it can suspend. This card's "none of their Digimon with no digivolution cards can suspend" effect only affects Digimon with no digivolution cards. If a Digimon later obtains a digivolution card, this effect will no longer affect it.
