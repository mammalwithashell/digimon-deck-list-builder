## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Trash the bottom digivolution card of all of your opponent's Digimon. Then, up to 3 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards can't attack or block until the end of your opponent's next turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.
