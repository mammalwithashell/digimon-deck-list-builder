## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Activate 1 of the effects below. ・1 of your other Digimon may digivolve into a level 4 purple Digimon card with [Free] in its traits from your trash for the cost. ・This Digimon and one of your other Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand for the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and one of your other Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand for the cost.

## Official Q&A

- **Q3374** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect to DNA digivolve, and this caused the memory gauge to move to my opponent's side. This brought the end of the turn. Can I use this card's inherited effect to DNA digivolve again? — A: Yes, you can.
