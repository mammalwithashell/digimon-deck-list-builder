## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon has [Greymon] or [Omnimon] in its name and an effect would delete it or return it to your hand or deck, you may trash 2 cards of the same level in this Digimon’s digivolution cards to prevent it from leaving play.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1803** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s inherited effect reads, “you may trash 2 cards of the same level”. Does this mean I have to trash 2 cards that are the same level as this card? — A: No. This effect requires you to trash two cards in this Digimon’s digivolution cards that are the same level as each other. The level of this Digimon doesn't matter. For example, if there are two level 5 cards in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, you can trash both to prevent this Digimon from being deleted or returned to your hand or deck.
- **Q1804** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s inherited effect reads, “you may trash 2 cards of the same level”. If this card is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, can I include it as one of the trashed cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1805** (2024-03-28) Q: An opponent’s effect reduced this Digimon’s DP to zero, deleting it. Does this card’s inherited effect activate? — A: No. When a Digimon is deleted after its DP is reduced to zero, it's considered to be deleted by the game rules, not an effect. This card’s effect only activates when the card is deleted by an effect, so it won't activate in this instance.
