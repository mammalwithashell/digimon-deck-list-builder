## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1470** (2024-08-01) Q: What does this card's (Rule) mean, exactly? (*This card's "Also treated as Name: [Sistermon Noir] and Trait: [Virus]" has been changed to "(Rule) Also treated as Name: [Sistermon Noir] and Trait: [Virus]") — A: This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also has the name [Sistermon Noir] and the [Virus] trait.
- **Q5224** (2025-10-03) Q: I have BT10-085 [Sistermon Ciel]. Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to play a BT6-084 [Sistermon Ciel] or ST12-13 [Sistermon Ciel] that's also treated as [Sistermon Noir]? — A: No, it can't be played. "With the same names" also refers to a card with even just 1 of the same name as any of your Digimon. This effect can't play cards with the same name as [Sistermon Ciel]. _(related: BT10-085, ST12-13)_
