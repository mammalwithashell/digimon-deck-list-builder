### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Tamer card or Option card with [Diaboromon] in its text and 1 card with the [Unidentified] trait among them to the hand. Trash the rest.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If this Digimon had the [Unidentified] trait, you may play 1 [Diaboromon] token without paying the cost. (Digimon/Cost 14/Lv. 6/White/Mega/Unknown/Unidentified/3000 DP)
