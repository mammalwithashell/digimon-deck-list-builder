## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Tamer card or Option card with [Diaboromon] in its text and 1 card with the [Unidentified] trait among them to the hand. Trash the rest.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If this Digimon had the [Unidentified] trait, you may play 1 [Diaboromon] token without paying the cost. (Digimon/Cost 14/Lv. 6/White/Mega/Unknown/Unidentified/3000 DP)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-036 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3758** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include only either a Tamer card or Option card with [Diaboromon] in its text or 1 card with the [Unidentified] trait, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3759** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include both a Tamer card or Option card with [Diaboromon] in its text and a card with the [Unidentified] trait, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
