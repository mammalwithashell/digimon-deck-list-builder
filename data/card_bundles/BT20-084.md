## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4412** (2025-01-24) Q: What is a [Trash] effect? — A: A [Trash] effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the trash. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the trash.
- **Q4413** (2025-01-24) Q: When I play a [Sistermon Ciel], in what order can I activate that Digimon's [On Play] effect and this card's [Trash] [All Turns] effect? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q4414** (2025-01-24) Q: When I play a [Sistermon Ciel], can I activate this card's [Trash] [All Turns] effect first, digivolve that Digimon into this card, then activate the [On Play] effect on the played [Sistermon Ciel]? — A: No, you can't.
