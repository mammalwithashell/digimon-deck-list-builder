## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] If there are 4 or more total Digimon, play 1 [Fujitsumon] Token suspended to your battle area. (Digimon/Purple/3000 DP/"[All Turns] This Digimon can't unsuspend. [On Deletion] Trash 1 card in your hand.") If there are 3 or fewer, play it suspended to your opponent's battle area.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When an effect plays an opponent's Digimon, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX5-058 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3651** (2026-03-13) Q: Which player's token does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect play as an opponent's Digimon? — A: The token of the player that activated this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is played as an opponent's Digimon. If the token played by this effect is removed from the field or the game ends, the token is returned to that player.
- **Q3652** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a [Fujitsumon] Token during the turn I activated BT8-097 [Crimson Blaze]'s [Main] effect? — A: Yes, it can be played. This card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect plays one of your cards. Cards can be played by your effects even after a "your opponent can't play Digimon by effects" effect has activated.
- **Q3653** (2026-03-13) Q: If a token has an [On Deletion] effect, then it's removed from the game by the token rules, does its [On Deletion] effect still trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3654** (2026-03-13) Q: Does this card's inherited effect trigger when an effect plays a Digimon to the opponent's breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q3834** (2024-05-24) Q: During a turn when this card's [When Digivolving] effect has activated, can I use EX5-058 [Octomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a [Fujitsumon] Token into my opponent's battle area? — A: Yes, you can. This card's [When Digivolving] effect prevents your opponent from playing or moving Digimon with 6000 DP or less. EX5-058 [Octomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is an effect where you play a token, therefore you aren't prevented from playing it.
- **Q6034** (2026-03-13) Q: Does this card's inherited effect also trigger when one of my effects plays an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it triggers.
