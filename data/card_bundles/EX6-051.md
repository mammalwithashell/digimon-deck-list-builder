## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] If your opponent has 5 or fewer cards in their hand, delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon. If they have 7 or more cards, they trash 1 card in their hand. [On Deletion] If your opponent has 10 or more cards in their trash, you may play 1 [DanDevimon] from your trash without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Your opponent may trash 1 card in their hand. If they didn't trash, you may play 1 level 3 purple Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.
