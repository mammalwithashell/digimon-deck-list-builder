## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT24-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5719** (2025-12-25) Q: What position on the memory gauge does "if you have 5 or more memory" refer to? — A: It refers to when the memory gauge is at 5 or further to the left on your side.
- **Q5720** (2025-12-25) Q: Do I have to suspend this card and <Draw 1> for this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect even if I have 5 or more memory at the timing when the effect activates? — A: Yes, you must suspend it and <Draw 1>.
- **Q5721** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to activate a [When Digivolving] effect on another card that was given a "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" effect? — A: No, you can't. However, if a card with an [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect was given a "can't activate [When Digivolving] effects" effect, you can use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to activate that effect as an [On Play] effect.
- **Q6029** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to activate another card's [On Play] or [When Digivolving] effect even if it has used up all of its [X Per Turn] uses? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6251** (2026-05-08) Q: This card is prevented from suspending. If I have 5 or more memory at the timing when this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect activates, do I gain <Draw 1>? — A: Yes, you gain <Draw 1>.
- **Q6945** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I activate an [On Play] or [When Digivolving] effect gained through <Succession> with this card's [End of Your Turn] effect? — A: Yes, you can.
