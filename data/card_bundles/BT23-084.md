## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5257** (2025-10-03) Q: I used BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards to attack, and I used its 1st <Alliance> to suspend another of my Digimon. BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s [When Attacking] effect triggers at the same time, I use it to activate its [On Play] effect, play this card, and I use this card's [On Play] effect to DNA digivolve this card and the Digimon I suspended with <Alliance> into a [Shakkoumon] in the hand. Can I then use BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s 2nd <Alliance> to suspend [Shakkoumon]? — A: Yes, you can. If BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards attacks, the 2 instances of <Alliance> and the [When Attacking] effect will trigger simultaneously. If you use the 1st instance of <Alliance> to suspend your Digimon, use the [When Attacking] effect to activate the [On Play] effect, and play this card, its [On Play] effect will derived trigger and activate first. If this card's [On Play] effect is used to DNA digivolve the card suspended by the 1st instance of <Alliance> and this card into a [Shakkoumon], it will digivolve unsuspended, therefore the 2nd instance of <Alliance> can suspend that [Shakkoumon]. In addition, the DP that BT23-101 [Hudiemon] gains from the 1st instance of <Alliance> will remain, even if it DNA digivolves. _(a ruling on BT23-027; this card is named in it)_
