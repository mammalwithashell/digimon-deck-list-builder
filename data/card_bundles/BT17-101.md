[Trash] [Your Turn] When one of your level 6 Digimon with [Pulsemon] in its text is played, 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into this card. [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -16000 DP for the turn. If DNA digivolving, you may set the memory to 3 on your opponent's side. Then, if a Tamer card is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, gain 1 memory and ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞. ## Official Q&A
- It triggers at the timing when this card is in the trash and your level 6 Digimon with [Pulsemon] in its text is played. [Trash] means that an effect can trigger and activate when the card is in the trash.
