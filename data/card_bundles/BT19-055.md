## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Deletion] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Among them, add 1 card with [Knightmon] in its text or the [Twilight] trait to the hand and place 1 such card under your Tamers. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-055 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3113** (2024-09-20) Q: If cards that are targets of this effect are revealed from the deck, do I have to add as many cards to my hand and place under my Tamers as possible? — A: Yes, you must add as many cards to your hand and place under your Tamers as possible.
- **Q3114** (2024-09-20) Q: If the cards I reveal for this card's [On Deletion] effect include only 1 card with [Knightmon] in its text or the [Twilight] trait, can I place that card under my Tamer? — A: No, you can't. If you only reveal 1 of the applicable cards, you add it to your hand.
