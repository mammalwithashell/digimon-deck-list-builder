## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5343** (2025-11-07) Q: My opponent has a Digimon. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [When Digivolving] effect by not choosing a card to delete? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent has Digimon, you must choose the card with the highest level among them and delete it.
- **Q5344** (2025-11-07) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with an effect that prevents deletion. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [When Digivolving] effect by choosing the opponent's Digimon with the effect that prevents deletion? — A: Yes, you can.
