## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Eosmon] and 1 [Menoa Bellucci] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, by placing this Digimon as 1 of your [Eosmon]'s bottom digivolution card, you may play 1 [Menoa Bellucci] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When another [Eosmon] is played, this Digimon may digivolve into 1 [Eosmon] in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 3.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can add just 1 card to your hand when only 1 or the other is revealed.
