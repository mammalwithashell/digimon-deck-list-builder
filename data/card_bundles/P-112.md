## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Eosmon] and 1 [Menoa Bellucci] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, by placing this Digimon as 1 of your [Eosmon]'s bottom digivolution card, you may play 1 [Menoa Bellucci] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When another [Eosmon] is played, this Digimon may digivolve into 1 [Eosmon] in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 3.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4216** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck with this card's [On Play] effect don't contain both [Eosmon] and [Menoa Bellucci], do I still get to add a card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can add just 1 card to your hand when only 1 or the other is revealed.
- **Q4217** (2024-03-28) Q: When the cards revealed from my deck with this card's [On Play] effect contain both [Eosmon] and [Menoa Bellucci], am I allowed to add just one card to my hand? — A: No, you can't. You must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q4218** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's inherited effect to digivolve into [Eosmon] in my hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
