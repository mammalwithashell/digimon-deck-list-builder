## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] By placing 1 [Three Musketeers] trait card from your hand or trash as any of your Digimon's bottom digivolution cards, ＜Draw 1＞. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By trashing 1 Option card from any of your Digimon's digivolution cards, you may use 1 [Three Musketeers] trait Option card from your trash with the cost reduced by 3.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6390** (2026-06-19) Q: I have a Tamer with [Three Musketeers] in its text. Can I use BT25-085 [BeelStarmon/Fly Bullet] as [Fly Bullet] and then use Arts Digivolve to digivolve this card into that [BeelStarmon], ignoring its digivolution conditions and without paying the cost? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6393** (2026-05-08) Q: What does a card with "X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolution requirements, DNA digivolution, DigiXros requirements, burst digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, a card with [Knightmon] in its text would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q6394** (2026-05-08) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card digivolves. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q6395** (2026-06-19) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash BT25-085 [BeelStarmon/Fly Bullet] from my Digimon's digivolution cards, then use the trashed card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6396** (2026-06-19) Q: I used this card's [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash an Option card from digivolution cards and then use the trashed Option card. Can I then activate the used Option card's "when this card is trashed from digivolution cards" effect? — A: No, you can't. A used Option card is treated as not being in any area until its [Main] effect has been resolved and the card would be trashed. Upon the timing when a card is no longer considered a card in the trash, it will be considered to be placed in a different area, therefore the effect can't activate.
