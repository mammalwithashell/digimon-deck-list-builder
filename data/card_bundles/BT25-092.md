## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] By trashing 1 card with [Three Musketeers] in its text or the [TS] trait from your hand, ＜Draw 1＞ and gain 1 memory. [Main] By suspending this Tamer and trashing 1 Option card from your hand or your Digimon's digivolution cards, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Three Musketeers] in its text or the [TS] trait in the hand or trash with the cost reduced by 1.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to suspend this Tamer and trash an Option card from your hand or your Digimon's digivolution cards.
