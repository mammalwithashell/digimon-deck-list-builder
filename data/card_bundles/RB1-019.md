## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Place all level 3 Digimon face down on top of their owners' security stacks in any order. Then, all of your opponent’s level 4 or higher Digimon get -3000 DP and gain ＜Security Attack -1＞ until the end of their turn. [When Attacking] By trashing 1 card with [Numemon] in its name in this Digimon's digivolution cards, place 1 of your opponent’s Digimon face down at the bottom of their security stack.
