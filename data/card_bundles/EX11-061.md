## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When any of your Digimon digivolve into a [Puppet] trait Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, you may play 1 level 3 [Puppet] trait Digimon card from your hand without paying the cost. At turn end, delete the Digimon this effect played.

- **Q5916** (2026-02-06) Q: What is the processing order for an effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion of a Digimon played by this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: The pending processing for the effect that triggers at the end of the turn and the deletion at the end of the turn are considered to be processing that triggers simultaneously.
