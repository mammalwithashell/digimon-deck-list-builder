## Printed text (official)
### Effect
{Hand} [Counter] ＜Blast Digivolve＞ (Your Digimon may digivolve into this card without paying the cost.) ＜Raid＞ ＜Reboot＞ ＜Blocker＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's play cost 7 or lower Digimon or Tamers. ### Special Rule
＜Overflow (-4)＞ (As this card moves from the field or under a card to an area other than those, lose 4 memory.)

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
