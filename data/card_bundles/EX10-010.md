## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-010 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5013** (2025-09-05) Q: Both I and my opponent have 1 EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] in our battle areas. If this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect gives +3000 DP to my opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE], what happens to the [All Turns] effects and DP of both our EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s? — A: The [All Turns] effects on both EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s activate and their DPs become 15000. Because the DP of your opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] becomes 15000, the [All Turns] effect on your EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] will activate. In turn, this causes the [All Turns] effect on your opponent's EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] to activate, it can no longer be affected by the +3000 DP from this card's effect, and this [All Turns] effect will give it +3000 DP. In addition, the conditions for both [All Turns] effects will still be met even after your opponent's turn ends and this card's effect is lost, and the DP becomes 15000 for both. _(a ruling on EX10-007; this card is named in it)_
- **Q5020** (2025-09-05) Q: What does "effects don't affect" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q5021** (2025-09-05) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen. For example, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect.
- **Q5022** (2025-09-05) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q5023** (2025-09-05) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "effects don't affect" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q5024** (2025-09-05) Q: If a card has an "effects don't affect" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q5025** (2025-09-05) Q: A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
- **Q5026** (2025-09-05) Q: My opponent only has 1 BT5-112 [Omnimon Zwart Defeat] in the battle area. If that BT5-112 [Omnimon Zwart Defeat] is deleted and this card is chosen as its [On Deletion] effect, is this card deleted? — A: Yes, it's deleted. If this card's [All Turns] effect activates while your opponent has a Digimon with 13000 DP or more, as soon as that Digimon leaves, the effect will no longer be activated. _(related: BT5-112)_
- **Q5202** (2025-09-05) Q: Both I and my opponent have 1 EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE] in our battle areas, and I have a Digimon with an original DP of 13000 or more. Since I have a Digimon with an original DP of 13000 or more, the [All Turns] effects on both EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s activate and their DPs become 15000. If my Digimon with an original DP of 13000 or more leaves the battle area, what happens to the [All Turns] effects and DP of both our EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s? — A: The [All Turns] effects on both EX10-010 [BlackWarGreymon ACE]s remain activated and their DPs become 15000.
