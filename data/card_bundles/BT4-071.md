### Effect
[Your Turn] When one of your other Digimon with [D-Brigade] in its type is deleted, reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. You may play 1 [Commandramon] among them without paying its memory cost. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-071 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1222** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 2 copies of this Digimon in my battle area, and both are deleted at the same time by an opponent's skill. Do both copies activate their [On Deletion] skills? — A: No. The effects for both copies are triggered, but since the cards themselves have been deleted and sent to the trash, they're no longer in the battle area, which means their effects can't be activated.
