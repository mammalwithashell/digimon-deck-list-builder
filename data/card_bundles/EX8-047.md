### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Mineral] or [Rock] trait and 1 card with the [LIBERATOR] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. (Rule) Trait: Has [Mineral] Type.
