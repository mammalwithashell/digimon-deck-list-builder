## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Rush＞ ＜Piercing＞ [When Digivolving] Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the highest level. Then, if a card with [Belphemon] in its name is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, this Digimon attacks without suspending. [End of Attack] This Digimon may digivolve into [Belphemon: Sleep Mode] in the trash, ignoring digivolution requirements and without paying the cost.
