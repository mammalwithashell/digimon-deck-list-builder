## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] By trashing 1 card in your hand, delete 1 of your Digimon with the lowest level and 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest level. [On Deletion] You may play 1 [Agumon] from your trash without paying the cost. If you have a Tamer with [Tai Kamiya] in its name, that Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it deletes this Digimon. However, this effect reads "By trashing 1 card in your hand," therefore you can choose to not activate the effect by not trashing a card in your hand.
