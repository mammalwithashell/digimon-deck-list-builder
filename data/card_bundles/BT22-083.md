## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost. [Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [All Turns] When attack targets change, by suspending this Tamer, for the turn, your opponent's Digimon's effects don't affect 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name or the [CS] trait and it gets +3000 DP.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When attack targets change, if this Digimon is [Eater Eve], 1 of your Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
