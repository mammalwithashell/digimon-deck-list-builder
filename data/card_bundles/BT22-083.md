## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost. [Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [All Turns] When attack targets change, by suspending this Tamer, for the turn, your opponent's Digimon's effects don't affect 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name or the [CS] trait and it gets +3000 DP.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When attack targets change, if this Digimon is [Eater Eve], 1 of your Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4950** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "effects don't affect" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q4951** (2025-07-04) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen.
- **Q4952** (2025-07-04) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q4953** (2025-07-04) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "effects don't affect" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q4954** (2025-07-04) Q: If a card has an "effects don't affect" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q4955** (2025-07-04) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
