## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Breeding][Your Turn] All of your Digimon can't digivolve. [Breeding][Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When a [Royal Knight] trait Digimon card would be played, you may reduce the play cost by 4. Further reduce it by 1 for each of this Digimon's digivolution cards. [Breeding][Start of Your Main Phase] Reveal the top card of your Digi-Egg deck, then place that card and all of your [Royal Knight] trait Digimon as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards.

### Inherited Effect
[Breeding][Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an Option card with the [Royal Knight] trait is placed in the battle area, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-007 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2259** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's [Breeding] [Your Turn] effect also prevent my Digimon from DNA digivolving or burst digivolving? — A: That's correct, such digivolutions are also not possible.
- **Q2260** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's 1st [Breeding] [Your Turn] effect allow my Tamers to digivolve using a "you may digivolve as if your Tamer is a level 3 Digimon" effect? — A: No, they can't be digivolved.
- **Q2261** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's 1st [Breeding] [Your Turn] effect allow my Tamers to digivolve using digivolution requirements for digivolving from Tamers? — A: Yes, they can be digivolved.
- **Q2262** (2024-06-21) Q: What does "further reduce it by 1 for each of this Digimon's digivolution cards" in this card's 2nd [Breeding] [Your Turn] effect mean, exactly? — A: This effect additionally increases the numerical value of the play cost reduction in "when a [Royal Knight] trait Digimon card would be played, you may reduce the play cost by 4" in this card's 2nd [Breeding] [Your Turn] effect. For example, if this Digimon has 1 digivolution card, the numerical value of the play cost reduction will be 5.
- **Q2263** (2024-06-21) Q: Can I choose to not reveal the top card of my Digi-Egg deck and not place my [Royal Knight] trait Digimon as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards for this card's [Breeding] [Start of Your Main Phase]? — A: No, you must perform as much of the processing as possible.
- **Q2264** (2024-06-21) Q: If multiple cards are placed as the bottom digivolution cards for this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect, what order are the cards placed? — A: The player that activated the effect chooses the order of the bottom digivolution cards that are placed. Note that the cards placed by this effect must be placed under any already placed digivolution cards.
- **Q2265** (2024-05-24) Q: When I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect to place a BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE] under this card as a digivolution card, is BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE] processed? — A: No, it isn't processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, <Overflow> isn't processed because a card from the area is placed under another card.
- **Q2340** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have a Digimon card with the [Royal Knight] trait in my hand, do I have to place it under my [Yggdrasill_7D6] in the breeding area as its bottom digivolution card for this card's [On Deletion] effect? — A: No. The cards in your hand count as private information, so you don't have to place that card. _(a ruling on BT13-093; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2369** (2024-05-24) Q: I used this card's [On Play] effect to trash a BT13-007 [King Drasil_7D6] with a BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE] in its digivolution cards. Is BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE]'s <Overflow> processed at such times? — A: Yes, it's processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, <Overflow> is processed because a card is placed in the trash from under another card. _(a ruling on BT13-112; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2463** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect move a level 2 Digimon or cards like [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] or [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] from the breeding area to the battle area? — A: It can't move level 2 Digimon or cards like [BT13-007 King Drasil_7D6] because cards without DP can't be moved to the battle area. It can move [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper] because it has DP. _(a ruling on BT14-088; this card is named in it)_
