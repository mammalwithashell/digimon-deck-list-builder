## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may suspend 1 of your Digimon to suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers. Your opponent's cards suspended with this effect can't unsuspend until the end of their next turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers.

## Official Q&A
- No. You can choose 1 of your opponent’s suspended Digimon or Tamers with this card's effect, but if you didn't use this effect to suspend that card, the “can't unsuspend until the end of their next turn” part of the effect doesn't apply.
