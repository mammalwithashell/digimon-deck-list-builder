## Printed text (official)
### Effect
Your Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name can digivolve into this card in your hand for a memory cost of 3, ignoring this card's digivolution requirements. [When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon's DP. [Opponent's Turn] When your opponent's Digimon attacks, you may trash 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards to end the attack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-111 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1385** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I digivolve a Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name in the breeding area into this card? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q1386** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use a "digivolve your Digimon" effect to digivolve my Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name in the battle area into this card in my hand? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q1387** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I trash just 1 digivolution card for the conditions of this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Opponent's Turn] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q1388** (2026-05-08) Q: What does "end the attack" mean, exactly? — A: After this effect activates, the current timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. For example, if this effect activates during the attack declaration timing, it will make a transition to the end of attack timing. A transition to the counter timing or block timing won't occur, and the attack won't succeed.
- **Q1389** (2026-05-08) Q: Can an "end the attack" effect end an attack by a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: Yes, such attacks can be ended. "End the attack" effects are effects that change the timing, they don't affect an attacking Digimon.
- **Q1390** (2026-05-08) Q: Do [End of Attack] effects activate if I activate this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect and end the attack from an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, they activate.
- **Q2277** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect to play [BT5-111 Omnimon X Anti-body], [BT10-068 Gankoomon (X Antibody)], and [BT10-086 Omnimon (X Antibody)] from the digivolution cards of my Digimon in the breeding area? — A: Yes, you can. _(a ruling on BT13-019; this card is named in it)_
