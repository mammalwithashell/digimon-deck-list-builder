## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1866** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have cards in my trash with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in their traits, does this card's inherited effect let me digivolve any card into any of them, regardless of digivolution requirements? — A: No. This effect can’t ignore digivolution requirements. When activating this effect, you can only digivolve into a card with [Undead] or [Dark Animal] in its traits if it also meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q1867** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card’s inherited effect to digivolve into a card with a [When Attacking] effect. Does that effect activate immediately? — A: No. The [When Attacking] effect wasn't present when you declared the attack, so it doesn't activate.
