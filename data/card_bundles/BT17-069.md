## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If [Eiji Nagasumi] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 [Fenriloogamon] or [Kazuchimon] from your trash without paying the cost. At the next end of your opponent's turn, return that Digimon to the hand. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon or Tamers that have the [SoC] trait or [Pulsemon] in their text is played, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 10000 DP or less.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Fenriloogamon] in its name, the turn end condition is the opponent having 3 or more memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2830** (2024-03-28) Q: The Digimon played using this card's [When Digivolving] effect digivolved. Is that Digimon returned to the hand at the end of my opponent's turn? — A: Yes, it's returned. In such cases, only the top card is returned to the hand and all digivolution cards are trashed.
- **Q2831** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly? — A: Normally, the turn will end and the turn will switch when the memory moves to 1 or more on your opponent's side. But while this effect is activated, the turn won't end unless the memory moves to 3 or more on your opponent's side. Your turn will continue when your opponent's memory is at 1 or 2.
- **Q2832** (2024-03-28) Q: If the memory moves to 2 or less on my opponent's side during my opponent's turn, does the turn switch to my turn with this card's inherited effect? — A: No, this effect only applies during your turn. Your opponent's turn continues until the memory moves to 1 or more from your perspective.
- **Q2833** (2024-03-28) Q: The memory moved to 3 or more on my opponent's side and the turn ended while this card's inherited effect was activated. If the memory moves to 1 or 2 on my opponent's side due to an [End of Your Turn] effect, does my turn continue? — A: Yes, your turn continues.
- **Q2834** (2024-03-28) Q: When the memory moves to 3 or more on my opponent's side while this card's inherited effect is activated, does <Blitz> allow for attacking? — A: No. <Blitz> still requires the memory to be at 1 or more on your opponent's side for attacking. Attacking is possible when your opponent's memory is at 1 or more.
