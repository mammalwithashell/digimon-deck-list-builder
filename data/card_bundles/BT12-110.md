## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-110 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2246** (2024-03-28) Q: When exactly is the timing for "[Trash] [Your Turn]"? — A: When this card is in your trash, the effect can activate when one of your Digimon digivolves into [Beelzemon (X Antibody)]. [Trash] means that the card needs to be in your trash for the effect to activate.
- **Q2247** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens to the color requirements and cost when I activate this card's [Trash] effect? — A: When activating the effect from your trash, it's not considered to be using an Option card, so there are no color requirements and no cost to be paid.
- **Q2248** (2024-03-28) Q: Are "when using an Option card" effects triggered if I activate this card's [Trash] effect? — A: No. Activating an effect from your trash isn't considered to be using an Option card, so they don't trigger.
- **Q5528** (2025-12-25) Q: What is a {Trash} effect? — A: A {Trash} effect can be triggered/activated while its card is in the trash. Such effects can't be triggered or activated in areas other than the trash.
- **Q5529** (2025-12-25) Q: When one of my Digimon digivolves into [Beelzemon (X Antibody)], in what order can I activate this card's {Trash} [Your Turn] effect and the effects that trigger upon digivolution into that Digimon? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
