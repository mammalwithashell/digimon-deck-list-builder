## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would play this card, by placing 1 [Shoutmon] from play as a digivolution card of this Digimon, reduce the play cost by 1 and you may place cards in your trash as digivolution cards for a DigiXros. [On Play] Return all of the digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck in any order, and return that Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck. [Your Turn] All of your opponent's [Security] effects on Option cards don't activate.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -1: ∞ Digimon cards w/[Xros Heart] or [Blue Flare] trait & different card numbers

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2249** (2024-03-28) Q: When playing this card, can the [Shoutmon] I place in its digivolution cards for its effect come from my hand, breeding area, or trash? — A: No, it can't. The effect only allows you to place a [Shoutmon] from the battle area in its digivolution cards.
- **Q2250** (2024-03-28) Q: When I activate the "When this card would be played" effect and place [Shoutmon] in this card's digivolution cards, what happens to [Shoutmon]'s digivolution cards? — A: They're trashed.
- **Q2251** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card's effect places [Shoutmon] in this Digimon's digivolution cards, what happens if [Shoutmon] has [BT9-109 X Antibody] in its digivolution cards? — A: It's not being trashed by the effect, it's being trashed because of the rules, so [BT9-109 X Antibody] does still get trashed. _(related: BT9-109)_
- **Q2252** (2024-03-28) Q: When I activate the "When this card would be played" effect and place [Shoutmon] in this card's digivolution cards, can I first place cards for DigiXros and only then place [Shoutmon]? — A: No, you can't. DigiXros is performed after all ""would be played"" effects have activated. Therefore, you must first place [Shoutmon] in its digivolution cards for the effect and then place cards under the [Shoutmon] digivolution card for the DigiXros.
- **Q2253** (2024-03-28) Q: [Shoutmon]'s digivolution cards are trashed when I place it in this Digimon's digivolution cards for the "When this card would be played" effect. Can I DigiXros with those trashed cards and place them in this Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2254** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "∞ Digimon cards w/[Xros Heart] or [Blue Flare] trait & different card numbers" in this card's DigiXros requirements mean? — A: It means that you can DigiXros with as many cards as you like, as long as all the cards have the [Xros Heart] or [Blue Flare] trait and different card numbers.
- **Q2255** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's DigiXros requirements say "∞ Digimon cards," but is it still considered DigiXros if I place 0 cards? — A: No, it's not DigiXros if you don't place any cards.
- **Q2256** (2024-03-28) Q: If I place [Shoutmon] as a digivolution card for this card's "When you would play this card" effect, can I place another card with the same number as that [Shoutmon] as a digivolution card under this Digimon for this card's DigiXros? — A: Yes, you can. A [Shoutmon] placed as a digivolution card for this card's ""When you would play this card"" effect is not treated as placed by the DigiXros, so it is not included in this card's ""different card numbers"" DigiXros condition.
- **Q2848** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "if this effect returned level 7 white cards" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect mean that it will activate even if I return a card such as BT12-112 [Shoutmon X7: Superior Mode] that has 2 or more colors including white? — A: Yes, that’s correct. _(a ruling on BT17-077; this card is named in it)_
