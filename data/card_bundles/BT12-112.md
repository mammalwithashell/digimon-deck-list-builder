## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would play this card, by placing 1 [Shoutmon] from play as a digivolution card of this Digimon, reduce the play cost by 1 and you may place cards in your trash as digivolution cards for a DigiXros. [On Play] Return all of the digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck in any order, and return that Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck. [Your Turn] All of your opponent's [Security] effects on Option cards don't activate.
