## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Among them, add to the hand or place as any of your blue Digimon's bottom digivolution card 1 Digimon card with [Aqua] or [Sea Animal] in any of its traits. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. (Rule) Trait: Has [Aquatic] Type.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may return 1 level 3 Digimon to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-023 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2936** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a digivolution requirement that digivolves from a Tamer mean that the Tamer that will become a digivolution card is treated as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
- **Q2937** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q2938** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q2939** (2024-06-21) Q: If cards that are targets of this effect are revealed from the deck, do I have to add as many cards to my hand or place as bottom digivolution cards as possible? — A: Yes, you must add as many cards to your hand or place as bottom digivolution cards as possible.
- **Q6593** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6594** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6595** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
