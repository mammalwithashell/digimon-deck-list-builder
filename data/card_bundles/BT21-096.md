## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] For the turn, 1 of your [Marcus Damon]s is also treated as a 12000 DP Digimon, can't digivolve and gains ＜Rush＞. Then, that Digimon may attack your opponent's Digimon. Your opponent's unsuspended Digimon can also be attacked with this effect.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Marcus Damon] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- It causes that card to also be treated as a Digimon with X000 DP. For example, if a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon, it can attack like a standard Digimon, and it will gain inherited effects from cards stacked under it. However, even if a Tamer is played and then treated as a Digimon in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn.
