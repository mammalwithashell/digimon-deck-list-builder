[All Turns] When your breeding area is hatched in, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory. [End of Your Turn] By returning this Tamer to the bottom of the deck, ＜Draw 1＞. Then, you may play 1 Tamer card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Kari Kamiya] in its name from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-093 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2877** (2024-03-28) Q: Is this card's [All Turns] effect triggered by "play into an empty space in your breeding area without paying the cost" effects? — A: No, it doesn't trigger. Effects that play into the breeding area aren't considered hatching, so it doesn't trigger this effect.
- **Q2878** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to play a copy of this card, can I activate the [End of Your Turn] effect on the newly played copy of this card? — A: No, you can't. When this card is newly played, the [End of Your Turn] timing has already passed, and the effect can't activate.
