## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Partition (blue Lv.4 & green Lv.4)＞ [When Digivolving] Suspend all of your opponent's Digimon with as many or fewer digivolution cards as this Digimon. Then, if DNA digivolving, none of their Digimon can unsuspend until the end of their turn. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Suspend 1 of your opponent's unsuspended Digimon. If this effect didn't suspend, unsuspend this Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
＜Partition (blue Lv.4 & green Lv.4)＞ (When this Digimon with each of the specified digivolution cards would leave the battle area other than by your effects or a battle, you may play 1 each of the specified cards without paying the costs.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-025 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2622** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect activate when performing a non-DNA digivolve? — A: The "suspend all of your opponent's Digimon with as many or fewer digivolution cards as this Digimon" part activates even if not DNA digivolving. The "none of their Digimon can unsuspend until the end of their turn" part only activates if DNA digivolving.
- **Q2887** (2024-06-21) Q: When BT16-025 [Paildramon] would be deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: No, you can't activate it. <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> and this card's <Delay> effect will trigger simultaneously, but if you use this card's effect to digivolve first, the triggered <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> on BT16-025 [Paildramon] will be negated and won't activate. _(a ruling on BT17-097; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2888** (2024-05-24) Q: When BT16-025 [Paildramon] is deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: No, you can't activate it. BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect can't be activated because it doesn't trigger when the Digimon is deleted by an opponent's effect. _(a ruling on BT17-097; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2889** (2024-05-24) Q: When a Digimon with BT16-025 [Paildramon] in its digivolution cards is deleted by an opponent's effect, can I activate BT16-025 [Paildramon]'s <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> inherited effect after using this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve? — A: Yes, you can. <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> and this card's <Delay> effect will trigger simultaneously, and the triggered <Partition (blue Lv.4 + green Lv.4)> on BT16-025 [Paildramon] can be activated, even if you use this card's effect to digivolve first. _(a ruling on BT17-097; this card is named in it)_
