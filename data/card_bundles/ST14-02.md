## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] If you have 20 or more cards in your trash, this Digimon may digivolve into [Beelzemon] from your trash for a digivolution cost of 3, ignoring its digivolution requirements.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When a card is trashed from your deck, delete 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon.
