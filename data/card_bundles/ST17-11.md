## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 green Digimon card and 1 such Tamer card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ ・ You may play 1 [Terriermon] or [Lopmon] from your hand without paying the cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Suspend 2 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, place this card in the battle area.
