## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Barrier＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 [Witchelny] text card from your hand as the bottom security card. Then, by trashing your top security card, 1 of your Digimon may attack. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When your security stack is removed from, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7309** (2026-09-18) Q: What kind of card does "a card with X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolve requirements, DNA Digivolve, DigiXros requirements, Burst Digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, "a card with [Knightmon] in its text" would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q7310** (2026-09-18) Q: When performing a security check, if a [Security] effect, a "when this Digimon checks an opponent's security" effect, and a "when a security stack is removed from" effect trigger simultaneously, what order do they activate in? — A: The [Security] effect is activated first. When a [Security] effect is checked, it is activated immediately without waiting. Activate the other triggered effects in order, starting with the turn player's effects.
- **Q7311** (2026-09-18) Q: Due to this card's effect, the DP on one of my opponent's Digimon becomes 0. At this point in time, is that Digimon with 0 DP immediately deleted? — A: No, it is not immediately deleted at this point in time. All activated effects are resolved, after which a rule check occurs. At that point in time, all Digimon with 0 DP are deleted simultaneously.
- **Q7312** (2026-09-18) Q: My Digimon with [EX13-004 DemiMeramon] as a digivolution card digivolves into this card, and I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to trash a card from my security stack and attack with this card. At this time, in what order does this card's [All Turns] effect and [EX13-004 DemiMeramon]'s inherited effect activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if [EX13-004 DemiMeramon]'s inherited effect is activated first and this card digivolves, then this card will lose its [All Turns] effect so you will not be able to activate it. _(related: EX13-004)_
