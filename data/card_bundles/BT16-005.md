## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-005 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2601** (2024-06-21) Q: I have a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, and another of my Digimon with <Collision> is attacking. I used the <Collision> effect to give <Blocker> to an opponent's Digimon that didn't originally have <Blocker>, and it blocks. At such times, if both Digimon have the same DP, they both lose the battle, and they're deleted, does this card's inherited effect trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q2602** (2024-06-21) Q: A Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards battled an opponent's Digimon, and both Digimon lost the battle and were deleted. Can I activate this card's inherited effect at such times? — A: No, you can't activate it. The Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards is deleted before this card's inherited effect activates, therefore it can't be activated.
- **Q2603** (2024-06-21) Q: I have a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, other Digimon battled, and both Digimon lost the battle and were deleted. At such times, if both Digimon have <Blocker>, how many times does this card's inherited effect trigger? — A: It triggers once. If both Digimon lose a battle, both are deleted at the same time.
