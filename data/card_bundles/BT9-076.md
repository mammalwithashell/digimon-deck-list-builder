## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] You may trash 1 card in your hand. If you trash a purple card, delete 1 of your opponent’s level 3 Digimon. If you trash a yellow card, 1 of your opponent’s Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] If this Digimon has 2 or more colors, gain 2 memory.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. A 2-color card is treated as both colors, so you can activate both effects.
