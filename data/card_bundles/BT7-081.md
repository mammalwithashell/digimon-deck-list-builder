### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 5 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits and 1 Tamer card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your Tamers digivolves, gain 2 memory.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. Even if you revealed only a card with [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] in its traits or a Tamer card, you still add that card to your hand.
