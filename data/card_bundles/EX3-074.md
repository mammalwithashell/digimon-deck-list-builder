[When Digivolving] You may place 1 green or blue Digimon card with [Dramon] in its name from your hand under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. When DNA digivolving, you may play 1 green or blue Digimon card with [Dramon] in its name and 12000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3399** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [Start of Opponent's Main Phase] to suspend my [EX3-044 Breakdramon] or [EX3-074 Examon] and had my opponent attack. At such times, how do I process effects such as "when this Digimon is suspended" effects on these Digimon and [When Attacking] effects on the attacking Digimon? — A: When this Digimon is suspended effects and the [When Attacking] effect are triggered by a single effect, therefore they are considered to trigger simultaneously. According to the simultaneous trigger rules, first the turn player's [When Attacking] effects will activate, then the non-turn player's "when this Digimon is suspended" effects will activate. _(related: EX3-044)_
- **Q3401** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent used this card's [Start of Opponent's Main Phase] to suspend their [EX3-074 Examon]. [EX3-074 Examon]'s effect reads "when this Digimon becomes suspended, unsuspend it, and suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon." Can I attack my opponent's [EX3-074 Examon] before that effect unsuspends it? — A: Yes, you can. The attack declaration is made for this card's [Start of Opponent's Main Phase] effect before the suspended [EX3-074 Examon]'s effect, therefore [EX3-074 Examon] is still suspended at that time and can be an attack target.
- **Q3436** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "You may place 1 green or blue Digimon card with [Dramon] in its name from your hand under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate when DNA digivolving? — A: Yes, it activates. "You may place 1 green or blue Digimon card with [Dramon] in its name from your hand under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card" activates when digivolving normally or when DNA digivolving. The "When DNA digivolving, you may play 1 green or blue Digimon card with [Dramon] in its name and 12000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost" part activates only when DNA digivolving.
