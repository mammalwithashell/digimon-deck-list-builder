## Printed text (official)
### Effect
This card/Digimon is also treated as [DarkKnightmon] and [Tuwarmon]. [On Play][When Digivolving] You may place 1 Digimon card with [Knightmon] in its name or [Bagra Army] in its traits from your hand or trash under this Digimon as its top digivolution card. Then, if [Tuwarmon] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 3 of your opponent's Digimon. [On Deletion] You may return up to 2 black and/or purple Digimon cards from your Trash to your hand.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: [DarkKnightmon] × [Tuwarmon] When you would play this card, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each placed card reduces the play cost.
