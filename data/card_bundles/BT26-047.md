## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-047 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7040** (2026-08-18) Q: What does "may battle" mean, exactly? — A: This effect immediately initiates a battle. If this effect is activated, the chosen Digimon battle according to the standard rules.
- **Q7041** (2026-08-18) Q: If 2 Digimon including a Digimon that has an "effects don't affect" effect are chosen for a "may battle" effect, can those Digimon battle? — A: Yes, they can battle. A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect can still be chosen for an effect. In addition, a battle itself is a rule that compares DP, not an effect. Therefore, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen, and then it can battle. If it loses the battle, it will be deleted as normal according to the rules.
- **Q7042** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q7043** (2026-08-18) Q: This card has multiple effects that trigger when it's played or when it digivolves. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q7044** (2026-08-18) Q: What does "effects don't affect" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents a card from being affected by effects. For example, your Digimon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced by 3000 if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q7045** (2026-08-18) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen. For example, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect.
- **Q7046** (2026-08-18) Q: Can a card that has an "effects don't affect" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. It won't be affected by it, but it can be given an effect. However, if an effect such as <Security A.> is given to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects, the Digimon won't be considered to have that effect.
- **Q7047** (2026-08-18) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "effects don't affect" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q7048** (2026-08-18) Q: If a card with an "effects don't affect" effect gains an effect then later loses the "effects don't affect" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the gained effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q7049** (2026-08-18) Q: A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
