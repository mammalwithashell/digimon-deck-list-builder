## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If [Darcmon] and [HippoGryphonmon] are in this Digimon's digivolution cards, by deleting 1 of your other Digimon, you may play 1 [Ornismon] from your trash without paying the cost. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When one of your other Digimon is deleted, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the deleted Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, you can. This effect reads "you may play," so if you perform the conditions specified for "by X," you may choose whether or not to activate the effect shown in text.
