## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with ＜Save＞ in its text and 1 card with the [Hunter] trait among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Main] If you have 4 or more Tamers in play, by suspending this Tamer, 1 of your Digimon with ＜Save＞ in its text gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.
