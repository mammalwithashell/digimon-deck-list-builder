## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent’s Turn] When an opponent’s Digimon attacks, if a card with [Armor Form] in its traits or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, you may switch the target of attack to this Digimon. [All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted, you may place the top card of this Digimon on top of your security stack face down to prevent that deletion.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1837** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reads, “if a card with [Armor Form] in its traits or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards”. Does having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits count? — A: No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
- **Q1838** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect also activate when this card is unsuspended? — A: Yes, it activates. In such cases, this card will be unsuspended when it battles with your opponent's attacking Digimon.
- **Q1839** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect for an attack by an opponent's Digimon with a "this Digimon can't be blocked" effect and switch the target of the attack to this card? — A: Yes, you can. This card's [Opponent's Turn] effect isn't considered blocking.
- **Q1840** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when this card has no digivolution cards? — A: No. When there are no digivolution cards, you can't perform "you may place the top card of this Digimon on top of your security stack face down," so you can't activate the effect.
