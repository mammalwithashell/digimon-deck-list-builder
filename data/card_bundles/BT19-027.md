### Effect
＜Decode (Blue Lv.5)＞ (When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than in battle, you may play 1 level 5 blue Digimon card from its digivolution cards without paying the cost.) ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-027 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3082** (2024-09-20) Q: What does this card's (Rule) mean, exactly? — A: This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also has the [Aquatic] type trait.
- **Q3083** (2024-09-20) Q: When I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to return this card to the bottom of the deck, can I activate this card's <Decode (Blue Lv.5)> and then return 1 of my opponent's level 6 or lower Digimon to the bottom of the deck? — A: Yes, you can activate <Decode (Blue Lv.5)> and return a Digimon to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q3084** (2024-11-22) Q: I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to return this card itself to the bottom of the deck, I use this card's <Decode (Blue Lv.5)> to play a card with an [On Play] effect, then I attempt to return my opponent's EX6-054 [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] to the bottom of the deck, and EX6-054 [Lucemon: Chaos Mode]'s [All Turns] effect plays a card with an [On Play] effect. At such times, do the [On Play] effects on cards played by these effects trigger simultaneously? — A: Yes, they trigger simultaneously. _(related: EX6-054)_
- **Q3085** (2024-11-22) Q: If I activate this card's [End of Your Turn] effect, return this card itself to the bottom of the deck, and use this card's <Decode (Blue Lv.5)> to play BT19-025 [MetalGreymon], at what timing is the DigiXros performed? — A: You perform the DigiXros at the timing when the card is played. You perform the DigiXros when this card's <Decode (Blue Lv.5)> is used to play BT19-025 [MetalGreymon], then you choose an opponent's Digimon to return to the bottom of the deck for this card's [End of Your Turn] effect. _(related: BT19-025)_
