## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Rush＞ [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon. [End of Attack] [On Deletion] You may trash 1 [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait card from your hand. If this effect trashed, ＜Draw 2＞. Then, you may play 1 red, blue or green Tamer card with inherited effects from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=AD1-002 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6052** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [End of Attack] [On Deletion] effect without trashing a card from my hand? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6903** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a digivolution requirement that digivolves from a Tamer mean that the Tamer that will become a digivolution card is treated as a Digimon when it digivolves? — A: No, it digivolves from the Tamer as-is. It isn't treated as a Digimon that digivolves, therefore "when a Digimon would digivolve" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. In addition, this digivolution requirement can be used to digivolve a Tamer even if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect activates.
- **Q6904** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a digivolution bonus draw performed even when a Tamer digivolves? — A: Yes, you perform a digivolution bonus draw. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6905** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: This card has <Rush>, so it can attack. Unless the other cards have an effect that allows them to attack the turn they were played, they can't attack.
- **Q6906** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q6907** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Security effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: No, it doesn't.
- **Q6908** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the Inherited effect in the lower text on a Tamer card in digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
