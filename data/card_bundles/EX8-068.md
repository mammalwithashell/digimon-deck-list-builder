## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have no face-up security cards, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Security] [All Turns] While you have 1 or more memory, none of your [DS] trait Digimon can be deleted in battle. [Main] Add your bottom security card to the hand. Then, place this card face up as the bottom security card.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card with the [DS] trait from your hand without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-068 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3954** (2024-11-22) Q: Is the "while you have no face-up security cards" condition met when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, it's met.
- **Q3955** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I also activate this card’s [Main] effect when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you can't add a card from your security stack to your hand, therefore you only place this card in your security stack.
- **Q3956** (2024-11-22) Q: What position on the memory gauge does "if you have 1 or more memory" refer to? — A: It refers to when the memory gauge is at 1 or further to the left on your side.
- **Q3957** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens to cards placed face up in the security stack by effects? — A: They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
- **Q3958** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens upon a security check for a security card that is placed face-up? — A: The check is performed with the card left revealed. Other than rules for cards left revealed, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security checks.
- **Q3959** (2024-11-22) Q: Does a card's [Security] effect trigger upon a security check with that card placed face-up? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3960** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens if I shuffle a security stack that includes security cards placed face-up? — A: Any face-up cards are placed face down, then you shuffle the cards. After shuffling, all cards are left face-down.
- **Q3961** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card placed face-up in my security stack. An opponent's Digimon digivolved, and I now have 1 or more memory. If my opponent's digivolved Digimon activates an effect and my Digimon with the [DS] trait attacks at such times, can this card's [All Turns] effect prevent my Digimon from being deleted in that battle? — A: Yes, it is not deleted. This card's [All Turns] effect will activate as soon as you have 1 or more memory, therefore none of your Digimon with the [DS] trait will be deleted in battle.
