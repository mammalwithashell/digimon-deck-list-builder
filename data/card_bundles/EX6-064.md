## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Beast], [Beastkin], [Holy Beast] or [Cherub] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [Your Turn] When an effect suspends one of your Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Beastkin], [Holy Beast] or [Cherub] trait in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.
