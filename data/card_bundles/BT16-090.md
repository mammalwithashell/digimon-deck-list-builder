## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Main] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your [Ukkomon] and trashing 1 of your Digimon in the breeding area, you may play 1 [BigUkkomon] from your hand to an empty space in your breeding area for a play cost of 3.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-090 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2684** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to trash 1 Digi-Egg card in the breeding area? — A: Yes, you can. Digi-Egg cards in the battle area and breeding area are treated as Digimon, so they can be trashed.
- **Q2685** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I perform just "deleting 1 of your [Ukkomon]" or "trashing 1 of your Digimon in the breeding area" to play a [BigUkkomon] into the breeding area for this card's [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't. You must perform both "deleting 1 of your [Ukkomon] and trashing 1 of your Digimon in the breeding area" to play a [BigUkkomon] into the breeding area.
- **Q2686** (2024-03-28) Q: After performing "deleting 1 of your [Ukkomon]" and "trashing 1 of your Digimon in the breeding area," can I choose to not play a [BigUkkomon] into the breeding area for this card's [Main] effect? — A: Yes, the effect reads “you may play."
- **Q2687** (2024-05-24) Q: Is <Overflow> processed when this card's [Main] effect trashes a Digimon with <Overflow> in the breeding area? — A: Yes, it's processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, it's processed because a card from the area is placed in the trash.
- **Q2688** (2024-05-24) Q: Is <Overflow> processed when this card's [Main] effect trashes a Digimon in the breeding area that has a card with <Overflow> in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it's processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, it's processed because it's placed into the trash from under a card.
