## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Pulsemon] in its text and 1 card with the [Abadin Electronics] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] While this Digimon has [Pulsemon] in its text, it gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-039 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2632** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include only either a card with [Pulsemon] in its text or a card with the [Abadin Electronics] trait, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q2633** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include both a card with [Pulsemon] in its text and a card with the [Abadin Electronics] trait, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
