## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ (If your opponent has 1 or more memory, this Digimon may attack.) [When Digivolving] This Digimon unsuspends. [All Turns] When your opponent's effects would delete this Digimon or return it to the hand or deck, by trashing 1 level 6 Digimon card from its digivolution cards, it isn't deleted or returned to the hand or deck.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. If you activate first, you declare the attack and suspend the Digimon at that point in time. If you then activate the "Unsuspend this Digimon" effect, you can unsuspend this Digimon itself.
