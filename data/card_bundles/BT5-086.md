### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ (If your opponent has 1 or more memory, this Digimon may attack.) [When Digivolving] This Digimon unsuspends. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-086 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1356** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card digivolves, can I activate <Blitz> first to attack, then use the "Unsuspend this Digimon" effect to unsuspend this Digimon? — A: Yes. If you activate <Blitz> first, you declare the attack and suspend the Digimon at that point in time. If you then activate the "Unsuspend this Digimon" effect, you can unsuspend this Digimon itself.
- **Q1357** (2024-03-28) Q: When this Digimon is deleted, I activate this card's [All Turns] effect to have this Digimon remain in the battle area. Does this cause "When another Digimon is deleted" effects to activate? — A: No. This card's [All Turns] effect prevents other effects from deleting this Digimon or returning it to its owner's hand or deck, so it isn't treated as being deleted by the game rules.
