## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards. That Digimon can't attack or block until the end of your opponent's next turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards. That Digimon can't attack or block until the end of your next turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST2-14 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q624** (2024-03-28) Q: If one of my opponent's Digimon has this card's effect used on it and is suspended, can I attack that Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q625** (2024-03-28) Q: If one of my opponent's Digimon has this card's effect used on it and it digivolves and now has 1 or more digivolution cards, how does this card's effect resolve? — A: This card can only be used to target cards that meet its requirements upon activation. So the effect would still apply, and even though the target Digimon has gained digivolution cards, it still can't attack or block.
