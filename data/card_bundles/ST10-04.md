## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow Digimon card and 1 purple Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. [Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a card with [Archangel] or [Fallen Angel] in its traits, reduce the memory cost of the digivolution by 2.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] You may DNA digivolve this Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play into a Digimon card in your hand by paying its DNA digivolve cost.

- **Q727** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [On Play] effect force me to reveal 3 cards from the top of my deck? — A: Yes. The effect reads, “Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck,” so if you don’t have 3 cards left in your deck, reveal as many as you can.
- **Q728** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's [On Play] effect reveals cards from the top of my deck. If I reveal both yellow and purple cards, do I have to add both of them to my hand? — A: Yes. The effect reads, “Add 1 card...to your hand,” so if you reveal a card that meets the requirements, you must add it to your hand.
- **Q729** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly? — A: At the end of your turn, this effect allows you DNA digivolve a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon in your hand that has [DNA Digivolve]. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
- **Q730** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card's inherited effect, can I DNA digivolve into to DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolve]? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolve].
- **Q731** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
- **Q3812** (2024-03-28) Q: I activated this card's [Your Turn] effect when I played [ST10-04 Gatomon]. At such times, I want to digivolve [ST10-04 Gatomon] into [Angewomon] or [LadyDevimon] with the cost reduced by 1, but can I use [ST10-04 Gatomon]'s [Your Turn] effect to reduce the digivolution cost again by 2? — A: Yes, that's correct.
