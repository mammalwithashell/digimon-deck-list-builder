## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Partition (black Lv.4 & yellow Lv.4)＞ [When Digivolving] Until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of their Digimon. Then, if DNA digivolving, place 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the number of cards in your or their security stack at the bottom of their security stack. (Rule) Trait: Has [Angel] type.

### Inherited Effect
＜Partition (black Lv.4 & yellow Lv.4)＞ (When this Digimon with each of the specified digivolution cards would leave the battle area other than by your effects or a battle, you may play 1 each of the specified cards without paying the costs.)

## Official Q&A
- The "until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of their Digimon" part activates even if not DNA digivolving. The "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the number of cards in your or their security stack at the bottom of their security stack" part only activates if DNA digivolving.
