## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT16-063 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2651** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect activate when performing a non-DNA digivolve? — A: The "until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon isn't affected by the effects of their Digimon" part activates even if not DNA digivolving. The "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the number of cards in your or their security stack at the bottom of their security stack" part only activates if DNA digivolving.
- **Q2652** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect place 1 of my opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the total number of cards in my or their security stack at the bottom of their security stack? — A: No, this effect places 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as the number of cards in either your or their security stack at the bottom of their security stack, not the total number of cards.
- **Q2653** (2024-11-22) Q: I have 5 security cards, and my opponent has 1 security card. Can I refer to the number of my opponent's security cards for this card's [When Digivolving] effect so I don't place their Digimon at the bottom of the security stack? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent has a Digimon with as high or lower a level than the number of either your or your opponent's security cards, you must place those Digimon at the bottom of their security stack for this card's [When Digivolving] effect.
