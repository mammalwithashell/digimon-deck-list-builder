## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX2-028 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3320** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card’s [End of Attack] effect, I place this Digimon under one of my other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card. What happens to the digivolution cards under this Digimon? — A: They're trashed. Only this card will be placed in your Digimon's digivolution cards by this effect.
- **Q3321** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [End of Attack] effect place this card under one of my Digimon that doesn't have a level? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q3322** (2024-03-28) Q: This card has two inherited effects. If this card becomes a digivolution card, can I activate both effects? — A: Yes, you can activate both.
- **Q3323** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Attack] effect on this Digimon itself to place this card under this Digimon as its bottom digivolution card? — A: No, you can't. This card's Japanese [End of Attack] effect was corrected so that the target is "your other Digimon." Please see the "Errata Cards" on the official website.
