## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving][When Attacking][Once Per Turn] You may return 1 card with [Hybrid] in its traits from this Digimon's digivolution cards to your hand to return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the same level as the returned card to its owner's hand. Trash all of the digivolution cards of that Digimon. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When an effect adds a card to your hand, you may unsuspend 1 of your Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- It means the effect can be activated both [When Digivolving] and [When Attacking]. You can activate it as a [When Digivolving] effect when you digivolve this Digimon, then activate it again on following turns as a [When Attacking] effect by attacking with this Digimon.
