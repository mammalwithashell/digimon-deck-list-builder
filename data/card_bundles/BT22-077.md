＜Iceclad＞ ＜Blocker＞ [When Digivolving] If this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards, trash any 4 digivolution cards from your opponent's Digimon. ## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4941** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
- **Q4942** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if this Digimon's stack doesn't have 2 or more same-level cards, this card's [When Digivolving] effect will return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 1 or fewer digivolution cards to the bottom of the deck.
- **Q4985** (2025-07-04) Q: I have an unsuspended BT22-077 [Dianamon] with this card in its digivolution cards in the battle area. At the end of my turn, can I activate this card's inherited effect to attack, then use BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect to unsuspend? — A: Yes, you can. If you use this card's inherited effect to attack, then use BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect to unsuspend, the counter timing will occur once all other processing has been resolved.
- **Q4986** (2025-07-04) Q: I have a suspended BT22-077 [Dianamon] with this card in its digivolution cards in the battle area. At the end of my turn, can I activate BT22-077 [Dianamon]'s [End of Your Turn] effect, unsuspend, and then use this card's inherited effect to attack? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6247** (2026-05-08) Q: At the end of my turn, can I use another effect to DNA digivolve using 2 cards including this Digimon, then activate this card's inherited effect once it becomes a digivolution card? — A: No, you can't. This card isn't yet a digivolution card at the End of Your Turn timing, therefore this card's inherited effect doesn't trigger.
