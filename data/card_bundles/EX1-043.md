## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives, you may unsuspend this Digimon. [Your Turn] For each Digimon card with [Insectoid] in its traits in this Digimon's digivolution cards, it gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. It doesn't matter which of your Digimon is the one deleting the opponent's Digimon in battle and surviving. As long as it has [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits, you can unsuspend this Digimon.
