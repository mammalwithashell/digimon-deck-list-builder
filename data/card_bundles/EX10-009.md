## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] [On Deletion] Delete all of your opponent's lowest DP Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, trash their deck's top 5 cards. [When Attacking] If your opponent has 10 or more cards in their trash, you may play 1 red or purple 5000 DP or lower Digimon card from your trash to your empty breeding area without paying the cost. [End of Your Turn] This Digimon may attack. (Rule) Trait: Has [Fallen Angel] Type.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-009 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5016** (2025-09-05) Q: My opponent has no Digimon. I activated this card's [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] effect, but none of my opponent's Digimon were deleted. At such times, is the "if this effect didn't delete" condition met? — A: Yes, it's met.
- **Q5017** (2025-09-05) Q: My opponent only has 1 Digimon, and it has an effect that prevents deletion. I activated this card's [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] effect, but none of my opponent's Digimon were deleted. At such times, is the "if this effect didn't delete" condition met? — A: Yes, it's met.
- **Q5018** (2025-09-05) Q: My opponent has 1 Digimon with 6000 DP and an effect that prevents deletion, and they have 1 Digimon with 6000 DP and no effects. Is the "if this effect didn't delete" condition met if I activate this card's [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] effect and delete their Digimon other than the Digimon with an effect that prevents deletion? — A: No, it isn't met. The "if this effect didn't delete" condition is only met when no Digimon are deleted by this effect. If even 1 Digimon is deleted, this condition isn't met.
- **Q5019** (2025-09-05) Q: Do [On Play] effects trigger for Digimon played into the breeding area by this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: No, they don't trigger.
- **Q5656** (2025-12-25) Q: When my EX10-009 [Creepymon] attacks, in what order do that Digimon's [When Attacking] effect and this card's {Trash} [Your Turn] effect activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if you use this card's {Trash} [Your Turn] effect to digivolve that Digimon before activating EX10-009 [Creepymon]'s [When Attacking] effect, you won't be able to activate that [When Attacking] effect.
