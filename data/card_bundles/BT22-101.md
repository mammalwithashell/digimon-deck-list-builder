[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Tamer unsuspends, if you don't have [Alphamon], this Tamer may digivolve into [Alphamon] in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.
