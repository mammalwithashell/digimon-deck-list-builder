## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent's Turn][Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon digivolves into a level 5 or higher Digimon, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-051 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3236** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [Opponent’s Turn] effect activate when an opponent’s Digimon in their breeding area digivolves into a level 5 or higher Digimon? — A: No, the effect doesn't count Digimon in breeding areas.
- **Q3237** (2024-03-28) Q: Does “the same name as this Digimon” refer to Digimon with the name [Infermon]? — A: No. It’s not referring to this card’s name, but the name of the Digimon that has this card in its digivolution cards.
- **Q3238** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent digivolves one of their Digimon into a level 5 or higher Digimon, and deletes this Digimon with its [When Digivolving] effect. Do I gain 1 memory from this Digimon's [Opponent's Turn] effect? — A: No, this Digimon is deleted before its effect can activate.
