## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn. If there are 3 or more Digimon, it further gets -3000 DP.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by your effects, by deleting 1 of your Tokens or other [Puppet] trait Digimon, it doesn't leave.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST19-11 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q857** (2024-04-19) Q: Does this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect count both my Digimon and my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it does. If there are 3 or more total Digimon between you and your opponent, you further reduce this DP-reducing effect by 3000.
- **Q858** (2024-04-19) Q: What does "it further gets -3000 DP" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect mean, exactly? — A: If the conditions are met, the -3000 DP effect will further get -3000 DP for a total reduction of -6000 DP for 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
- **Q859** (2024-04-19) Q: What does "when [...] would leave the battle area" in this card's [All Turns] inherited effect mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
