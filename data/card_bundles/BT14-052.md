## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-052 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2420** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "(Rule) Name: Treated as having [Leomon]" mean, exactly? — A: This is a special rule for this card that treats it as a card that has [Leomon] in its name. This is a rule and not an effect, therefore this card is treated as having [Leomon] in its name when the card is in your hand, breeding area, or any other area.
- **Q2421** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card be selected as the target of an effect that targets exactly [Leomon] due to the text "(Rule) Name: Treated as having [Leomon]"? — A: No, it can't. This card's name is treated as having [Leomon] in its name, but it is not treated as [Leomon].
