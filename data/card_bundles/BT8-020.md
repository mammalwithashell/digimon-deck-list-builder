### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] You may DNA digivolve this Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play into a Digimon card in your hand by paying its DNA digivolve cost.

## Official Q&A
- At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon in your hand that has [DNA Digivolution]. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
