### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] You may DNA digivolve this Digimon and one of your other Digimon in play into a Digimon card in your hand by paying its DNA digivolve cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-020 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1708** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s inherited effect work, exactly? — A: At the end of your turn, this effect allows you to DNA digivolve a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards, along with one of your other Digimon, into a Digimon in your hand that has [DNA Digivolution]. Note that this happens before it becomes your opponent’s turn.
- **Q1709** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card's inherited effect, can I DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn't have [DNA Digivolution]? — A: No. You can't ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn't have [DNA Digivolution].
- **Q1710** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
