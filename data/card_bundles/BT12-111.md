### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, you may place up to 5 Digimon cards with the [Bagra Army] trait from your trash as digivolution cards of this Digimon. [Opponent's Turn] When an opponent's Digimon digivolves or attacks, by trashing 5 cards in this Digimon's digivolution cards, return all Tamers to their owners' hands.
