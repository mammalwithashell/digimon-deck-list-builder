## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] [On Deletion] By trashing 1 card with [Chuumon] or [Sukamon] in its name from your hand or your Digimon's digivolution cards, you may change the base name, color and DP of 1 of your opponent’s Digimon to [Sukamon], white and 3000 until their turn ends.

### Special Play Condition
Assembly -4: 3 Lv.4 or lower Digimon cards w/[Sukamon] in name

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any other Digimon with [Sukamon] in their names are deleted, reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 play cost 3 or lower Digimon card with [Chuumon] or [Sukamon] in its name among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-031 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7294** (2026-09-18) Q: What does a "change the base name, color and DP of 1 of your opponent’s Digimon to [Sukamon], white and 3000" effect do, exactly? — A: This effect changes 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a Digimon whose original name is [Sukamon], its original color is white, and its original DP is 3000.
- **Q7295** (2026-09-18) Q: If a card such as [BT15-035 Geremon] has a (Rule) that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the (Rule) will also be changed. For example, [BT15-035 Geremon]'s (Rule) gives it an original name of [Geremon/Numemon], but if its original card information is changed by this effect, its original name becomes just [Sukamon]. _(related: BT15-035)_
- **Q7296** (2026-09-18) Q: If a card such as [EX1-005 Tyrannomon] has an effect that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the effect remains as-is. For example, if [EX1-005 Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and it will also be treated as green due to [EX1-005 Tyrannomon]'s effect, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon. _(related: EX1-005)_
- **Q7297** (2026-09-18) Q: If a card is changed to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, then it digivolves or its top card is trashed by <De-Digivolve> or some other effect, what happens to that card's information? — A: It remains as a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]. The gained effect is carried over even if the Digimon digivolves or one or more of its top cards are trashed.
- **Q7298** (2026-09-18) Q: What happens if a card is changed to a white card with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, then its top card is trashed by <De-Digivolve> or some other effect and the top card becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card? — A: It remains changed to a white card with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, but any card information that it didn't originally have can't be changed. If it becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card, or if it becomes a card that didn't originally have DP, then it will remain without DP.
- **Q7299** (2026-09-18) Q: Does this card's inherited effect trigger when either my or my opponent's Digimon with [Sukamon] in its name gets deleted? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q7300** (2026-09-18) Q: Can "from your hand or your Digimon's digivolution cards" reference any Digimon's digivolution cards, including my opponent's? — A: No. It can only reference your Digimon's digivolution cards.
