## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-037 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7014** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I link a card without <Link> with a "you may link" effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7015** (2026-08-18) Q: What does "may battle" mean, exactly? — A: This effect immediately initiates a battle. If this effect is activated, the chosen Digimon battle according to the standard rules.
- **Q7016** (2026-08-18) Q: If 2 Digimon including a Digimon that has an "effects don't affect" effect are chosen for a "may battle" effect, can those Digimon battle? — A: Yes, they can battle. A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect can still be chosen for an effect. In addition, a battle itself is a rule that compares DP, not an effect. Therefore, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen, and then it can battle. If it loses the battle, it will be deleted as normal according to the rules.
- **Q7017** (2026-08-18) Q: This card has 3 different cards in its App Fusion requirements. What combinations are possible for App Fusion? — A: App Fusion is possible with the following combinations. ●[Weathermon] with [Rocketmon] link card ●[Weathermon] with [Newsmon] link card ●[Rocketmon] with [Weathermon] link card ●[Rocketmon] with [Newsmon] link card ●[Newsmon] with [Weathermon] link card ●[Newsmon] with [Rocketmon] link card
