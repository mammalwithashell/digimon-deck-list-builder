## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon. Then, 1 of their Digimon can't unsuspend until the end of their turn. [All Turns] When this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle, if you have 3 or more security cards, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack. If you have 3 or fewer, gain 2 memory.
