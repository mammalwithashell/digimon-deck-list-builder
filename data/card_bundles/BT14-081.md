## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 level 4 or lower card with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait from your trash without paying the cost. If [Eiji Nagasumi] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, add 2 to the number of cards this effect may play. [When Attacking][Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your opponent's level 3 or lower Digimon, unsuspend this Digimon. For each of your Digimon, add 1 to the level this effect may choose. [Your Turn] The turn end condition is the opponent having 3 or more memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-081 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2449** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's [Your Turn] effect mean? — A: Normally, the turn will end and switch when the memory moves to 1 or more on your opponent's side. But while this effect is activated, the turn won't end unless the memory moves to 3 or more on your opponent's side. Your turn will continue when your opponent's memory is at 1 or 2.
- **Q2450** (2024-03-28) Q: If the memory moves to 2 or less on my opponent's side during my opponent's turn, does the turn switch to my turn with this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: No, this effect only applies during your turn. Your opponent's turn continues until the memory moves to 1 or more from your perspective.
- **Q2451** (2024-03-28) Q: The memory moved to 3 or more on my opponent's side and the turn ended while this card's [Your Turn] effect was activated. If the memory moves to 1 or 2 on my opponent's side due to an [End of Your Turn] effect, does my turn continue? — A: Yes, your turn continues.
- **Q2452** (2024-03-28) Q: When the memory moves to 3 or more on my opponent's side while this card's [Your Turn] effect is activated, does <Blitz> allow for attacking? — A: No. <Blitz> still requires the memory to be at 1 or more on your opponent's side for attacking.
- **Q2453** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play just 2 of the target cards from my trash when [Eiji Nagasumi] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards and I have 3 or more level 4 or lower cards with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait in my trash? — A: No, you can't. This effect is a "You may play" effect, but it doesn't include "up to," therefore you must choose to either play no cards or as many as possible.
- **Q2454** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if I declare a "pass" when I have this Digimon? — A: The memory will move to 3 on your opponent's side, and the turn will end if there are no other effects pending activation, etc.
