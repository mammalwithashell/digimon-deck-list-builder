＜Rush＞ (This Digimon can attack the turn it comes into play.) [On Play] You may place 1 [Kinkakumon] and/or 1 [Ginkakumon] from your trash in this card's digivolution cards in any order. If you place 2 cards with this effect, trigger ＜Draw 1＞ (Draw 1 card from your deck) and gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-075 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1464** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card’s [On Play] effect to place a [Ginkakumon Promote] from my trash in this card’s digivolution cards? — A: No. This card’s [On Play] effect can only be used to place cards whose names match either [Kinkakumon] or [Ginkakumon] exactly, therefore you can't place [Ginkakumon Promote] in digivolution cards.
- **Q1465** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have both [Kinkakumon] and [Ginkakumon] in my trash, does this card’s [On Play] effect force me to place both cards in this card’s digivolution cards? — A: No, "You may place..." means that you can choose if you want to place them as digivolution cards or not. However, if you choose to place, you must place as many digivolution cards as possible. So, if you have both [Kinkakumon] and [Ginkakumon], you can't choose to place only one of them.
