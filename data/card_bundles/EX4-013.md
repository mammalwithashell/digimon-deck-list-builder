## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Play this card without battling and without paying the cost. At the end of the turn, return this Digimon to your hand. [On Play][When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If no Digimon was deleted by this effect, suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon, and that Digimon doesn't unsuspend during your opponent's next unsuspend phase.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-013 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3450** (2024-03-28) Q: I use the [Security] effect to play this card, then it was deleted during that same turn. In such cases, can I return this card from my trash to my hand at the end of the turn? — A: No, you can't. In such cases the card remains placed in your trash.
- **Q3451** (2024-03-28) Q: When an opponent's Digimon isn't deleted by this card's [On Play][When Attacking] effect, can I specify 1 of my opponent's suspended Digimon and prevent it from unsuspending during my opponent's next unsuspend phase? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3452** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if this card is flipped over upon a security check from an opponent's Digimon's attack in a state where a "your opponent's [Security] effects don't activate" effect has activated? — A: Since the [Security] effect didn't activate, the battle proceeds normally. At the end of the battle, this card is trashed.
