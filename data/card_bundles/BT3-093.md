## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 memory or less, set your memory to 3. [On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 blue and 1 green Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.
