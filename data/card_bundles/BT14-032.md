## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Add the top card of your security stack to the hand. Then, you may place 1 card with [Sukamon] in its name from your hand on top of your security stack.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-032 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2405** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s effect work if it activates when I have 0 cards in my security stack? — A: You can't add a card to the hand because you have 0 cards in your security stack, but you may perform "place 1 card with [Sukamon] in its name from your hand on top of your security stack."
- **Q2406** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [On Play] effect to add 1 card from my security stack to the hand, do I have to reveal the added card to my opponent? — A: No, there are no requirements for the card you add to your hand with this effect, so you don't have to reveal it to your opponent.
