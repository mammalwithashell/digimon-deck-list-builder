## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Breeding] [Start of Your Main Phase] Place the top card of your Digi-Egg deck as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, and delete all of your Digimon. If this effect deleted, place 1 [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait card from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card. [Breeding] [End of Opponent’s Turn] By deleting this Digimon with 7 or more digivolution cards with different names, you may play 1 [Ogudomon] from your trash without paying the cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Breeding] [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When a [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait Digimon card would be played, you may reduce the play cost by 3. If this Digimon has 5 or more digivolution cards with different names, you may reduce the play cost by 4 instead.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-006 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3694** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I have to delete all of my Digimon for this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] effect even when I have 0 cards in my Digi-Egg deck and can't place a card in digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you must delete them.
- **Q3695** (2024-03-28) Q: If [Leviamon] and [Leviamon (X Antibody)] are in this card's digivolution cards, are they considered "cards with different names"? — A: Yes, they are. When cards such as [Leviamon] and [Leviamon (X Antibody)] differ in that one has [X Antibody] in its name and the other doesn't, you can reference the cards as if they have different names.
- **Q3696** (2024-03-28) Q: Does [Gate of Deadly Sins] itself in digivolution cards also count for this card's "if this Digimon has 5 or more digivolution cards with different names"? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q3697** (2024-03-28) Q: If multiple copies of this card are in digivolution cards, does its inherited effect overlap? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3698** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I have to activate this card's inherited effect when a Digimon card would be played? — A: No, you can choose not to.
- **Q3699** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I also activate this card's inherited effect when a Digimon would be played by an effect? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3700** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's inherited effect to reduce the digivolution cost by 3 even if this card has 5 or more digivolution cards with different names? — A: Yes, you can.
