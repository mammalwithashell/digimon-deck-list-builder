## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [On Deletion] By trashing 1 card with [Numemon] or [Sukamon] in its name in your hand, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gains ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of their turn. ＜Rule＞ Name: Also treated as [Numemon].

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gains ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of their turn.

## Official Q&A
- This is a special rule for this card that treats it as a card that has the [Numemon] name. This is a rule and not an effect, therefore this card is treated as having the [Numemon] name when the card is in your hand, breeding area, or any other area.
