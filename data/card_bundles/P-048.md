### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may place 3 non-Digi-Egg cards from your trash at the bottom of your deck in any order to unsuspend this Digimon and 1 of your Tamers. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When a card is returned from your trash to your deck, gain 1 memory.

- **Q4166** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect when I have 2 or less non-Digi-Egg cards in my trash? — A: No, you can't.
