## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if there are 6 or fewer total cards in both players' security stacks, reduce the cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] By placing 1 other Digimon as the top security card, trash both players' top security cards. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When your security stack is removed from, you may play 1 play cost 8 or lower [Angel], [Archangel] or [Iliad] trait card from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6315** (2026-05-08) Q: In what order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q7004** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to play [BT25-044 Junomon], can I activate [BT25-044 Junomon]'s 1st effect and reduce the play cost by a total of 10? — A: Yes, the play cost will be reduced by a total of 10. _(a ruling on BT26-033; this card is named in it)_
