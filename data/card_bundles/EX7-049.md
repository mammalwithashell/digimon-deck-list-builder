### Effect
[On Play] [When Attacking] ＜De-Digivolve 4＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. [When Digivolving] None of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon can digivolve until the end of their turn. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by your effects, you may play 1 Digimon card with the [Rock Dragon] or [Earth Dragon] trait from your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX7-049 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3853** (2024-05-24) Q: Can my opponent digivolve a level 4 or lower Digimon that isn't affected by effects in the battle area during their next turn after this card's [When Digivolving] effect has activated? — A: Yes, they can digivolve it. Digimon that aren't affected by effects aren't affected by this card's [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q3854** (2024-05-24) Q: Can my opponent digivolve a level 4 or lower Digimon in the breeding area during their next turn after this card's [When Digivolving] effect has activated? — A: Yes, they can digivolve it. This card's [When Digivolving] effect only affects your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon in the battle area. It doesn't affect Digimon in the breeding area.
- **Q3855** (2024-05-24) Q: Can my opponent digivolve a level 4 or lower Digimon that was played after this card's [When Digivolving] effect has activated? — A: No, they can't digivolve it. This card's [When Digivolving] effect also affects Digimon that were played after it activated.
- **Q3856** (2024-05-24) Q: I activated this card's [All Turns] effect when this card in the battle area was placed for a DigiXros. Can I place the card that was played by this [All Turns] effect for that DigiXros? — A: No, you can't place it for that DigiXros. You declare the DigiXros at the same time as when you choose the cards to place for the DigiXros. Once placed, additional cards to place can't be chosen.
- **Q6719** (2026-06-19) Q: If I choose this card in my battle area with the DigiXros effect on [EX3-014 Dorbickmon], can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: EX3-014)_
