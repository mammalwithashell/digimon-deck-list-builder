## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand] [Counter] ＜Blast Digivolve＞ (One of your Digimon may digivolve into this card without paying the cost.) [On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete up to 15000 DP total worth of your opponent's Digimon. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] For every 10 total cards in both players' trashes, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

### Special Rule
＜Overflow (-5)＞ (As this card moves from the battle area or under a card to another area, lose 5 memory.)

## Official Q&A
- No, it means for every 10 cards in the total sum of cards in your trash and the cards in your opponent's trash. For example, if you have 12 cards in your trash and your opponent has 8 cards in their trash, the total would be 20. Therefore, you trash 2 cards from your opponent's security stack.
