## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-014 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6258** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I activate this card's [Main] effect even when my opponent doesn't have any Digimon that can be deleted? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6259** (2026-05-08) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 4000 DP or less. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [Main] effect by not choosing a card to delete? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent has a Digimon with 4000 DP or less, you must choose that card and delete it.
- **Q6260** (2026-05-08) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 4000 DP or less. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [Main] effect by choosing an opponent's Digimon with an effect that prevents deletion and 4000 DP or less? — A: Yes, you can.
