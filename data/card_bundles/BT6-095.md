## Printed text (official)
### Effect
If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Delete all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-095 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1481** (2024-03-28) Q: "If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play, you may use this Option card without meeting its color requirements.” How does this effect work, exactly? — A: If you have a Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its type in play in your battle area, you can use this Option card even if you don’t have a red Digimon or Tamer in play.
- **Q1482** (2024-03-28) Q: What does “all of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP” mean, exactly? — A: The effect deletes your opponent’s Digimon with the lowest DP. If multiple Digimon are tied for lowest, all of them are deleted.
