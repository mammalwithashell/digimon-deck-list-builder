## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3523** (2024-03-28) Q: I played a Digimon with 5000 DP or less in a state where my opponent used this card's [When Digivolving][On Deletion] effect to give all of my Digimon -5000 DP. Can I activate my played Digimon's [On Play] effect before it's deleted by its DP becoming 0? — A: No, you can't. If a card is played at the same time as deletion by DP becoming 0, the [On Play] effect is triggered, but it can't activate because the card is deleted before it activates.
- **Q3524** (2024-03-28) Q: Do I have to activate this card's [End of Attack] effect? — A: Yes, you must activate it whenever possible.
- **Q3525** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's [End of Attack] effect also activate when my opponent has no Digimon in play? — A: Yes, it does.
