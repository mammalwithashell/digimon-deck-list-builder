## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-010 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3704** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to pay 3 cost even when I don't have any [Legend-Arms] trait or level 6 Digimon? — A: No, you can't. The activation conditions include paying 3 cost and placing this card as 1 of your [Legend-Arms] trait or 1 of your level 6 Digimon's bottom digivolution card, therefore the effect can't be activated just by paying 3 cost.
- **Q3705** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use a Digimon that was played this turn or a suspended Digimon to attack with this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't. This effect does not allow you to attack with Digimon that are in a state where they can't attack.
- **Q3706** (2024-03-28) Q: If a [RagnaLoardmon] with this card in digivolution cards is deleted in battle against a Security Digimon, does that Security Digimon's [Security] effect activate? — A: No, it doesn’t activate. The activation timing for the [Security] effect is before the battle with the Security Digimon, so even if a [RagnaLoardmon] with this card in its digivolution cards is deleted in battle, the [Security] effect can't be activated.
