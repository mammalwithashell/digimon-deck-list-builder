## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if this Digimon is attacking, you may switch the target of attack to 1 of your opponent’s suspended Digimon. [Your Turn] This Digimon gets +4000 DP. [End of Attack][Once Per Turn] If [GranKuwagamon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon and unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-055 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1849** (2024-03-28) Q: I activate this card’s [When Digivolving] effect in the middle of an attack to suspend an opponent's Digimon. Can this card's effect then switch the target of attack to one of my opponent’s other suspended Digimon? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q1850** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s effect reads, “If [GranKuwagamon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards”. Does having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits count? — A: No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [GranKuwagamon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
- **Q1851** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I switch the target of attack to an opponent's Digimon with a "this Digimon can't be attacked" effect when I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect during an attack? — A: Yes, you can. A "this Digimon can't be attacked" effect prevents a Digimon from being chosen as the target upon an attack declaration, therefore an attack can be switched to a Digimon with such an effect by using an effect that switches the target after the attack declaration.
