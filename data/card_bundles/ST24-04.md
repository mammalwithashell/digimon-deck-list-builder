## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST24-04 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6206** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if I use this card's [When Moving] [On Play] effect and only 1 card with the [DATA SQUAD] trait is among the revealed cards? — A: You add that card to your hand.
- **Q6207** (2026-05-08) Q: If I use this card's [When Moving] [On Play] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q6208** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6209** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q6210** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
