## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] This Digimon can digivolve into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits in your hand for its digivolution cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-040 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3226** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving with this card's [When Attacking] effect, paying the digivolution cost moves the memory gauge to 1 or more on my opponent’s side. What happens to the attack? — A: The attack continues, but the turn doesn't switch over yet. If the memory gauge is still at 1 or more on your opponent’s side after the attack has ended and all effects triggered by it have been activated, only then does the turn end.
- **Q3227** (2024-03-28) Q: I attack with [EX1-035 Kabuterimon] and digivolve into this card with its [When Attacking] effect. Can I then digivolve again during the same attack with this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, the time to trigger [When Attacking] effects has already passed, so the effect doesn't activate. _(related: EX1-035)_
- **Q3228** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have a card in hand with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits, does this card’s [When Attacking] effect force me to digivolve when attacking? — A: No. The effect reads, “This Digimon can digivolve,” so you can choose not to.
- **Q3229** (2024-03-28) Q: When digivolving into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits in my hand with this Digimon's [When Attacking] effect, can I ignore that card's digivolution requirements? — A: No, you can’t ignore its digivolution requirements.
