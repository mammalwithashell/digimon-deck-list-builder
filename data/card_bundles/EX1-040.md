## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] This Digimon can digivolve into a Digimon card with [Insectoid] or [Ancient Insect] in its traits in your hand for its digivolution cost.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] When this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
- The attack continues, but the turn doesn't switch over yet. If the memory gauge is still at 1 or more on your opponent’s side after the attack has ended and all effects triggered by it have been activated, only then does the turn end.
