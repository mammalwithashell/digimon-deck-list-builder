## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Choose 1 other Digimon. By playing 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card from the chosen Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost, delete the chosen Digimon. [On Deletion] You may play 1 card with a play cost of 5 or less from under your Tamers without paying the cost.

DigiXros -1: [Nene Amano]×[Shademon]/[Luminamon]
