## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if your opponent has 2 or more Digimon, return 1 of their level 4 or lower Digimon to the hand. [On Deletion] You may play 1 [Blue Flare] or [Xros Heart] trait Digimon card with a play cost of 5 or less from under your Tamers without paying the cost. Then, ＜Save＞.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, it is. In this case, even if de-digivolves an opponent's Digimon into a Digi-Egg card without DP, it won't be trashed until this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, so that card is still treated as a Digimon. Therefore, this effect's "if your opponent has 2 or more Digimon" condition is still met, and you can return 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to the hand. After returning it, the Digi-Egg card is trashed. In addition, if a Digi-Egg card is returned to the hand by this effect, that card is placed at the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck.
