## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [Royal Knight] in its traits in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Unsuspend 1 of your Digimon. Then, if that Digimon is [Jesmon GX], activate 1 of its [When Digivolving] effects.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT10-110 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2039** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a [Jesmon GX] that has <Security Attack> in play, while my opponent has a [BT10-042 Venusmon] in play. If I use this card and target my [Jesmon GX], can this card's effect activate [Jesmon GX]'s [When Digivolving] effects? — A: No. Because [Jesmon GX]'s [When Digivolving] and [When Attacking] effects cannot be activated due to your opponent's [BT10-042 Venusmon], even this card can't activate those effects in these circumstances. _(related: BT10-042)_
