## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-014 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3876** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, you can suspend either player's Digimon.
- **Q3952** (2026-02-06) Q: When multiple "when your Digimon attacks, you may digivolve that Digimon" effects trigger, I activated the 1st effect, then the digivolved Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect triggered. At such times, in what order can I activate the other "when your Digimon attacks, you may digivolve that Digimon" effects and that [When Digivolving] effect? — A: The [When Digivolving] effect activates first. As soon as your Digimon attacks, the "when your Digimon attacks, you may digivolve that Digimon" effects trigger simultaneously. You activate the 1st effect, digivolve a Digimon, then its [When Digivolving] effect triggers, and you therefore activate it first due to the derived trigger rule.
