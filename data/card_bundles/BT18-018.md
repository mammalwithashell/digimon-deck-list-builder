## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] For each color in this Digimon's digivolution cards, trash any 1 digivolution card from your opponent's Digimon and suspend 1 of their Digimon. Then, this Digimon may attack. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon deletes your opponent's Digimon in battle, it gains ＜Security A. +1＞ for the turn and unsuspends.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-018 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2925** (2024-06-21) Q: Can I use this card's digivolution requirement to digivolve for a cost of 5 even when my [Takuya Kanbara] has more than 5 [Hybrid] trait cards under it? — A: Yes, it can digivolve.
- **Q2926** (2024-06-21) Q: If this card gains <Piercing> from another card's effect, then this card attacks and deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle, do I perform the security check for <Piercing> after this card's [Your Turn] effect activates? — A: Yes. After this card's [Your Turn] effect activates, then you perform the security check for <Piercing>. This card's [Your Turn] effect and <Piercing> trigger simultaneously, but the activated <Piercing> is processed immediately before the attack ends, therefore <Security A. +1> is first gained from this card's [Your Turn] effect, then you perform the security check for <Piercing>.
- **Q2927** (2024-06-21) Q: BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto] digivolved into this card, and the memory gauge moved to 1 on my opponent's side. Then, I used this card’s [When Digivolving] effect to attack and used this card's [Your Turn] effect to unsuspend it. Can I then use the [End of Your Turn] inherited effect on BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto] in this card's digivolution cards to attack a player? — A: Yes, it can attack. When digivolving into this card, the turn end conditions won't be met until all of the resulting processing has been resolved. In this case, this card's [When Digivolving] effect, [Your Turn] effect, and any effects triggered by them must first be resolved, and then the turn end conditions will be met. Then, the end of the turn timing will occur and BT18-088 [Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto]'s [End of Your Turn] effect will trigger. _(related: BT18-088)_
- **Q4289** (2025-01-24) Q: If there are 3 colors in this card's digivolution cards, can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to choose and trash 3 digivolution cards from my opponent's Digimon, then choose and suspend 3 of their Digimon? — A: Yes. For each color in this Digimon's digivolution cards, you choose and trash 1 digivolution card from your opponent's Digimon and suspend 1 of their Digimon.
- **Q6583** (2026-06-19) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q6584** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6585** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card cannot attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q6586** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6587** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6588** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
