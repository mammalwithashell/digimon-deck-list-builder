## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may play 1 white Tamer card with a play cost of 4 or less from your hand without paying its memory cost. Then, your opponent may play 1 Tamer card from their hand without paying its memory cost.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] You may play 1 white Tamer card with a play cost of 4 or less from your hand without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1469** (2024-03-28) Q: If I don’t play a Tamer using this card’s [On Play] effect, does that prevent my opponent from playing a Tamer card, as well? — A: No. Even if you choose not to play a Tamer, your opponent can still use this card’s effect to play a Tamer of their own.
- **Q2845** (2025-07-04) Q: My BT6-083 [Eosmon] was played, then this card's [All Turns] effect and another effect triggered simultaneously. If another effect activates and the DP of my played BT6-083 [Eosmon] changes from 4000 to 5000, then I activate this card's [All Turns] effect, what is the DP of Digimon I can delete? — A: 5000 DP or less. If the same reference to a "played" trigger condition being performed isn't being made for an effect when it has a "when X is played" trigger condition, the state when the effect is processed will be referenced. In this case, processing was performed when your BT6-083 [Eosmon] had 5000 DP, therefore you can delete a Digimon with 5000 DP or less. _(a ruling on BT17-076; this card is named in it)_
