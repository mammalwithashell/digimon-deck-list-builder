## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] For the turn, 1 of your [Marcus Damon]s is also treated as a 3000 DP Digimon, can't digivolve, and gains ＜Rush＞ and ＜Alliance＞. Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your yellow or red Tamers are deleted, you may place 1 [Marcus Damon] from your trash as the top security card.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your yellow or red Tamers are deleted, you may place 1 [Marcus Damon] from your trash as the top security card.

## Official Q&A
- It causes that card to also be treated as a Digimon with X000 DP. For example, if a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon, it can attack like a standard Digimon, and it will gain inherited effects from cards stacked under it. However, even if a Tamer is played and then treated as a Digimon in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn.
