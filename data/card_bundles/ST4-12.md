### Effect
[When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon can't attack or block until the end of their next turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST4-12 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q654** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect on an opponent's Digimon. Can I attack that Digimon if it's suspended? — A: Yes. The effect simply prevents the Digimon from attacking or blocking. You can still attack it.
- **Q655** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect on an opponent's Digimon. If that Digimon digivolves into a different Digimon, can it attack and block again? — A: No, the effect persists, even if they digivolve.
- **Q656** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect on an opponent's Digimon. If that Digimon activates an effect that allows it to attack other Digimon or players, what happens? — A: The effect preventing it from attacking takes priority. Your opponent's Digimon still can't attack.
