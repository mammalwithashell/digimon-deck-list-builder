## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1202** (2024-03-28) Q: When I digivolved into this card, my opponent had 3 or less cards in their hand. Do I still gain memory from the [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you don't.
- **Q1203** (2024-03-28) Q: What exactly does "this Digimon is unblockable" mean? — A: If you have a Digimon with this effect, your opponent's Digimon can't block against its attacks.
- **Q1204** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "this Digimon is unblockable" mean this Digimon can't attack my opponent's suspended Digimon? — A: No. Attacking suspended Digimon isn't blocking, so you can attack them.
