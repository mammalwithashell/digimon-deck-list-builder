## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By trashing 1 card in your hand, you may return 1 [Titan] trait card from your trash to the hand. After, if played by an effect, delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When your hand is trashed from, if this Digimon has the [Titan] trait, delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-080 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6382** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "after" in this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] [When Digivolving] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't trash 1 card in your hand, you can't process the part after "after" in this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q6383** (2026-05-08) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect to trash a card that has a "when this card is trashed from the hand" effect and return the trashed card to the hand. Can I then activate the trashed card's "when this card is trashed from the hand" effect? — A: No, you can't activate it.
- **Q6384** (2026-05-08) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect to trash a card in the hand and return the trashed card to the hand. Can I then activate a "when your hand is trashed from" effect? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6385** (2026-05-08) Q: When this card was played by an effect, can I use its [On Play] [When Attacking] effect to trash a card in my hand but then choose to not delete an opponent's Digimon? — A: No, you can't. If this card was played or digivolved by an effect, you must delete an opponent's Digimon.
- **Q6715** (2026-06-19) Q: Can I choose a Digi-Egg card for the card returned to the hand with this card's [On Play] [When Attacking] effect? — A: Yes, you can. However, if you choose a Digi-Egg card, it will be returned to the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck.
