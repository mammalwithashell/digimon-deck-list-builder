## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-014 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3453** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's effect also activate when an opponent's Digimon with the [Blue Flare] or [Twilight] trait is played during my turn? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q3454** (2024-03-28) Q: During my turn, a card with the [Blue Flare] trait was played and this card's effect was triggered. If a card with the [Twilight] trait is played by another effect before this card's effect activates, can I perform both <Draw 1> and "return 1 Digimon card with DigiXros requirements from your trash to your hand" in this card's effect? — A: No, you can't. If Digimon with the [Blue Flare] and [Twilight] traits are played by separate effects, this card's effect will separately trigger for each trait. Because of the rule that states that an effect triggered afterward will take precedence for activation, for the situation stated in your question, ""return 1 Digimon card with DigiXros requirements from your trash to your hand"" will activate by playing the Digimon with the [Twilight] trait, but because the effect is a [Once Per Turn] effect, <Draw 1> doesn't activate by playing the Digimon with the [Blue Flare] trait.
- **Q3455** (2024-03-28) Q: This card has the [Blue Flare] trait, so do I get <Draw 1> from this card's own effect when this card is played? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q3456** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I perform both <Draw 1> and "return 1 Digimon card with DigiXros requirements from your trash to your hand" in this card's effect when a card with both the [Blue Flare] and [Twilight] traits is played during my turn? — A: Yes, you can perform both.
