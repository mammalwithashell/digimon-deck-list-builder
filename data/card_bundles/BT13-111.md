## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played from the hand, if you have no Digimon, reduce the play cost by 2 for every 5 total cards in both players' trashes. ＜Rush＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's 6000 DP or lower Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, delete 1 of their 13000 DP or higher Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- No, it means every 5 cards in yours and your opponent's trashes combined. For example, if you have 12 cards in your trash and your opponent has 8 cards in theirs, that makes a total of 20 cards, so the play cost is reduced by 8.
