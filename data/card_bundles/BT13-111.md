## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played from the hand, if you have no Digimon, reduce the play cost by 2 for every 5 total cards in both players' trashes. ＜Rush＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's 6000 DP or lower Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, delete 1 of their 13000 DP or higher Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-111 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2364** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "for every 5 cards in both players' trash" mean for every 5 cards in either mine or my opponent's trash? — A: No, it means every 5 cards in yours and your opponent's trashes combined. For example, if you have 12 cards in your trash and your opponent has 8 cards in theirs, that makes a total of 20 cards, so the play cost is reduced by 8.
- **Q2365** (2024-03-28) Q: When an "Add 1000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" effect has activated, does this card's [On Play][When Digivolving][When Attacking] effect become "Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 7000 DP or less. If no Digimon was deleted by this effect, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 14000 DP or more"? — A: No. "Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less" becomes "Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 7000 DP or less," but 13000 DP isn't the maximum for "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 13000 DP or more," therefore it isn't affected and remains as "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 13000 DP or more."
