## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] You may delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon, or play 1 of each [Royal Knight] trait Digimon with different names from the digivolution cards of your Digimon in the breeding area without paying the costs. If this effect played, trash your Digimon in the breeding area, and all your Digimon gain ＜Rush＞ for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2366** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "you may play 1 of each Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait and a different name from the digivolution cards of Digimon in the breeding area without paying the costs" in this card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect mean that I can play a Digimon card with the [Royal Knight] trait without the name [Omnimon]? — A: No, this effect plays 1 of each Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait and different names from among the digivolution cards. For example, if the target digivolution cards are [Omnimon], [Alphamon], [Alphamon], [Gallantmon], and [Gallantmon (X Antibody)], you would leave 1 [Alphamon] since it has the same name, then play[Omnimon], [Alphamon], [Gallantmon], and [Gallantmon (X Antibody)]. Note that the [Omnimon] where this effect activated isn't a digivolution card, therefore you can use this effect to play 1 [Omnimon] from among the target cards.
- **Q2367** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect to play Digimon, can I choose just the cards I want to play from among the cards I can play? — A: No, you can't. You must play all of the Digimon that you can play or play no Digimon.
- **Q2368** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's [On Play][When Digivolving] effect reads "trash your Digimon in the breeding area" for when a Digimon is played by this effect, but what happens to the remaining digivolution cards under this Digimon? — A: The digivolution cards are also trashed.
- **Q2369** (2024-05-24) Q: I used this card's [On Play] effect to trash a BT13-007 [King Drasil_7D6] with a BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE] in its digivolution cards. Is BT17-078 [Omnimon ACE]'s <Overflow> processed at such times? — A: Yes, it's processed. <Overflow> is processed when a card is sent from the area or from under a card to another area. In this case, <Overflow> is processed because a card is placed in the trash from under another card. _(related: BT17-078, BT13-007)_
