## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 8000 DP or less. If this effect didn't delete, until the end of your opponent's turn, this Digimon gains ＜Piercing＞ and gets +3000 DP. [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon are deleted, gain 2 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3070** (2024-09-20) Q: My opponent has a Digimon with 8000 DP or less. Do I have to choose a Digimon to delete for this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Yes, you do. If your opponent has a Digimon that can be chosen, you must choose that Digimon.
- **Q3071** (2024-09-20) Q: My opponent has BT14-062 [Datamon] and a Digimon with 8000 DP or less. If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to choose BT14-062 [Datamon] without deleting a Digimon, is the "if this effect didn't delete" condition met? — A: Yes, it is. If your opponent has a Digimon that can't be deleted but can be chosen for this effect, the "if this effect didn't delete" condition can be met by choosing that Digimon. _(related: BT14-062)_
