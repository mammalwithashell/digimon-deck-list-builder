## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [Dex] or [DeathX] in its name in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Delete 1 Digimon without [X Antibody] in its traits. If there are 3 or more Digimon in play, delete all Digimon without [X Antibody] in their traits instead.

### Security Effect
[Security] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon without [X Antibody] in its traits.
