## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Trash the top 3 digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon's DP. Then, 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards can't attack until the end of their turn. [All Turns] This Digimon gains all effects of cards with [Gammamon] in their names in this Digimon's digivolution cards.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon gains all effects of cards with [Gammamon] in their names in this Digimon's digivolution cards.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, they do. This card's "This Digimon gains all effects of cards with [Gammamon] in their names in this Digimon's digivolution cards" effect is [All Turns], so it activates as soon as digivolution is determined. Therefore, [When Digivolving] effects are gained and activate.
