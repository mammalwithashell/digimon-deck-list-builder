## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Security] Gain 1 memory, and add this card to its owner's hand. [Main] Place this card as the bottom digivolution card of 1 of your Digimon without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] Effects can't trash [X Antibody] in this Digimon's digivolution cards. [When Attacking] This Digimon can digivolve into a Digimon card with the [X Antibody] trait in your hand for the digivolution cost.

### Security Effect
[All Turns] Effects can't trash [X Antibody] in this Digimon's digivolution cards. [When Attacking] This Digimon can digivolve into a Digimon card with [X Antibody] in its traits in your hand for its digivolution cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-109 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1606** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [When Attacking] effect place one of my [DeadlyAxemon] with BT9-109 [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards at the bottom of this Digimon’s digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it can. In such cases, [DeadlyAxemon] becomes a different Digimon, therefore the BT9-109 [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards is trashed according to the rules.
- **Q1917** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card have inherited effects even though it's an Option card? — A: Yes. This card's [Main] effect can place it in a Digimon’s digivolution cards, and it has inherited effects. As with Digi-Egg cards and Digimon cards, this card's inherited effects can activate while it's placed in a Digimon’s digivolution cards.
- **Q1918** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's inherited effect digivolve a Digimon that doesn't meet the digivolution requirements into a Digimon card with [X Antibody] in its traits in my hand? — A: No, you can’t ignore digivolution requirements when using this card's effect.
- **Q1919** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate an effect that reduces the digivolution cost when digivolving using this card's inherited effect? — A: Yes, as long as the activation conditions are met.
- **Q1920** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card be trashed by <Digi-Burst> effects on Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards? — A: No. Trashing by <Digi-Burst> is also trashing by an effect.
- **Q1921** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if this card becomes the top card after a Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards is affected by <De-Digivolve>? — A: As with Digimon without DP, this card can't exist in the battle area as the top card, therefore it's trashed. This isn't considered deletion. If this card has other digivolution cards under it, they are also trashed.
- **Q1922** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if a "trash the bottom 2 digivolution cards of your opponent's Digimon" effect activates on a Digimon with this card as its bottom digivolution card? — A: Effects can't trash this card, therefore only the digivolution card on top of this card is trashed.
- **Q1923** (2024-03-28) Q: There are 8 digivolution cards under [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper], and this card is the bottom card. Does the "you may trash 7 or more digivolution cards from the bottom of 1 of your [EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper]s to set this Digimon's play cost to 0" effect activate when my [EX2-055 Reaper] is played? — A: It can activate in such cases if you declare to trash 8 cards and then trash 7 cards except for [BT9-109 X Antibody]. _(related: EX2-007, EX2-055)_
- **Q1927** (2024-03-28) Q: If [BT9-109 X Antibody] is in this card’s digivolution cards, can this card’s [End of Your Turn] effect return [BT9-109 X Antibody] from this card’s digivolution cards to the bottom of my deck? — A: Yes. You're not trashing it, so there’s nothing preventing you from returning it to your deck.
- **Q2143** (2024-03-28) Q: My Digimon with [Veedramon] in its name attacks and becomes suspended.
