## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 black or yellow card with 2 or more colors and 1 Tamer card with [Cody Hida] in its name among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and another of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-121 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4232** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include only either a black or yellow card with 2 or more colors or a Tamer card with [Cody Hida] in its name, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q4233** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "black or yellow card with 2 or more colors" in this card's [On Play] effect mean that I can add a black/yellow or yellow/black card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4234** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a black or yellow card with 2 or more colors and a Tamer card with [Cody Hida] in its name, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
