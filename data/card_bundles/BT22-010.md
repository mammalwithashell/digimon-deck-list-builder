## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-010 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4866** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I pay just 1 cost for this card's [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to pay 2 cost.
- **Q4867** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [Main] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't pay 2 cost, the part after "then" in this card's [Main] effect won't let this Digimon attack.
- **Q4962** (2025-07-04) Q: If I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to activate BT22-010 [Meramon]'s [Main] effect, when do I process the part of the effect after "if this activated any effect"? — A: After all of BT22-010 [Meramon]'s [Main] effect has resolved, then you process the part of the effect after "if this activated any effect." If you use BT22-010 [Meramon]'s effect to attack, you gain 1 memory after the attack declaration. _(a ruling on BT22-092; this card is named in it)_
