## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When you would use this card, if you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards in play, reduce the memory cost of this card by 2. [Main] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 13000 DP or less. Then, 1 of your Digimon with [Greymon] in its name may attack your opponent.

### Security Effect
[Security] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-095 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1898** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to attack my opponent with 1 of my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name that is suspended or was played in the same turn? — A: No, you can't. This card's effect doesn't let you attack with a Digimon that's in a state where it can't attack.
- **Q1899** (2024-03-28) Q: If a digivolution card has the [X Antibody] trait, does it meet this card's "if you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards in play" condition? — A: No. [X Antibody] in this effect's text refers to the card name, therefore only a card with [X Antibody] as its card name will meet the condition. It doesn't refer to the [X Antibody] trait.
- **Q1900** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [Main] effect to attack, does the attacking Digimon's [When Attacking] effect activate? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q1901** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [Main] effect to have one of my opponent's Digimon attack when one of my Digimon with [Greymon] in its name has a "this Digimon can also attack your opponent's unsuspended Digimon" effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's effect only allows you to attack a player.
