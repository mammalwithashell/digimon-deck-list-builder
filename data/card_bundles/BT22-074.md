## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] [Once Per Turn] By paying 3 cost, delete 1 of your opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon. If this effect didn't delete, this Digimon gains ＜Security A. +1＞ for the turn. Then, this Digimon may attack. [On Deletion] ＜Draw 2＞ and trash 1 card in your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card with the [Flame] or [CS] trait from your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-074 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4933** (2025-07-04) Q: If I don't delete an opponent's Digimon for this card's [Main] effect, can I use the part of the effect after "then" to have this Digimon attack? — A: Yes, it can attack.
- **Q4934** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I pay just 1 cost for this card's [Main] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Main] effect require you to pay 3 cost.
- **Q4935** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [Main] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't pay 3 cost, the part after "then" in this card's [Main] effect won't let this Digimon attack.
- **Q4936** (2025-07-04) Q: My opponent has a level 5 or lower Digimon. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [Main] effect by not choosing a card to delete? — A: No, you can't. If your opponent has a level 5 or lower Digimon, you must choose that card and delete it.
- **Q4937** (2025-07-04) Q: My opponent has a level 5 or lower Digimon. Can I meet the "if this effect didn't delete" condition in this card's [Main] effect by choosing an opponent's level 5 or lower Digimon with an effect that prevents deletion? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q6248** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I activate this card's [Main] effect even when my opponent doesn't have any Digimon that can be deleted? — A: Yes, you can.
