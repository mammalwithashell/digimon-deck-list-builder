### Effect
[Security] Play this card without battling and without paying the cost. [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Tamers. [On Deletion] Delete 1 of your opponent’s Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-112 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1395** (2024-03-28) Q: What does "without battling" in this card's [Security] effect mean? — A: If this card is flipped over as a Security Digimon, it's played in its owner's battle area without battling the attacking Digimon. Even if the attacking Digimon has lower DP than this card, no battle occurs, and the attacking Digimon is not deleted.
- **Q1396** (2024-03-28) Q: This card is flipped over during a security check. If the attacking Digimon has extra security checks remaining from <Security Attack +>, what happens? — A: After playing this Digimon, the remaining checks are performed.
- **Q1397** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [When Digivolving] effect be used to delete a Digimon that digivolved from a Tamer? — A: No, it can't.
- **Q5026** (2025-09-05) Q: My opponent only has 1 BT5-112 [Omnimon Zwart Defeat] in the battle area. If that BT5-112 [Omnimon Zwart Defeat] is deleted and this card is chosen as its [On Deletion] effect, is this card deleted? — A: Yes, it's deleted. If this card's [All Turns] effect activates while your opponent has a Digimon with 13000 DP or more, as soon as that Digimon leaves, the effect will no longer be activated.
