## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] If [Magnadramon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, all of your opponent’s Digimon and Security Digimon get -1000 DP for the turn for each card in your security stack. [End of Attack][Once Per Turn] You may add the top card of your security stack to your hand to unsuspend this Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [Magnadramon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
