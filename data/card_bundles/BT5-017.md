- **Q1299** (2024-03-28) Q: This card has 2 [When Digivolving] effects. If <Blitz> resolves first, does the other [When Digivolving] effect resolve after the attack ends? — A: No. If you activate <Blitz> first, after activating any [When Attacking] or [When one of your Digimon attacks] effects, you activate the other [When Digivolving] effect before the counter timing occurs.
