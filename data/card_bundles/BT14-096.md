## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if you have a Tamer with [Mimi Tachikawa] in its name, 1 of your opponent's Digimon doesn't unsuspend until the end of their turn.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- No, it can't. If you have a Tamer with [Mimi Tachikawa] in its name at the timing when this effect activates, your opponent's Digimon that was affected by the "doesn't unsuspend" effect doesn't unsuspend even if your Tamer with [Mimi Tachikawa] in its name is later removed from the battle area.
