## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-082 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1877** (2024-03-28) Q: When playing this card from my trash with this card’s [On Deletion] effect, does it return to the battle area with any digivolution cards it had prior to being deleted? — A: No, only this card itself is returned to play. Any digivolution cards it had are placed in your trash.
- **Q1878** (2024-03-28) Q: When playing this card from my trash with this card's [On Deletion] effect, do any effects it had prior to being deleted persist once it re-enters play? — A: No. It’s treated as a new Digimon that just entered play. Any effects it had prior to being deleted are not carried over.
