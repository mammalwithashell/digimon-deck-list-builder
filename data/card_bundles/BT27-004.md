## Printed text (official)
### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your other Digimon with [Chessmon] in their names are played, this Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Chessmon] in its name in the hand with the cost reduced by 2.

## Official Q&A
- The [When Digivolving] effect is activated first. When your Digimon was played, the [On Play] effect and the inherited effect triggered simultaneously. Activating the inherited effect to digivolve the Digimon caused its [When Digivolving] effect to newly trigger, so it is activated first according to the derived triggering rule.
