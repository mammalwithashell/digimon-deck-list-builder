## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, their effects can't play Digimon or Tamers from the trash. [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 8000 DP or less. For each of their Digimon and Tamers, add 2000 to this DP deletion effect's maximum.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-014 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5225** (2025-10-03) Q: This card has multiple effects that trigger when it's played or when it digivolves. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q5226** (2025-10-03) Q: What does a "your opponent can't play XX" effect do, exactly? — A: Such effects prevent your opponent from playing XX. With this card's effect, your opponent's effects can't play Digimon cards or Tamer cards from the trash. This effect affects the opponent player.
- **Q5227** (2025-10-03) Q: Can an opponent's Digimon be played from the trash if I activate a "your opponent's effects can't play Digimon cards or Tamer cards from the trash" effect, and in the same turn, I activate an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: Yes, it can be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon" is an effect that prevents your opponent's effects from playing Digimon. Your effects can still play an opponent's Digimon from the trash. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q5228** (2025-10-03) Q: Can my Digimon be played from the trash if I activate a "your opponent's effects can't play Digimon cards or Tamer cards from the trash" effect, and in the same turn, my opponent activates an effect such as EX5-060 [Dragomon]'s [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect that plays an opponent's Digimon card? — A: No, it can't be played. "Your opponent can't play Digimon" is an effect that prevents your opponent's effects from playing Digimon. Your Digimon can't be played from the trash by an opponent's effect. _(related: EX5-060)_
- **Q5229** (2025-10-03) Q: What does an "add X000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" effect do? — A: Such effects increase the maximum value shown for DP-based deletion effects. For example, while "[Your Turn] Add 1000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete" is activated, "[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 3000 DP or less" will allow you to delete an opponent's Digimon with 4000 DP or less.
- **Q6249** (2026-05-08) Q: If I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, can my opponent use an effect to play a Digimon or Tamer into their breeding area? — A: No, they can't.
