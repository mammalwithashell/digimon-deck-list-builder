## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your Digimon gains "[Opponent's Turn] When this Digimon becomes suspended, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost less than or equal to this Digimon's" and "[Opponent's Turn] This Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards."

### Security Effect
[Security] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT13-108 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2361** (2024-03-28) Q: My Digimon is affected by this card's "This Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards" effect. Is it affected by the [Security] effect of my opponent's Option cards? — A: No, it isn't affected by [Security] effects, either.
- **Q2362** (2024-03-28) Q: Can "isn't affected by your opponent's Option cards" effects prevent the "When this Digimon is suspended, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost less than or equal to this Digimon" effect given to an opponent's Digimon by this card? — A: No, they can't. The "When this Digimon is suspended, delete all of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost less than or equal to this Digimon" effect from this card is a Digimon effect. It isn't considered to be an Option card effect.
