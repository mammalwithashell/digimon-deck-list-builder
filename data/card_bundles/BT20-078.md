## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-078 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4401** (2025-01-24) Q: Which effect activates first, a [When Digivolving] effect on an opponent's Digimon that digivolved, or this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player activates their effect first.
- **Q4402** (2025-01-24) Q: When does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger, exactly? — A: It will trigger when a Digimon digivolves by an effect such as the effect on P-103 [Offense Training]. It won't trigger for effects that aren't digivolution effects, such as BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s effect that treats a Tamer as a level 3 red Digimon or BT6-060 [Deputymon]'s effect that allows digivolution requirements to be ignored. _(related: P-103, BT4-011, BT6-060)_
