## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-049 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2801** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's inherited effect to delete another of my level 3 Digimon cards with the [Beast] trait and then play that deleted Digimon card from the trash without paying the cost? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2802** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's inherited effect to delete a level 3 Digimon card with an [On Deletion] effect and the [Beast] trait, then I played that deleted Digimon card from the trash without paying the cost. Can I activate the deleted Digimon's [On Deletion] effect at such times? — A: No, you can't. If a card is removed from the trash before a triggered [On Deletion] effect activates, that [On Deletion] effect can't be activated.
