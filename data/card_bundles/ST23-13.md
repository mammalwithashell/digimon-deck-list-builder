[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] You may place the top card of your deck face down under this Tamer. Then, if your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When effects trash cards from under this Tamer, by suspending this Tamer, 1 of your [Glowing Dawn] trait Digimon gets +3000 DP until your opponent's turn ends.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST23-13 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6186** (2026-05-08) Q: If I use this card's [Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q6187** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q6188** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q6189** (2026-05-08) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
