## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] 1 of your Digimon gains "[On Deletion] Play this card without paying its memory cost. Any [On Play] effects on Digimon played with this effect don't activate" for the turn.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT3-109 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1147** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon affected by this card was deleted. What happens to its digivolution cards? — A: Its digivolution cards don't return to play, and remain in the trash.
- **Q1148** (2024-03-28) Q: When a Digimon affected by this card comes back into play after being deleted, does it retain any effects that were on it prior to deletion? — A: It loses all effects.
- **Q1149** (2024-03-28) Q: When a Digimon affected by this card comes back into play after being deleted, can it attack that same turn? — A: No, it's considered to have just entered play, so it can't attack.
- **Q2730** (2024-03-28) Q: I used BT3-109 [Back for Revenge!] on this Digimon, then I used this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve into [AncientGreymon]. After this Digimon is deleted at the end of this turn, does BT3-109 [Back for Revenge!]'s effect play this card or [AncientGreymon] into the battle area? — A: Digimon carry over effects after digivolving, therefore it plays [AncientGreymon].
- **Q2761** (2024-03-28) Q: I used BT3-109 [Back for Revenge!] on this Digimon, then I used this Digimon's [When Digivolving] effect to digivolve into [AncientGarurumon].
