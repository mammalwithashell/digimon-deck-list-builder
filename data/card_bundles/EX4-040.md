[On Play] If you don't have [Nene Amano] in play, you may play 1 [Nene Amano] from your hand without paying the cost. [On Deletion] Reveal the top card of your deck. If that card has the [Blue Flare] or [Twilight] trait, add it to your hand. Trash the rest.
