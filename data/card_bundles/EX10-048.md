## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, by deleting 1 of your Digimon with [Myotismon] in its text, reduce the play cost by 4. [On Play] [On Deletion] 1 of your purple Digimon gains ＜Blocker＞ and ＜Retaliation＞ until your opponent's turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may play 1 purple Tamer card from your trash suspended without paying the cost.
