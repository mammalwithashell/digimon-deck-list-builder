## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns] When one of your Digimon is played or digivolves, by suspending this Tamer, if you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, gain 1 memory. If you have one with [Garurumon] in its name, gain 1 memory. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name may attack a player.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-081 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2855** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to gain 2 memory if I have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name and a Digimon with [Garurumon] in its name? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q2856** (2024-03-28) Q: When I play a Digimon with [Greymon] or [Garurumon] in its name, then its DP becomes 0 and is deleted upon play due to a persistent effect, can I activate this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, you can. Even if the played Digimon is removed before the effect activates, it triggers because the "when [...] Digimon is played" condition is met, then the effect can activate. However, the "if you have a Digimon with [XX] in its name" condition is no longer met once the Digimon is removed, therefore you don't gain 1 memory.
- **Q2857** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to attack using my Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name even when it's suspended? — A: No, you can't. This card's [End of Your Turn] effect does not allow you to attack with Digimon that are in a state where they can't attack.
- **Q2858** (2024-03-28) Q: I attacked using BT5-086 [Omnimon]'s <Blitz> and unsuspended it using the other [When Digivolving] effect. After the attack ends, can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to attack again? — A: Yes, you can. If BT5-086 [Omnimon] digivolves during the main phase, you can use the [End of Your Turn] effect to attack again after the attack from <Blitz> ends. _(related: BT5-086)_
- **Q2859** (2024-03-28) Q: When I have 2 copies of this Tamer and 2 unsuspended Digimon with [Omnimon] in their names, can I activate the [End of Your Turn] effect on both copies of this card and attack using both of the Digimon with [Omnimon] in their names? — A: No, you can't. The effect will trigger on both copies, but after the attack declaration is made for the 1st [Omnimon] and effects such as [When Attacking] effects have finished activating, the effect on the 2nd copy of this card that was pending activation will activate before the counter timing. However, a new attack declaration can't be made during an attack, and only the 1st [Omnimon]'s attack can be performed as a result.
