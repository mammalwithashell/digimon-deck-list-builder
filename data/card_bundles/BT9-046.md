## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Insectoid] or [Machine] in its traits and 1 [X Antibody] among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name. You can only add a card whose name is specifically [X Antibody] to your hand. A card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
