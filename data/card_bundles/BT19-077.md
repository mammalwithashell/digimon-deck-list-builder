## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] You may play 1 Digimon card with 2000 DP or less from your hand without paying the cost. [Main] By suspending this Digimon, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2. [All Turns] This Digimon can't attack or block. [On Deletion] Place this card as the top security card.
