## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [Armor Form] in its traits in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements.[Main] Trash the top card of 1 of your Digimon with [Armor Form] in its traits. Then, you may digivolve 1 of your Digimon into a Digimon card in your hand with [Armor Form] in its traits for its digivolution cost. If you do, unsuspend that Digimon.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 level 3 Digimon card with [Free] in its traits from your hand or trash without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-110 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1789** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card to trash the top card of 1 of my Digimon with [Armor Form] in its traits. Can I then digivolve one of my other Digimon into a Digimon card in my hand with [Armor Form] in its traits? — A: Yes, you can. If you do, only the Digimon you chose to digivolve will unsuspend.
- **Q1790** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card to trash the top card of 1 of my Digimon with [Armor Form] in its traits. Can I then choose NOT to digivolve one of my other Digimon into a Digimon card in my hand with [Armor Form] in its traits? — A: Yes, you can. If you choose this option, however, no Digimon will be unsuspended with this effect.
- **Q1791** (2024-03-28) Q: When using this card’s effect to digivolve into a Digimon in my hand with [Armor Form] in its traits, can I ignore its digivolution requirements? — A: No. Digivolution requirements cannot be ignored unless an effect explicitly instructs you to ignore them.
