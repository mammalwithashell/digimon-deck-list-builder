## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 green Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, place this card in your battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (Trash this card in your battle area to activate the effect below. You can't activate this effect the turn this card enters play.) ・Gain 2 memory.
