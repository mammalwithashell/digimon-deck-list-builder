## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with the [Angel] or [Archangel] trait and 1 card with the [Three Great Angels] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has the [Angel], [Archangel] or [Three Great Angels] trait, ＜Draw 1＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-017 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3712** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include only either a Digimon card with the [Angel] or [Archangel] trait or a card with the [Three Great Angels] trait, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q3713** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's [On Play] effect include both a Digimon card with the [Angel] or [Archangel] trait and a card with the [Three Great Angels] trait, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
