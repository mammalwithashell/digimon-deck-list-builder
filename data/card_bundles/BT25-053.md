## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-053 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6329** (2026-05-08) Q: If this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect couldn't suspend a Digimon, does that mean it can't give that Digimon "can't unsuspend"? — A: No, even if this effect didn't suspend the target, it can still give it "can't unsuspend."
- **Q6330** (2026-05-08) Q: In what order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
