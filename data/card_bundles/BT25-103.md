## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-103 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6488** (2026-05-08) Q: Multiple effects trigger when this card attacks. In what order do they activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q6489** (2026-05-08) Q: An opponent's Digimon attacks, and its [When Attacking] effect causes a Digimon to be played by an effect. I then activate the [All Turns] effect on my BT25-028 [Dianamon] and DNA digivolve into this card. Can I activate this card's [When Attacking] [Counter] effect during the counter timing after that? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: BT25-028)_
- **Q6490** (2026-05-08) Q: This card attacks, and its [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect and [When Attacking] [Counter] effect trigger simultaneously. If I activate the [When Attacking] [Counter] effect first and end this attack, can I then activate the [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect? — A: Yes, you can. However, if there is an [End of Attack] effect, it will be a derived triggering effect, therefore that effect will activate before the [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect.
- **Q6491** (2026-05-08) Q: What does "end the attack" mean, exactly? — A: After this effect activates, the current timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. For example, if this effect activates during the counter timing, the counter timing makes a transition to the end of attack timing. A transition to the block timing won't occur, and the attack won't succeed.
- **Q6492** (2026-05-08) Q: Can an "end the attack" effect end an attack by a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: Yes, such attacks can be ended. "End the attack" effects are effects that change the timing, they don't affect an attacking Digimon.
- **Q6493** (2026-05-08) Q: If an attack is ended by an "end the attack" effect, do [End of Attack] effects still trigger? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q6717** (2026-06-19) Q: After activating this card's [Counter] effect, can I activate another [Counter] effect? — A: No. Only one [Counter] effect can be activated per an attack.
