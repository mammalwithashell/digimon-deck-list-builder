## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-009 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2910** (2024-06-21) Q: What does the "your opponent can't gain memory other than by Tamer effects" effect do, exactly? — A: With this effect, your opponent's memory will no longer increase by any effects except Tamer effects. This effect affects the opponent player.
- **Q2911** (2024-06-21) Q: Can I use the [All Turns] effect on BT17-087 [Marcus Damon] to gain memory if it's treated as a Digimon and suspends while my opponent has "your opponent can't gain memory other than by Tamer effects" activated? — A: Yes, you can gain memory. When an effect is activated on a card that's treated as both a Digimon and a Tamer, the effect is treated as both a Digimon effect and a Tamer effect, therefore you can gain memory. _(related: BT17-087)_
