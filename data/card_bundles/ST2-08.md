## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST2-08 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q611** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect apply even if my opponent only has 1 Digimon with no digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q612** (2024-03-28) Q: One of my Digimon has this card as a digivolution card and attacks one of my opponent's Digimon that has 1 digivolution card. If this Digimon has another When Attacking effect that activates and trashes that digivolution card, does this card's inherited effect activate? — A: Yes. Once that Digimon has no digivolution cards left, this card's inherited effect activates.
- **Q613** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect apply if my opponent has a Digimon with no digivolution cards in their breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't check Digimon in the breeding area.
- **Q614** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's inherited effect apply when my opponent doesn't have any Digimon in their battle area? — A: No, it doesn't. Your opponent has to have a Digimon in their battle area for it to activate.
