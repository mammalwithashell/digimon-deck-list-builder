## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played from the hand, if there're 6 or fewer total cards in both players' security stacks, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play][When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent’s Digimon. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When a Digimon becomes suspended, you may have 1 of your opponent's Digimon get -4000 DP and gain ＜Security A. -1＞ until the end of their turn.

## Official Q&A
- It means the total number of cards in both you and your opponent's security stacks. For example, if you have 3 cards in your security stack and your opponent has 2 cards in their security stack, the total security cards will be 5 cards.
