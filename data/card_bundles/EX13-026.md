## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-026 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7279** (2026-09-18) Q: If I use this card's [When Moving] [On Play] effect to place a card under a Tamer with cards under it, in what order do I place the card? — A: The card is placed on the bottom of the cards under the Tamer.
- **Q7280** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I change the stacking order of face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7281** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I search/look at face-down cards under a Tamer? — A: Only the player of those cards can search/look at them. The opponent player can't search/look at them.
- **Q7282** (2026-09-18) Q: What happens if a face-down card under a Tamer is trashed? — A: It's placed face-up in the trash.
- **Q7283** (2026-09-18) Q: What happens if there is only one specified card among the cards revealed by this card's [When Moving] [On Play] effect? — A: That card is added to the hand.
- **Q7284** (2026-09-18) Q: Does the card that gets placed face down by this card's [When Moving] [On Play] effect have to meet the specified card's requirements? — A: Yes, the card placed face down must also be a specified card.
