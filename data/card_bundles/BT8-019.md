## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Your opponent chooses 1 of their Digimon. Delete all Digimon other than this Digimon and your opponent's chosen Digimon. For each Digimon deleted by this effect, gain 1 memory. [Your Turn] When an opponent's Digimon is deleted, this Digimon gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn for each of your opponent's Digimon deleted.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-019 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1703** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect work, exactly? — A: Your opponent chooses 1 of their Digimon. Next, delete all Digimon in play, from both you and your opponent, except for this Digimon and the Digimon your opponent chose. Then you gain 1 memory for every Digimon that was deleted by this effect.
- **Q1704** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect delete Digimon in breeding areas, too? — A: No, Digimon in breeding areas cannot be affected by effects, so they aren't deleted.
- **Q1705** (2024-03-28) Q: For this card’s [When Digivolving] effect, can my opponent choose a Digimon in their breeding area? — A: No, Digimon in breeding areas cannot be affected by effects, so they can't be chosen. Additionally, note that this effect doesn't delete Digimon in breeding areas, regardless of whether they could be chosen or not.
- **Q1706** (2024-03-28) Q: How does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect work if my opponent has no Digimon in play? — A: All of your other Digimon besides this one are deleted, and you gain 1 memory for each Digimon deleted in this way.
- **Q1707** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card's [When Digivolving] effect deletes Digimon in play, can you and your opponent activate <Decoy>, <Armor Purge>, and similar effects to prevent their deletion? — A: Yes, you can.
