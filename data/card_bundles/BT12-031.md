### Effect
[When Digivolving] Suspend all of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards. Then, return 1 of your opponent's suspended Digimon to its owner's hand. By returning 1 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode] from this Digimon's digivolution cards to its owner's hand, place all of your opponent's suspended Digimon at the bottom of their owners' decks instead. [All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP for each color in its digivolution cards. While it has 2 or more colors in its digivolution cards, it gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ and ＜Blocker＞.
