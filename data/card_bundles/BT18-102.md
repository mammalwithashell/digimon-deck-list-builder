## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-102 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3054** (2024-06-21) Q: When using this card's [When Attacking] effect to place multiple Tamer cards as my bottom security cards, can I choose the order of the cards that are placed? — A: Yes, you can choose the order of the security cards that are placed.
- **Q3055** (2024-06-21) Q: Can I use this card's digivolution requirement to digivolve for a cost of 6 even when my [Takuya Kanbara] or [Koji Minamoto] has more than 10 [Hybrid] trait cards under it? — A: Yes, it can digivolve.
- **Q3056** (2024-06-21) Q: Does this card's <Blast Digivolve> allow me to use the "[Takuya Kanbara]/[Koji Minamoto] w/10 [Hybrid] trait cards under" digivolution requirement to digivolve? — A: No, you can't use that digivolution requirement with <Blast Digivolve>.
- **Q3057** (2024-06-21) Q: When using this card's [When Attacking] effect to place multiple Tamer cards as my bottom security cards, can I choose the order of the cards that are placed? — A: Yes, you can choose the order of the security cards that are placed.
- **Q6665** (2026-06-19) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q6666** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6667** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card cannot attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q6668** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6669** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6670** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
