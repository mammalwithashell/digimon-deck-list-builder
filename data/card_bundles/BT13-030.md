## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] For each of your Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait and each of your blue Tamers, trash the top 2 digivolution cards of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play a Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait or a blue Tamer, return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards to the hand.
