### Effect
＜Fragment《3》＞ [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may place up to 3 [Mineral] or [Rock] trait cards from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] By trashing up to 3 [Mineral] or [Rock] trait cards from any of your Digimon's digivolution cards, to 1 of your opponent's Digimon, reduce the play cost by 2 until their turn ends for each card trashed.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-033 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5094** (2025-09-05) Q: This card has multiple effects that trigger when it digivolves or when it attacks. In what order can they be activated? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order.
- **Q5095** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to place [Mineral] or [Rock] trait Digi-Egg cards from my trash in digivolution cards? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5096** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I activate this card's 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect but choose to not to place any [Mineral] or [Rock] trait cards from my trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If this card's 1st [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect activates, you must place at least 1 card in digivolution cards. However, if you choose to not activate this effect, it won't activate and you don't place any cards in digivolution cards.
- **Q5097** (2025-09-05) Q: If an opponent's Digimon has a play cost of 5 and I use this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to reduce its play cost by 6, does its play cost become -1? — A: No, it doesn't. The minimum value is 0, the resulting play cost can't be negative.
- **Q5098** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash a total of 3 digivolution cards from my multiple different Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5099** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I activate this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect but choose to not to trash any [Mineral] or [Rock] trait cards from my Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect activates, you must trash at least 1 card. However, if you choose to not activate this effect, it won't activate and you don't trash any cards.
- **Q5100** (2025-09-05) Q: I use this card's 2nd [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to trash EX10-028 [Landramon] and reduce the play cost of an opponent's Digimon by 2 from 6, so now its play cost is 4. Can I then use EX10-028 [Landramon]'s inherited effect to delete that Digimon? — A: Yes, you can. _(related: EX10-028)_
