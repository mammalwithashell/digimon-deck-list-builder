## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if your opponent has 10 or more cards in either their hand or trash, reduce the play cost by 5. [On Play] [When Digivolving] Your opponent trashes cards in their hand so that they have 6 cards left. If this effect didn't trash, you may play 1 level 5 or lower [Fallen Angel] trait Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] If your opponent has 6 or more cards in their hand, they trash 1 of them. Then, if they have 5 or fewer, delete 1 of their lowest level Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. This card's 1st effect activates when your opponent has 10 or more cards in their hand or 10 or more cards in their trash, then the play cost will be reduced by 5. It doesn't activate when the total number of cards in their hand and trash is 10.
