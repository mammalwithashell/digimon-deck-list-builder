## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Trash] [All Turns] When one of your [DoruGreymon] would be deleted, by digivolving it into this card without paying the cost, prevent that deletion. [When Digivolving] Trash 1 card in your hand. Then, ＜Draw 1＞. If [DoruGreymon] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards or this digivolved from the trash, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 6 or less instead.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may choose 1 of your Digimon. Delete 1 of your chosen Digimon and 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as that Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-067 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2821** (2024-03-28) Q: When exactly is the timing when this card's [Trash] [All Turns] effect triggers? — A: It triggers at the timing when this card is in the trash and your [DoruGreymon] would be deleted. [Trash] means that an effect can trigger and activate when the card is in the trash.
- **Q2822** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card's [Trash] effect to digivolve when my [DoruGreymon] would be deleted by its DP becoming 0, can I prevent that deletion? — A: Yes, you can. However, the DP-reducing effect will be carried over after the [DoruGreymon] effect activates and digivolution occurs, therefore deletion will occur again if the DP is still 0 after the effect activates and digivolution occurs.
- **Q2823** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [Trash] effect to digivolve when my [DoruGreymon] would be deleted due to its DP being reduced by 10000. Its DP became 0 due to the DP-reducing effect, but can I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect after it digivolves at such times? — A: No, you can't activate it. The Digimon is deleted before the [When Digivolving] effect activates.
- **Q2824** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 6 or less instead" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect activate instead of "Trash 1 card in your hand. Then, <Draw 1>."? — A: No, it activates instead of "Then, <Draw 1>."
- **Q2825** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card’s inherited effect to choose my Digimon with a "can't be deleted by effects" effect and only delete an opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as that Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
