## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Trash] [All Turns] When one of your [DoruGreymon] would be deleted, by digivolving it into this card without paying the cost, prevent that deletion. [When Digivolving] Trash 1 card in your hand. Then, ＜Draw 1＞. If [DoruGreymon] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards or this digivolved from the trash, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 6 or less instead.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn] You may choose 1 of your Digimon. Delete 1 of your chosen Digimon and 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as high or lower a level as that Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- It triggers at the timing when this card is in the trash and your [DoruGreymon] would be deleted. [Trash] means that an effect can trigger and activate when the card is in the trash.
