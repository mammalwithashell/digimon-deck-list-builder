## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. Place 1 of them on top of your security stack face down. Add the remaining card to your hand. Then, place this card in your battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (Trash this card in your battle area to activate the effect below. You can't activate this effect the turn this card enters play.) ・Gain 3 memory.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. You're revealing cards from your deck, which means they’re public information until you choose one to place face down on top of your security stack. You must show the card you place in your security stack to your opponent first.
