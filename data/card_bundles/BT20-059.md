## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if [Gankoomon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, until the end of your opponent's turn, none of your Digimon are affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects. [Opponent's Turn] All of your Digimon with [Sistermon] or [Huckmon] in their names or the [Royal Knight] trait gain ＜Reboot＞ and ＜Blocker＞.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT20-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4392** (2025-01-24) Q: What does "isn't affected by [...] effects" mean, exactly? — A: This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digmon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
- **Q4393** (2025-01-24) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be chosen for an effect? — A: Yes, it can be chosen.
- **Q4394** (2025-04-04) Q: Can a card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect be given an effect? — A: Yes, it can. However, it won't be affected by it.
- **Q4395** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card is affected by an effect, then it later gains an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that was affecting it? — A: As soon as it gains the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it will no longer be affected.
- **Q4396** (2025-01-24) Q: If a card has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, it gains an effect, then it later loses the "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect, what happens to the effect that it gained? — A: It will be affected by the effect as soon as it can be affected by effects.
- **Q4397** (2025-06-13) Q: A card that has an "isn't affected by [...] effects" effect was given an effect that triggers at a timing such as [When Attacking]. Will the effect trigger if that card later meets the trigger conditions? — A: If the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the effect won't trigger.
