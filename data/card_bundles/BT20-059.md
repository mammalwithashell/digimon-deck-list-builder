## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if [Gankoomon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, until the end of your opponent's turn, none of your Digimon are affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects. [Opponent's Turn] All of your Digimon with [Sistermon] or [Huckmon] in their names or the [Royal Knight] trait gain ＜Reboot＞ and ＜Blocker＞.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] While this Digimon is [Jesmon GX], all of your Digimon gain ＜Reboot＞ and ＜Blocker＞.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents Digimon from being affected by effects. For example, your Digmon won't suspend if it's chosen for a "suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" effect, and its DP won't be reduced if it's chosen for a "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP" effect.
