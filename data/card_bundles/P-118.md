## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 green or blue card with 2 or more colors and 1 Tamer card with [Ken Ichijoji] in its name among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and another of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-118 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4226** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include only either a green or blue card with 2 or more colors or a Tamer card with [Ken Ichijoji] in its name, can I only add 1 card to my hand? — A: Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
- **Q4227** (2024-03-28) Q: Does "green or blue card with 2 or more colors" in this card's [On Play] effect mean that I can add a green/blue or blue/green card to my hand? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4228** (2024-03-28) Q: If the cards I reveal from my deck for this card's effect include both a green or blue card with 2 or more colors and a Tamer card with [Ken Ichijoji] in its name, can I choose to only add one of them to my hand, and place the remaining cards at the bottom of my deck? — A: No. If you have cards that are targets of this effect, you must add as many cards to your hand as possible.
