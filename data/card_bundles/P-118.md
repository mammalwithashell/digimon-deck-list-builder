## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 green or blue card with 2 or more colors and 1 Tamer card with [Ken Ichijoji] in its name among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Your Turn] This Digimon and another of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in your hand.

## Official Q&A
- Yes, even if you only reveal one of the applicable cards, you can still add it to your hand.
