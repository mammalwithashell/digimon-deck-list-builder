## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 purple Tamer card from your trash without paying its memory cost. Then, for each Tamer you have in play, delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon. [On Deletion] For each Tamer you have in play, you may play 1 level 3 Digimon card from your trash without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
- Yes. Even if you don’t play a Tamer from your trash, everything following “Then,” still activates.
