## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Blocker＞ (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.) [Your Turn] While your opponent has a Digimon with a digivolution card in play, this Digimon can't attack.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When a digivolution card of an opponent's Digimon is trashed, return 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon to its owner's hand.
