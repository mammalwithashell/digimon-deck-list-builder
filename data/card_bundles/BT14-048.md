## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] If attacked a Digimon with higher DP than this Digimon, this Digimon may digivolve into 1 level 6 Digimon card with [Leomon] in its name in your hand for a digivolution cost of 6, ignoring its digivolution requirements.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Leomon] in its name, it gets +2000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. This card's [When Attacking] effect is an effect that activates before your opponent blocks, therefore the activation timing has already passed by the time of the block timing.
