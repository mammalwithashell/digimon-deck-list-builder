## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your Digimon may attack without suspending. Then, for every 2 of your Digimon and Tamers' colors, activate 1 effect below: ・This Digimon may battle 1 of your opponent's Digimon. ・By returning 5 cards from your opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, ＜Recovery +1＞. [All Turns] This Digimon gains all colors in its digivolution cards, and +1000 DP for each of your Digimon and Tamers' colors.

### Special Play Condition

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-077 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7468** (2026-09-18) Q: What does "may battle" mean, exactly? — A: This effect immediately initiates a battle. If this effect is activated, the chosen Digimon battle according to the standard rules.
- **Q7469** (2026-09-18) Q: If 2 Digimon including a Digimon that has an "effects don't affect" effect are chosen for a "may battle" effect, can those Digimon battle? — A: Yes, they can battle. A card that has an "effects don't affect" effect can still be chosen for an effect. In addition, a battle itself is a rule that compares DP, not an effect. Therefore, a Digimon that isn't affected by effects can be chosen, and then it can battle. If it loses the battle, it will be deleted as normal according to the rules.
- **Q7470** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I choose a Digi-Egg card for the card returned to the deck with this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: Yes. However, if you choose a Digi-Egg card, it is returned to the bottom of the Digi-Egg deck instead.
- **Q7471** (2026-09-18) Q: Can I only return 4 cards from my opponent's trash to their deck to meet the conditions of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't. All of the conditions within "by doing X" must be fulfilled. The conditions for this [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect require you to return 5 cards from the opponent's trash to the bottom of their deck.
- **Q7472** (2026-09-18) Q: If an effect that activates another effect one time "for every XX" can activate multiple types of effects multiple times, can I activate the same effect multiple times and/or activate different effects? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q7473** (2026-09-18) Q: If an effect that activates another effect one time "for every XX" can activate multiple types of effects multiple times, how is the effect activation order determined? — A: An effect to activate is chosen, then the next effect to activate is chosen after the previous effect has been resolved. This is repeated one time for every XX.
- **Q7474** (2026-09-18) Q: If the number of XX changes during activation of effects by an effect that activates another effect one time "for every XX," does the number of activations also change? — A: No, it doesn't. The number of XX is referenced at the point in time the effect that activates another effect one time "for every XX" is activated.
- **Q7475** (2026-09-18) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect reference this card's own color as well? — A: Yes, it can. Additionally, it can also reference the color gained by this card's [All Turns] effect.
- **Q7476** (2026-09-18) Q: My Digimon and Tamers' colors currently total four. I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect and choose the first effect to have it battle an opponent's Digimon with an [On Deletion] effect, and that Digimon gets deleted. If I then choose the second effect and return that deleted card to the bottom of the deck, will it prevent that returned card's [On Deletion] effect from activating? — A: Yes, it will prevent it from activating. If a card with an effect pending activation leaves the area before the effect is activated, then the effect can't be activated.
- **Q7477** (2026-09-18) Q: When I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect and choose its first effect to have it battle one of my opponent's Digimon, my opponent plays a new Digimon with one of their Digimon's immediate-type effects. After that happens, can I use the chosen first effect to battle the opponent's newly played Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q7478** (2026-09-18) Q: I have this card, 1 "red and blue Digimon," and 1 "red and yellow Tamer" in the battle area. If I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, how many times can I activate "activate 1 effect below"? — A: Two times. In this situation, your Digimon and Tamers' colors are red, blue, yellow, and white, so you can activate it twice.
