## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying the cost. [Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [All Turns] When any of your Digimon with [Leomon] in their names or the [Boss] or [DATA SQUAD] trait are played, by placing this Tamer as any of those Digimon's bottom digivolution card, your opponent's effects can't return 1 of your Digimon to hands or decks, or trash its stacked cards until their turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon with [Leomon] in its name or the [Boss] or [DATA SQUAD] trait gets +3000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- This means that the effect prevents processing of "(perform XX on) stacked cards." For example, a "can't trash stacked cards" effect prevents trashing the top stacked cards by (which trashes the top stacked cards) or similar effects, and it prevents trashing the bottom stacked cards by "trash a digivolution card" or similar effects. However, because the effect only prevents processing "(perform XX on) stacked cards," XX is still permitted to be performed on the Digimon itself, for instance by effects that tell you to "perform XX" on the Digimon.
