## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4870** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I activate this card's <Decode (Red/Black Lv.3)> and <Decode (Blue/Yellow Lv.3)> to play a combination of 1 red level 3 card and 1 black level 3 card, or 1 blue level 3 card and 1 yellow level 3 card? — A: No, you can't. When this card would be removed from the battle area other than by a battle, you can play a combination of 1 red or black level 3 card from <Decode (Red/Black Lv.3)> and 1 blue or yellow level 3 card from <Decode (Blue/Yellow Lv.3)>.
- **Q4871** (2025-07-04) Q: This card has 3 level 4 cards and 3 level 5 cards in its digivolution cards. How many of my opponent's Digimon do I return to the bottom of the deck using this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: 2 Digimon. For every pair of 2 same-level cards, you choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon and return it to the bottom of the deck.
