## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve 3＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, delete up to 6 play cost's total worth of their Digimon. [When Attacking] By trashing up to 3 level 6 or higher cards from this Digimon's digivolution cards, for each card trashed, activate the effect below. Then, if you trashed 3 cards, trash your opponent's top 2 security cards. ・Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-073 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3519** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I choose to not delete any of my opponent's Digimon by not choosing them for this card's [When Digivolving] effect? — A: No, you can't. You must delete at least 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
- **Q3520** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I activate this card's [When Attacking] effect, but then choose to not trash any cards in this card's digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. If this card's [When Attacking] effect activates, you must trash at least 1 card from digivolution cards. However, if you choose to not activate this effect, it won't activate, and no digivolution cards will be trashed.
- **Q3521** (2025-10-03) Q: If I trash 2 digivolution cards for this card's [When Attacking] effect, do I delete 2 of my opponent's Digimon or Tamers at the same time? — A: No, the 2 Digimon or Tamers aren't deleted at the same time. You repeat this card's [When Attacking] effect of deleting 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost for each card trashed from digivolution cards. In this case, you trashed 2 digivolution cards, therefore you first delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost, then you again delete 1 of their Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost.
- **Q3522** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has BT14-062 [Datamon] and a Digimon with a play cost of 7. If I trash 2 digivolution cards for this card's [When Attacking] effect, can I delete BT14-062 [Datamon] and the Digimon with a play cost of 7? — A: No, you can't. You repeat this card's [When Attacking] effect of deleting 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost for each card trashed from digivolution cards. In this case, you choose BT14-062 [Datamon] as the Digimon with the lowest play cost, but its effect prevents it from being deleted both times. _(related: BT14-062)_
- **Q6033** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [When Attacking] effect and trashed 1 of this card's digivolution cards. If this effect activates again during the same turn and 2 more of this card's digivolution cards are trashed, is the "if you trashed 3 cards" condition met? — A: No, it isn't met. This condition is only met if you trashed 3 cards for a single activation.
