## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If your opponent has a suspended Digimon in play, gain 2 memory. [Your Turn] When you attack with a level 5 green Digimon, you may suspend this Tamer to reveal 3 cards from the top of your deck. You may digivolve 1 green level 6 Digimon card among them onto the attacking Digimon without paying its memory cost. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT5-089 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1360** (2026-03-13) Q: My opponent has a suspended Digimon with <Reboot>. Upon my turn, can I use this card's [Start of Your Turn] effect to gain 2 memory before that opponent Digimon unsuspends in the unsuspend phase? — A: Yes, you can. [Start of Your Turn] effects trigger and activate before cards are unsuspended for the unsuspend phase.
- **Q1361** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to reveal cards from my deck, but then choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can choose to not digivolve. In such cases, you return all of the revealed cards to the bottom of your deck in any order.
- **Q1362** (2026-03-13) Q: When a digivolution would occur by this card's [Your Turn] effect, when exactly is the timing for the digivolution bonus draw and where do I draw from? — A: You perform the digivolution bonus draw when this card is placed on top of that Digimon for the digivolution, and you draw from the unrevealed cards in your deck. A digivolution includes the bonus draw in accordance with the rules. After the card to digivolve is stacked and the digivolution bonus draw is performed, you process the remaining effects.
- **Q1363** (2026-03-13) Q: If I use this card's [Your Turn] effect to digivolve, can I activate the digivolved card's [When Digivolving] effect before returning the remaining cards to the bottom of the deck? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q1364** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect when I have 2 or fewer cards in my deck? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you reveal as many cards from your deck as possible.
- **Q1365** (2026-03-13) Q: 2 copies of this card are in the battle area. Can I activate the [Your Turn] effect on the 2 copies of this card, reveal 6 cards from the top of my deck, and then digivolve my Digimon into a card among them? — A: No, you can't. When your Digimon attacks, the [Your Turn] effects on the 2 copies of this card trigger simultaneously. Effects that trigger simultaneously can be activated 1 at a time, therefore you can activate the [Your Turn] effect on the 1st copy of this card, and once all effects have been resolved, you can then activate the [Your Turn] effect on the 2nd copy of this card.
- **Q1366** (2026-03-13) Q: When a green level 5 Digimon with a [When Attacking] effect attacks, in what order can this card's [Your Turn] effect and the attacking Digimon's [When Attacking] effect activate? — A: The effects trigger simultaneously, so the player can choose the activation order. However, if this card's [Your Turn] effect is activated first and that Digimon digivolves, its [When Attacking] effect is considered to be lost and can no longer activate.
- **Q1367** (2026-03-13) Q: My green Digimon that isn't level 5 attacks, and another effect digivolves it into a green level 5 Digimon. Can I then activate this card's [Your Turn] effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. This card's [Your Turn] effect triggers when a green level 5 Digimon attacks. It doesn't trigger when other Digimon attack, therefore it can't be activated.
