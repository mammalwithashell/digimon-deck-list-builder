## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] By trashing 1 Digimon card with the [Machine], [Cyborg] or [SoC] trait in your hand, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT15-097 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2594** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use this card when my opponent has a Digimon with a play cost of 3 and a Tamer with a play cost of 4, can I delete my opponent's Tamer with a play cost of 4 as the Tamer with the lowest play cost? — A: No, you can't. You must delete the card with the lowest play cost from among all your opponent's Digimon and Tamers for this effect. In the example given in the question, you would delete the Digimon with a play cost of 3 as the card with the lowest play cost.
