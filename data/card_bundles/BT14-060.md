## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 card with the [D-Brigade] or [DigiPolice] trait and a play cost of 3 or less among them without paying the cost. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. (Rule) Name: Also treated as [Commandramon].

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects, by deleting 1 of your other Digimon with the [D-Brigade] trait, prevent it from leaving.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2428** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "(Rule) Name: Also treated as [Commandramon]" mean, exactly? — A: This is a special rule for this card that treats it as a card that has the [Commandramon] name. This is a rule and not an effect, therefore this card is treated as having the [Commandramon] name when the card is in your hand, breeding area, or any other area.
- **Q2429** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card be selected as the target of an effect that targets exactly [Commandramon] due to the text "(Rule) Name: Also treated as [Commandramon]"? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q2430** (2024-03-28) Q: If one of the cards revealed from my deck by this card's [When Attacking] effect is a Tamer card with the [D-Brigade] or [DigiPolice] trait and a play cost of 3 or less, can I play that card? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2431** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "When [...] would [...] leave the battle area" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area" refers to when this Digimon is placed in the trash, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
