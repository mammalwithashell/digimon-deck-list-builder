### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top card of your deck. If it's a green Digimon card, add it to your hand. Otherwise, place it at the bottom of your deck.


## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST4-03 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3192** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card is revealed from my deck with an effect like the one on [ST4-03 Tentomon], can I add it to my hand as if it was a green Digimon? — A: No. This card’s effects aren't activated when you reveal it from your deck with an effect. As such, the card isn't considered to be a green Digimon card, and can’t be added to your hand. _(a ruling on EX1-005; this card is named in it)_
