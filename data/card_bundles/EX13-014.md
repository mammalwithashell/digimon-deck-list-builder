- **Q7243** (2026-09-18) Q: What kind of card does "a card with X in its text" refer to? — A: It refers to a card that contains the specified text or icon in its name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule), digivolve requirements, DNA Digivolve, DigiXros requirements, Burst Digivolve, App Fusion, Link, or Assembly requirements. For example, "a card with [Knightmon] in its text" would include cards with the name [DarkKnightmon] and cards with the text [Knightmon] in their effects.
- **Q7244** (2026-09-18) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when this card itself is played? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q7245** (2026-09-18) Q: When I activate this card's [All Turns] effect and my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area due to processing the 1st part, an immediate-type effect such as "when [...] would leave" removes this card from the battle area.
