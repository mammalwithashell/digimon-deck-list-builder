## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-006 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2370** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's inherited effect digivolve a Digimon in the breeding area into a Digimon with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait that was trashed from my hand? — A: No, it can’t. Digimon in the breeding area are not included in the target of this effect.
- **Q2371** (2024-03-28) Q: When a Digimon card with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait is trashed from my hand, can I use this card's inherited effect to digivolve into that card regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q2372** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's inherited effect to digivolve without paying the digivolution cost? — A: No, you must pay the digivolution cost.
