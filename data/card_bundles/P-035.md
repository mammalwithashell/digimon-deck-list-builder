## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 red Digimon card among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order. Then, place this card in your battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (Trash this card in your battle area to activate the effect below. You can't activate this effect the turn this card enters play.) ・Gain 2 memory.

### Security Effect
[Security] Place this card in its owner's battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-035 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4149** (2024-03-28) Q: When I place this card in my battle area, can I use a red Option card even if I have no other red Digimon or Tamers in play? — A: No. Having this card in your battle area doesn't fulfill color requirements.
- **Q4150** (2024-03-28) Q: When I place this card in my battle area, can I activate this card's <Delay> effect even if I have no other red Digimon or Tamers in play? — A: Yes, if this card is in your battle area, you can activate <Delay>, regardless of color requirements. Color requirements only matter when using Option cards.
- **Q5315** (2025-10-03) Q: When this card is used to check my opponent's security stack, P-035 [Red Memory Boost!] is checked, and its [Security] effect places that card in the battle area. Can I then use this card's [Your Turn] effect to trash the P-035 [Red Memory Boost!] that was placed in the battle area? — A: Yes, you can. If this card is used to check your opponent's security stack and P-035 [Red Memory Boost!] is revealed, this card's [Your Turn] effect will trigger at the same time. The [Security] effect will activate first, therefore P-035 [Red Memory Boost!]'s [Security] effect places that card in the battle area. You can then use the [Your Turn] effect that's pending activation on this card to trash that P-035 [Red Memory Boost!].
