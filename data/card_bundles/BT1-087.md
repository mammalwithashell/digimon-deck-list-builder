## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set your memory to 3. [On Play] Look at your security stack, then reveal 1 card in it and add it to your hand. If that card is yellow, trigger ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞. (Place the top card of your deck on top of your security stack.) Then shuffle your security stack.

### Inherited Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Play this card without paying its memory cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT1-087 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q950** (2024-03-28) Q: I have both this card and [ST2-12 Matt Ishida] in play. How do I activate their effects? — A: You can resolve their effects in any order. For example, if you're starting the turn at 1 memory, you can resolve this card's effect first to go up to 3 memory, then activate [ST2-12 Matt Ishida]'s effect to go to 4 memory and above.
- **Q951** (2024-03-28) Q: This card's On Play effect says "Look at your security stack." What does this mean, exactly? — A: It allows you to look at all of the cards in your security stack without revealing them to your opponent. You then pick one of those cards, reveal it, and add it to your hand.
- **Q952** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if I use this card's On Play effect to add a card from my security stack to my hand that isn't yellow? — A: Shuffle your deck without triggering <Recovery +1 (Deck)>.
- **Q1250** (2024-03-28) Q: While this Tamer is in my battle area, I use [BT1-087 Matt Ishida]'s effect to add a yellow card from my security stack to my hand, triggering <Recovery +1 (Deck)> and adding a card to my security stack. The number of cards in my security stack remained the same, but can I still suspend this card to gain 1 memory? — A: Yes, because a card was removed from your security stack. _(a ruling on BT4-097; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2414** (2024-03-28) Q: [BT1-087 T.K. Takaishi]’s effect adds a yellow card from my security stack to my hand, and <Recovery +1 (Deck)> adds a card to my security stack. The number of cards in my security stack ultimately remained the same, but does 1 of my opponent's Digimon still get -7000 DP and does this Digimon still gain <Security A. +1> from this card’s effect? — A: Yes. A card was added to your security stack with <Recovery +1 (Deck)>, therefore 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP and this Digimon gains <Security A. +1>. _(a ruling on BT14-041; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3213** (2024-03-28) Q: I have a Digimon in play with this card in its digivolution cards. [BT1-087 T.K. Takaishi]’s effect adds a yellow card from my security stack to my hand, and adds a card to my security stack with <Recovery +1 (Deck)>. The number of cards in my security stack ultimately remained the same, but can I still gain 1 memory from this card’s inherited effect? — A: Yes. <Recovery +1 (Deck)> added a card to your security, so you gain 1 memory. _(a ruling on EX1-029; this card is named in it)_
