## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX3-063 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2891** (2024-06-21) Q: [All Turns] When my Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted other than by one of my effects during my opponent's turn, I used this card's [All Turns] effect to digivolve that Digimon into EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]. Can I then use <Blitz> in EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]'s [When Digivolving] effect to attack? — A: No, you can't. Only the turn player can attack. In this case, your EX3-063 [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode] can't attack because it isn't your turn. _(a ruling on BT17-097; this card is named in it)_
