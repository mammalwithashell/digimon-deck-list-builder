## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Unsuspend this Digimon. Then, if [WereGarurumon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, return 1 of your opponent’s level 4 or lower Digimon to its owner’s hand.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [WereGarurumon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
