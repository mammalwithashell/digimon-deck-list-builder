## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may place 1 [Sistermon Ciel] from your hand or trash at the bottom of this Digimon's digivolution cards to delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 5 or less. [On Deletion] Return 1 card with [Jesmon], [Huckmon], or [Sistermon] in its name other than [Sistermon Ciel (Awakened)] from your trash to your hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-083 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1648** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I place a [Sistermon Ciel (Awakened)] from my hand or trash at the bottom of this Digimon's digivolution cards with this card's [On Play] effect? — A: No, you can't. This effect can only place [Sistermon Ciel].
- **Q1649** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's [On Deletion] effect return [BaoHuckmon], [SaviorHuckmon], or [Sistermon Blanc (Awakened)] from my trash to my hand? — A: Yes. Those cards have [Huckmon] and/or [Sistermon] in their names, so you can add one of them to your hand.
- **Q1650** (2024-08-01) Q: What does this card's (Rule) mean, exactly? (*This card's "Also treated as Name: [Sistermon Noir (Awakened)] and "Also treated as Trait: [Virus]" have been changed to "(Rule) Also treated as Name: [Sistermon Noir (Awakened)] and Trait: [Virus]") " — A: This is a special rule for this card that always treats it as if it also has the name [Sistermon Noir (Awakened)] and the [Virus] trait.
