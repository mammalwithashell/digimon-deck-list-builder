## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX8-016 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3877** (2024-11-22) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to suspend either my Digimon or my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, you can suspend either player's Digimon.
- **Q3878** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card in the battle area, and it's suspended. Can my opponent's BT8-018 [Marsmon] attack their unsuspended Digimon? — A: No, it can't attack. If a "can" or "may" effect activates at the same timing as a "can't" effect, the "can't" effect takes precedence. _(related: BT8-018)_
- **Q3879** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card in the battle area, and it's suspended. If my opponent's Digimon attacks my suspended Digimon, can my opponent use <Raid> on the attacking Digimon to switch the target of the attack to my unsuspended Digimon? — A: Yes, they can. This card's [Opponent's Turn] effect only allows suspended Digimon to be chosen as the attack target upon the attack declaration. After attacking a suspended Digmon, it will be possible to use <Raid> to switch the target of the attack to an opponent's unsuspended Digimon.
- **Q3880** (2024-11-22) Q: I have this card in the battle area, and it's suspended. If an opponent's Digimon isn't affected by effects, can it attack Digimon other than suspended Digimon? — A: Yes, it can attack them.
