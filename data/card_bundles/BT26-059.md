## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if your hand has fewer cards than your opponent's, reduce the cost by 6. [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By trashing 1 card in your hand, if it's your turn, you may play 1 [Titan] trait Digimon card from your trash with the cost reduced by 7. This effect can't play [Plutomon]. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When hands are trashed from, you may delete all of your opponent's lowest level Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-059 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7074** (2026-08-18) Q: My opponent and I have the same number of cards in our hands. When I play this card from my hand, can I activate its first effect? — A: No, you can't. The card is still in your hand when you announce you are playing it from your hand. Therefore, the number of cards in your hand and your opponent's hand remains the same.
- **Q7075** (2026-08-18) Q: My opponent and I have the same number of cards in our hands. When I play this card from any area other than my hand, can I activate this card's first effect? — A: No, you can't.
- **Q7076** (2026-08-18) Q: Can I activate this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect during my opponent's turn and trash a card in my hand? — A: Yes, you can. However, you can't process the part of the effect that follows after "if it's your turn."
- **Q7077** (2026-08-18) Q: If I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect to play [BT26-045 GranKuwagamon], can I activate [BT26-046 GranKuwagamon]'s 1st effect and reduce the play cost by a total of 11? — A: Yes, the play cost will be reduced by a total of 11. _(related: BT26-045)_
- **Q7078** (2026-08-18) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger when cards are trashed from either player's hand? — A: Yes, it does.
