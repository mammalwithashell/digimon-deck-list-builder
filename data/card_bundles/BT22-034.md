## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When effects trash this card from the security stack, you may play this card without paying the cost. [On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP until their turn ends. By trashing your top security card, instead 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP until their turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn.

## Official Q&A
- The processing shown after "instead" in the effect replaces the standard processing. For example, if an effect reads "Delete your opponent's Digimon with 6000 DP or less. If you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, instead delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP," and you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name, you delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest DP instead of deleting 1 of their Digimon with 6000 DP or less.
