### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon becomes suspended, for each other suspended Digimon with [Vegetation], [Plant],or [Fairy] in one of its traits you have in play, gain 1 memory. [End of Your Turn][Once Per turn] If you have 2 or more suspended Digimon with [Vegetation], [Plant], or [Fairy] in one of their traits, return 1 of your opponent's suspended Digimon at the bottom of its owner's deck.
