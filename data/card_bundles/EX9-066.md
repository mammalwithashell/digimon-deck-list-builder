### Effect
[On Play] You may return 1 Digimon card with [Greymon], [Garurumon] or [Omnimon] in its name from your trash to the hand. If this effect didn't return, ＜Draw 1＞. [All Turns] When any of your Digimon are played or digivolve, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory if you have a Digimon with [Greymon] in its name.
