## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Security] You may play 1 [Lucemon] from your trash without paying the cost. [Breeding] [All Turns] When any of your [Lucemon: Satan Mode] would leave the battle area, by moving this Digimon to the battle area, they don't leave. ## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents all of your 0 DP Digimon from being deleted. It prevents deletion from effects as well as deletion from the rules (including the rules that delete 0 DP Digimon upon a rule check and deletion upon losing a battle).
