## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card would be played, if you don't have a level 5 or lower Digimon, reduce the play cost by 5. [Start of Your Main Phase] [On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Angel], [Archangel], [Three Great Angels] or [Seven Great Demon Lords] trait among them to the hand. Trash the rest. [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 of your level 6 Digimon on top of your security stack, this Digimon may digivolve into [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX6-018 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3714** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's "when you would play this card" effect also activate when this card would be played by an effect? — A: Yes, it activates.
- **Q3715** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to place 1 of my level 6 Digimon on top of my security stack even when I don't have [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in my trash? — A: Yes, you can. Even if the part after the conditions specified with "by X" in an effect can't be resolved, the effect can still activate.
- **Q3716** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to place 1 of my level 6 Digimon on top of my security stack but choose to not digivolve this Digimon into [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in my trash? — A: Yes, that's correct.
- **Q5002** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to digivolve into a BT7-111 [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in the trash? — A: No, you can't. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements. _(related: BT7-111)_
