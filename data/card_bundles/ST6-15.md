## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=ST6-15 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q676** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to delete one of my Digimon with a [When Deleted] effect. Can I activate the [When Deleted] effect before this card's "delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon" effect? — A: No. You must fully activate this card's effect, then activate the [When Deleted] effect.
- **Q677** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card to delete one of my own Digimon, and an opponent's Digimon. If they both have [When Deleted] effects, which effect resolves first? — A: They activate at the same time, which means the turn player resolves their effect first.
