## Official Q&A
- No, it can't. For this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, you chose 1 of your opponent's Digmon with no digivolution cards to gain "can't suspend or activate [When Digivolving] effects." The effect will continue affecting the chosen Digimon even if it later gets a digivolution card.
