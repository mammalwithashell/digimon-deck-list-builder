## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have a Tamer with the [Light Fang] or [Night Claw] trait, gain 1 memory.

### Inherited Effect
[Main][Once Per Turn] By placing the top card of this Digimon with the [Light Fang] or [Night Claw] trait as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, gain 2 memory.

## Official Q&A
- It means that you place the top card of the Digimon with this card in its digivolution cards as the bottom digivolution card. For example, if you have a level 4 Digimon with just this card in its digivolution cards, you place that level 4 Digimon card under this card. This will turn this Digimon into a level 3 Digimon with the level 4 Digimon card as a digivolution card.
