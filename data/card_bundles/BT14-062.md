### Effect
[All Turns] This Digimon can't be deleted by your opponent's effects.

## Official Q&A
- This effect prevents deletion when this Digimon is the target of an effect with "delete" in its card text. Deletion will still occur if you lose a battle, both players lose a battle, or if this Digimon's DP is reduced to 0.
