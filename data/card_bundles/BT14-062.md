## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT14-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2433** (2024-03-28) Q: This card’s [All Turns] effect reads "This Digimon can't be deleted by your opponent's effects." How does this effect work, exactly? — A: This effect prevents deletion when this Digimon is the target of an effect with "delete" in its card text. Deletion will still occur if you lose a battle, both players lose a battle, or if this Digimon's DP is reduced to 0.
- **Q3071** (2024-09-20) Q: My opponent has BT14-062 [Datamon] and a Digimon with 8000 DP or less. If I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to choose BT14-062 [Datamon] without deleting a Digimon, is the "if this effect didn't delete" condition met? — A: Yes, it is. If your opponent has a Digimon that can't be deleted but can be chosen for this effect, the "if this effect didn't delete" condition can be met by choosing that Digimon. _(a ruling on BT19-015; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3522** (2025-10-03) Q: My opponent has BT14-062 [Datamon] and a Digimon with a play cost of 7. If I trash 2 digivolution cards for this card's [When Attacking] effect, can I delete BT14-062 [Datamon] and the Digimon with a play cost of 7? — A: No, you can't. You repeat this card's [When Attacking] effect of deleting 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers with the lowest play cost for each card trashed from digivolution cards. In this case, you choose BT14-062 [Datamon] as the Digimon with the lowest play cost, but its effect prevents it from being deleted both times. _(a ruling on EX4-073; this card is named in it)_
