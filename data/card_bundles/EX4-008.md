## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Trash the top 2 cards of both players' decks. Then, you may return 1 [Guilmon] or 1 card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name from your trash to your hand.

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] You may return 1 [Guilmon] or 1 card with [Growlmon] or [Gallantmon] in its name from your trash to your hand.
