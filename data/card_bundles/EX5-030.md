## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Leomon] in its name in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 1. (Rule) Name: Also treated as having [Leomon].

### Inherited Effect
[On Deletion] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP until the end of their turn.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can’t ignore digivolution requirements when using this effect. You can only digivolve this card into an applicable Digimon card with [Leomon] in its name.
