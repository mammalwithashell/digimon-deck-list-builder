## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 play cost 7 or lower Digimon card with the [NSp] trait from your hand without paying the cost. If DNA digivolving, you may instead play up to 2 such Digimon cards with different colors from your hand without paying the cost. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -7000 DP for the turn for each of your Digimon.

## Official Q&A
- This card can DNA digivolve using a level 6 green or yellow Digimon and a level 6 black or blue Digimon.
