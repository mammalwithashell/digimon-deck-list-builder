## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Attacking] This Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Leomon] in its name in the hand or trash.

### Inherited Effect
[End of Attack] By deleting this Digimon, you may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
- No, this card's effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
