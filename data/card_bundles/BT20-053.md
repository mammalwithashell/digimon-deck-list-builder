## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 [Dorumon] or [Ryudamon] from your hand to your empty breeding area without paying the cost. Then, if during an attack, until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects and gets +5000 DP.

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, you may change the attack target to this Digimon.
