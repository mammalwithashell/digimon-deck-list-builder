## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may play 1 [Agumon] or [Gabumon] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When one of your level 6 Digimon with [Greymon] or [Garurumon] in its name would leave the battle area outside of a battle, ＜Delay＞. ・That Digimon and a card in the hand may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card with [Omnimon] in its name in the hand.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Matt Ishida] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
- "When [...] would leave the battle area other than by battle" refers to when that Digimon is placed in the trash other than as a result of a battle, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
