## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] You may play 1 [Agumon] or [Gabumon] from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area. [All Turns] When one of your level 6 Digimon with [Greymon] or [Garurumon] in its name would leave the battle area outside of a battle, ＜Delay＞. ・That Digimon and a card in the hand may DNA digivolve into a Digimon card with [Omnimon] in its name in the hand.

### Security Effect
[Security] You may play 1 card with [Tai Kamiya] or [Matt Ishida] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-095 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2881** (2024-03-28) Q: What does this card's "when [...] would leave the battle area other than by [...] a battle" mean, exactly? — A: "When [...] would leave the battle area other than by battle" refers to when that Digimon is placed in the trash other than as a result of a battle, returned to the hand/deck, placed in the security stack, moved to the breeding area, or placed under another card.
- **Q2882** (2024-03-28) Q: With this card's <Delay> effect, can I DNA digivolve into a Digimon card in my hand that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolution]? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve into a Digimon card that doesn’t have [DNA Digivolution].
- **Q2883** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's <Delay> effect allow me to DNA digivolve into a card other than the Digimon specified by [DNA Digivolution] in the hand? — A: No. You can’t ignore the digivolution requirements, so you can’t DNA digivolve using Digimon that aren’t specified in [DNA Digivolution].
- **Q4432** (2025-01-24) Q: If I use this card's <Delay> to DNA digivolve to DNA digivolve using 2 Digimon including one that would leave the battle area, does the DNA digivolved Digimon leave the battle area? — A: No, it doesn't leave the battle area. DNA digivolution results in a digivolution into a new Digimon that is considered a different Digimon from the one that would leave the battle area.
