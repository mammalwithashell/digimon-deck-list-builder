## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX1-005 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2082** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as EX1-005 [Tyrannomon] has an effect that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the effect remains as-is. For example, if EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s effect will also treat it as green, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon. _(a ruling on BT11-043; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2480** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as EX1-005 [Tyrannomon] has an effect that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the effect remains as-is. For example, if EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s effect will also treat it as green, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon. _(a ruling on BT14-097; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3192** (2024-03-28) Q: When this card is revealed from my deck with an effect like the one on [ST4-03 Tentomon], can I add it to my hand as if it was a green Digimon? — A: No. This card’s effects aren't activated when you reveal it from your deck with an effect. As such, the card isn't considered to be a green Digimon card, and can’t be added to your hand. _(related: ST4-03)_
- **Q3193** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is in my battle area, can I digivolve it into a Digimon with “Green/Level 4” digivolution requirements? — A: If it’s your turn, then yes, you can.
- **Q3194** (2024-03-28) Q: If this Digimon is in my breeding area, can I digivolve it into a Digimon with “Green/Level 4” digivolution requirements? — A: No, you can't. Effects don't activate from breeding areas, so this Digimon isn't treated as green, and you can't digivolve it into a Digimon that requires a green level 4 Digimon.
