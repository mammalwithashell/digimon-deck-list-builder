## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 2 level 6 or higher cards among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. [End of Your Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon, you may play 1 Digimon card with the [Dark Masters] trait from your hand to an empty space in your breeding area without paying the cost.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT15-062 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2545** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] effect to add a card to my hand when only 1 level 6 or higher card is among the revealed cards? — A: Yes, you can. In such cases, you add as many cards to your hand as possible.
- **Q2546** (2024-03-28) Q: Do [On Play] effects activate for Digimon played into the breeding area by this card's [End of Your Turn] effect? — A: No, the effect doesn’t activate for Digimon in breeding areas.
- **Q2547** (2024-03-28) Q: Do "when a Digimon is played" effects trigger for Digimon played into the breeding area by this card's [End of Your Turn] effect? — A: No, Digimon in breeding areas aren't referenced by effects, so they don't trigger.
- **Q2548** (2024-03-28) Q: I used this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to play a Digimon into the breeding area, and another effect moved the memory to 0 or more, and my turn continued. If the Digimon that was played into the breeding area is then moved to the battle area by an effect, can that Digimon attack? — A: No. It can't attack because the "Digimon can't attack the turn they came into play" rule still applies to Digimon played into the breeding area.
- **Q2549** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to play a Digimon into the breeding area when it's affected by a "Digimon can't be played by effects" effect? — A: No, you can't. A "can't be played by effects" effect affects a player when it activates, therefore the act of playing a Digimon itself won't be possible, whether in the breeding area or the battle area.
