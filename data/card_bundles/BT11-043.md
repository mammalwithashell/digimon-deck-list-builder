## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play][When Digivolving] If your opponent has 16 or more cards in their trash, or you have 3 or more cards with [Sukamon] in their names in your trash, change 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon] until the end of your opponent's turn. [When Attacking] For each other Digimon with [Sukamon] in its name in play, this Digimon gains ＜Security Attack +1＞ for the turn.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] When this Digimon would be deleted, by deleting 1 other Digimon with [Sukamon] in its name, prevent that deletion.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT11-043 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1334** (2024-03-28) Q: This card that was targeted by [BT11-043 KingSukamon]'s effect and had its name changed to [Sukamon] is deleted. If I have no other [Palmon] in the trash, can I place this same card at the bottom of the digivolution cards of one of my green Digimon from the trash with it's [On Deletion] effect? — A: Yes, you can. The [On Deletion] effect is activated while treated as [Sukamon], but the name changing effect is lost when the card is deleted and placed in the trash, so you can treat this card as a [Palmon] card. _(a ruling on BT5-047; this card is named in it)_
- **Q2078** (2024-03-28) Q: Can this card's inherited effect delete an opponent's [Sukamon] to prevent itself from being deleted? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2079** (2024-06-21) Q: I have [Sukamon] A and [Sukamon] B with this card in their digivolution cards in the battle area. When [Sukamon] A would be deleted, I attempted to delete [Sukamon] B using this card's inherited effect. On the other hand, when [Sukamon] B would be deleted, I attempted to delete [Sukamon] A using this card's inherited effect. At such times, can [Sukamon] A interrupt this card's inherited effect again? — A: No, it can't interrupt it. Until the processing for the 1st interruption has resolved, only 1 activation is possible for each "when [...] would" effect. In your case, until the deletion of the 1st only [Sukamon] A is resolved, only 1 interruption is possible for each "when [...] would" effect, so because [Sukamon] A's effect has already interrupted, it can't interrupt again.
- **Q2080** (2024-09-20) Q: What does a "change 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]" effect do, exactly? — A: This effect changes 1 of your opponent's Digimon into a Digimon whose original name is [Sukamon], its original color is white, and its original DP is 3000.
- **Q2081** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as BT15-035 [Geremon] has a (Rule) that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the (Rule) will also be changed. For example, BT15-035 [Geremon]'s (Rule) gives it an original name of [Geremon/Numemon], but if its original card information is changed by this effect, its original name becomes just [Sukamon]. _(related: BT15-035)_
- **Q2082** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card such as EX1-005 [Tyrannomon] has an effect that gives it card information, what happens when this card's effect changes it to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP? — A: The card information gained from the effect remains as-is. For example, if EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s original card information is changed, its original color will become white, and EX1-005 [Tyrannomon]'s effect will also treat it as green, making it a 2-color white and green Digimon. _(related: EX1-005)_
- **Q2083** (2024-09-20) Q: If a card is changed to a white Digimon with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, then it digivolves or its top card is trashed by <De-Digivolve> or other effect, what happens to that card's information? — A: It remains as a white Digimon with 3000 DP and an original name of [Sukamon]. The gained effect is carried over even if the Digimon digivolves or one or more of its top cards are trashed.
- **Q2084** (2024-09-20) Q: What happens if a card is changed to a white card with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, then its top card is trashed by <De-Digivolve> or other effect and the top card becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card? — A: It remains changed to a white card with an original name of [Sukamon] and 3000 DP, but any card information that it didn't originally have can't be changed. If it becomes a Tamer or Digi-Egg card, or if it becomes a card that didn't originally have DP, then it will remain without DP.
