## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until your opponent's turn ends, give 1 of their Digimon or Tamers "[End of Your Turn] Delete 1 of your Digimon." [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon are played or deleted, by trashing any 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: 2 Digimon cards w/[Bagra Army] trait When this would be played, you may place specified cards from your hand/battle area under it. Each one reduces the play cost.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [All Turns] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
