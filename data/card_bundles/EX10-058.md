## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX10-058 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5157** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I trash just 1 digivolution card for the conditions of this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [All Turns] effect require you to trash the specified number of digivolution cards.
- **Q5158** (2025-09-05) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving effect to give "[End of Your Turn] Delete 1 of your Digimon" to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, that effect won't trigger.
- **Q5159** (2025-09-05) Q: If I give "[End of Your Turn] Delete 1 of your Digimon" to an opponent's Digimon, it activates, and my opponent chooses one of their Digimon that isn't affected by my effects, is that Digimon deleted? — A: Yes, it's deleted. After giving "[End of Your Turn] Delete 1 of your Digimon" to an opponent's Digimon, it activates as your opponent's own effect.
- **Q5160** (2025-09-05) Q: If I use this card's [All Turns] effect to trash 2 cards including EX10-044 [Damemon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards, then play the trashed EX10-044 [Damemon] from the trash, can I activate EX10-044 [Damemon]'s inherited effect? — A: No, you can't activate it. When a card with an effect that's pending activation leaves its current area while activation is pending, the effect can no longer be activated. In this case, being trashed from digivolution cards will trigger EX10-044 [Damemon]'s inherited effect, but it leaves the trash before activation, therefore it doesn't activate. _(related: EX10-044)_
- **Q5168** (2025-09-05) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to place 3 cards including EX10-058 [Lilithmon] from my trash as this Digimon's top digivolution cards and delete my opponent's Digimon. This card's [All Turns] effect gives it EX10-058 [Lilithmon]'s [All Turns] effect at such times, but does it trigger from this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect deleting my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it triggers.
