## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with 8000 DP or less, and return 1 of your opponent's level 6 or higher Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by one of your effects, play 1 [BlitzGreymon] and 1 [CresGarurumon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the costs. Then, place this Digimon at the bottom of your security stack face down.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX4-060 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3501** (2026-03-13) Q: I activated this card's [When Digivolving] effect, and when my opponent's Digimon would leave the battle area for the 1st process, an immediate-type effect such as a "when [...] would leave" effect caused this card to be removed from the battle area. Can I then process the rest of this effect? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect activates, it is to be fully resolved even if the card that activated the effect is removed from that area during the processing.
- **Q6031** (2026-03-13) Q: 1 [BlitzGreymon] and 1 [CresGarurumon] are in this card's digivolution cards. Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to play just [BlitzGreymon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: No, you can't. You must play the specified cards for this card's [All Turns] effect whenever possible.
- **Q6032** (2026-03-13) Q: There is just 1 [BlitzGreymon] in this card's digivolution cards. Can I use this card's [All Turns] effect to play just [BlitzGreymon] from this Digimon's digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it can be played.
