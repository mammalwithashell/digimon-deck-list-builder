## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Kyubimon], [Taomon] or [Sakuyamon] in its name and 1 [Rika Nonaka] or use cost 2 or higher Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When you use Option cards with a use cost of 2 or more, give 1 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ until their turn ends.
