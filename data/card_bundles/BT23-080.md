## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-080 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5355** (2025-10-03) Q: An opponent's Digimon with <Piercing> attacks my [CS] trait Digimon, then when my Digimon would be deleted in battle, I activate this card's [All Turns] effect and place my Digimon on top of the security stack. At such times, can <Piercing> on my opponent's attacking Digimon activate? — A: No, it can't. <Piercing> triggers when a Digimon with that effect attacks and the opponent's Digimon is deleted in battle. Your [CS] trait Digimon that would be deleted is placed on top of the security stack by this card's [All Turns] effect, preventing that deletion. Therefore, <Piercing> on your opponent's Digimon doesn't trigger or activate.
