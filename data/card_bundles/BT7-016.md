## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] ＜Blitz＞ (This Digimon can attack when your opponent has 1 or more memory.) [Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon is blocked, unsuspend it, and gain 1 memory for each digivolution card this Digimon has with [Hybrid] in its traits.

## Official Q&A
- The [Your Turn] effect is activated immediately after this Digimon is blocked and it's attack target is changed. So, since the effect is triggered by your opponent blocking this Digimon, it will finish resolving before the battle against the blocking Digimon.
