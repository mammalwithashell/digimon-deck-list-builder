## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[All Turns][Once Per Turn] When another Digimon with [Etemon] or [Sukamon] in its name is deleted, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP and gains ＜Security Attack -1＞ until the end of your opponent's turn. [Opponent's Turn] All of your Digimon with [Etemon] or [Sukamon] in their names gain ＜Blocker＞ and can't be returned to hands or decks.
