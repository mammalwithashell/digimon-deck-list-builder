## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Until the end of your opponent’s turn, your opponent’s Digimon with play costs of 7 or less can’t attack players, and cards can’t be added to security stacks by your opponent’s effects.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT9-103 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q747** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if I use this card while my opponent has [BT9-103 Kongou]'s effect activated? — A: Due to [BT9-103 Kongou]'s effect, cards can't be placed in security stacks, therefore this card's "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack" can't be activated, and the target Digimon will be left as-is. _(a ruling on ST10-14; this card is named in it)_
- **Q1908** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card's "cards can't be added to security stacks by your opponent's effects" mean that effects that add cards to security stacks on "your opponent's Digimon with play costs of 7 or less" can't be activated? — A: No. Cards can't be added to security stacks by your opponent's effects regardless of the card type or level (the effects will activate, but cards still can't be added to security stacks). The "your opponent's Digimon with play costs of 7 or less" refers to the "can't attack players" effect.
- **Q1909** (2024-03-28) Q: What happens if my opponent activates this card's effect and I use [ST10-14 Chaos Degradation]? — A: Due to this card's effect, cards can't be placed in security stacks, therefore [ST10-14 Chaos Degradation]'s "place 1 of your opponent's Digimon face down at the top or bottom of your opponent's security stack" can't be activated, and the target Digimon will be left as-is. _(related: ST10-14)_
- **Q1910** (2024-03-28) Q: While my opponent has this card's effect activated, I used [BT10-086 Omnimon (X Antibody)]'s effect to reveal all of my opponent's security cards. After choosing and trashing 1 card among them, can the rest be placed in the security stack? — A: Yes, the rest are placed in the security stack. When returning cards that were revealed from a private area such as the deck or security stack by an effect, it isn't considered adding cards to such areas, therefore this card's effect doesn't apply. _(related: BT10-086)_
- **Q1960** (2025-11-21) Q: It's currently my turn after the turn when my opponent activated BT9-103 [Kongou]'s effect. If I activate this card's [When Digivolving] effect and use an Option card, can I place that Option card on top of the security stack instead of placing it in the trash? — A: No, you can't place it in the trash. BT9-103 [Kongou]'s effect prevents cards from being added to your security stack, so the used Option card is trashed. _(a ruling on BT10-041; this card is named in it)_
- **Q3464** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's [Opponent's Turn] effect when [BT9-103 Kongou]'s effect is preventing cards from being added to my security stack? — A: You can activate the effect, but you can't add cards to your security stack, therefore the revealed card can't be added to your security stack and is trashed by the rules. _(a ruling on EX4-023; this card is named in it)_
