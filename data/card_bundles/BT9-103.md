## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Until the end of your opponent’s turn, your opponent’s Digimon with play costs of 7 or less can’t attack players, and cards can’t be added to security stacks by your opponent’s effects.

### Security Effect
[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effect.

## Official Q&A
- No. Cards can't be added to security stacks by your opponent's effects regardless of the card type or level (the effects will activate, but cards still can't be added to security stacks). The "your opponent's Digimon with play costs of 7 or less" refers to the "can't attack players" effect.
