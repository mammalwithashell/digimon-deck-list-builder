## Official Q&A

- **Q863** (2024-04-19) Q: Does "if there are 3 or more Digimon" in this card's [Main] effect count both my Digimon and my opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it does. If there are 3 or more total Digimon between you and your opponent, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -12000 DP.
- **Q864** (2024-04-19) Q: What does "it further gets -6000 DP" in this card's [Main] effect mean, exactly? — A: If the conditions are met, the -6000 DP effect will further get -6000 DP for a total reduction of -12000 DP for 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
