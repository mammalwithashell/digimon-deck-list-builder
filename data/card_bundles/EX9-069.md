## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX9-069 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4829** (2025-06-13) Q: Does this card's [Your Turn] effect trigger when a face-down card is placed as a digivolution card under my Digimon in the breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q4830** (2025-06-13) Q: 2 copies of this card are in the battle area. The [Start of Your Main Phase] effects on both copies of this card trigger simultaneously, and I activate that effect on the 1st copy of this card. At such times, in what order do the already triggered [Start of Your Main Phase] effect on the 2nd copy of this card and the triggered [Your Turn] effects on the 2 copies of this card activate? — A: The [Your Turn] effects on the 2 copies of this card will derived trigger, so you can choose whether they will activate, and then you can activate them. Then, the [Start of Your Main Phase] effect on the 2nd copy of this card can activate. The [Start of Your Main Phase] effect on the 2nd copy of this card triggers the [Your Turn] effects on the 2 copies of this card again, therefore if this card is unsuspended, you can also activate the [Your Turn] effects during this timing.
- **Q4978** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [Your Turn] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't suspend this card, the part after "then" in its [Your Turn] effect can't give you <Draw 1>.
