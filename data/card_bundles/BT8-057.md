## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Opponent's Turn] While all of your Digimon are suspended, your opponent can't use Option cards. [Your Turn] When this Digimon becomes unsuspended during your unsuspend phase, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT8-057 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1736** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this Digimon in play. During my turn, I use an Option card like [BT1-109 Smashed Potatoes] or [EX1-071 Win Rate: 60%!] which has an effect that activates “the next time one of your Digimon digivolves”. After that, all of my opponent’s Digimon become suspended. If I then digivolve one of my Digimon, are the effects from the Option cards I used earlier negated? — A: No. You already used the Option cards and activated their effects, so this card’s [Opponent’s Turn] effect can’t negate them. _(related: BT1-109, EX1-071)_
- **Q1737** (2024-03-28) Q: My opponent has this Digimon in play. If I have an Option card with <Delay> that I used from my hand earlier, can I activate its <Delay> effect? — A: Yes, you can. Activating <Delay> on Option cards that are already in your battle area doesn't count as using an Option card.
