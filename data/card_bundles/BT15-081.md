- **Q2575** (2026-03-13) Q: Does this card's {Trash} [All Turns] effect also trigger when an effect plays a Digimon into the opponent's breeding area? — A: No, it doesn't trigger.
- **Q2576** (2026-03-13) Q: Does this card's {Trash} [All Turns] effect also trigger when one of my effects plays an opponent's Digimon? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q2577** (2026-03-13) Q: [On Play] When an opponent's Digimon with an [On Play] effect is played by an effect, in what order do that Digimon's [On Play] effect and this card's {Trash} [All Turns] effect activate? — A: They trigger simultaneously, so the turn player can activate their effect first.
- **Q2578** (2026-03-13) Q: This card is in my trash. My [Leviamon] checked the opponent's security stack, and a Digimon or Tamer was played by a [Security] effect. Can I use this card's {Trash} [All Turns] effect to digivolve the attacking [Leviamon] at such times? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2579** (2026-03-13) Q: A [Leviamon] with <Security A. +1> attacked a player, and a Digimon or Tamer was played by a [Security] effect upon the 2nd security check. If [Leviamon] digivolves into this card at such times, how many remaining security checks does that Digimon have? — A: It has 1 remaining security check.
- **Q4707** (2025-06-13) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [When Digivolving] effect even if the "if" condition in the 1st process isn't met? — A: Yes, you can process it. Even if your opponent doesn't have as many or more total Digimon and Tamers as you, this card's [When Digivolving] effect will delete 1 of your opponent's level 3, 1 of their level 5 and 1 of their level 7 Digimon.
- **Q4735** (2025-06-13) Q: This card is placed in the battle area while EX5-063 [Leviamon] and BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)] are in the trash. When an opponent's Digimon is played by an effect, can I use this card's <Delay> effect to play EX5-063 [Leviamon], then digivolve that Digimon into BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)] using its own {Trash} [All Turns] effect? — A: Yes, you can. When the opponent's Digimon is played by an effect, this card's <Delay> effect and BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]'s {Trash} [All Turns] effect will trigger simultaneously. You activate this card's <Delay> effect first and play EX5-063 [Leviamon] from the trash. EX5-063 [Leviamon]'s effect derived triggers, then BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]'s triggered {Trash} [All Turns] effect can digivolve EX5-063 [Leviamon] into BT15-081 [Leviamon (X Antibody)]. _(related: EX5-063)_
