## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Tamer with the [Hunter] trait in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] Suspend all of your opponent's Digimon and Tamers. Your opponent's cards don't unsuspend during their next unsuspend phase.

## Official Q&A
- No, it doesn't unsuspend. This card reads "Your opponent's cards don't unsuspend during their next unsuspend phase," and this also affects cards that were later played or moved to the battle area.
