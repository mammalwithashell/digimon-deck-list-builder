## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-032 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5277** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to give "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks" to a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? — A: You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the gained effect won't trigger.
- **Q5278** (2025-10-03) Q: What cards can be played by this card's [All Turns] effect? — A: 1 level 4 or lower yellow or black Digimon card, or 1 level 4 or lower [CS] trait Digimon card.
- **Q5279** (2025-10-03) Q: What cards can be played using this card's inherited effect? — A: 1 level 4 or lower yellow or black Digimon card, or 1 level 4 or lower [CS] trait Digimon card.
- **Q6250** (2026-05-08) Q: If this Digimon has BT23-027 [Angemon] in its digivolution cards and would be deleted in battle, can I first activate <Barrier> to prevent the deletion, then use this card's [All Turns] effect to play a Digimon card from digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it can be played. In such cases, you can also play BT23-027 [Angemon] from digivolution cards. However, if you first activate this card's [All Turns] effect to play BT23-027 [Angemon], its inherited effect will be lost, therefore you won't be able to activate <Barrier>. _(related: BT23-027)_
