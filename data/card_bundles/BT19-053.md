## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT19-053 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3099** (2024-09-20) Q: I have this card, BT19-053 [QueenBeemon], and another Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait in my battle area. My opponent uses BT5-110 [All Delete] and I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when multiples of my other Digimon would leave the battle area at the same time, and all of my other Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait are prevented from leaving. What happens if BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect activates afterward? — A: BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect places all of your Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait into your security stack. In such cases, this card's [All Turns] effect prevents all of your Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait from being deleted, but if BT19-053 [QueenBeemon]'s [All Turns] effect activates afterward, you can place all of your [Royal Base] trait Digimon that were affected by the deletion effect into your security stack. _(related: BT5-110)_
- **Q3106** (2024-09-20) Q: If I use this card's [When Attacking] effect to play a Digimon when this card attacks, can I suspend the played Digimon using this card's <Alliance>? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q3107** (2024-09-20) Q: Does this card's [All Turns] effect also trigger when this card itself would leave the battle area other than in battle? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3108** (2024-09-20) Q: If I activate this card's [All Turns] effect when multiples of my Digimon with the [Royal Base] trait would leave the battle area at the same time, can I place all of those Digimon face-up at the bottom of my security stack? — A: Yes, you can place all of those Digimon face-up at the bottom of your security stack. This card's [All Turns] effect affects all Digimon without having to choose them.
- **Q3109** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens to cards placed face up in the security stack by effects? — A: They become face-up security cards that remain revealed. Other than rules that specify face-up security cards, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security cards.
- **Q3110** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens upon a security check for a security card that is placed face-up? — A: The check is performed with the card left revealed. Other than rules for cards left revealed, the rules apply in the same manner as standard security checks.
- **Q3111** (2024-11-22) Q: Does a card's [Security] effect trigger upon a security check with that card placed face-up? — A: Yes, it triggers.
- **Q3112** (2024-11-22) Q: What happens if I shuffle a security stack that includes security cards placed face-up? — A: Any face-up cards are placed face down, then you shuffle the cards. After shuffling, all cards are left face-down.
