## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Main] Reveal the top 2 cards of your deck. Add 1 red card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck. Then, place this card in the battle area. [Main] ＜Delay＞ (By trashing this card after the placing turn, activate the effect.) ・1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a red Digimon card in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.

### Security Effect
[Security] Place this card in the battle area.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-103 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q682** (2025-10-03) Q: Can I digivolve this Digimon to a [Gallantmon] in my hand when an effect to digivolve one of my Digimon is activated if my opponent has a level 6 or higher Digimon? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q2624** (2025-01-24) Q: When does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger, exactly? — A: It will trigger when a Digimon is played by an effect such as the effect on BT5-092 [Nokia Shiramine] or when a Digimon digivolves by an effect such as the effect on P-103 [Offense Training]. It won't trigger for effects that aren't digivolution effects, such as BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s effect that treats a Tamer as a level 3 red Digimon or BT6-060 [Deputymon]'s effect that allows digivolution requirements to be ignored. _(related: BT5-092, BT4-011, BT6-060)_
- **Q2727** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q2736** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Tamer card placed under a Digimon become a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it becomes a digivolution card. If that Digimon would leave the field, the Tamer card will be trashed like a normal digivolution card.
- **Q3136** (2024-09-20) Q: Can I activate this card's [Main] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-103 [Offense Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: No, you can't. You can't activate a [Main] effect at the same time as another effect.
- **Q4016** (2024-03-28) Q: If I use an effect such as P-103 [Offense Training]'s <Delay> that digivolves a Digimon, can my [Agumon] digivolve into this card in the hand when I have 2 or fewer security cards? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q4188** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve into a card in my hand, can it digivolve regardless of its digivolution requirements? — A: No, this card's <Delay> effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card that meets the digivolution requirements.
- **Q4189** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to burst digivolve or DNA digivolve into a card in my hand? — A: No, you can't burst digivolve or DNA digivolve.
- **Q4190** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I use this card's <Delay> effect to digivolve a Tamer into a Digimon card with a "your Tamer is treated as a Digimon and can digivolve" effect? — A: No, you can't. This card's <Delay> effect digivolves 1 Digimon. It doesn't digivolve Tamers.
- **Q4191** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card's <Delay> effect but choose to not digivolve? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q4402** (2025-01-24) Q: When does this card's [All Turns] effect trigger, exactly? — A: It will trigger when a Digimon digivolves by an effect such as the effect on P-103 [Offense Training]. It won't trigger for effects that aren't digivolution effects, such as BT4-011 [Agunimon]'s effect that treats a Tamer as a level 3 red Digimon or BT6-060 [Deputymon]'s effect that allows digivolution requirements to be ignored. _(related: BT4-011, BT6-060)_
- **Q5210** (2025-10-03) Q: I have 2 or fewer security cards or 3 or more [Hero] trait Tamers with different names. Can I activate this card's [Your Turn] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-103 [Offense Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: Yes, you can.
- **Q5587** (2025-12-25) Q: Can I activate this card's {Hand} [Main] effect at the same time as an effect such as P-103 [Offense Training]'s effect that digivolves? — A: No, you can't.
