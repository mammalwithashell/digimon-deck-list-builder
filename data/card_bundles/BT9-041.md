## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may play 1 yellow or red Tamer card from your hand without paying its memory cost. Then, if [RizeGreymon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon’s digivolution cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn for each yellow or red Tamer you have in play. [Your Turn] For each Tamer you have in play, this Digimon gets +1000 DP.

## Official Q&A
- No. This effect specifies a card name, so you must have a card whose name is either [RizeGreymon] or [X Antibody] in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. Having a card with [X Antibody] in its traits doesn't count.
