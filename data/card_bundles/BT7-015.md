## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When playing this card from your hand, reduce its memory cost by 1 for each Option card in you and your opponent's trashes. [On Play] Return all cards with [Three Musketeers] in their traits and all Option cards from both players' trashes to the bottom of their owners' decks. If 7 or more cards were returned using this effect, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with [Three Musketeers] in its traits or 8000 DP or less.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT7-015 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1517** (2024-03-28) Q: Is this card’s play cost reduced by 1 for each Option card in ONLY my trash or my opponent’s trash? — A: No, its play cost is reduced by the total number of Option cards in both you and your opponent's trashes.
- **Q1518** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card’s [On Play] effect to return cards from both players’ trashes to the bottom of their owners’ decks. Who decides which order the cards are placed in? — A: The player who activated the effect decides the order in which their cards and their opponent's cards are returned to the bottom of each player's decks.
