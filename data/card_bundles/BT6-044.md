## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash the top card of your security stack to reveal the top 6 cards of your deck. Add up to 2 level 6 or lower Digimon cards among them to your hand. Trash the remaining cards. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When a card is removed from your security stack, if you have 3 or fewer security cards, trigger ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞. (Place the top card of your deck on top of your security stack.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT6-044 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1428** (2024-03-28) Q: Does this card’s [When Digivolving] effect force me to trash the top card of my security stack? — A: No, this card’s [When Digivolving] effect reads “you may,” so you can choose whether or not to trash the top card of your security stack.
- **Q1429** (2024-03-28) Q: Can I activate this card’s [When Digivolving] effect when my security stack is empty? — A: No. You must trash a card from your security stack in order to activate it.
- **Q1430** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card’s [When Digivolving] effect to trash a card from my security stack. If there are 3 or fewer cards left in my security stack, does this card’s [All Turns] effect trigger? — A: Yes, it does.
- **Q1431** (2024-03-28) Q: I have 2 or more copies of this Digimon in play. My opponent attacks with a Digimon, removing a card from my security stack. Do all of the effects on copies of this Digimon activate simultaneously, activating <Recovery +1 (Deck)> for each one? — A: Fundamentally, this is possible. However, the effects don’t activate simultaneously, but one at a time in order. Once <Recovery> results in you having 4 cards in your security stack, any remaining copies of the effect don't activate. Any [BT6-044 Dynasmon] whose effects don’t activate can still be activated later in the turn.
- **Q1432** (2024-03-28) Q: An opponent’s Digimon performs a security check, flipping over a card with a [Security] effect. Which activates first: This card’s [All Turns] effect, or the [Security] effect? — A: The [Security] effect activates first. After the [Security] effect and any effects triggered by its activation have finished activating, this card’s [All Turns] effect activates.
