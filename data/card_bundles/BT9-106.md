## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a Digimon with [X Antibody] in its traits in play, you may use this card without meeting its color requirements. [Main] You may digivolve 1 of your Digimon into a Digimon card with [Dex] or [DeathX] in its name from your trash for its memory cost.

### Security Effect
[Security] Add this card to its owner's hand.

## Official Q&A
- No, this effect does not allow ignoring digivolution requirements, so you can only digivolve into a card from your trash that meets the digivolution requirements and has [Dex]/[DeathX] in its name.
