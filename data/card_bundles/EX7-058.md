## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Until the end of your opponent's turn, give 1 of their Digimon "[End of Attack] Delete this Digimon." Then, if [LadyDevimon] or [X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 [Volée & Zerdrücken] Token. (Digimon/Lv. 4/Purple/5000 DP/＜Blocker＞＜Retaliation＞)

### Inherited Effect
[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon are deleted, you may play 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.

- You can give that effect to such Digimon, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects upon the trigger timing, the gained effect won't trigger.
