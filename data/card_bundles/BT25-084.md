## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By trashing 1 card in your hand, delete all of your opponent's highest DP Digimon. After, if played or digivolved by an effect, trash their top security card. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing 2 cards in your hand, it doesn't leave. [All Turns] When your hand is trashed from, delete 1 of your opponent's lowest DP Digimon.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-084 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6397** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "after" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't trash 1 card in your hand, you can't process the part after "after" in this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] effect.
- **Q6398** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I trash just 1 card in my hand for this card's 1st [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this 1st [All Turns] effect require you to trash the specified number of cards in your hand.
- **Q6399** (2026-05-08) Q: I activated this card's 1st [All Turns] effect when this card's DP becomes 0 and it would be deleted by a rule check, then it was prevented from being deleted. Can I then activate the 2nd [All Turns] effect? — A: No, you can't. After deletion is prevented by activating the 1st [All Turns] effect, a rule check occurs again, and this card is deleted because it has 0 DP. The 2nd [All Turns] effect can't activate because this card is deleted before it can activate.
- **Q6400** (2026-05-08) Q: When "trash 1 card in your hand" is performed twice, does a "when your hand is trashed from" effect trigger twice? — A: Yes, it triggers twice.
- **Q6401** (2026-05-08) Q: When "trashing 2 cards in your hand" is performed once, does a "when your hand is trashed from" effect trigger twice? — A: No, it only triggers once.
