## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-081 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7115** (2026-08-18) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect reduce an opponent's Digimon's DP even if I didn't play a card with it? — A: Yes, you can still reduce the DP of an opponent's Digimon even if you don't play a card with the effect.
- **Q7116** (2026-08-18) Q: All of my Digimon are currently getting -5000 DP, and I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a Digimon with 5000 DP or less. At such times, is the played Digimon deleted before the part of the effect that follows after "Then" is processed? — A: No, it is not immediately deleted at this point in time. All activated effects are resolved, after which a rule check occurs. At that point in time, all Digimon with 0 DP are deleted simultaneously.
