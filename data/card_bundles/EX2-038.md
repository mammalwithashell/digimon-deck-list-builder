- **Q3332** (2024-03-28) Q: If I have multiple Tamers in play, can I activate the same [When Digivolving] effect multiple times with this card's [When Attacking] effect? — A: Yes. You can activate the same [When Digivolving] effect as many times as you have Tamers in play.
