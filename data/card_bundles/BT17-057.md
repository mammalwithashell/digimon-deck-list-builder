## Printed text (official)
### Effect
While you have a black Tamer, cards from your trash may also be placed for this card's DigiXros. [On Play] [When Digivolving] By placing 1 [Cyborg] or [SoC] trait card from your trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, delete up to 7 play cost's total worth of your opponent's Digimon. [All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an opponent's effect, by trashing 2 [Cyborg], [Machine] or [SoC] trait cards in its digivolution cards, prevent it from leaving.

### Special Play Condition
DigiXros -2: [Machinedramon]×1 Lv.5 Digimon card w/[Cyborg] trait
