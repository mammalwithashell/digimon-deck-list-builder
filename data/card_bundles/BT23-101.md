  [Digivolve] Lv.3 w/[CS] trait: Cost 4 [Digivolve] While you have 4 or more [Hudie] trait Tamers, [Erika Mishima]: Cost 3

## Printed text (official)
### Effect
＜Alliance＞ [On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower [CS] trait card from your hand without paying the cost. Then, to 1 of your opponent's Digimon, give -3000 DP for the turn for each of your [Hudie] trait Digimon. [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By returning 1 of your [CS] trait Tamers to the hand, activate 1 of this Digimon's [On Play] effects.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT23-101 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q5257** (2025-10-03) Q: I used BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards to attack, and I used its 1st <Alliance> to suspend another of my Digimon. BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s [When Attacking] effect triggers at the same time, I use it to activate its [On Play] effect, play this card, and I use this card's [On Play] effect to DNA digivolve this card and the Digimon I suspended with <Alliance> into a [Shakkoumon] in the hand. Can I then use BT23-101 [Hudiemon]'s 2nd <Alliance> to suspend [Shakkoumon]? — A: Yes, you can. If BT23-101 [Hudiemon] with BT23-084 [Erika Mishima] in its digivolution cards attacks, the 2 instances of <Alliance> and the [When Attacking] effect will trigger simultaneously. If you use the 1st instance of <Alliance> to suspend your Digimon, use the [When Attacking] effect to activate the [On Play] effect, and play this card, its [On Play] effect will derived trigger and activate first. If this card's [On Play] effect is used to DNA digivolve the card suspended by the 1st instance of <Alliance> and this card into a [Shakkoumon], it will digivolve unsuspended, therefore the 2nd instance of <Alliance> can suspend that [Shakkoumon]. In addition, the DP that BT23-101 [Hudiemon] gains from the 1st instance of <Alliance> will remain, even if it DNA digivolves. _(a ruling on BT23-027; this card is named in it)_

- **Q5389** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack in the same turn? — A: No, it can't. Even if a card digivolves from a card that was placed on the field in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn. It can only attack in the turns after it's played.
- **Q5571** (2025-12-25) Q: Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to reduce the DP of multiple Digimon? — A: No, you can't. You choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon, then that Digimon's DP is reduced by this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q5572** (2025-12-25) Q: I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a card with an [On Play] effect. At such times, can I activate the [On Play] effect on the played card before processing the part of this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect after "then"? — A: No, you can't. Once all of the processing is resolved for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect, then you can activate the [On Play] effect on the played card.
- **Q5573** (2025-12-25) Q: All of my Digimon get -5000 DP. I used this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to play a Digimon with 5000 DP or less. At such times, is the played Digimon deleted before the part of the effect after "then"? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. Once all of the processing for this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect is resolved, then all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted at the same time.
- **Q6708** (2026-06-19) Q: Does the digivolution requirement that this card digivolve from a Tamer mean the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card is treated as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: No. Digivolve from the Tamer as such, and do not treat it as if it is a digivolving Digimon. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects don't trigger. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you can still digivolve from a Tamer with this digivolve requirement.
- **Q6709** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6710** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6711** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6712** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
