## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] Reveal the top 4 cards of your deck. Add 1 yellow card with [Angel], [Cherub], [Throne], [Authority], [Seraph], or [Virtue] in one of its traits and 1 card with [Four Great Dragons] in its traits among them to your hand. Place the rest at the bottom of your deck in any order.

### Inherited Effect
[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play a Digimon with [Four Great Dragons] in its traits, 1 of those Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn. (This Digimon may attack the turn it was played.)

## Official Q&A
- Yes, as long as either a yellow card with the [Angel], [Cherub], [Throne], [Authority], [Seraph], or [Virtue] trait or a card with the [Four Great Dragons] trait is among the cards revealed, you can add it to your hand.
