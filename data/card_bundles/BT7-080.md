## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] You may play 1 Tamer card with an inherited effect from your hand without paying its memory cost. [All Turns][Once Per Turn] When one of your Digimon with a Tamer card in its digivolution cards is deleted, you may play 1 Tamer card from your trash without paying its memory cost.
