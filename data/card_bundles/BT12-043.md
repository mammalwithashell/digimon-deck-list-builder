## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] For each yellow and/or red Tamer you have in play, 1 of your opponent's Digimon and all of your opponent's Security Digimon get -3000 DP for the turn. [Your Turn] All of your [Marcus Damon]s in play get +3000 DP and gain ＜Security Attack +1＞. (This Digimon checks 1 additional security card.)

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT12-043 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2173** (2026-03-13) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving] effect to choose opponent's Digimon and reduce their DP for each of my yellow or red Tamers? — A: No, you can't. You choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon, then that Digimon and all security Digimon get -3000 DP for each of your yellow or red Tamers.
- **Q2174** (2026-03-13) Q: If a [Marcus Damon] Tamer isn't treated as a Digimon and doesn't have DP, does this card's [Your Turn] effect treat it as having 3000 DP and give it <Security A. +1>? — A: It won't be treated as having DP, but it will gain <Security A. +1>. If a card doesn't have DP, it won't be treated as having DP, and DP can't be added to it. DP can be added if a [Marcus Damon] Tamer is treated as a Digimon and has DP.
