## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT4-109 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q1274** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to give a Digimon +3000 DP. If that Digimon now has 16000 DP or more, does it gain <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>? — A: Yes, it does. Even if the Digimon's original DP is less than 16000, if it becomes 16000 or greater after getting +3000 DP from this card's effect, it gains <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>.
- **Q1275** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to give one of my Digimon <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>. If another effect reduces that Digimon to 15000 or less, does it lose <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>? — A: No. Once the Digimon meets the conditions to gain <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>, it keeps them until the end of your opponent's next turn, even if its DP is reduced to 15000 or less.
- **Q1276** (2024-03-28) Q: I use this card's effect to give a Digimon +3000 DP, but it still has less than 16000 DP. If I then use another DP-boosting effect to raise its DP to 16000 or more, does it gain <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>? — A: No, it doesn't. The Digimon must have 16000 DP at the time this card's effect is activated for it to gain <Blocker>, <Reboot>, and <Security Attack +1>.
