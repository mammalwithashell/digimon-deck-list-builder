## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3. [End of Your Turn] By suspending this Tamer, activate 1 of the effects below: ・1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name or the [Free] trait in the hand. Reduce this effect's paid cost by 1 for each of your opponent's Digimon. ・2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into a [Free] trait Digimon card in the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=EX13-070 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q7440** (2026-09-18) Q: If a Digimon such as [BT5-021 Syakomon] with a "players can't reduce play costs" effect is in the battle area, can I activate this card's [End of Your Turn] effect and digivolve 1 of my Digimon into a specified card? — A: You can activate the effect and digivolve, but its cost won't be reduced. _(related: BT5-021)_
- **Q7441** (2026-09-18) Q: There are two copies of this card in the battle area. Can I activate both of their [End of Your Turn] effects and digivolve one specified card with its cost reduced by the combined amount from both effects? — A: No, you can't. You can't simultaneously activate multiple effects that digivolve a card.
