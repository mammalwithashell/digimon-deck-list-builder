## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[Hand] [Main] By placing 1 [Lobomon] and 1 [KendoGarurumon] from your trash under 1 of your [Koji Minamoto]s, digivolve it into this card as if that card is a level 4 blue Digimon for a digivolution cost of 3. [When Digivolving] By returning 1 card with the [Hybrid] trait from this Digimon's digivolution cards to the hand, 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers can't suspend until the end of their turn.

### Inherited Effect
[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has the [Hybrid] or [Ten Warriors] trait, return 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to the hand.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT17-026 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q2772** (2024-03-28) Q: When I use this card's [Main] effect to digivolve [Koji Minamoto] as a level 4 blue Digimon, can I digivolve by paying a cost of 4 using the standard digivolution requirements? — A: No, you can't. If digivolution is performed using the [Main] effect, you must digivolve for a digivolution cost of 3.
- **Q6568** (2026-06-19) Q: If an effect digivolves onto a Tamer as if it is a Digimon, do I treat the Tamer that becomes a digivolution card as if it is a Digimon when digivolving? — A: Yes, treat the Tamer as if it is a Digimon when digivolving. "When digivolving" effects and "when a Digimon digivolves" effects are triggered. Additionally, if a "Digimon can't digivolve" effect has activated, you cannot digivolve a Tamer as if it is a Digimon with this effect.
- **Q6569** (2026-06-19) Q: Do I perform a digivolution bonus draw even if I digivolve from a Tamer? — A: Yes, you do. A digivolution bonus draw is performed for any kind of digivolution.
- **Q6570** (2026-06-19) Q: If this card digivolves from a Tamer that was played this turn, can it attack this turn? — A: No, it can't. A card cannot attack on the turn it is played, even if it digivolves from a card placed on the field. It can only attack if it was not played that turn.
- **Q6571** (2026-06-19) Q: Is a Tamer card placed under a Digimon considered a digivolution card? — A: Yes, it is. If the Digimon leaves the field, that Tamer is trashed like normal digivolution cards.
- **Q6572** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the security effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: No, it does not.
- **Q6573** (2026-06-19) Q: Does a Digimon gain the inherited effects in the lower text of a Tamer card in its digivolution cards? — A: Yes, it does.
