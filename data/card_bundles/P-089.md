## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[When Digivolving] You may trash up to 3 blue cards from your hand. For each card trashed by this effect, you may choose 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers and trash it. Then, 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers without cards under it can't suspend until the end of your opponent's turn. [Opponent's Turn][Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, end that attack by returning 3 cards with [Jellymon] in their text from your trash to the bottom of your deck.

## Official Q&A
- No, you can't. A "by" condition can't be met if only some of the required actions are performed. The conditions for this [Opponent's Turn] effect require you to return the specified number of cards to the bottom of the deck.
