## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=P-189 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4979** (2025-07-04) Q: What order do a [Security] effect, "when [...] performs a security check" effect, and "when a card is removed from [...] security stack" effect activate when they trigger simultaneously upon a security check? — A: [Security] effects take precedence for activation. Upon a security check, a [Security] effect will immediately activate without pending activation. For other triggered effects, the turn player activates their effects first.
- **Q6520** (2026-05-08) Q: If this card is checked from a security stack, does its [Security] effect activate, then does it battle with the attacking Digimon? — A: Yes, its [Security] effect activates, then it battles.
