## Printed text (official)
### Effect
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards, 1 of your opponent's Digimon can't unsuspend until their turn ends.

### Inherited Effect
[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon deletes your opponent's Digimon in battle, gain 1 memory.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT22-047 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q4898** (2025-07-04) Q: What does "if this Digimon's stack has 2 or more same-level cards" mean, exactly? — A: It refers to when there are 2 or more cards with the same level among all of the stacked cards. Examples include when a level 5 Digimon has a level 5 digivolution card or when a level 6 Digimon has 2 level 5 digivolution cards.
- **Q4899** (2025-07-04) Q: Can this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect suspend a different card from the one that's given "can't unsuspend until the end of their turn"? — A: Yes, it can.
- **Q4900** (2025-07-04) Q: Can I activate this card's inherited effect even if my opponent's Digimon and the Digimon that has this card in its digivolution cards are deleted at the same timing? — A: No, you can't activate it.
