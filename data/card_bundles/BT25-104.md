## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT25-104 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6494** (2026-05-08) Q: Does a burst digivolve without notes also trash the top card of the stack at the end of the turn it digivolved, as with normal burst digivolve? — A: Yes, you trash it. The standard rules apply even when notes aren't shown.
- **Q6495** (2026-05-08) Q: I used [Final Shining Burst], and its [Main] effect caused the DP of my opponent's Digimon to become 0. At such times, is the Digimon with a DP of zero deleted? — A: No, it isn't deleted yet. After trashing the used Option card or digivolving by Arts Digivolve, all of the Digimon with 0 DP are deleted upon the rule check. Then, if the deleted card has an [On Deletion] effect, it will trigger simultaneously with this card's [When Digivolving] effect.
- **Q6496** (2026-05-08) Q: What does "activate 1 [Main] effect on this card's Option side" mean, exactly? — A: A [Main] effect shown on the DUAL card's Option side will activate. Activated effects on the Option side are treated as Option card effects.
- **Q6497** (2026-05-08) Q: Is an "activate 1 [Main] effect on this card's Option side" effect considered to be using an Option card? — A: No, it isn't considered to be using an Option card. This effect directly activates a [Main] effect shown on the DUAL card's Option side.
- **Q6498** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I use this card's [When Digivolving][When Attacking] effect to activate the [Main] effect on the Option side and give -15000 DP to a Digimon that isn't affected by Digimon effects? — A: Yes, you can. If an effect on the Option side is activated, it's considered to be an Option card effect. Because it isn't a Digimon effect, the DP will be reduced by -15000.
- **Q6499** (2026-05-08) Q: What does an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect do, exactly? — A: It causes that card to also be treated as a Digimon with X000 DP. For example, if a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon, it can attack like a standard Digimon, and it will gain inherited effects from cards stacked under it. However, even if a Tamer is played and then treated as a Digimon in the same turn, it can't attack during that turn.
- **Q6500** (2026-05-08) Q: If a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon using an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, is it no longer considered a Tamer? — A: No, it is treated as both a Digimon and a Tamer.
- **Q6501** (2026-05-08) Q: If a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon using an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect and it activates an effect, is it treated as both a Digimon effect and a Tamer effect? — A: Yes, it's treated as an effect of both. Because the card is treated as both a Digimon and a Tamer, the activated effect is treated as both a Digimon effect and a Tamer effect.
- **Q6502** (2026-05-08) Q: If a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon using an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, then another effect causes its DP to become 0, is it deleted upon a rule check? — A: Yes, it's deleted upon the rule check timing.
- **Q6503** (2026-05-08) Q: If a Tamer is also treated as a Digimon using an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, and later it gains another "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" effect, what happens? — A: In the case of multiple triggered effects that state "is also treated as an X000 DP Digimon", the later effect overwrites the play cost, level and DP. However, if the card was under the effect of a persistent effect that states "is also treated as an X000 DP Digimon", that persistent effect will continue to overwrite the information. Any gained effects such as <Rush> will be added on top of that.
- **Q6504** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I gain memory using an effect on a Tamer that's treated as a Digimon with X000 DP due to an effect while my opponent has "your opponent can't gain memory other than by Tamer effects" activated? — A: Yes, you can. When an effect is activated on a card that's treated as both a Digimon and a Tamer, the effect is treated as both a Digimon effect and a Tamer effect, therefore you can gain memory.
- **Q6505** (2026-05-08) Q: If an opponent's card has "isn't affected by Digimon effects" and it's chosen for an "is also treated as a Digimon with X000 DP" Tamer effect that treats it as a Digimon, will the chosen opponent Digimon be affected by effects? — A: No, it won't be affected by effects. When an effect is activated on a card that's treated as both a Digimon and a Tamer, the effect is treated as both a Digimon effect and a Tamer effect, therefore the chosen opponent Digimon won't be affected by effects.
- **Q6506** (2026-05-08) Q: I have a [Marcus Damon] that is treated as a Digimon with 6000 DP. My Digimon in the battle area digivolves into this card, and I use this card's [All Turns] effect to increase that [Marcus Damon]'s DP to 12000 and give it <Rush>. What happens to that [Marcus Damon] if this card is then removed from the battle area? — A: It will be treated as a Digimon with 6000 DP, and it will lose <Rush>. This card's [All Turns] effect is always activated while it's in the battle area. As soon as this card is removed from the battle area, the effect will be lost, and the [Marcus Damon] will be treated as it was before.
- **Q6507** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I reference this card as an Option card with the traits shown on this card? — A: Yes, you can. This card is treated as an Option card with the [DATA SQUAD] trait.
- **Q6947** (2026-08-18) Q: The battle area currently contains this card with digivolution cards and a Marcus Damon that is being treated as a Digimon due to this card's effect. My opponent activates the effect "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -X000 DP until the end of turn. Then, <De-Digivolve X> 1 of their Digimon" and reduces Marcus Damon's DP to 0 then deletes this card with <De-Digivolve>. At this time, the Marcus Damon has lost its DP and is no longer treated as a Digimon due to this card's deletion. Is it deleted upon a rule check? — A: No, it is not deleted. The Marcus Damon loses its DP and is no longer being treated as a Digimon before a rule check occurs. Therefor, it is no longer subject to the rule "Digimon with 0 DP are destroyed" when a rule check occurs, and it is not destroyed.
