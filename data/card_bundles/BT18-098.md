## Printed text (official)
### Effect
When this card is trashed from the security stack, activate its [Security] effects. While you have a yellow Digimon with the [Data] or [Witchelny] trait, you may ignore this card's color requirements. [Main] By trashing your top security card, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP until the end of their turn. Then, if you have 2 or fewer security cards, place this card as your bottom security card.

## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT18-098 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q3049** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I activate the "when an effect trashes this card from the security stack" effect when this card is revealed from the security stack or when searching/looking at this card from the security stack? — A: No, you can't. You can only activate the "when an effect trashes this card from the security stack" when this card is directly trashed from the security stack.
- **Q3050** (2026-05-08) Q: Can I process the part of the effect after "then" in this card's [Main] effect without meeting the "by" condition? — A: No, you can't. If you don't trash your security card, you can't process the part after "then" in this card's [Main] effect.
