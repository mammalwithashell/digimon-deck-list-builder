## Official Q&A
_Source: https://world.digimoncard.com/rule/?card_no=BT26-012 (via data/card_official_qa.json)_
- **Q6966** (2026-08-18) Q: There are two copies of this card in the battle area. Can I activate both of their [Main] effects and play or use one specified card with its cost reduced by the combined amount from both effects? — A: No, you can't. You can't simultaneously activate multiple effects that play or use a card.
- **Q6967** (2026-08-18) Q: If a Digimon with the effect "Players can't reduce play costs," such as [ST12-03 Solarmon], is in the battle area, can I activate this card's [Main] effect and play a specified card? — A: You can activate it and play the card, but the cost will not be reduced. _(related: ST12-03)_
- **Q6968** (2026-08-18) Q: If a Digimon with the effect "Players can't play Digimon by effects," such as [BT9-047 Pomumon], is in the battle area, can I activate this card's [Main] effect and play a specified card? — A: You can activate it, but the card can't be played. _(related: BT9-047)_
