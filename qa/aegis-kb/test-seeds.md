# Official-ruling test seeds: most-played implemented cards (2026-10-08)

These seeds come from Bandai's official Q&A rulings, now mirrored in `data/card_official_qa.json`
and the card bundles (see `README.md`). They cover the 30 DSL-implemented cards that appear most
often in `data/deck_library.json` decklists, with up to 3 non-boilerplate rulings each (75
rulings). The full backlog of 2,386 rulings is in `implemented-rulings.json`.

How to read the table:
- **Coverage**: `covered` means an existing test asserts exactly this ruled outcome. `partial`
  means a nearby test misses the ruling's edge. `missing` means no test. `blocked` means a card
  the ruling needs has no YAML.
- **YAML reading**: from *reading* the YAML and engine source. **Nothing was run.** Treat
  `suspect` as "write the test first and see", not as a confirmed bug.
- **Cross-listed rulings**: some rulings are listed on a card's official page only because the
  card is *named* in a ruling about another card (`about` in `data/card_official_qa.json`). Those
  rows name the card the ruling is actually about, and their test belongs with that card.
- **Assertions**: pseudo-Rust in the `DebugRunner` vocabulary used under
  `code/digimon-engine/tests/cards_behavioral/`. They are written to include a control wherever
  a false pass is possible.

## Highest-value findings (write these tests first)

Of these, I re-checked the Gallantmon, Agumon - Bond of Bravery and Omnimon Alter-S items myself
against the YAML. Nothing in this section has been run.

1. **Gallantmon (P-186), Q4629 and Q4630**: the printed text "Delete 1 Digimon with 13000 DP or
   more" is mandatory, but the YAML's `select_any_permanent` is `optional: true`. Recovery is
   also gated on `binding_absent` ("nothing was picked") instead of "nothing was deleted", so a
   pick that can't be deleted skips the Recovery the ruling grants. The existing test checks
   only the clause flag.
2. **ShineGreymon: Ruin Mode (EX4-074), Q3524**: the [End of Attack] delete has the same
   optional-on-mandatory pick.
3. **Digimon Emperor (BT8-094), Q1770**: an engine path. `decode_breeding` →
   `move_from_breeding` moves on to the main phase with no memory check, so a breeding-phase
   move that hands the opponent memory probably still gives the turn player a main phase.
4. **Omnimon (X Antibody) (BT20-102), Q4419**: the forced end-of-turn attack doesn't check
   whether an attack is already in progress. With two copies, the second copy may grant a
   second attack.
5. **Physical Training (P-105) / Wisdom Training (P-108), Q4196 and Q4208**: the `<Delay>`
   digivolve offers any same-colour hand Digimon, including illegal picks that waste the Delay.
   DCGO offers only legal targets, so these are dead choices in the RL action space.
6. **Agumon - Bond of Bravery (LM-021), Q4016**: the YAML lacks the printed alt-digivolve
   "[Digivolve] While you have 2 or fewer security cards, [Agumon]: Cost 3"; `alt_paths` has
   only the Red Lv.6 circle and Blast Digivolve.
7. **DeathXmon (BT9-112)**: the cost reduction counts every permanent in the opponent's battle
   area, including Options placed there, but the card counts only Digimon and Tamers.
8. **Dan Yuki & Kanan Yuki (BT24-085)**: the [End of Turn] clause is gated on having a [TS]
   Digimon, which the printed text doesn't say. Check DCGO `BT24_085.cs`.
9. **MedievalGallantmon (EX8-074)**: the YAML header says black/green, "any Lv.5, cost 4"; the
   official DB and `cards.json` say green/red, from green or red Lv.5, cost 3. Check the image
   and the `alt_paths`.
10. **Omnimon Alter-S (EX9-021)**: two [When Digivolving] tests accept "nothing deleted" as a pass.
    The YAML header comment lists only the Blue circle, but production `evo_costs` has both, so
    that part is comment-only.

Readings that need a run to settle (0-DP deletion racing a breeding-move trigger): Ukkomon
(BT16-082) Q2670, Ukkomon (P-123) Q4238, Lui Ohwada (P-130) Q4243. Also Venusmon (BT10-042)
Q1964 (current tests stage the Security Attack modifier by hand).

  - Q4016: Agumon - Bond of Bravery (LM-021)

- **Possible missing alt path on Agumon - Bond of Bravery (LM-021).** The YAML has no "[Digivolve] While you have 2 or fewer security cards, [Agumon]: Cost 3" path, even though the official bundle lists it. The test file mentions it only in its header as `xros_req`. Unless the engine adds that path from card JSON somewhere I didn't find, the card can't digivolve from Agumon. Also check whether `burst_digivolve: marker: true, cost: 0` is the right encoding for <Blast Digivolve>.

The YAML has the blue circle and the [DM]-trait path but no red standard circle. A non-DM red Lv.6 currently cannot digivolve into it. Check this against the card image.

### Batch 1

- **P-186 (Gallantmon) has two likely bugs in the same clause.** (a) `select_any_permanent: optional: true` lets the player skip a mandatory delete and take Recovery instead, which Q4629 forbids. (b) Recovery is gated on `binding_absent`, not on whether a deletion actually happened, so a chosen-but-protected target gives no Recovery, which contradicts Q4630. The fix needs a mandatory select with fizzle-on-zero-candidates, plus a "deleted nothing" condition such as a deleted-count binding.
- **EX4-074 (ShineGreymon: Ruin Mode), [End of Attack]:** same pattern. `optional: true` on `select_opponent_permanent` exposes PASS when targets exist. The YAML comment claims the select is "still requiring a choice when targets do", but `optional: true` means PASS is offered. This contradicts Q3524 and the mandatory printed text.

Neither path calls `check_turn_end()`, unlike the breeding-digivolve path at `breeding.rs:451`. So a memory swing during the breeding phase still grants a main phase. If a deferred turn end does fire inside the drain, the later `enter_main_phase()` would act on the next player instead. Either way it needs a test.

- **Blocked related cards:** Mimi Tachikawa (BT1-089) and Lighdramon (BT8-053) have no YAML, so the rulings that name them can't be tested with the real cards. The engine paths can still be exercised with stand-ins: `move_from_breeding_by_effect` for Q1769, and a synthetic green+blue Digimon for Q4704.
- **P-130 (Lui Ohwada) `on_move` clause:** it fires on any own `on_move` event with no "from breeding" scope (only owner and kind), and checks for *any* unsuspended P-130 rather than the source. That works for one copy, but with two copies on the field both would see the other as unsuspended. Worth a check.

### Batch 3

- **BT20-102 Q4419 (possible engine bug).** Nothing in the `force_attack` / `may_attack_now` path checks for an attack already in progress, and the two copies have separate once-per-turn counters. Depending on when copy 2's trigger drains (inside the first attack or after it), the engine may allow a second attack that the ruling forbids. This is the highest-value test in the batch.
- **P-105 / P-108 hand-card filter (suspect; affects RL).** After a Digimon is chosen, the `select_hand` step offers every Digimon of the right colour in hand, whether or not it can legally digivolve onto that Digimon. The YAML comments say an illegal pick "no-ops without paying", so the agent can spend its Delay on a choice that was never legal. DCGO limits the choice to Digimon with a legal digivolution. Q4196 and Q4208 pin this.
- **P-105 vs P-108 structural drift.** The two cards have near-identical text but differently shaped YAMLs. P-105 uses `select_reveal` + `place_remainder_on_deck` + an explicit `place_self_as_delay_option`, and its `select_hand` is not wrapped in `if binding_exists: target`. P-108 uses `select_reveal_buckets` + `order_remainder` + auto-seating. Consider moving P-105 to P-108's shape: "bottom in any order" ordering choice, guarded hand pick.
- **BT24-085 unprinted gate.** The `[End of Your Turn]` condition requires a `[TS]` Digimon on your field (`any_permanent … trait_has: TS`). The printed text has no such requirement: with no TS Digimon you could still suspend the Tamer to use the TS Option. Check this against DCGO `BT24_085.cs`.
- **BT4-097 Kari Kamiya condition.** The condition is `any_permanent { card_number_is: BT4-097, is_unsuspended }`, not a check on the source itself. With two Karis, one of them suspended, the suspended copy's trigger would pass. This is unrelated to the rulings here but worth a ticket.
- **Rulings about other cards' effects.** Q1415 (about ModokiBetamon, BT6-021) and Q1252 (about Kari Kamiya, BT4-097) are filed under the Option card in the batch but rule on another card. BT6-021 is not implemented, so Q1415 can be tested now only with a synthetic `CannotGainMemoryExceptFromTamers` modifier. Q5575's subject, Bukamon (BT24-002), has only a `.json` stub.
- **BT1-090 judge-quiz Q10/Q11** apply Gravity Crush's deltas directly with `gain_memory_for_player(b,-2)`, so they do not exercise the YAML. They give no coverage for the floodgate interaction.
