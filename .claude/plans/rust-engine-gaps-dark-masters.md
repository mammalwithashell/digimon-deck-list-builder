# Rust engine gap-closure plan — Dark Masters

## Goal

Close the **53 blocking** and **0 partial** engine gaps below so that **Dark Masters** can be fully implemented in `digimon-engine/` without stubs, approximations, or auto-selections (per CLAUDE.md §17–18).

This prompt was generated by `/assess-archetype-rust` on 2026-04-18. Re-run that skill if card text, the deck list, or the engine API has changed.

**Audit headline:** 58 unique cards, **0 SUPPORTED, 0 PARTIAL, 58 BLOCKED.** None of the cards are even partially expressible against today's `digimon-engine/` scripting surface — every card requires at least one missing primitive. The archetype is dominated by (a) a `[Dark Masters]` Lv6 chassis with conditional play-cost reduction, transient-summon EOT cleanup, color-conditional self-place-as-security, and a name-restricted digivolve target, (b) Ace Lv6 Counter cards that all gate on `<Blast Digivolve>` from a Counter window the engine doesn't expose, and (c) a deep tail of inherited-effect DigiEggs whose triggers never fire because `enqueue_from_permanent` doesn't walk the digivolution stack.

The same archetype audits already in `docs/RUST_ENGINE_GAPS.md` (Medusamon, DNA Omnimon, TS Olympos) re-surface here with new card additions; only **21 new gap entries** were filed because the existing 32 entries already cover most of the primitive surface. Read those existing entries first — many of the foundational fixes will close large swathes of Dark Masters in one stroke.

## Foundational gaps (close these first — they unblock the rest)

These are NOT ordered by Dark Masters card count; they are ordered by **dependency depth**. Every Dark Masters Digimon card depends on at least one of these.

1. **Inherited triggered-effect dispatch — `enqueue_from_permanent` must walk digivolution stack** (NEW, 🔴, 6 cards directly + every Lv3+ inherited-trigger card archetype-wide). This is THE foundational gap: today, any `Effect::inherited(card)` with a triggerable timing (`OnDeletion`, `EndOfYourTurn`, `WhenAttacking`, `OnSecurityCheck`, etc.) silently never fires when the card is an under-card. The fix is mechanical (extend the existing `is_under == effect.inherited` guard from tensor/mask into `enqueue_from_permanent`). Without this, every DigiEgg in the audit pool (BT3-006, BT14-001, BT15-006, BT16-006, BT17-001, BT24-001) is dead. See `docs/RUST_ENGINE_GAPS.md` section "Inherited triggered-effect dispatch".

2. **Native printed keyword parsing** (existing 🔴, 25+ Dark Masters cards). Architectural: `CardData` needs `keywords: Vec<Keyword>` ingestion from `effect_text`. Every Lv6 in the pool prints `<Blast Digivolve>`, `<Reboot>`, `<Blocker>`, `<Raid>`, `<Rush>`, `<Vortex>`, `<Alliance>`, `<Piercing>`, `<Retaliation>`, or parametric `<De-Digivolve N>` / `<Security A. ±N>`. Without native parsing, every card needs a per-card `Effect::on_play(...).grant_keyword(...)` boilerplate AND face-keyword tensor slots stay zero. See `docs/RUST_ENGINE_GAPS.md` section "Native printed keyword parsing".

3. **Phase-granular turn timings** (existing 🔴, ~20 Dark Masters cards) — broaden to include `EndOfOpponentsTurn` dispatch (NEW, 4 cards). `WhenAttacking`, `EndOfAttack`, `EndOfBattle`, `StartOfYourMainPhase`, `StartOfYourTurn` are enum-declared but unfired (or have no builder constructor). `EndOfOpponentsTurn` is enum-declared but `game_phases.rs::end_turn` only fires `EndOfYourTurn` for the ending player. Every Lv6 Dark Masters Digimon's `[On Play][When Attacking]` body and every `[End of Opponent's Turn]` revival clause depends on this. See `docs/RUST_ENGINE_GAPS.md` sections "Phase-granular turn timings" and "`EndOfOpponentsTurn` effect timing not dispatched".

## Gaps to close (grouped by subsystem)

### Subsystem A — Selection & action-space infrastructure (selection-kind expansion)

4. **Selection: opponent-as-selecting-player, cross-side target, union-zone (hand OR trash), DNA-pair, multi-pick from reveal** (existing 🔴, 12 Dark Masters cards). Adds new `select_hand_or_trash`, `select_hand_or_battle_area` (the latter is a NEW union-zone sub-kind surfaced by Dark Masters' EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper), `select_dna_pair`, opponent-as-selector variants.
5. **Selection: ordered permutation** (existing 🔴, 4 Dark Masters cards). Required for "Return the rest to the bottom of the deck" tail of every reveal-top-N effect.
6. **Selection: multi-select with aggregate-sum constraint** (existing 🔴, no new cards but coupled to gap #41).
7. **In-effect branch-choice selector** (existing 🔴, 1 Dark Masters card — EX4-051 BlitzGreymon's "Activate 1 of the effects below" with three branches).

### Subsystem B — Zone manipulation helpers

8. **Zone-manipulation: play-from-hand / trash without paying cost (+ cost override)** (existing 🔴, 13+ Dark Masters cards). Returns `Option<PermanentHandle>` so gap #29 can chain provenance tagging.
9. **Effect-driven play of a Digimon from hand to an empty breeding-area slot** (NEW, 4 cards). Sibling helper that targets the breeding zone instead of the battle area.
10. **Effect-initiated play from face-up security stack (search-then-play-free)** (NEW, 6 cards). Distinct from BT14-033 Patamon's digivolve-from-security; Dark Masters needs the *play* shape with a face-up filter.
11. **Zone-manipulation: return-to-hand / return-to-deck / bounce self / trash-from-hand** (existing 🔴, 10 Dark Masters cards). Many sub-shapes; `ctx.trash_from_hand(player, hand_index)` is foundational.
12. **Zone-manipulation: reveal-top-N deck + add-to-hand + hatch** (existing 🔴, 6 Dark Masters cards).
13. **Zone-manipulation: security stack operations (trash top, place top/bottom, trash N, Recovery +N, shuffle security)** (existing 🔴, 5+ Dark Masters cards). Includes the `[On Deletion] place this Digimon face up as the bottom security card` color-conditional self-place-as-security pattern that all four EX10-* Dark Masters Lv6s need.
14. **Search-own-security-stack primitive (face-up-only filter)** (existing 🔴, 1 Dark Masters card — P-216).
15. **Forced opponent hand reduction primitive** (NEW, 1 card — BT19-075 MoonMillenniummon).
16. **Place card at a specific stack position (bottom-source / under another permanent) + alt-digivolve + stack reorder** (existing 🔴, 7 Dark Masters cards). Includes `EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper`'s "from in play or your hand" union-source-zone variant and `EX9-068 Analogman`'s face-down placement.
17. **Cast-time stack-construction for cost reduction** (NEW, 1 card — BT15-102 Apocalymon). Sibling to DigiXros (parity §4.7e).
18. **Zone-manipulation: effect-initiated digivolve (free / reduced / trait-filter / DNA / Blast)** (existing 🔴, 1 new Dark Masters card — EX4-051 BlitzGreymon).
19. **De-Digivolve N primitive (single + mass)** (existing 🔴, 10 Dark Masters cards — every Counter-Blast Lv6 in the pool uses this).

### Subsystem C — Modifier registry expansions

20. **Player-scoped modifier registry** (existing 🔴, 5 Dark Masters cards). Add `CannotPlayDigimonByEffect`, `IgnoreColorRequirement`, plus NEW Dark-Masters-driven variants: `CannotSuspend` (player-scope), `CannotAddSecurityByEffect`. The registry itself is the cross-cutting infrastructure.
21. **`CannotAttackPlayer` modifier enforcement (mask + combat)** (NEW, 2 cards — BT9-103, EX2-046). Modifier exists; mask and combat don't consult it. Pure wiring fix.
22. **Conditional digivolve-target restriction (filter on candidate top-card name/trait/level/color)** (NEW, 7 cards — every Lv6 Dark Masters Digimon "can only digivolve into [Apocalymon]" or "into white Digimon").
23. **Condition-gated modifier entries + new `Expiry` variants** (existing 🔴, 1 Dark Masters card direct — EX10-010 — plus prerequisite for gap #24).
24. **Player-scope mass `CannotSuspend` aura on opponent (condition-gated and / or stack-depth-filtered)** (NEW, 2 cards — EX8-026, BT16-026). Depends on gap #20 (player-scope registry) + gap #23 (condition-closure modifier entries).
25. **Permanent-scoped modifier to suppress effect activation by timing** (existing 🔴, 1 Dark Masters card — BT19-093 Queen Device).
26. **Grant Security A. ±N modifier to a targeted permanent (parametric SecurityAttackChange)** (existing 🔴, 2 Dark Masters cards).

### Subsystem D — Cost-payment / activation-cost generalization

27. **Dynamic cost reduction at `BeforePayCost`** (existing 🔴, 16 Dark Masters cards). Closure-valued, selection-gated, suspend/return/trash/delete-as-cost. The generic `.pay_cost(|ctx| bool)` builder hook is the unifying need.
28. **Generic `.activation_cost(...)` builder hook for triggered abilities** (NEW, 7 cards — BT4-097, BT8-090, ST6-14, BT8-094, EX9-068, BT13-102, RB1-035). Sibling to gap #27 but for triggered abilities (not BeforePayCost). Both should land in the same `EffectBuilder` API expansion.

### Subsystem E — Combat interrupts (Counter / Block / Alliance window infrastructure)

29. **Counter window + `<Blast Digivolve>` activation flow ([Hand][Counter] play path)** (NEW, 8 cards — every Ace Counter Lv6 in the audit pool). Foundational Counter-window machinery + `Effect::counter(card)` builder + `ctx.prompt_blast_digivolve` primitive. Couples with parity §2.3 (Block / Counter / Alliance interrupt phases).
30. **Raid target-switch interrupt (scripting-surface, not mask-only) + effect-driven attack redirect** (existing 🔴, 1 Dark Masters card — EX10-010 BlackWarGreymon).
31. **`<Reboot>` keyword enforcement in opponent's unsuspend phase** (existing 🔴, 4 Dark Masters cards).
32. **`OnAllyAttack` / `OnOpponentAttack` observer timings not fired** (NEW, 1 card — BT15-008 Muchomon).

### Subsystem F — Leave-field replacement framework + dependent keywords

This is its own architectural cluster — every keyword in (34)–(36) builds on the framework in (33).

35. **`<Scapegoat>` keyword (delete-another-own-Digimon-as-cost variant)** (NEW, 1 card — LM-043 Darkdramon). Built atop gap #33.
36. **OnDeletion cause discriminator** (NEW, 1 card — BT17-068 Mephistomon). Distinct from gap #33's replacement-time cause attribution; this is the *observer* path's cause flag.

### Subsystem H — Provenance / lifetime tracking + scheduled effects

## Ask

Produce a phased implementation plan (per `superpowers:writing-plans`) that:

1. **Foundational tier first.** Phase 1 must close gaps #1 (inherited triggered-effect dispatch), #2 (native keyword parsing), and #3 (phase-granular turn timings + `EndOfOpponentsTurn`). These three are pre-requisites for almost every other gap — without them, fixing zone-manipulation or modifier work in isolation produces dead code paths.

2. **Group remaining gaps by subsystem** (Subsystems A–J above) and order by dependency:
   - Subsystem F (leave-field replacement framework) is architectural — flag it for a standalone spec under `docs/superpowers/specs/` BEFORE coding. Subsystem J (Option-card play flow + Plug-In) likely needs its own spec too.
   - Subsystem D (`.pay_cost` / `.activation_cost` generic builder hooks) should land before Subsystems C and G that consume them.
   - Subsystem E (combat interrupts) is its own architectural cluster — flag for a standalone spec; gap #29 (Counter window + Blast Digivolve) is the highest-blast-radius single fix in the audit (8 Ace Counter cards) and should drive the spec.
   - Subsystem B (zone manipulation) gates on the play-free helpers returning `Option<PermanentHandle>` — make that signature decision in Phase 2 so Subsystem H (provenance / EOT delete rider) can chain.
   - Subsystem G (global observers) gates on Subsystem F's cause-attribution + Subsystem D's `.activation_cost`.

3. **For each phase, list:**
   - Exact API surface to add (function signatures, enum variants — copy from the `Suggested API shape` lines in `RUST_ENGINE_GAPS.md`; do not restate, reference by gap title).
   - Tests to write FIRST per CLAUDE.md §18 — name the `digimon-engine/tests/*.rs` file and the failing test case (one per primitive, exercising at least one Dark Masters card).
   - Affected Rust files (file paths from the References section above).
   - Parity implications: does this resolve or create a `docs/RUST_PYTHON_PARITY.md` entry? (Several do — see RUST_PYTHON_PARITY §1.4 for `EndOfOpponentsTurn`, §2.3 for Counter/Block/Alliance, §2.5b for `OnSecurityCheck`, §2.5a for `play_from_security`, §4.2b for color-bypass, §4.6d for selection kinds, §4.7e for DigiXros, §4.7x for context-aware modifier queries.)

4. **Flag architectural items for standalone specs:**
   - Leave-field replacement framework (Subsystem F)
   - Combat interrupt phases (Subsystem E, especially Counter window)
   - Option-card play flow + Plug-In/Link mechanic (Subsystem J's gap #50)
   - Player-scoped modifier registry expansion (Subsystem C's gap #20 — adds a new top-level data structure to `ModifierRegistry`)
   - Native keyword parsing (Subsystem foundational gap #2 — touches `card_data.rs`, `card_registry.rs`, ingestion pipeline)

5. **Do NOT attempt to close every gap in one phase.** Prefer **6–8 phases** with clear stop points and review checkpoints. After each phase, the orchestrator should re-run `/assess-archetype-rust Dark Masters` to verify the audit verdict counts shift (PARTIAL / SUPPORTED counts increasing as gaps close), and a smaller per-batch run of `/batch-implement-cards-rust` can land cards once their gating gaps are closed.

6. **Sequencing constraint — do NOT begin `/batch-implement-cards-rust Dark Masters` until at minimum:**
   - Phase 1 (foundational tier #1–#3) is landed.
   - Subsystem D (`.pay_cost` / `.activation_cost` builder hooks) is landed.
   - At least one of Subsystem E / F / J is landed (otherwise the highest-frequency Lv6 chassis cards in the pool — EX10-* and the Ace Counter Lv6s — remain BLOCKED even with all the foundational work done).

7. **Estimate scope.** With 53 distinct primitives required and 5–6 of them being architectural (specs needed), this is a multi-week effort. The plan should include a `Phase 0` that lands the 4–5 standalone specs in parallel before the first implementation phase begins.
