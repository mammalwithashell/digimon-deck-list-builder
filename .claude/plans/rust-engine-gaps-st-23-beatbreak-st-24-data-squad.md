# Rust engine gap-closure plan — ST-23 BEATBREAK + ST-24 DATA SQUAD starter decks

## Goal

Close the 12 blocking and 4 partial engine/DSL gaps below so that the ST-23 BEATBREAK and ST-24 DATA SQUAD starter decks (30 cards combined) can be fully implemented in `code/digimon-engine/` without stubs, approximations, or auto-selections (per CLAUDE.md §17–18).

Both starter decks introduce a shared new substrate — the **Tamer face-down stash** mechanic — that does not exist anywhere in the engine today. Cards stash face-down cards under their Tamers (deck top, hand cards, or themselves in the case of in-battle-area Options); later effects pay activation costs by trashing the bottom face-down stashed card from under a chosen Tamer; some Tamers themselves trigger when "effects trash cards from under this Tamer". This substrate gates roughly two-thirds of the 30 cards. The remaining gaps are local to one card each.

This prompt was generated by `/assess-archetype-rust` on 2026-05-17. Re-run that skill if card text, deck list, or engine API has changed.
