## Bundled community art

The desktop client bundles community-made art, used with thanks. If you are a
rights holder and want something removed, please open an issue.

- **Card sleeves** — from [WE-Kaito / digimon-tcg-simulator](https://github.com/WE-Kaito/digimon-tcg-simulator).
- **Digimon pixel sprites** — from [Project Drasil](https://project-drasil.online).

These assets live under `code/frontend/src/design/assets/` and are indexed by
`code/frontend/src/design/assets/manifest.ts`. An in-app credits surface
(`code/frontend/src/design/components/Credits.tsx`) mirrors this attribution.
